# Decision of the President of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers No. (20) of 2018 on the appointment of assistant prosecutors

Prime Minister,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And Law / Act No. (10) of 2002 regarding the Public Prosecution, as amended by Law / Act No. (4) of 2005,

And upon the proposal of the Attorney General,

Has decided as follows:

## Materials (1-2)

### Article 1

Appointed to the position of Assistant Prosecutor are:

1- Mr. Mohammed Hamad Mohammed Ali Noura.

2- Mr. Ali Abdulrahman Ali Safar Al-Haidous.

3- Mr. Abdulrahman Abdullatif Hassan Abdullah Al-Ishaq.

4- Mr. Talal Hussein Abdullah Mohammed Reda.

### Article 2

All relevant authorities, each within its area of competence, must implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the date of its issuance. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.
