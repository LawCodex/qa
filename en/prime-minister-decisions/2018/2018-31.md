# Decision of the Prime Minister No. (31) of 2018 amending certain provisions of Decision No. (1) of 2017 Nominating members of the National Committee for Information Security

Prime Minister,

After reviewing the Constitution,

and the Amiri Resolution No. (19) of 2016 establishing the National Information Security Committee,

and Prime Minister's Decision No. (1) of 2017 Nominating Members of the National Information Security Committee, and its amendments,

has decided as follows:

## المواد (1-2)

### Article 1

Mr. Nasser Khalaf Al-Kaabi replaces Mr. Abdullah Mohammed Al-Nuaimi as the representative of Qatar Central Bank in the membership of the National Committee for Information Security.

### Article 2

All relevant authorities, each within its competence, must implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the date of its issuance. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Abdullah Ben Naser Ben Khalifa Al Thani — Prime Ministers
