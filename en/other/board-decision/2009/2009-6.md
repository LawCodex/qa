# Decision No. 6 of 2009 By the Board of Directors of Qatar Financial Markets Authority On Issuing Rules and Procedures for Complaints

The Board of Directors,

Having reviewed Law No. 33 of 2005 on Qatar Financial Markets Authority and Qatar Securities Company,

as amended by Decree-Law No. 14 of 2007;

The Decision of the Board of Directors of the Qatar Financial Markets Authority at its

second meeting in 2009 on issuing rules for complaints;

The Regulation of Qatar Financial Markets Authority; and

The proposal of the Chief Executive Officer of the Qatar Financial Markets Authority;

Hereby decide the following:

**System of Rules and Procedures for Complaints**

**Introduction**

These rules and procedures include the determination of the general framework and directives for the filing of a complaint by a person who claims to have sustained physical damage as a result of the acts or omissions of others during the performance of any activity or job organised by the Qatar Financial Markets Authority (“the Authority”) and subject to its control and supervision.

These rules and procedures also explain how to deal with a received complaint, the studies and research required for preparation of the final report thereon, and recommendations for its resolution.

**Article 1**

**Complainant**

Any person who claims to have sustained physical damage as a result of the conduct, acts or omissions of others shall be entitled to file a complaint.

Any person who observes acts by others that violate the applicable legislation and who is likely to be adversely affected by such acts shall be entitled to file a complaint.

**Article 2**

**Subject of Complaint**

A complaint may relate to the performance of any activity or job organised by the Authority and subject to its control and supervision.

**Article 3**

**Defendant**

A complaint shall be filed against all natural or juristic persons licensed by the Authority or its employees, whether they are financial markets, deposit bodies or financial services companies, or against the bodies which issue securities.

A complaint may also be filed against persons and other bodies engaged in the performance of any activity related to financial markets or of a job subject to the control and supervision of the Authority without a licence or approval by the Authority to do so.

**Article 4**

**Submitting a Complaint**

The complaint shall be submitted to the Authority in accordance with the adopted rules and procedures and on the prescribed form.

**Article 5**

**Historical and Chronological Sequence of Complaints**

Complaints shall be studied and considered in the sequence in which they are received, unless there is a justifiable reason for non-compliance with this rule.

**Article 6**

**Technical Analysis of Complaint**

The technical analysis and study of the complaint’s basis, and the drawing of preliminary inferences, shall be performed by the person involved in the management of complaints as a prelude to the preparation of the final report thereon.

**Article 7**

**Compensation and Amount**

Compensation to the Complainant shall be limited to physical damages including the direct economic loss sustained from the actual injury or opportunity cost that can be objectively determined by a competent court.

**Article 8**

**Settlement by Reconciliation (amicable solution)**

A complaint may be settled between the parties by reconciliation in cases involving inadvertent or non-essential mistakes, or where one or both parties express their intention to settle.

**Article 9**

**Right of Waiver of Complaint**

The Complainant may waive his complaint but without prejudice to the Authority taking all necessary procedures, where justified, against any person in violation of the applicable legislation in force.

**Article 10**

**Criminal Offence**

Where it appears justified to inform other competent official bodies about suspected criminal offences requiring sanctions under other legislation applicable in the State, the matter shall be referred to the Department of Legal Affairs and Enforcement in the Authority to complete the necessary procedures in this regard.

**Article 11**

**Discontinuation of Legal Procedures**

Legal proceedings against any person who violates the applicable legislation may be discontinued where the violation is the result of an honest mistake and causes no serious harm, and whether or not the injured party receives compensation.

**Article 12**

**Complainant’s Rights**

The Complainant under his complaint may only have the right to recover the physical rights or financial compensation for the loss suffered or by the amount of the opportunity cost, or both if proved.

**Article 13**

**Violations Against Other Parties**

Where an examination of the complaint reasonably infers that there is another prima facie violation committed by the Defendant or another party which has caused damage to a party or parties other than the Complainant, the Defendant shall be liable to remedy such damage. Such violation shall be included in the final report and legal action shall be taken against the Defendant even where the violation caused no harm.

**Article 14**

**Proceeds of Offences and Remedies**

All gains and profits accrued by the Defendant as a result of committing or remedying an offence shall be forfeited in favour of the Authority.

**Article 15**

**Preparation of Final Report**

The final report, including the results of the studies and research on the complaint, shall be prepared and submitted to the Chief Executive Officer of the Authority, together with any recommendations.

**Article 16**

The Complainant shall personally submit his complaint to the Authority on the prescribed form and shall in particular state the following:

His full name.

The number of his authorized contribution to deal in securities.

His phone number.

The Defendant's name.

The basis of the complaint and date of its occurrence.

The date of submission of the complaint.
