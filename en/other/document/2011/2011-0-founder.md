# Instrument for Adding Her Highness Sheikha Mozah bint Nasser as a Founder of Qatar Foundation for Education, Science and Community Development

The Founder,

Having perused Decree-Law No. 21 of 2006 on private foundations for the public benefit, amended by Decree-Law No. 16 of 2007;

The incorporation instrument establishing Qatar Foundation for Education, Science and Community Development issued on 16/5/1998;

The instrument re-incorporating Qatar Foundation for Education, Science, and Community Development issued on 22/1/2007;

The Articles of Association of Qatar Foundation for Education, Science, and Community Development;

And as an appreciation from us for the special efforts made by Her Highness Sheikha Mozah bint Nasser in establishing this great organization and leading it towards its noble goals, besides implementing her vision in the progressive development and prosperity of our beloved country;

Hereby decides the following:

First: adding Sheikha Mozah bint Nasser as a founder of Qatar Foundation for Education, Science and Community Development and delegating Her Highness in executing all the powers assigned to the founders, in accordance with Decree-Law No. 21 of 2006, referred to above, and the basic system of the foundation.

Second: the necessary procedures for registering and declaring this instrument shall be made in accordance with the law.

Hamad Bin Khalifa Al-Thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
