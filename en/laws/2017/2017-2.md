# Law No. (2) of 2017 issuing the Arbitration Law in civil and commercial matters

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

after reviewing the Constitution,

and the Civil and Commercial Procedures Law issued by Law No. (13) of 1990, and its amending laws,

and the Judicial Authority issued by Law No. (10) of 2003, as amended by Decree No. (21) of 2010,

and the Civil Law issued by Law No. (22) of 2004,

and the Commercial Law issued by Law No. (27) of 2006, as amended by Law No. (7) of 2010,

and the E-Commerce Law issued by Decree 34 No. (16) of 2010,

and the Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards, issued by the approval for the State of Qatar to join it by Decree 34 No. (29) of 2003,

and upon the proposal of the Minister of Justice,

and upon the Draft Law submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

and after seeking the opinion of the Shura Council,

we have decided on the following Law / Act:

## الفصل الأول: تعاريف وأحكام عامة (1-6)

### Article 1

For the application of the provisions of this Law, the following words and expressions shall have the meanings set forth beside each of them, unless the context requires otherwise:

Minister: Minister of Justice.

Ministry: Ministry of Justice.

Arbitration: A consensual legal method for dispute resolution instead of resorting to the judiciary, whether the entity undertaking the arbitration procedures, pursuant to the parties' agreement, is a permanent arbitration center or not.

Arbitration Agreement: The agreement stipulated in Article (7/Item 1) of this Law.

Parties: The party or parties to the dispute who have agreed to refer it to arbitration.

Arbitral Authority: The Authority constituted of a single arbitrator or an odd number of arbitrators, to decide on the dispute referred to arbitration.

Other Authority: The entity chosen by the parties in their agreement, in accordance with what is permitted by this Law, to specialize in carrying out specific functions related to assistance and supervision of arbitration, whether it is a permanent arbitration center or institution.

Competent Court: The Division of Civil and Commercial Arbitration Disputes at the Court of Appeal, or the Primary Division at the Civil and Commercial Court of Qatar Financial Centre, based on the parties' agreement.

Competent Judge: Enforcement Judge at the Primary Court, or Enforcement Judge at the Civil and Commercial Court of Qatar Financial Centre if the parties agree on that

Plaintiff/Claimant/Complainant: The party to the agreement who initiates the request to refer the dispute to arbitration.

Defendant: The party to the agreement against whom the dispute is referred to arbitration.

Arbitration Centers: Any legal entity licensed to conduct arbitration in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

### Article 2

1- Without prejudice to the provisions of the International Agreements in force in the State, the provisions of this Law shall apply to any Arbitration between parties from public law or private law entities, regardless of the nature of the legal relationship subject to the dispute, if this Arbitration is conducted in the State, or if it is an international commercial Arbitration conducted abroad and its parties have agreed to subject it to the provisions of this Law.

2- The agreement on Arbitration in administrative contract disputes shall be with the approval of the Prime Minister or his delegate.

Public law entities may not under any circumstances resort to Arbitration to resolve disputes arising among themselves.

3- Arbitration shall be commercial, for the application of the provisions of this Law, if the dispute arises from a legal relationship of an economic nature, whether contractual or non-contractual, including commercial, investment, financial, banking, industrial, insurance, tourism transactions, or any other transactions of an economic nature.

4- Arbitration shall be international, for the application of the provisions of this Law, if its subject is a dispute relating to international trade, in the following cases:

a- If the principal place of business of the parties to the Arbitration Agreement, at the time of concluding the Agreement, is located in different countries. If any of the parties has more than one place of business, the closest place of business to the subject matter of the Arbitration Agreement will be considered. If a party to the Arbitration Agreement has no place of business, his habitual residence will be considered.

b- If the principal place of business of all parties to the Arbitration Agreement is located in the same State at the time of concluding the Arbitration Agreement, and one of the following places is located outside this State:

- The place of Arbitration as specified in the Arbitration Agreement or as referred to in the method of its determination.

- The place where a substantial part of the obligations arising from the relationship of the parties is executed.

- The place most closely connected to the subject of the dispute.

c- If the subject of the dispute covered by the Arbitration Agreement is related to more than one State.

d- If the parties agree to resort to a permanent Arbitration institution whose headquarters is located inside or outside the State.

### Article 3

In cases where this Law / Act allows the parties to Arbitration to choose the procedure to be followed in a certain matter, each of them may authorize others to choose this procedure. For the purposes of this provision, any institution or center for Arbitration inside the State or outside it is considered as others.

### Article 4

1- Unless the parties agree otherwise, written notices or messages shall be delivered as follows:

A) Personally to the recipient, or at their place of business, or at their usual residence, or at their postal address known to the parties or specified in the Arbitration Agreement or in the document regulating the relationship addressed by the Arbitration.

B) If it is impossible to ascertain any of the addresses mentioned in the previous clause after making the necessary inquiries, the written notice or message is deemed to have been received if it is sent to the last place of business, usual residence, postal address, email address, or fax number known to the recipient, by registered mail or by any other means allowing Evidence / Proof of delivery in writing.

C) The written notice or message sent by fax or email is considered received on the date it was sent, unless the sender receives an automatic message indicating a transmission error.

D) In all cases, the written notice or message is considered received if it was received or sent before 6 p.m. in the State where it was received; otherwise, receipt is deemed to have occurred the next day.

E) For the purpose of calculating the periods stipulated in this article, the period begins to be counted from the day following the day the receipt occurred. If the last day of the period coincides with an official holiday or a working holiday at the place of business or workplace of the recipient, the period extends to the next working day. Official holidays or working holidays that fall during the period are included in its calculation.

2- The provisions of this article do not apply to judicial notices before the courts.

### Article 5

If one of the parties becomes aware of a violation of a provision of this Law / Act that the parties are permitted to violate, or of a condition of the Arbitration Agreement, and nevertheless continues with the Arbitration proceedings without promptly objecting to such violation within the agreed time period, or without undue delay when there is no agreement, it shall be considered as a waiver of their right to object.

### Article 6

The other Power, or the competent Court, if the parties do not agree on that Power, shall perform the functions stipulated in the articles (11/Item 5, 6) and (13/Item 1, 2) and (14/Item 1), and (16/Item 3) of this Law / Act.

## Chapter Two: Arbitration Agreement (7-9)

### Article 7

1- The Arbitration Agreement is the agreement of the parties, whether they are legal persons or natural persons possessing the legal capacity to contract, to resort to Arbitration to resolve all or some of the disputes that have arisen or may arise between them concerning a specific legal relationship, whether contractual or non-contractual. The Arbitration Agreement may be separate or in the form of an Arbitration clause included in a Contract.

2- Arbitration is not permitted in matters where Conciliation is not allowed.

3- The Arbitration Agreement must be in writing, otherwise it is void. The Arbitration Agreement is considered written if it is included in a document signed by the parties, or in the form of paper or electronic correspondence, or in any other form effected by means of communication that enables proof of receipt in writing.

4- The Arbitration Agreement is considered to meet the writing requirement if one of the parties claims the existence of the agreement in the Lawsuit / Legal Action memorandum or the reply memorandum without the other party denying it in their defense.

5- Reference in a contract to a document containing an Arbitration clause is regarded as an Arbitration Agreement, provided that such reference makes the clause part of the contract.

6- Without prejudice to any legislative text stipulating the termination of substantive rights or obligations due to death, and unless the parties agree otherwise, the Arbitration Agreement does not terminate upon the death of one of the parties and may be continued to be executed by or against the representatives of that party as the case may be.

### Article 8

1- The Court, to which a dispute is brought and concerning which there is an Arbitration agreement, shall rule inadmissible the Lawsuit / Legal Action if the Defendant raises such plea before submitting any request or defense regarding the subject of the Lawsuit / Legal Action, unless the Court decides that the agreement is invalid, null, without effect, or cannot be enforced.

2- The filing of the Lawsuit / Legal Action referred to in the previous paragraph does not prevent the commencement or continuation of Arbitration proceedings and the issuance of the Judgment / Ruling therein.

### Article 9

In the case where the Arbitration panel, or any other person granted a certain authority by the parties, is not competent or unable to act effectively at the appropriate time, the competent judge may, upon the request of one of the parties, order the taking of interim or precautionary measures, including the measures stipulated in Article (17/Item 1) of this Law / Act, whether before the commencement of the Arbitration proceedings or during their course, and such a request is not considered a waiver by the applicant of invoking the Arbitration agreement.

## Chapter Three: Arbitration Panel (10-17)

### Article 10

The Arbitration panel is composed of one or more arbitrators, according to the agreement of the parties; if they do not agree on the arbitrators, the number shall be three.

If there are multiple arbitrators, their number must be odd; otherwise, the Arbitration is invalid.

### Article 11

1- The appointment of the arbitrator shall be made from among the approved arbitrators registered in the Arbitrators Register at the Ministry; however, any person may be appointed as an arbitrator if the following conditions are fulfilled:

A- Full legal capacity.

B- Not having been finally convicted in a felony or misdemeanor involving moral turpitude or dishonesty, even if his reputation has been restored.

C- Of good conduct and reputation.

2- It is not required that the arbitrator be of a specific nationality, unless the parties agree otherwise or the Law stipulates otherwise.

3- The arbitrator accepts the appointment in writing, or by following one of the means provided for in (Article 7/Item 3) of this Law, and must disclose in writing when offered the arbitration any circumstances that might give rise to doubts as to his impartiality or independence. This obligation remains in force even if such circumstances arise after his appointment.

4- Without prejudice to the provisions of Items (6) and (7) of this article, the parties may agree on the procedure to be followed in appointing the arbitrator or arbitrators.

5- In the absence of an agreement, the following shall apply:

A) If the arbitral tribunal is composed of a sole arbitrator, and the parties do not agree on the arbitrator within thirty days from the date of written notification by the claimant to the other parties to do so, any of the parties may request his appointment from the other authority or the competent Court, as the case may be.

B) If the arbitral tribunal is composed of three arbitrators, each party shall appoint one arbitrator, and the two appointed arbitrators shall agree on the appointment of the third arbitrator. If one of the parties fails to appoint his arbitrator within thirty days from receiving a request from the other party to do so, or if the two appointed arbitrators fail to agree on the appointment of the third arbitrator within thirty days from the date of appointment of the last of them, the appointment shall be made by the other authority or the competent Court, as the case may be, at the request of one of the parties.

6- In the event there are appointment procedures agreed upon by the parties, any of the parties may request the other authority or the competent Court, as the case may be, to take the necessary action, unless the specific agreement on appointment procedures provides otherwise for completing this action, in any of the following cases:

A) If one of the parties fails to take an action required by the agreed procedures.

B) If the parties, or the arbitrators, cannot reach the required agreement according to these procedures.

C) If a third party fails to perform any task assigned to him according to these procedures.

7- Any decision issued by the other authority or the competent Court, as the case may be, regarding a matter stipulated in Items (5) and (6) of this article, shall be final and not subject to Legal Challenge / Appeal by any means of Appeal.

8- Upon appointing the arbitrator, the other authority or the competent Court, as the case may be, must consider the nature and circumstances of the dispute and the qualifications required of the arbitrator in accordance with the parties' agreement, and the considerations that ensure the appointment of an independent and impartial arbitrator. If an individual arbitrator or a third arbitrator is appointed, it shall take into account the arbitrator’s nationality in relation to the nationality of the parties.

9- All parties, including any center or body for appointing arbitrators or any arbitrator already appointed, must be notified of any request submitted to the other authority or the competent Court, as the case may be, for the appointment of an arbitrator. The request must include a summary of the nature of the dispute, the specific terms set out in the Arbitration Agreement, and all the steps taken to appoint any remaining member of the arbitral tribunal.

10- When appointing an arbitrator as stated above, the other authority or the competent Court, as the case may be, may choose him from the Arbitrators Register at the Ministry, from the arbitrators’ lists at other arbitration centers or from any other lists it deems appropriate, and must carry out the necessary due diligence to select the arbitrator suitable for the circumstances of the dispute. Any expenses incurred for this, including the appointment fees for the arbitral tribunal, shall be considered arbitration expenses.

11- The arbitrator may not be held liable for performing his arbitration duties, unless he performs them with bad faith, collusion, or gross negligence.

### Article 12

An arbitrator may not be dismissed unless there are circumstances that raise justified doubts about their impartiality or independence, or if they lack the qualifications agreed upon by the parties. None of the disputing parties may dismiss the arbitrator whom they appointed or took part in appointing, except for reasons that become evident after the appointment has been made.

### Article 13

1- The parties may agree on the procedures for challenging the arbitrator; if there is no such agreement, a request to challenge the arbitrator shall be submitted in writing to the Arbitration Authority, stating the reasons for the challenge, within fifteen days from the date on which the party requesting the challenge becomes aware of the formation of this Authority or of the circumstances justifying the challenge. If the arbitrator whose challenge is requested does not step down, or the other party does not agree to the challenge, the request shall be referred to the other Authority or the competent Court, as the case may be, and the Decision issued by them shall not be subject to a Legal Challenge / Appeal by any means whatsoever. The Arbitration Authority shall suspend the Arbitration proceedings until the challenge request is resolved.

2- The other Authority or the competent Court, as the case may be, when rendering a Judgment / Ruling to remove the arbitrator, may decide the entitlements of that arbitrator regarding fees and expenses, or the recovery of any fees or expenses paid to him.

3- A request to challenge will not be accepted from someone who has previously submitted a challenge to the same arbitrator in the same Arbitration, unless a new reason appears for challenging him again that differs from the first reason, or if the applicant became aware of it after submitting the first challenge.

### Article 14

1- If the arbitrator is unable to perform his duty, does not commence it, or ceases to perform it in a way that causes unjustified delay in the arbitration procedures, and does not voluntarily step down, and the parties do not agree on his removal, the other authority or the competent court, as the case may be, may order the termination of his duty upon the request of any of the parties, and its decision in this regard shall be final and not subject to Legal Challenge / Appeal by any means of Legal Challenge / Appeal.

2- The resignation of the arbitrator or the termination of his duty by the parties shall not be considered evidence of the existence of any of the reasons stipulated in Article (12) of this Law / Act.

### Article 15

1- An alternative arbitrator shall be appointed to replace the arbitrator whose mission has ended due to recusal, dismissal, resignation, or for any other reason, following the applicable procedures for the appointment of the arbitrator whose mission has ended.

2- The parties may, after the appointment of the alternative arbitrator, agree on the extent to which the Arbitration proceedings undertaken before the appointment of the alternative arbitrator shall remain valid; otherwise, the reconstituted Arbitral Tribunal shall decide as it deems appropriate in this regard.

### Article 16

1- The Arbitral Tribunal shall rule on pleas related to its lack of jurisdiction, including pleas based on the non-existence, invalidity, nullity, expiration, or non-applicability of the Arbitration Agreement to the subject matter of the dispute. The arbitration clause shall be deemed an independent agreement from the other Contract Terms, and the nullity, annulment, or termination of the Contract shall not affect the arbitration clause it contains, as long as this clause is valid in itself.

2- The pleas referred to in the preceding paragraph must be raised no later than the date for submitting the Defendant’s defense memorandum as provided for in Article (23) of this Law / Act, and the right to submit any of these pleas is not forfeited by a party appointing an arbitrator or participating in their appointment. As for the plea of the Arbitral Tribunal exceeding its jurisdiction during the hearing of the dispute, it must be raised as soon as the issue of exceeding the jurisdiction of the Arbitral Tribunal arises during the arbitration proceedings.

In all cases, the Arbitral Tribunal may accept a late plea if it finds that the delay is justified.

3- The Arbitral Tribunal may decide on any of the pleas referred to in this article prior to ruling on the merits, or within the Arbitration Judgment issued on the subject of the dispute. If it rejects the plea, the party whose plea has been rejected may file a Legal Challenge / Appeal against it before the other Power or the competent Court, as the case may be, within thirty days from the date of being notified of this Judgment / Ruling, and the decision of the other Power or the Judgment / Ruling of the competent Court shall not be subject to Legal Challenge / Appeal by any means of appeal. This Legal Challenge / Appeal does not prevent the Arbitral Tribunal from continuing the arbitration proceedings and issuing its Judgment / Ruling therein.

### Article 17

1- Unless the parties agree otherwise, the Arbitration panel may, upon the request of any of the parties, order interim measures or issue temporary rulings as required by the nature of the dispute or for the purpose of preventing irreparable harm, including any of the following:

a) Maintaining the status quo or restoring it to what it was until the dispute is resolved.

b) Taking any action to prevent imminent or occurring harm or interference with the Arbitration process itself, or preventing an action likely to cause any of the above.

c) Providing a means to preserve assets by which any subsequent decisions may be enforced.

d) Preserving evidence that may be important or essential for resolving the dispute.

The Arbitration panel may require the party requesting these measures to provide sufficient security to cover the expenses of the interim measure ordered or the temporary ruling issued.

2- The Arbitration panel may modify, suspend, or cancel any interim measure it has ordered or temporary ruling it has issued, upon the request of any party or Sua sponte / Motu Proprio in cases of necessity, after notifying the other parties.

3- The party who has been granted an order for an interim measure or temporary ruling in their favor, after obtaining written permission from the Arbitration panel, may request the competent Judge to order enforcement of the order or ruling issued by the Arbitration panel or any Part thereof. Copies of any request for authorization or enforcement under this article shall be sent to the other parties. The competent Judge shall order the enforcement of the mentioned order or ruling unless it is contrary to the law or public order.

4- The party requesting the measure or ruling shall bear the costs and compensation for any damages caused by that measure or ruling to any party, if the Arbitration panel later decides that the measure or ruling should not have been issued under the circumstances. The Arbitration panel may require that party to pay the costs and compensation at any time during the proceedings.

## Chapter Four: Arbitration Procedures (18-27)

### Article 18

The Arbitration Authority must adhere to neutrality and equality between the parties, and provide each of them with a full and equal opportunity to present their claim, defense, and objections.

Additionally, the Authority must avoid any unnecessary delay or expenses, in order to achieve a fair and prompt means of resolving the dispute.

### Article 19

1- Subject to the provisions of this Law / Act, the parties may agree on the procedures for Arbitration, including the rules of evidence to be followed by the Arbitration tribunal, and they have the right to subject these procedures to the applicable rules in any institution or Arbitration center inside the State or outside it.

2- The Arbitration tribunal may, subject to the provisions of this Law / Act, apply the procedures it deems appropriate, including its authority to accept the evidence presented and assess its relevance, usefulness, and importance to the subject matter of the dispute, unless there is an agreement between the parties to determine the procedures of Arbitration in accordance with the previous paragraph of this article.

### Article 20

1- The parties may agree on the place of Arbitration inside the State or abroad; if there is no agreement, the Arbitration panel shall determine that place, taking into account the circumstances of the Lawsuit / Legal Action and the convenience of the place for its parties.

2- This does not prejudice the authority of the Arbitration panel to meet at any place it deems appropriate to conduct any procedure of Arbitration, such as hearing the statements of the disputing parties, Witnesses, or experts, or reviewing documents, inspecting objects or funds, or holding deliberations among its members, unless the parties agree otherwise.

### Article 21

Arbitration proceedings commence on the day the Defendant receives the request to refer the dispute to Arbitration, unless the parties agree otherwise.

### Article 22

1- The parties may agree on the language or languages to be used in Arbitration proceedings. If they do not agree on this, the Arbitration Authority shall determine the language or languages to be used in these proceedings. This agreement or determination applies to written statements, memoranda, and oral pleadings submitted by any of the parties, as well as to any Ruling made or message sent or Ruling issued by this Authority, unless the parties’ agreement or the determination regarding language provides otherwise.

2- The Arbitration Authority may order that all or some of the documents submitted in the Lawsuit / Legal Action be accompanied by a translation into the language or languages used in Arbitration. In the case of multiple languages, the translation may be limited to some of them.

### Article 23

1- The Plaintiff/Claimant/Complainant shall, within the period agreed upon by the parties or set by the Arbitration panel, submit a written memorandum of his Lawsuit / Legal Action, including his name and address, an explanation of the facts of the Lawsuit / Legal Action, identification of the matters in dispute, and his requests.

2- The Defendant shall, within the period agreed upon by the parties or set by the Arbitration panel, submit a written memorandum of his defense in response to the memorandum of the Plaintiff/Claimant/Complainant, and may include in this memorandum any ancillary requests related to the subject of the dispute or assert a right arising from it in order to claim set-off.

3- The parties may agree on the information to be included in the memoranda referred to in the two preceding paragraphs of this article.

4- Without prejudice to the right of the Arbitration panel to request the parties to submit documents at any stage of the Arbitration proceedings, the parties may append to their memoranda all documents related to the subject matter, and may refer to documents and other evidence they intend to present.

5- Unless the parties have agreed otherwise, each party may amend or supplement his requests or defenses during the course of the Arbitration proceedings, unless the Arbitration panel decides not to allow it to prevent delay in resolving the dispute.

### Article 24

1- The Arbitration tribunal holds hearings for Pleadings to enable the parties to explain the subject of the Lawsuit / Legal Action, present their arguments and evidence, or to hear statements, unless the Arbitration tribunal is satisfied with the submission of memoranda and written documents or the parties agree otherwise.

2- The Arbitration tribunal hears Witnesses and experts without an oath.

3- The Arbitration tribunal notifies the parties of the dates of Pleadings sessions or hearings, inspections, or examination of documents that it decides to hold, sufficiently in advance as determined by the Arbitration tribunal, unless the parties agree on specific notification dates.

4- Unless the parties agree otherwise, the events of the sessions, meetings, and inspections held by the Arbitration tribunal are recorded in a Report / Official Record and a copy is delivered to each party. Additionally, besides writing, these events may be recorded using other appropriate means according to procedures determined by the Arbitration tribunal or agreed upon by the parties.

5- A copy of any memoranda, documents, or other papers submitted by one party to the Arbitration tribunal is sent to the other party, and a copy of all expert reports, documents, and other evidence presented to the Arbitration tribunal and which may be relied upon in making its decision is sent to each party.

6- Each party to the dispute may appoint one or more lawyers to represent them, and may seek assistance from experts or translators. The Arbitration tribunal may, at any time, request any party to prove the capacity granted to its representative according to the form required by Law / Act or determined by the Arbitration tribunal.

### Article 25

Unless the parties agree otherwise:

a) The Arbitration Panel shall terminate the Arbitration proceedings if the Plaintiff/Claimant/Complainant does not submit the statement of claim in accordance with Article (23/Item 1) of this Law / Act, unless a valid excuse has been provided.

b) The Arbitration Panel shall proceed with the Arbitration proceedings if the Defendant does not submit the Statement of Defence in accordance with Article (23/Item 2) of this Law / Act, without this in itself being considered an acknowledgment by the Defendant of the Plaintiff/Claimant/Complainant's claims.

c) The Arbitration Panel may continue with the Arbitration proceedings and decide on the dispute based on the evidence and proof available, if one of the parties fails to attend a session or to submit requested evidence, documents, or information.

### Article 26

1- Unless the parties agree otherwise, the Arbitration Authority may:

A) Appoint one or more experts to provide it with a report, written or oral, on certain matters determined by the Authority, provided that each party is notified with a copy of its decision specifying the task Attributed to the expert and the deadline set for submitting the report.

B) Request any of the parties to provide the expert with any information relevant to the dispute, or allow the expert to access, examine, or inspect any relevant documents, objects, or funds.

2- The Arbitration Authority shall send a copy of the expert's report to each party, granting them the opportunity to express their opinion on it, and each party has the right to review the documents and records that the expert examined and relied upon in the report.

3- The Arbitration Authority may, after the expert's report is submitted, whether Sua sponte / Motu Proprio or at the request of one of the parties, hold a hearing session to listen to the expert's statements, in which the parties have the opportunity to hear and discuss the expert regarding the contents of the report. Each party may in this session engage one or more experts of its own to provide an opinion on the matters addressed by the expert's report, unless the parties agree otherwise.

4- The fees and expenses of the expert appointed by the Arbitration Authority shall be paid by the parties, in accordance with the determination of the Arbitration Authority.

5- The Arbitration Authority shall resolve any dispute arising between the expert and any party in this regard.

### Article 27

1- The Arbitration panel or any of the parties, after the approval of the Arbitration panel, may request assistance from the competent Court to obtain evidence relevant to the subject of the dispute, including technical expertise work and examination of evidence. If the Arbitration panel finds that the requested assistance is necessary to decide on the subject of the dispute, it may suspend the Arbitration proceedings until such assistance is obtained, and this will result in suspending the time period specified for issuing the Arbitration Judgment / Ruling.

2- The competent Court may execute the request for assistance, within the limits of its authority and in accordance with the applicable rules for obtaining evidence, including authorizing judicial delegation, or issuing a Judgment / Ruling against any of the Witnesses who fail to attend or refuse to answer with the penalties stipulated in Articles (278), (279) of the indicated Civil and Commercial Procedures Law.

## Chapter Five: Arbitration Ruling and Termination of Proceedings (28-32)

### Article 28

1- The Arbitration panel settles the dispute in accordance with the legal rules agreed upon by the parties. If they agree to apply the Law / Act or legal system of a specific country, the substantive rules of that Law / Act shall be followed without the rules related to conflict of laws, unless the parties explicitly agree otherwise.

2- If the parties do not agree on the legal rules to be applied, the Arbitration panel shall apply the Law / Act determined by the rules of conflict of laws.

3- The Arbitration panel may not settle the dispute based on principles of justice and fairness without adhering to the provisions of the Law / Act, unless the parties expressly permit it.

4- In all cases, the Arbitration panel settles the dispute in accordance with the terms of the Contract, and takes into consideration the commercial customs and practices followed in such type of transactions.

### Article 29

Decisions, orders, and awards of the Arbitration Authority composed of more than one arbitrator shall be issued by majority vote after deliberation is conducted in the manner determined by the Authority, unless the parties agree otherwise. However, decisions on procedural matters may be issued by the arbitrator presiding over the Authority if authorized to do so by the parties or all members of the Arbitration Authority.

### Article 30

1- If the parties agree, during Arbitration proceedings, to settle the dispute between them, the Arbitration Authority shall terminate the proceedings, and if the parties request Evidence / Proof of the settlement and its terms and the Arbitration Authority has no objection thereto, the Authority shall record the settlement in the form of an agreed Arbitration Judgment / Ruling.

2- The agreed Arbitration Judgment / Ruling shall be subject to the same provisions governing the Arbitration Judgment / Ruling stipulated in the following Article. It must state that it is an Arbitration Judgment / Ruling, and this Judgment / Ruling shall have the same Capacity, effect, and enforceability as other arbitral Judgments / Rulings.

### Article 31

1- The arbitration judgment shall be issued in writing and signed by the arbitrator or arbitrators. If the arbitral tribunal consists of more than one arbitrator, the signatures of the majority of the arbitrators shall be sufficient, provided that the reason for the absence of the other signatures is stated in the judgment.

2- The arbitration judgment must be reasoned, unless the parties agree otherwise, or the legal rules applicable to the arbitration proceedings do not require reasons to be stated, or if the arbitration judgment is an agreed judgment in accordance with the previous article of this Law.

3- The arbitration judgment shall include the names and addresses of the parties, the names, addresses, nationalities, and capacities of the arbitrators, a copy of the Arbitration Agreement, the date of issuance of the judgment, and the place of arbitration in accordance with what is specified in Article (20/Item 1) of this Law, and the arbitration judgment shall be deemed issued in that place. The judgment shall also include a summary of the parties' requests, statements, documents, the operative part of the judgment, and its reasons, if stating them is required.

4- The judgment must include the amount of arbitration costs, including fees and expenses, the party obligated to pay them, and payment procedures, unless the parties agree otherwise.

5- The arbitral tribunal shall issue the judgment concluding the entire dispute within the period agreed upon by the parties. If there is no agreement, the judgment must be issued within one month from the date of closing the pleadings. In all cases, the tribunal may decide to extend this period for no more than an additional month, unless the parties agree or consent to otherwise.

6- The arbitral tribunal may, Sua sponte / Motu Proprio or at the request of one of the parties, reopen the pleadings before the arbitration judgment is rendered, if it deems it appropriate.

7- If, during the arbitration proceedings, a matter beyond the jurisdiction of the arbitral tribunal or a forgery challenge to a document submitted to it arises, or criminal proceedings are taken regarding its forgery or another criminal act, the arbitral tribunal may continue considering the subject of the dispute if it deems that deciding on this matter or on the forgery or other criminal act is not necessary to decide on the subject of the dispute. Otherwise, the proceedings shall be suspended until a Final Judgment is rendered in this regard, resulting in the suspension of the time period set for issuing the arbitration judgment.

8- After the issuance of the arbitration judgment, each party shall be delivered a signed copy by the arbitrators in accordance with Item (1) of this Article within fifteen days from its issuance. The arbitration judgment or parts thereof may not be published except with the consent of the parties. .

9- The arbitration proceedings shall conclude by the issuance of the arbitration judgment ending the entire dispute, or by a Decision issued by the arbitral tribunal in the following cases:

A- If the parties agree to terminate the proceedings.

B- If the claimant abandons the arbitration dispute, unless the tribunal decides, at the request of the Defendant, that the latter has a genuine and legitimate interest in the continuation of the proceedings until the dispute is resolved.

C- If the tribunal finds that the continuation of the proceedings has become futile or impossible for any other reason.

10- The jurisdiction of the arbitral tribunal shall end upon the conclusion of the arbitration proceedings, taking into account the provisions of Articles (32) and (33/Item 5) of this Law.

11- The arbitral tribunal shall send to the administrative unit concerned with arbitration affairs at the Ministry an electronic copy of the arbitration judgment or the Decision ending the dispute, as the case may be, within two weeks of its issuance.

### Article 32

1- Unless the parties agree otherwise, any party may, within seven days from the date of receiving the Arbitration Judgment, or within the period agreed upon by the parties, provided that the other parties are notified, request the Arbitration panel to:

A) Correct any material, computational, or typographical errors that may have occurred in the Arbitration Judgment.

B) Interpret a specific point in the Arbitration Judgment or a specific Part thereof, if the parties have agreed to this.

If the Arbitration panel finds the request justified, it shall make the correction in writing or issue a Judgment of interpretation within seven days from the date of receiving the request. The interpretation or correction shall be considered a Part of the final Arbitration Judgment.

2- The Arbitration panel may, provided that the parties are notified, correct, Sua sponte / Motu Proprio, any error mentioned in paragraph (1/A) of this article within seven days from the date of issuing the Arbitration Judgment.

3- Unless the parties agree otherwise, any party may, provided that the other parties are notified, request the Arbitration panel within seven days from the date of receiving the Arbitration Judgment to issue an additional Arbitration Judgment for claims submitted during the Arbitration proceedings which were omitted by the Arbitration Judgment. If the Arbitration panel finds the request justified, it must issue the additional Arbitration Judgment within seven days from the date the request was submitted.

4- The Arbitration panel may, if necessary, extend the period within which it must correct the Arbitration Judgment or issue an interpretation thereof or issue an additional Arbitration Judgment, for a similar duration to the original period.

5- The correction shall be recorded on the original copy of the Judgment and signed by the Arbitration panel, and the same provisions regarding the Arbitration Judgment stipulated in Article (31) of this Law / Act shall apply to the interpretation of the Arbitration Judgment and the additional Arbitration Judgment. The other parties shall be notified of their issuance.

6- If it is proven impossible for the Arbitration panel that issued the Judgment to convene to consider the request for correction or interpretation or Judgment on omitted claims, the matter may be referred to the competent Court for decision, unless the parties agree otherwise.

## Chapter Six: Legal Challenge / Appeal against the Arbitration Ruling (33)

### Article 33

1- A Judgment / Ruling issued in Arbitration may not be subject to Legal Challenge / Appeal by any method of Appeal, except by way of annulment, In accordance with the provisions of this Law, before the competent Court.

2- A Lawsuit / Legal Action for annulment of the Judgment / Ruling may not be admitted unless the applicant presents evidence proving any of the following cases:

A- That one of the parties to the Arbitration Agreement, at the time of its conclusion, lacked or was deficient in capacity in accordance with the law governing his capacity, or that the Arbitration Agreement is invalid, in accordance with the law agreed by the parties to apply to this agreement, or In accordance with the provisions of this Law if there is no such agreement.

B- That the applicant for annulment was not properly notified of the appointment of one of the arbitrators or the Arbitration proceedings, or it was impossible for the applicant to present his defense for any other reason beyond his control.

C- That the Judgment / Ruling decided on matters not included in the Arbitration Agreement, or exceeded the limits of this agreement. However, if the parts of the Judgment / Ruling concerning matters subject to Arbitration can be separated from those not subject to Arbitration, annulment shall only affect the latter parts.

D- That the formation of the Arbitration panel, appointment of arbitrators, or Arbitration procedures were conducted in a manner contrary to what was agreed by the parties, unless such agreement conflicts with any of the provisions of this Law that may not be contravened by the parties, or, in the absence of agreement, that this was done in a manner contrary to this Law / Act.

3- The competent Court shall, Sua sponte / Motu Proprio, annul the Judgment / Ruling if the subject of the dispute is not capable of settlement by Arbitration according to the law of the State, or if the Judgment / Ruling contravenes public order of the State.

4- The Lawsuit / Legal Action to annul the Judgment / Ruling shall be filed before the competent Court within one month from the date the parties are served a copy of the Judgment / Ruling or from the date the applicant for annulment is notified of the Judgment / Ruling or the issuance of the correction decision or interpretation Judgment / Ruling or the additional Arbitration stipulated in Article (32) of this Law / Act, unless the parties agree in writing to extend the period for filing the annulment Lawsuit / Legal Action.

5- Unless the parties agree otherwise, the competent Court may stay the proceedings of the Lawsuit / Legal Action, upon a request from one of the parties, if it deems it appropriate, for a period it specifies, in order to grant the Arbitration panel the opportunity to continue the Arbitration proceedings or to take any other action the Arbitration panel considers would eliminate the grounds for annulment.

6- The Judgment / Ruling of the competent Court shall be final and not subject to Legal Challenge / Appeal by any method of Appeal.

## Chapter Seven: Recognition and Enforcement of Arbitration Awards (34-35)

### Article 34

1- The Judgments / Rulings of the arbitrators acquire the authority of res judicata and are enforceable In accordance with the provisions of this Law, regardless of the State in which they were issued.

2- The request to enforce the Judgment / Ruling shall be submitted in writing to the competent Judge, accompanied by a copy of the Arbitration Agreement, the original Judgment / Ruling or a signed copy thereof in the language in which it was issued, and a translation of the Judgment / Ruling into Arabic by an accredited entity if it was issued in a foreign language, unless the parties agreed on an alternative method for the enforcement of the Judgment / Ruling.

3- A request for the enforcement of an Arbitration Judgment / Ruling shall not be accepted unless the time limit for filing a Case/Lawsuit for the annulment of this Judgment / Ruling has expired.

### Article 35

Recognition or enforcement of any Arbitration Award may not be refused, regardless of the State in which it was issued, except in the following two cases:

1- Upon the request of the party against whom enforcement of the Award is sought, if this party presents to the competent Judge, to whom the request for recognition or enforcement is submitted, evidence proving one of the following cases:

a) That one of the parties to the Arbitration Agreement, at the time of its conclusion, was incapacitated or had limited capacity in accordance with the law governing their capacity, or that the Arbitration Agreement is invalid, according to the law agreed upon by the parties to govern the agreement, or according to the law of the State in which the Award was issued if no agreement was made.

b) That the party against whom enforcement of the Award is sought was not properly notified of the appointment of an arbitrator or Arbitration proceedings, or was unable to present their defense for any other reason beyond their control.

c) That the Arbitration Award decided on matters not covered by the Arbitration Agreement, or exceeded its limits. However, if the parts of the Award related to matters subject to Arbitration can be separated from those related to matters not subject to Arbitration, recognition or enforcement may be given to those parts of the Arbitration Award that decided matters covered by the Arbitration Agreement or did not exceed it.

d) That the formation of the Arbitration tribunal, the appointment of the arbitrators, or the Arbitration proceedings were carried out in violation of the law or the parties’ agreement, or, in the absence of an agreement, that it was carried out in a manner contrary to the law of the country where the Arbitration took place.

e) That the Arbitration Award is no longer binding on the parties, or has been annulled or its enforcement suspended by one of the Courts of the State in which that Award was issued or according to its law.

2- The competent Judge may refuse recognition or enforcement of the Arbitration Award, on its own initiative, in the following two cases:

a) If the subject matter of the dispute is not permitted to be resolved by Arbitration in accordance with the law of the State.

b) If the recognition or enforcement of the Award would be contrary to the public policy of the State.

If the competent Judge finds that the Arbitration Award for which recognition or enforcement is sought is challenged by annulment before the Court in the State where it was issued, he may postpone the enforcement order as he deems appropriate, and may, at the request of the party requesting recognition or enforcement, order the other party to provide such security as he deems appropriate.

3- Raising a Grievance against the order issued refusing enforcement of the Arbitration Award or enforcing it may be done before the competent Court within thirty days from the date of issuance of the order.

## Chapter Eight: Arbitration Centers and Arbitrators Accreditation (36-38)

### Article 36

A license to establish Arbitration centers and branches of foreign Arbitration centers within the State, as well as the conditions and rules for granting or revoking the license and determining the prescribed fees in this regard, shall be issued by a decision of the Minister.

### Article 37

A register shall be established at the Ministry for the registration of arbitrators approved by a decision of the Minister.

The Minister shall issue a decision regarding the conditions and rules for registering arbitrators in the aforementioned register and deleting them, as well as determining the prescribed fees in this regard.

### Article 38

Existing Arbitration centers at the time this Law comes into effect must adjust their status to comply with its provisions and the decisions issued in this regard, within six months from the date of its implementation.

The Cabinet / Council of Ministers may decide, based on the minister’s proposal, to extend this period for one or more similar terms.
