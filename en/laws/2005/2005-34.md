# Law No. (34) of 2005 concerning investment Free Zones

We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, Deputy Emir of the State of Qatar,

after reviewing the Constitution,

and upon the proposal of the Minister of Economy and Commerce,

and the Draft Law submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

and after seeking the opinion of the Shura Council,

have issued the following Law / Act:

## Part I (1)

### Definitions (1)

#### Article 1 Amended

For the application of the provisions of this Law, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings set forth opposite each of them, unless the context requires otherwise:

Free Zones: A geographical area within the territory of the State with defined area and boundaries, designated for practicing Economic Activities, and considered outside the customs territory of the State.

Authority: Free Zones Authority.

Council: Authority Board of Directors.

Chairman / President: Chairman of the Council.

Chief Executive Officer (CEO): Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of the Authority.

Economic Activity: Any commercial, industrial, agricultural, tourism, media, professional, craft, logistic, technical, service, or warehousing activity.

Registered Companies: Companies and other entities registered with the Authority to conduct Economic Activity in the Free Zones in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the decisions issued for its implementation.

Regulations: The regulations issued by the Council.

## Part 2 (2-16)

### Establishment of Investment Free Zones and Enjoyed Privileges, Exemptions and Guarantees (2-16)

#### Article 2 Amended

Free Zones are established by a decree, based on a proposal from the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, and their boundaries and coordinates are determined according to the maps and cadastral and planning schemes attached to the decree.

#### Article 3

#### Article 3 Canceled

A decision is issued by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the minister, to determine the advantages, exemptions, and guarantees granted to each area as stipulated in this Law / Act, or any other advantages, in accordance with the economic importance of each area and its role in economic development.

#### Article 4 Amended

It is permissible, in accordance with the regulations, for limited liability companies, partnership contracts, or any other types of companies or entities to be established or created in the Free Zone, whether owned by one or more natural or legal persons, citizens, residents, or others, without being bound by the percentage of non-Qatari capital ownership in companies or by the increase of their authorized capital, as stipulated by the relevant laws.

Companies registered in the Free Zones shall carry out the licensed economic activity in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the regulations, and the contracts concluded between them and the Authority.

#### Article 5

#### Article 5 Canceled

Any entity established or licensed to engage in any activity within the zone may be fully owned by non-Qataris, whether residents or non-residents in the country, whether individuals, companies, or other entities.

#### Article 6 Amended

Companies registered and licensed to operate in or through the free zone, in accordance with the provisions of this Law / Act and the regulations, are not required to obtain another License or approval or permit or registration in the State to conduct this activity.

#### Article 7 Amended

The provisions of the Income Tax Law or any laws imposing any type of direct or indirect taxes shall not apply to the free zone for a period of twenty years, renewable for a similar period or periods by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

#### Article 8 Amended

All equipment, machinery, means of transport, devices, goods, raw materials, supplies, spare parts, and any other materials or components imported, stored, manufactured, produced, developed, or processed by companies, projects, establishments, or other entities operating in the free zone are not subject to customs duties when imported into the free zone or exported outside the State.

#### Article 9 Amended

Goods and products exported from the free zone to the local market within the State are subject to customs duties.

#### Article 10 Amended

Registered companies are permitted to import and export foreign currency without restrictions through the Banks operating in the free zone, in accordance with the decisions and instructions issued by Qatar Central Bank in this regard.

#### Article 11 Amended

Subject to the provisions of International Agreements and the regulations issued regarding employees in the free zone, registered companies shall have the freedom to appoint their employees and organize their rights and conditions, provided that these rights are not less than those stipulated in the Labour Law referred to.

#### Article 12 Amended

The funds or activities of registered companies shall not be subject, for the duration of their operations in the free zone, to any nationalization procedures or restrictions on private ownership.

#### Article 13 Amended

Registered companies operating in the free zone, and no others, shall determine the prices of their products and services.

#### Article 14 Amended

Licenses of registered companies may not be cancelled or suspended except in the case of a violation of the licensing conditions, and only by a Decision / Resolution from the Chief Executive Officer (CEO).

The concerned party has the right to Raising a Grievance before the Council within fifteen days from the date of being notified of the Decision / Resolution to cancel or suspend the license. The Council shall decide on the Raising a Grievance with a reasoned Decision / Resolution within thirty days from the date of its submission. If this period passes without a response to the Raising a Grievance, it shall be considered an implicit rejection. The Decision / Resolution on the Raising a Grievance or the implicit rejection shall be final, and the person raising the grievance may file a Legal Challenge / Appeal against the Decision / Resolution of rejection of the Raising a Grievance before the Regulatory Tribunal established at the Qatar Financial Centre pursuant to the aforementioned Qatar Financial Centre Law, within sixty days from the date of being notified of this Decision / Resolution or from the date of the implicit rejection, as the case may be.

#### Article 15 Amended

Registered companies operating in the free zone may import, either by themselves or through others, what they need for their establishment, expansion, or operation, including production requirements, materials, machinery, equipment, spare parts, services, and means of transport suitable for the nature of their activity, without the need to register in the importers' register and without prior authorization.

Additionally, such registered companies operating in the free zone may export their products, either directly or through intermediaries, without a License and without the need to register in the exporters' register and without prior authorization.

#### Article 15 (Duplicate 1)

Registered companies, from the date of their registration, shall enjoy the following exemptions for the licensed economic activity within the free zone:

1- Exemption of profits from transit trade of goods in the free zone from all types of taxes, provided that the transactions related to this trade are managed and executed in the public warehouses established in the free zone.

2- Exemption from the restrictions stipulated in the Law / Act Regulating commercial agencies.

#### Article 15 (Duplicate)

It is permissible, by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the Chairman / President, to grant additional incentives and benefits to registered companies that invest in sectors or economic projects that are considered strategic, or that work to increase the proportion of local components in their products.

#### Article 16 Amended

The entry of the following goods into the free zone is prohibited:

1- Flammable materials, except for the fuels necessary for operation that are permitted by the Authority and according to the conditions it determines.

2- Radioactive materials, except those exempted under the provisions of the Law / Act.

3- War weapons, ammunition, and explosives of any kind, except with prior authorization from the competent authorities.

4- Goods that violate the provisions of legislation and international agreements related to the protection of Intellectual Property.

5- Dangerous psychoactive substances and narcotics of all kinds and their derivatives, except as required for the manufacture of medicines and pharmaceutical materials.

6- Goods prohibited from being imported into the State.

## Part 3 (17-38)

### Qatar Authority for Investment Free Zones (17-38)

#### Chapter One (17-20)

##### Establishment, Objectives and Functions of the Authority (17-20)

###### Article 17 Amended

A public authority called the "Free Zones Authority" shall be established, which will have legal personality, an independent budget, and will enjoy administrative and financial independence.

###### Article 18 Amended

The Authority is affiliated with the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, and its headquarters shall be in the city of Doha. It may establish other offices in the free zone or outside it, as it deems appropriate to achieve its objectives and carry out its duties and powers.

###### Article 18 Amended Canceled

The Authority is affiliated with the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, and its headquarters shall be in the city of Doha. It may establish other offices in the region or abroad, as it deems appropriate to achieve its objectives and exercise its functions and powers.

###### Article 18 (Duplicate)

The Board of Directors, after the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, may designate any buildings and facilities outside the free zone to serve as administrative headquarters for companies registered in the free zones, within the scope of the license issued to them, in accordance with the regulations set by the Council.

###### Article 19 Amended

The Authority aims to manage and develop the Free Zones in accordance with the best International Standards, encourage and attract investments in the fields of Scientific Research, technology, production, export, and others. To achieve this, it exercises the following powers and authorities:

1- Conduct studies and identify proposed sites for the establishment of Free Zones, the activities that can be undertaken therein, and ways to support them, and submit the necessary Recommendations in this regard to the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

2- Prepare the infrastructure, headquarters, facilities, administrative services, and any other services necessary to achieve the objectives of the Authority.

3- Supervise and monitor the management of Free Zones, follow up on their affairs, and develop the necessary plans and programs for their development.

4- Issue Licenses, approvals, and permits for companies and other entities wishing to engage in economic activity in the Free Zone or through it, as specified by the regulations.

5- Determine and collect fees and service charges it provides.

6- Collect any taxes due to the State.

7- Facilitate work procedures between registered companies operating inside the Free Zone and other entities outside it.

8- Enter into arrangements with suppliers to provide Information Technology (IT) and Telecommunications Services and equipment.

9- Establish companies and enter into the necessary agreements to develop the infrastructure for any Free Zone.

10- Develop the Authority’s regulations related to the establishment, management, development, and operation of Free Zones and organize work within them.

11- Undertake any authorized economic activities by establishing companies or participating in registered companies.

12- Appoint an advisory board of experts with experience in similar authorities’ activities to provide advice to the Authority on its strategy for achieving its objectives.

13- Appoint employees, consultants, and other service providers.

14- Cooperate and enter into arrangements with authorities, entities, and organizations engaged in similar activities.

15- Undertake any tasks or duties entrusted to it by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers related to achieving its objectives.

###### Article 20 Amended

The regulations organize all matters necessary for implementing this Law / Act and achieving its objectives, specifically the following:

1- Establishment, registration, and operation of companies, projects, and other entities operating in or through the free zone, and clarification of their rights and obligations, as well as the provisions related to their liquidation.

2- Granting and acceptance of guarantees.

3- Regulations governing the granting of licenses and equity rights to land and facilities of the free zone, or their leasing to registered companies.

4- Prohibition of money laundering and unlawful financial transactions, in coordination with Qatar Central Bank.

5- Confidentiality and Information Protection.

6- Intellectual Property Rights.

7- Protection of the environment and public health.

8- Conditions and standards for planning and developing any free zone.

9- Compensation and financial penalties resulting from violations of the provisions of this Law / Act or the regulations.

#### Chapter Two (21-30)

##### Management of the Authority (21-30)

###### Article 21 Amended

The management of the Authority shall be undertaken by a Board of Directors consisting of a Chairman, a Vice Chairman, and members with experience and competence from various relevant sectors, who are appointed by an Amiri decision based on a proposal by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

The term of the Council shall be four years, renewable for a similar term or terms.

The determination of the remuneration for the Chairman, Vice Chairman, and members of the Council shall be issued by a decision of the Emir.

The Council shall have a Secretary, chosen by the Council, whose responsibilities and financial remuneration shall be determined.

###### Article 22

###### Article 22 Canceled

The term of the Council is three years, renewable for one or more similar periods.

###### Article 23

The Council of Ministers shall have all the authorities and powers necessary to manage the affairs of the Authority and the zones and achieve its objectives, in particular the following:

1- Setting the policies and general plans for the Authority.

2- Issuing the Organizational Structure of the Authority, and approving the regulations necessary for managing its affairs and organizing and operating the zones.

3- Providing the infrastructure, facilities, staff, services, and support necessary for managing and operating the zones in accordance with the best International Standards.

4- Approving the annual budget of the Authority and its final account.

5- Appointing zone managers and defining their assigned responsibilities and duties.

6- Undertaking any tasks, duties, or responsibilities assigned to it by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers related to the activities of the Authority.

###### Article 24

The Chairman of the Council represents the Authority before the judiciary and in its relations with others.

###### Article 25 Amended

The Council may select from among its members a delegate member to the Authority, who shall oversee the implementation of the policies and decisions of the Council, be responsible for that before the Council, and the Council shall determine his remuneration.

The Council may assign the delegate member to supervise the implementation of some administrative, financial, and technical functions required by the nature of the Authority's work.

The Council may delegate some of its powers or functions to one or more Committees composed of some of its members, and the Council may set the regulations that govern the working procedures of this Committee or Committees.

###### Article 26

The Council meets at the invitation of its Chairman / President at least once every three months, and whenever necessary. Each member of the Council must be notified in writing of the time, place, and date of the meeting at least forty-eight hours before it convenes, unless necessity dictates otherwise.

The meeting of the Council shall not be valid unless the majority of its members are present, provided that the Chairman / President or his deputy is among them.

###### Article 27

تصدر قرارات المجلس بأغلبية أصوات الأعضاء الحاضرين، وعند تساوي الأصوات يرجح الجانب الذي منه الرئيس.

###### Article 28

A written and signed Decision / Resolution by all members of the Council, or one of its Committees, shall be valid and effective as if it were issued at a meeting of the Council or the Committee.

###### Article 29

The Chairman of the Council, his Deputy, any of its members, or any employee of the Authority may not have a direct or indirect personal interest in contracts concluded with the Authority or on its behalf, or in projects carried out by it. No member may participate in voting at a meeting concerning a matter in which he has a direct or indirect personal interest.

###### Article 30 Amended

The Authority shall have a Chief Executive, appointed by decision of the Council, who, under its supervision and within the framework of the Authority's general policy, shall manage its administrative, financial and technical affairs In accordance with the provisions of this Law and the prescribed regulations and rules, within the limits of its annual budget, and implement the decisions of the Council. He shall in particular have the following powers:

1- Proposing plans and programs for the Authority.

2- Proposing the Organizational Structure of the Authority and the regulations necessary for its management and the operation of the Free Zones.

3- Registering companies and other entities wishing to engage in Economic Activities in the Free Zones.

4- Issuing Licenses and certificates related to engaging in Economic Activities in the Free Zones.

5- Proposing the annual budget draft for the Authority and submitting it to the Council for approval.

6- Preparing draft legislations and decisions necessary for the Authority's work and submitting them to the Council to take appropriate action.

7- Preparing an annual report on the Authority's achievements and work programs and submitting it to the Council.

8- Preparing the final financial statement of the Authority for the ended fiscal year and submitting it to the Council.

9- Signing contracts and agreements with others as authorized In accordance with this Law and its regulations.

10- Proposing fees and service charges provided by the Authority, as well as administrative and financial penalties and procedures for their collection.

11- Any other tasks assigned by the Council.

#### Chapter Three (31-38)

##### The Financial Structure of the Authority (31-38)

###### Article 31 Amended

The financial year of the Authority begins on the first day of January and ends on the last day of December each year, provided that the first financial year of the Authority starts from the date of implementation of this Law / Act and ends on the last day of December of the following year.

###### Article 32 Amended

The financial resources of the Authority consist of the following:

1- The funds and allocations provided to it by the State.

2- Fees and service charges it provides.

3- Rentals from properties located within the free zone.

4- Financial penalties it imposes.

5- Other revenues it generates.

###### Article 32 Amended Canceled

The financial resources of the Authority consist of the following:

1- The funds and allocations allocated to it by the State.

2- The fees for the services it provides.

3- The rents of properties located within the area.

4- The financial penalties it imposes.

5- Other revenues it generates.

###### Article 33 Amended

The Authority may borrow, including in a currency other than the currency of the State, to achieve its objectives, after the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

Any of the revenues, funds, or properties of the Authority may be provided as collateral for such loans or their interest.

###### Article 33 Amended Canceled

The Authority may borrow, including funds in a currency other than the State's currency, to achieve its objectives.

Approval from the Cabinet / Council of Ministers must be obtained for any loans that exceed, in total, an amount of one hundred million Qatari riyals, or its equivalent in any other currency.

Any revenues, funds, or properties of the Authority may be provided as collateral for those loans or their interest.

###### Article 34

The Authority may retain any surplus it achieves, whether it was included in the budget estimates or not, or decide to return it to the State, within the limits of the funding it received from it.

The Authority may use this surplus to pay its debts, or to form a general reserve, or any other reserves as the Council deems appropriate.

The Authority may also invest its funds, which are not immediately required for expenditure, in investment avenues authorized by the Council.

###### Article 35

The Authority shall keep sufficient accounting records to show and disclose its transactions, so that these records at any time accurately present the revenues, expenses, assets, liabilities, and financial position of the Authority.

The Council shall, within a period not exceeding three months after the end of each financial year, prepare the accounts of the Authority in accordance with internationally accepted accounting principles and ensure that they are audited by independent auditors from an accredited audit firm that has an Office in the State.

The auditors shall state in their report whether these accounts provide a true and fair view of the financial position of the Authority during the relevant financial year, and disclose the assets and liabilities of the Authority at the end of that financial year. The auditors shall include in their report any other matters as they deem appropriate.

###### Article 36

The Cabinet / Council of Ministers may appoint one or more auditors to audit the accounts of the Authority, and the auditor shall at all times have the right to review the books, records, and documents of the Authority, and to request any data he deems necessary to perform his duty. He may verify the assets and liabilities of the Authority, and if he is unable to exercise these rights, he shall submit a report to the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

###### Article 37

The Council shall submit to the Cabinet / Council of Ministers a detailed report on the Authority’s activities, projects, workflow, and financial position, within a period not exceeding three months from the end of the fiscal year, including its proposals and recommendations, accompanied by a copy of the auditor’s report.

###### Article 38

لمجلس الوزراء في أي وقت، أن يطلب من الهيئة تقديم تقارير عن أوضاعها الإدارية والمالية والفنية، أو أي وجه من أوجه نشاطها أو أي معلومات تتعلق بها. وله أن يصدر للهيئة توجيهات عامة بشأن ما يجب عليها اتباعه في الأمور المتعلقة بنشاطها.

## Part 4 (39-49)

### General and Final Provisions (39-49)

#### Article 39 Amended

Subject to the provisions stipulated in this Law / Act, criminal laws and penalties applied in the State shall apply to the areas.

However, performing any activity in the free zone, under a License issued in accordance with the provisions of this Law / Act or the regulations, shall not constitute a breach of any criminal Law / Act or any other Law / Act applied in the State.

#### Article 40

All Law / Act, regulations, and civil rules applied in the State apply to the areas, except for those that conflict with the provisions of this Law / Act and regulations.

#### Article 41 Amended

Activities carried out in the State outside the free zone, by an entity established within it or operating from it, regarding contracts, transactions, or arrangements concluded between this entity and persons residing in the State or entities established in it outside the free zone, are subject to the laws and regulations in force in the State.

#### Article 42 Amended

The regulation of the License for the residence of workers in the Free Zones and their family members shall be determined by a decision from the Minister of Interior, which includes the following:

1- Entry visas for non-Qataris, including temporary entry visas granted directly at the ports of entry.

2- Recruitment of Workers and the regulation of their residence in the Free Zone.

#### Article 42 (Duplicate)

The Qatar Central Bank is responsible for formulating and monitoring policies related to the regulation, licensing, oversight, and supervision of all activities of banks and financial institutions operating in the free zone.

#### Article 43

The laws and regulations governing civil service in the State do not apply to the Authority or any of its employees. The Authority has the power to establish its internal regulations regarding the rules, conditions, and circumstances that apply to its staff.

#### Article 44 Amended

The civil and commercial Court, established at the Qatar Financial Centre pursuant to the aforementioned Qatar Financial Centre Law, is competent to adjudicate all civil and commercial disputes and lawsuits between companies registered in the Free Zones, between the Authority and individuals or companies registered in the Free Zones, or between companies registered in the Free Zones on one hand and individuals residing in the State or companies or entities established outside the Free Zones on the other hand, regardless of the legal nature of the relationship subject to the dispute, unless the parties agree to resolve the dispute through alternative means.

#### Article 44 Amended Canceled

A center for dispute resolution shall be established, specializing exclusively in the settlement of disputes related to free zones, by means of conciliation and arbitration.

The Cabinet / Council of Ministers shall issue, based on the proposal of the Minister of Justice, a decision on the Regulation of the center, its jurisdictions, and the manner of Enforcement of Judgments and decisions it issues.

#### Article 45 Amended

The Authority is exempt from the application of the provisions of Law / Act No. (2) of 2015, and Law / Act No. (24) of 2015, as referred to.

#### Article 46

The Chairman and members of the Board of Directors of the Authority, its employees, and persons working under its supervision shall not bear civil liability arising from their acts or omissions, carried out in good faith, during the performance of the duties entrusted to them to implement the provisions of this Law / Act.

#### Article 47

All members of the Authority's Board of Directors, employees, persons working under its supervision, and regional directors are considered public employees in the application of the provisions of the Penal Code.

#### Article 48 Amended

The Council issues the regulations and decisions necessary to implement the provisions of this Law / Act.

#### Article 49

All relevant authorities, each within its area of responsibility, shall implement this Law / Act. It shall be published in the official gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Deputy Amir of the State of Qatar
