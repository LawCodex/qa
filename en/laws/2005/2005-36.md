# Law No. 36 of 2005 Establishing a Free Zone for Qatar Science and Technology Park (QSTP)

We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, Deputy Emir of Qatar,

Having perused the Constitution,

Decree-Law No. 16 of 2003 pertaining to the establishment of a Free Zone for Qatar Foundation for

Education, Science and Community Development and related projects,

The draft law presented by the Council of Ministers,

And having consulted the opinion of the Shura Council,

Hereby promulgate the following law:

## Chapter One (1)

### Definitions (1)

#### Article 1

For the application of the provisions of this Law, the following words and expressions shall have the meanings indicated next to each of them, unless the context requires otherwise:

Institution: Qatar Foundation for Education, Science and Community Development.

Science and Technology Oasis: A scientific center affiliated with the Institution.

Free Zone: The Free Zone of the Science and Technology Oasis.

Board of Directors: Board of Directors of the Science and Technology Oasis.

Customs Area: All lands of the State and its territorial sea, except for the Free Zone of the Science and Technology Oasis and the other free zones.

Investment: Establishment and operation of a project that includes one or more of the following activities: research and development, production development, technical training, technical Consultation, establishment of companies for the purposes referred to, or provision of services to support entities undertaking the aforementioned activities.

License: The license granted by the Board of Directors to any project to conduct activity in the Free Zone.

Regulations: The regulations of the Free Zone announced by the Board of Directors regarding investment, licensing procedures, fees, and other regulatory matters in the Free Zone.

## Chapter Two (2-5)

### Establishment and Management of the Free Zone (2-5)

#### Article 2

An investment free zone is established for the Science and Technology Oasis, and its boundaries and coordinates are determined according to the maps, planning, and surveying diagrams attached to this Law / Act.

The boundaries of this zone may be modified by a decision from the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

#### Article 3

The free zone aims to enhance and support applied scientific and technological research, and to carry out investment activities to serve the objectives of the Science and Technology Oasis.

#### Article 4

The Free Zone is managed by the Board of Directors of the Science and Technology Park.

#### Article 5

The Board of Directors, in addition to the powers and authorities specified for it by the institution, manages the affairs of the free zone and achieves its objectives, particularly the following:

1- Issuing the Organizational Structure of the free zone and the regulations necessary for its management.

2- Issuing regulations related to Licenses within the free zone without adhering to government rules and regulations. The Board may issue regulations to organize work procedures between projects in the zone and other relevant entities in the State, especially the Ministries of Economy and Trade, Finance, Interior, and the Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Agriculture regarding the issuance of building Licenses, in coordination with these entities.

3- Issuing certificates of establishment and registration for companies, their branches, and other entities seeking Licenses in the free zone.

4- Issuing the necessary Licenses to practice activities in the free zone.

5- Concluding contracts and agreements related to the free zone.

6- Establishing companies that contribute to achieving the purposes of the zone.

## Chapter Three (6-10)

### Procedures within the Free Zone (6-10)

#### Article 6

Applications for licenses to carry out the authorized activities in the free zone are submitted to the Board of Directors in accordance with the regulatory rules of the zone.

#### Article 7

It is prohibited to carry out any activity within the free zone except after obtaining a License from the Board of Directors and within the scope of the License.

#### Article 8

One or more non-Qatari individuals or legal entities may own a project licensed to carry out activities within the free zone.

#### Article 9

The certificates of establishment and registration of companies and branches, and the licenses issued by the Board of Directors for any project, are recognized by all relevant authorities in the State, especially the Ministries of Economy, Trade, Finance, Interior, and the General Authority for Customs and Ports, if they are signed by the Delegate / Authorized Person appointed by the Board of Directors, and stamped with the institution's seal.

#### Article 10

The Board of Directors may invoke License Revocation if the licensee breaches any of the licensing conditions or if a period of six months has passed from the date of issuing the license without commencing the activities for which the license was granted.

## Chapter Four (11-16)

### Privileges and guarantees (11-16)

#### Article 11

Goods, materials, devices, tools, equipment, and means of transportation imported from abroad for use in the free zone by the licensed project are not subject to customs duties or other fees.

#### Article 12

Goods and products manufactured in the free zone are not subject to export-related customs duties.

#### Article 13

Goods leaving the free zone for the customs area are considered imported from abroad for the first time. Customs Duties are imposed on these goods in accordance with the applicable customs tariff and procedures.

#### Article 14

Projects licensed to carry out activities in the free zone and their employees are exempt from all taxes, including Income Tax, related to the activities they conduct in the free zone.

#### Article 15

The assets and activities of the licensed project are not subject to any confiscation procedures or restrictions related to the repatriation of capital, profits, and salaries in any currency to any place outside the free zone. Furthermore, dealing with or holding foreign currency is not subject to any restrictions within the free zone during the execution of activities.

Seizure of funds belonging to licensed projects in the free zone is prohibited, except by a judicial ruling.

#### Article 16

For projects licensed to engage in activities in the free zone, employing or sponsoring any person to assist in conducting its activities in the zone shall be in accordance with the following:

1- Giving priority to employing Qataris in order to continue the policy of Qatarization pursued by the State.

2- Not being restricted by the nationality of the labor force or by the relative quotas for non-Qatari labor that are applied in the State, provided that this labor does not belong to a country from which the State of Qatar has decided not to recruit workers.

3- Not being restricted by a specific period of employment for non-Qatari labor when transferring their sponsorship to licensed projects, in coordination with the Ministry of Interior.

## Chapter Five (17-20)

### Obligations of Licensed Projects (17-20)

#### Article 17

Licensed projects operating in the Free Zone must comply with the following:

1- Limiting their activities to the scope of the granted license, and not engaging in any other activity unless licensed to do so.

2- Providing any documents, records, or accounts requested by the Board of Directors for the purpose of conducting any statistical operations.

3- Observing the laws, regulations, and instructions governing work in the Free Zone or those required to protect public order and morals and ensure security within the zone.

4- Notifying the Board of Directors in writing of the decision to stop or terminate the licensed project at least six months prior to the date of stopping or termination.

5- Paying any fees or expenses required by the project license.

#### Article 18

The following goods are prohibited from entering the Free Zone:

1- Flammable goods and commodities.

2- Radioactive materials.

3- Weapons and Ammunition and explosives of all types.

4- Goods that violate the laws of the State related to the protection of Intellectual Property Rights, literary, artistic, industrial, and commercial rights.

5- Narcotics of all types and their derivatives.

6- Goods coming from any State that is economically boycotted by the State of Qatar.

7- Any other goods whose entry is prohibited to the State.

However, the entry of any of the aforementioned goods is permitted, provided that a prior License is obtained from the competent government authorities, prior written approval from the Board of Directors, and the implementation of any conditions specified by the Board of Directors with respect to such approval.

#### Article 19

The following is prohibited within the free zone:

1- Practicing any profession or craft within the free zone without a prior License from the Board of Directors.

2- Using the License of a particular project to establish another project.

3- The project owner's transfer of the License granted to him to another party without prior approval from the Board of Directors.

4- Carrying out any activities or acts that violate public order or public morals in the State.

#### Article 20

If a License for a project is issued before the availability of buildings in the free zone, the Board of Directors may grant the project a temporary License to operate outside the free zone, considering this project, within the scope of that License, as operating within the free zone for the purposes of this Law and its implementing regulations. This License shall be limited to specific types and locations of work determined by the project, in coordination with the competent authorities.

## Chapter Six (21-22)

### Regulating Relationship with other Legislation (21-22)

#### Article 21

1- In matters not provided for in this Law / Act, the establishment and organization of companies or registration of branches of entities seeking to obtain Licenses in the free zone and the management of such projects in the free zone shall be exclusively and systematically subject to the provisions of this Law / Act and the implementing decisions thereof.

2- In the event a License is granted to practice any activity in the free zone for any person, company, branch, or other entity in accordance with this Law / Act, they shall not be required to obtain an additional License, approval, permit, membership, or registration in the State to practice this Licensed activity in the free zone.

3- The approval, authorization, License, supervision, or regulation of any activity exercised by a person, company, branch, or other entity Licensed in accordance with this Law / Act and the implementing decisions thereof is outside the competence of other authorities in the State, especially the Ministry of Economy and Commerce, the Commercial Register Office, Qatar Chamber of Commerce and Industry, and Doha Municipality.

#### Article 22

1- The criminal Laws / Acts and penalties in force in the State shall apply in the Free Zone. However, carrying out any activity licensed in the Free Zone in accordance with the license issued under this Law / Act and the implementing decisions thereof shall not constitute a violation or breach of any criminal Law / Act or other Law / Act applicable in the State.

2- Subject to the provisions of the preceding clause, the civil laws, rules, and regulations of the State shall apply in the Free Zone, including but not limited to contracts, transactions, or agreements concluded in the Zone between entities established therein or between entities and their employees and contractors, except for those excluded or contradicted by the regulations.

3- Without prejudice to any Ruling in this Law / Act conflicting with another Law / Act or other regulations in the State, the Board of Directors shall have the authority to grant visas, permits, and other documents related to or required for employment in any licensed activity within the Free Zone, in coordination with the Ministry of Interior.

## Chapter Seven (23-28)

### Sanctions (23-28)

#### Article 23

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, any person who enters the free zone or deals within it with any of the prohibited goods referred to in Article (18) of this Law / Act shall be punished with imprisonment for a period not exceeding one year and with a fine not exceeding ten thousand Riyals or with one of these penalties.

#### Article 24

He shall be punished by imprisonment for a period not exceeding six months and a fine not less than five thousand riyals, or by either of these two penalties, anyone who practices an activity in the free zone without a License, in accordance with the provisions of this Law / Act.

#### Article 25

Employees of the Free Zone who are authorized by a decision issued by the Attorney General, in agreement with the Chairman of the Board of Directors, shall have the Capacity of Law enforcement Officer in apprehending and substantiating Crimes that occur in violation of the provisions of this Law / Act, and the regulations and decisions implementing it.

#### Article 26

The Board of Directors issues the regulations and decisions necessary for implementing the provisions of this Law / Act.

#### Article 27

Decree 34 by Law No. (16) of 2003 referred to is cancelled.

#### Article 28

All relevant authorities, each within its area of responsibility, shall implement this Law / Act. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Deputy Amir of the State of Qatar
