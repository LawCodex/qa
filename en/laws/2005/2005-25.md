# Law No. (25) of 2005 regarding the Commercial Register

We, Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani, Emir of the State of Qatar,

After reviewing the Articles of Association, and in particular Articles (23), (34), (51) thereof,

And Law No. (11) of 1962 on the establishment of the Commercial Register, and its amending laws,

And Law No. (5) of 1970 defining the powers of ministers and specifying the competencies of ministries and other government bodies, and its amending laws,

And the Civil and Commercial Matters Law issued by Law No. (16) of 1971, and its amending laws,

And Law No. (3) of 1975 concerning commercial, industrial, and similar public shops, and its amending laws,

And the Commercial Companies Law issued by Law No. (5) of 2002,

And Law No. (8) of 2002 concerning the regulation of commercial agency activities,

And Law No. (22) of 2004 issuing the Civil Law,

And Decree by Law No. (32) of 2004 on the Regulation of the Ministry of Economy and Commerce and specifying its competencies,

Upon the proposal of the Minister of Economy and Commerce, and upon the Draft Law submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

And after consulting the Shura Council,

We have decided on the following Law:

## Chapter One (1-14)

### Specific provisions of the commercial register (1-14)

#### Article 1 Amended

For the application of the provisions of this Law, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings indicated opposite each of them, unless the context requires otherwise:

Ministry: Ministry of Economy and Commerce.

Minister: Minister of Economy and Commerce.

Department / Directorate concerned: the administrative Unit concerned at the Ministry.

Register: the register referred to in Article (2) of this Law.

#### Article 2 Amended

A commercial register or more shall be prepared in the competent Department / Directorate, in which the names of those subject to the provisions of this Law / Act and the data of their commercial activities shall be recorded.

#### Article 3 Amended

The following must be registered in the Commercial Register:

1- Individuals who wish to engage in commerce.

2- Commercial Companies.

3- Natural and legal persons who carry out Agency business.

4- Branches of foreign companies.

5- Commercial representation offices.

The branches of companies, commercial establishments, and Agencies shall be registered under the same Commercial Register number granted to the company, establishment, or Agency.

Also, the name of the merchant who has a branch or Agency in Qatar shall be registered in the Commercial Register if their main establishment is abroad.

In all cases, it is not permitted to add trade names to company branches that differ from the company's trade name, nor is it allowed to add activities that differ from the purposes specified for the company in its incorporation document or bylaws, and the registration of company branches in the Commercial Register does not result in the branches acquiring a separate legal personality from the company.

#### Article 4 Amended

The application for Record or annotation in the Commercial Register shall be submitted by the merchant, the Legal Representative of the legal entity, the manager of the Branch / Subdivision or the agency, as appropriate, to the Department / Directorate concerned, on the form prepared for this purpose, accompanied by the supporting documents.

The Department / Directorate concerned shall decide on the application and notify the applicant of its decision on the same day of submission, provided that the application is complete with all the required documents and information.

In case the application is rejected, the rejection decision must be justified. Failure to notify the applicant of the Department / Directorate’s decision on the application on the same day of submission shall be considered an implicit rejection.

Anyone whose application is rejected may Raising a Grievance to the Minister within thirty days from the specified date for deciding on the application.

The Minister shall decide on the Grievance within fifteen days from the date of its submission. The lapse of this period without a response to the Grievance shall be deemed an implicit rejection, and the decision on the Grievance shall be final.

The Executive Regulations of this Law / Act shall specify the procedures to be followed for submitting the application for Record or annotation, the period during which the application must be submitted, and the information that must be included in the application.

#### Article 5 Amended

The duration of the Registration in the Commercial Register shall be determined by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on the proposal of the Minister, according to the nature or type of activity subject to the Registration in the Commercial Register, and this duration shall be renewable for one or more similar periods. The competent Department / Directorate shall, upon the application of the concerned party, renew the Registration accordingly.

The renewal application shall be submitted by the concerned party within the thirty days preceding the expiry date of the Registration, in accordance with the conditions specified by the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act.

The competent Department / Directorate may delete the Registration in the event of failure to submit the renewal application after ninety days have lapsed from the date the concerned party is notified by registered letter or any other means that provides proof of notification.

By way of exception, the competent Department / Directorate may, in accordance with the requirements of public interest and upon the request of the concerned party, renew the Registration in the Commercial Register after the periods stipulated in the two preceding paragraphs, provided that the prescribed fee for the entire period is paid, calculated from the date of expiry of the Registration or renewal, as the case may be.

#### Article 6

Anyone registered in the Commercial Register must write, on the facade of their store and on all correspondence, publications, and papers related to their business, their trade name and registration number in Arabic.

#### Article 7

No natural or legal person may engage in commerce or establish a commercial establishment except after registering in the Commercial Register.

#### Article 8

It is prohibited for anyone with a Registration in the Commercial Register to allow others to exploit their Commercial Register, and it is also prohibited for others to exploit a Commercial Register that does not belong to them or to indicate having a Registration in the Commercial Register without actually obtaining it.

#### Article 9

Anyone registered in the Commercial Register must, in accordance with the prescribed procedures, request the annotation in the Commercial Register of any change or modification to the Record data within thirty days from the date of the Contract, Judgment / Ruling, or the incident that requires it.

#### Article 10 Amended

The competent Court Registry must send a copy of the judgments, orders, and decisions issued against any trader or any of the companies mentioned in items (2) and (3) of Article (3) of this Law / Act, within thirty days from the date of issuance, to the competent Department / Directorate in order to duly annotate it in the Commercial Register in the following cases:

1- Judgments declaring bankruptcy or revoking it, and judgments determining the date of cessation of payment of debts or amending it.

2- Judgments declaring the closure of bankruptcy proceedings and judgments reopening them.

3- Judgments of Rehabilitation.

4- Orders commencing Conciliation procedures, and judgments ratifying, revoking, annulling, or closing such procedures, and judgments ratifying judicial Conciliation or revoking or annulling it.

5- Judgments and decisions imposing guardianship on the trader, appointing administrators or agents for absentees, their dismissal, or lifting the guardianship.

6- Decisions granting permission to minors to trade in a commercial establishment, or revoking or restricting it.

7- Judgments imposing a Criminal Penalty, as well as the name of the administrator and date of appointment.

8- Judgments dissolving or annulling the company's board of directors or dismissing the managers.

9- Judgments dissolving, liquidating, or annulling companies and appointing or dismissing liquidators.

#### Article 11

The trader, or whoever the commercial establishment devolves to, or the Legal Representative of the legal entity, as the case may be, must request the deletion of the Registration in the Commercial Register in the following cases:

1- The trader retires from his trade.

2- The trader's death.

3- The liquidation of the legal entity ends or its activity ceases for any reason.

#### Article 12 Amended

The request to erase the Record must be submitted within thirty days from the date of the occurrence of any of the cases stipulated in the previous article. If the concerned party does not submit this request, the competent Department / Directorate shall erase the Record sua sponte / motu proprio after verifying the reason that necessitated it, and shall notify the concerned party within ten days following the erasure of the Record, by registered letter or any other means that ensures knowledge, and shall notify the competent administrative authorities to take the necessary procedures arising from this.

#### Article 13

Any interested party may obtain from the competent Department / Directorate an extracted copy from the Record, or a Testimony of certain data, or a negative Testimony in case of non-registration.

The extracted copy may not include the following:

1- Rulings declaring bankruptcy, if they have been annulled or the reputation has been restored.

2- Rulings and decisions of interdiction, if the interdiction has been lifted.

#### Article 14

The fees for Registration in the Commercial Register, its renewal, annotation, deletion, extracts, and copies shall be determined by a decision of the Minister, and the Minister's decision shall not be effective except after its approval by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

## Chapter Two (15-16)

### Sanctions (15-16)

#### Article 15 Amended

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, a fine not exceeding fifty thousand riyals shall be imposed on anyone who violates the provisions of Article (6) of this Law / Act.

#### Article 16 Amended

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, a penalty of imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, and a fine not exceeding two hundred thousand riyals, or either of these penalties, shall be imposed on anyone who:

1- Violates any of the provisions of Articles (7), (8) of this Law / Act.

2- Submits incorrect data or forged documents, or engages in fraud or deception to achieve record or annotation in the Commercial Register.

The court may also order, in addition, the erasure of the record from the Commercial Register, or the modification of its data, or the closure of the establishment or suspension of the activity for a period not exceeding one year.

#### Article 16 (Duplicate)

The minister, or whoever he delegates, may settle in the Crimes stipulated in this Law / Act, at any stage the Lawsuit / Legal Action is in, before a final Ruling is issued, in exchange for paying half the maximum prescribed fine and removing the causes of the violation. The settlement results in the extinguishment of the criminal Lawsuit / Legal Action and its consequent effects.

## Chapter Three (17-20)

### General provisions (17-20)

#### Article 17

Employees of the Ministry who are appointed by a decision of the Attorney General, in agreement with the Minister, shall have the Capacity of Law enforcement Officer in detecting and establishing Crimes committed in violation of the provisions of this Law / Act.

#### Article 18

The Minister shall issue the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act, as well as the decisions necessary for the implementation of its provisions. Until these Executive Regulations and decisions come into effect, the currently applied decisions and systems shall remain in force insofar as they do not conflict with the provisions of this Law / Act.

#### Article 19

Law / Act No. (11) of 1962 referred to above is hereby repealed, as is any provision contrary to the provisions of this Law / Act.

#### Article 20

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, shall implement this Law / Act, and it shall be published in the official gazette.

Hamad Bin Khalifa Al-Thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
