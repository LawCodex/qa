# Law No. (12) of 2020 on the Regulation of partnership between the government and private sectors

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

After perusal of the Constitution,

And Law No. (10) of 1987 regarding Public and Private State Property, and the amending laws thereof,

And the Law on the Financial System of the State issued by Law No. (2) of 2015,

And the Commercial Companies Law issued by Law No. (11) of 2015,

And the Law Regulating Tenders and Bids issued by Law No. (24) of 2015, as amended by Decree Law No. (18) of 2018,

And Law No. (1) of 2019 on the Regulation of Non-Qatari Capital Investment in Economic Activity,

And Amiri Decision No. (44) of 2008 adopting the comprehensive development vision "Qatar National Vision 2030",

And upon the proposal of the Minister of Commerce and Industry,

And upon the Draft Law submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

And after seeking the opinion of the Shura Council,

We have decided the following Law:

## المواد (1-30)

### Article 1

For the application of the provisions of this Law, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings indicated next to each of them, unless the context requires another meaning:

Ministry: Minister of Commerce and Industry.

Minister: Minister of Commerce and Industry.

Department / Directorate: The administrative unit within the Ministry.

Government Body / Organ: The Ministry or Body / Organ or Authority or public institution.

Private Sector: The private legal entity or coalition among a group of private legal entities.

Partnership: An agreement between the government Body / Organ and the private sector to implement and finance works or provide services, in accordance with one of the regimes specified in Article (3) of this Law.

Contracting Body / Organ: Any government Body / Organ that concludes a partnership contract with the private sector, In accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Project: The project that is decided to be executed or developed through partnership, In accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Project Committee: The Committee formed for each project in accordance with Article (6) of this Law.

Project Company: The existing or newly established company to implement the project.

Partnership Contract: The contract concluded by the contracting Body / Organ with the project company for the purpose of executing a project.

General Partnership Policy: The general principles of partnership established by the Department / Directorate and approved In accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Project Policy Document: A document regulating the main aspects of the project study, including supporting documents In accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Project Study: The comprehensive detailed study prepared In accordance with the provisions of Article (7) of this Law.

### Article 2

The partnership between the government entity and the private sector shall be established under the partnership contract, In accordance with the provisions of this Law and the general partnership policy adopted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on the proposal of the minister.

It is permissible, with the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on the proposal of the minister, to exempt one of the projects implemented through partnership from being subject to the provisions of this Law.

### Article 3

The partnership shall be according to one of the following systems:

1- Allocating land through lease or usufruct license, for development by the private sector.

2- Build-Operate-Transfer (B.O.T) system.

3- Build-Transfer-Operate (B.T.O) system.

4- Build-Own-Operate-Transfer (B.O.O.T) system.

5- Operate and Maintain (O.M) system.

6- Any other system approved by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on the proposal of the minister.

### Article 4

Each of the government entity or the Department / Directorate concerned may, Sua sponte / Motu Proprio or based on a proposal from the private sector, designate a project for implementation through partnership, and submit it to the minister for approval in principle.

### Article 5

The contracting party prepares a Report on the project idea, which includes a summary of the project, a statement of its suitability for implementation through partnership, and the identification of the tasks and responsibilities of each party.

The Report is submitted to the minister for forwarding to the Prime Minister, accompanied by his recommendations, for consideration of its approval before preparing the project study.

### Article 6

A committee is formed for each project separately, by a decision of the minister, in coordination with the relevant government entities. The committee must include representatives from the contracting authority, the competent administration, and the Audit Bureau.

The project committee shall undertake the following:

1- Preparing the project policy document and presenting it to the minister for approval.

2- Evaluating bids.

3- Negotiating contracts.

4- Submitting a recommendation to the contracting authority regarding the winning bidder.

### Article 7

The contracting authority, in coordination with the project committee, prepares the project study, which includes, in particular, the following:

1- The strategic and operational advantages of implementing the project.

2- A description of the project, the contract model, the duration of the partnership contract, and the specification of the tasks of both the contracting authority and the project company.

3- A detailed plan for implementing the project, including specifying the Schedule for implementation.

4- The government entity whose participation may be required for the implementation of the project, and the lands that must be allocated for its execution.

5- Estimation of the capital cost of the project, any necessary payments from any government entity, and any government support in the form of guarantees or otherwise.

6- Description of incentives that may be granted to the winning bidder, the project company, its shareholders, or its main or subcontractors.

7- The financial and technical standards, controls, and requirements that must be met by the bidders, and their ability and competence in the field of specialization.

8- Statement of the financial and technical advantages resulting from implementing the project through partnership, and a statement of the risks borne by the project company.

9- The contracting authority’s administrative, financial, and technical ability to fulfill its obligations arising from the partnership contract, and to monitor its implementation.

10- Determination of the authority responsible for supervising the implementation of the partnership contract.

The project study is presented to the minister, to be forwarded to the Prime Minister, accompanied by his recommendations, for consideration of its approval.

### Article 8

The contracting authority, in coordination with the competent Department / Directorate, is responsible for announcing the project to be offered. The announcement may be made through publication in local or international newspapers and bulletins, or the electronic website of either, as well as the unified electronic website for State procurement.

The project may be offered for contracting by one of the following methods:

1- Two-stage tender.

2- Limited tender according to the specified acceptance lists or Specifications.

3- Practice.

4- Competition.

5- Auction.

6- Direct agreement.

7- Any other contracting method approved by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on the minister's proposal.

### Article 9

The contracting authority, in coordination with the project committee, prepares the project bidding documents, which must specifically include the following:

1- General information relevant to the project and necessary for preparing and submitting the bid.

2- Technical and financial specifications of the project, and any other requirements to be fulfilled by the bidder.

3- The form of the project company and the conditions that must be met.

4- The main terms and conditions of the partnership contract.

5- The incentives granted to the winning bidder, the project company, its shareholders, main contractors, or subcontractors, including financial and tax incentives if any.

6- The criteria and method by which the bidding and bid evaluation are conducted.

7- The forms and documents required to be submitted by the bidder.

8- The value of the initial insurance and the method of calculating the value of the Performance Bond, if required.

9- The schedule for bidding procedures, including the deadline for bid submission.

### Article 10

A consortium consisting of more than one company may submit a bid, and the bid shall be submitted in the name of the consortium. None of the companies forming the consortium may submit another bid, directly or indirectly, whether individually or through another consortium, unless the tender documents state otherwise. Any bid submitted in violation of this will not be accepted.

### Article 11

Except in cases of contracting through direct agreement, the contracting authority must undertake company qualification procedures before starting the tender process, unless the project study exempts it from the qualification procedures.

The contracting authority is responsible for setting the qualification criteria and procedures, taking into account the nature of the project.

### Article 12

The process of selecting the winning bidder is subject to the principles of Transparency, freedom of competition, equal opportunities, and equality, in accordance with the rules stated in this Law / Act.

The winning bidder must meet the approved financial and technical standards, controls, and requirements, as well as have the ability and competence in their field of specialization.

### Article 13

Bids that do not comply with the terms and conditions of the tender documents will be excluded.

### Article 14

The Prime Minister of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on the proposal of the minister and the request of the contracting entity, may cancel the tender procedures in the following cases:

1- If only a single bid is submitted, or if only one bid remains after the excluded bids.

2- If all bids are accompanied by reservations or conditions that do not conform to the terms and provisions of the tender documents, or cannot be evaluated.

3- Other cases specified by the tender documents.

The tender procedures may also be cancelled by a decision of the Prime Minister of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers if the public interest requires it.

In all cases, the contracting entity shall notify the bidders of the cancellation decision by any means that ensures acknowledgement.

### Article 15

Bidders are not entitled to claim any compensation or other costs in the event that the tender process is cancelled, unless the tender documents state otherwise.

### Article 16

Before announcing the winning bid or notifying its submitter, the project committee submits its recommendation to select the winning bid to the minister to forward it to the Prime Minister of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, accompanied by his recommendations, in order to obtain approval for the award. The recommendation must be attached with a copy of the draft partnership contract and any information or data that may be required for approval of the award.

### Article 17

The partnership contract must specifically include the following:

1- The nature and scope of the works or services that the project company must perform and the conditions for their implementation.

2- Ownership of the project’s funds and assets, the parties' obligations regarding delivery and receipt of works and assets, and the provisions regarding Transfer of Ownership.

3- Responsibility for obtaining Licenses, permits, and approvals.

4- Mutual Financial Obligations and their relation to the method of financing.

5- Determining the sale price of the product or the service fee on which the project is based, the Bases and rules for determination, the Bases and rules for adjusting the price upward or downward, how to address Inflation rates, and matters related to changing Interest Rates, if applicable.

6- Means of quality assurance, tools for oversight and monitoring of financial, administrative, and technical follow-up for the operation, exploitation, and maintenance of the project.

7- Regulating the contracting party's right to modify the conditions for construction, equipment, maintenance, operation, and exploitation, as well as other obligations of the project company, and the Bases and mechanisms for Compensation / Indemnity for such modification.

8- Types and amounts of insurance on the project, risks of its operation or exploitation, implementation guarantees issued in favor of the contracting party, and provisions and procedures for their recovery.

9- Determining the Bases for risk distribution related to legal amendments, unexpected events, or force majeure, and Compensation / Indemnity as determined by circumstances.

10- The duration of the Contract, cases of early or partial termination, and the parties’ related rights.

11- Cases in which the contracting party has the right to unilateral termination of the Contract and the Financial Obligations arising from exercising this right.

12- Regulating the rules for recovering the project at the end of the Contract’s term, or in cases of unilateral, early, or partial termination.

### Article 18

The duration of the partnership contract is determined by agreement of the parties and shall not exceed thirty years. By way of exception, contracts for a longer period may be concluded, or existing contracts may be extended, in accordance with the requirements of public interest, after approval of the Prime Minister of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on the proposal of the minister.

### Article 19

The contracting party may allow the private sector to participate in establishing the project company. If the contracting party does not wish to participate in the project company, the bidder who wins the partnership Contract must establish the project company, and its sole purpose shall be to execute the project subject to the partnership Contract.

The contracting party may, according to the tender documents and the Assessment criteria contained therein, authorize the winning bidder to execute the project subject to the Contract without establishing the project company, if the bidder has the capability to execute the project in its current state with the available financial and technical resources.

### Article 20

The project company may, after obtaining the contracting authority’s approval and providing sufficient guarantees, obtain a loan from banks operating inside the State or outside it, secured by its contractual rights and assets.

### Article 21

Subject to the provisions of Article (25) of this Law / Act, the project company may collect fees or generate revenues or earn financial returns from the project or from its assets or from its users, as agreed with the contracting party under the partnership contract and In accordance with the provisions of this Law.

### Article 22

It is permissible to agree to amend the Contract of partnership if unforeseen circumstances arise after the conclusion of the Contract, including amendments to the legislation in force at the time of its conclusion, which result in a disruption of the Fiscal Balance in the Contract, in order to ensure the restoration of the Fiscal Balance.

### Article 23

The Prime Minister, based on the proposal of the Minister, may exempt the project company from all or some of the restrictions imposed by the prevailing legislations on companies owned by non-Qataris, including owning, benefiting from, or leasing real estate.

### Article 24

In addition to the obligations stipulated in this Law and the decisions issued for its implementation and the partnership contract, the project company shall be obliged to the following:

1- Not to dissolve the project company or change its legal form or reduce its capital except after the approval of the minister, and the Articles of Association of the project company must include a prohibition on trading its Equities / Stocks before the date of completion of construction, furnishing, or development works, as well as a prohibition on trading the Equities / Stocks owned by the majority shareholder after this date except after the approval of the minister.

In all cases, it is not permitted to pledge the Equities / Stocks of the project company for any purpose other than financing or refinancing the partnership project, and any procedure or act done in violation of the provisions of this paragraph shall be null and void.

2- Maintain and preserve the assets and property of the project, look after them, and use them for the purpose for which they were prepared.

3- Not to sell any properties that it may own according to the terms of the partnership contract, including buildings, assets, movable and immovable property belonging to the project, except for sales made for the purpose of implementing replacement and renewal programs, according to the conditions specified in the partnership contract and after approval from the competent Department / Directorate.

4- Submit all documents, information, and data requested by the competent Department / Directorate or the contracting authority, cooperate with their employees, and allow them access to its sites for inspection at any time.

5- Provide environmental and health requirements and safety measures for the project's employees and its beneficiaries.

6- Not to contract with subcontractors except after the approval of the contracting authority, provided that this does not affect the obligations of the project company as set forth by this Law, the decisions issued for its implementation, and the partnership contract.

### Article 25

The project company is committed to continuing to provide the contracted services, maintaining their quality, and ensuring Equality among all beneficiaries.

### Article 26

Upon the expiration of the partnership contract period, ownership of the project, its facilities, and all its requirements shall revert to the State without compensation or indemnity, unless otherwise agreed in the partnership contract.

### Article 27

The provisions of the Law regulating Tenders and Bids and the Law of the State Financial System referred to shall not apply to all procedures of offering, awarding, contracts, and other procedures conducted In accordance with the provisions of this Law.

### Article 28

The partnership Contract is subject to the provisions of Qatari Law / Act, and any agreement to the contrary shall be null and void.

The Qatari courts have jurisdiction to decide disputes arising from the Contract between its parties. However, the Contract may include another mechanism for settling such disputes with the approval of the Prime Minister of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal by the minister.

### Article 29

The minister shall issue the necessary decisions to implement the provisions of this Law / Act, and until they are issued and enforced, the existing decisions shall continue to be in effect as long as they do not conflict with the provisions of this Law / Act.

### Article 30

All competent authorities, each within its competence, shall implement this Law / Act. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
