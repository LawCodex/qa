# Law No. (15) of 2011 regarding Anti-Human Trafficking

We, Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani, Emir of the State of Qatar,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And the Criminal Procedures Law / Act issued by Law / Act (23) of 2004, as amended by Law / Act No. (24) of 2009,

And Decree No. (10) of 2009 approving accession to the United Nations Convention against Transnational Organized Crime of 2000,

And upon the proposal of the Supreme Council for Family Affairs,

And upon the Draft Law submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

And after taking the opinion of the Advisory Council,

We have decided the following Law / Act:

## Chapter One: Definitions (1)

### Article 1 Amended

For the application of the provisions of this Law, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings set forth beside each of them, unless the context requires otherwise:

Organized criminal group: A group composed, according to a certain organization, of at least three persons, working continuously or for a period of time with the aim of committing Crimes of human trafficking or Crimes of migrant smuggling alone or with others, in order to obtain, directly or indirectly, a financial benefit or other material benefit.

Crime of a transnational nature: Any Crime committed in more than one State, or committed in one State but prepared, planned, directed, supervised or financed in another State, or committed in one State by an organized criminal group engaged in criminal activities in more than one State, or committed in one State and resulting in serious consequences in another State.

Competent authorities: The authorities, bodies, and institutions concerned with the implementation of the provisions of this Law.

Child: Any person who has not reached eighteen years of age.

Illegal entry: Crossing borders without complying with the necessary conditions for legal entry into the receiving State.

Forged travel or identity document: Any travel or identity document that has been forged or materially altered by an entity or Body not legally authorized to prepare or issue the travel or identity document on behalf of any State, or that has been issued improperly or obtained by fraud, corruption, coercion, or by any other unlawful means, or used by a person other than its lawful holder.

## Chapter Two: Human Trafficking (2-4)

### Article 2

A person is considered to have committed the crime of human trafficking if he uses in any way a natural person, transports, hands over, harbors, receives, or obtains him, whether inside the State or across its national borders, if this is done by using force or violence or threatening to do so, or by kidnapping, fraud, deception, exploitation of Power, or exploitation of a state of weakness or need, or the promise of giving or receiving financial amounts or benefits in exchange for obtaining the consent of a person to traffic another person under his control, all if these acts are intended for exploitation in any form, including exploitation in prostitution or other forms of sexual exploitation, exploitation of children in this and in pornographic materials or begging, forced labor or service, slavery or practices similar to slavery or enslavement, or the removal of organs or human tissues, or Part thereof.

### Article 3

The victim's consent is not considered in any of the crimes of human trafficking whenever any of the means specified in the previous article are used.

It is not a requirement for the crime of trafficking in children or persons lacking legal capacity that any of the aforementioned means be used.

### Article 4

The victim is not criminally or civilly liable for any human trafficking crime, whenever it arises or is directly related to their status as a victim.

### Article 4 (Repeated)

Anyone who commits any of the following acts is considered to have committed the crime of migrant smuggling:

1- Arranging the illegal entry of a person or persons into a country of which they are neither nationals nor residents.

2- Using unlawful means to enable a non-Qatari or a person who is not a resident of Qatar to remain in the country without complying with the legally prescribed residency requirements.

3- Preparing, arranging, providing, or possessing forged travel documents or identification with the intention of facilitating migrant smuggling.

4- Organizing, directing, agreeing, participating in, or inciting the commission of any of the acts specified in this article.

### Article 4 (Repeated 1)

The victims of migrant smuggling crimes as stipulated in the previous article are considered aggrieved parties if any of the following cases apply to them:

1- The commission of the Crime through an organized criminal group.

2- Threatening their lives and exposing their safety to danger.

3- Treating them in an inhumane or degrading manner.

### Article 4 (Repeated 2)

Victims of any of the migrant smuggling crimes stipulated in this Law / Act are not held criminally liable.

## Chapter Three: Protection of Victims (5-10)

### Article 5 Amended

The competent authorities ensure the protection and physical and psychological safety of the victims, provide them with Healthcare Services, educational and social support, and work to create appropriate conditions for their rehabilitation and reintegration into society in a manner that respects their needs, human dignity, age, and gender. They also cooperate and coordinate with the victims' countries, or the countries in which they permanently reside, to ensure their safe return.

The Ministry of Interior shall bear any expenses incurred for the return of the victims.

### Article 6 Amended

The competent authorities must ensure the following rights for victims:

1- Protection of their personal privacy and identity.

2- Providing them the opportunity to express their situation and to be identified.

3- Access to advice regarding their rights, and informing them of the legal and administrative procedures followed.

4- Staying in the State until the investigation and trial procedures are completed.

5- Access to legal assistance, including the use of a lawyer.

6- Receiving appropriate Compensation / Indemnity for damages that may have been inflicted upon them.

7- Receiving necessary security protection.

8- Taking the necessary measures to legalize their status in the State, in coordination with the competent authorities.

9- Placement in one of the specialized centers for shelter or rehabilitation, or with an accredited entity that undertakes to provide accommodation for them if needed.

### Article 7

The competent authorities provide suitable shelters for victims, allowing their families, lawyers, and representatives of the relevant authorities to visit them, in addition to other guarantees established by any other law.

### Article 8

The competent authorities must adhere to the confidentiality of information obtained that is related to the crimes stipulated in this Law / Act, and not disclose it except to the extent required for the implementation of its provisions.

### Article 9

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, through its diplomatic and consular missions abroad, provides all necessary assistance to Qatari victims and prepares all suitable conditions to protect them and return them to the State as quickly as possible.

### Article 10

The competent criminal المحكمة hearing the criminal الدعوى arising from any of the الجرائم stipulated in this القانون must adjudicate the subject matter of the Civil Action / Civil Case arising from these الجرائم.

## Chapter Four: International Judicial Cooperation (11-12)

### Article 11 Amended

The competent judicial authorities cooperate with their foreign counterparts in combating crimes of human trafficking and migrant smuggling and pursuing their perpetrators, including Information Exchange, conducting Investigations, assistance, judicial delegations, delivery of objects, recovery of funds, and other forms of judicial cooperation. All of this is carried out within the framework of the rules established by the aforementioned Criminal Procedure Law, the effective bilateral or multilateral agreements in the State, or according to the principle of reciprocity, provided that it does not conflict with the fundamental principles of the legal system in the State.

### Article 12 Amended

The competent judicial authorities may order the enforcement of final criminal judgments issued by competent foreign judicial authorities regarding the seizure, confiscation, or recovery of funds obtained from human trafficking crimes and migrant smuggling crimes and their proceeds, in accordance with the rules and procedures contained in the Bilateral Agreements or multilateral agreements in force in the State, or according to the principle of reciprocity.

## Chapter Five: Penalties (13-28)

### Article 13

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, the penalties stipulated in this Law shall apply.

### Article 14

Punishable by imprisonment for a period not exceeding seven years and a fine not exceeding (250,000) two hundred and fifty thousand riyals, anyone who commits one of the human trafficking crimes stipulated in Article (2) of this Law / Act.

### Article 15

Shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding fifteen years and a fine not exceeding (300,000) three hundred thousand riyals, anyone who commits the Crime of human trafficking in any of the following cases:

1- If the victim is a female, a child, a person lacking legal capacity, or a person with a disability.

2- If the Crime results in the death of the victim or causes permanent disability or an incurable disease.

3- If the perpetrator is the spouse of the victim, or one of his or her ascendants or descendants, or one who has Guardianship, Legal Guardianship, or Power over him or her.

4- If the act is committed through threats of killing, serious harm, physical or psychological torture, or by a person carrying a weapon.

5- If the perpetrator is a public official or assigned to a public service, and committed the Crime by exploiting this position.

6- If the Crime is committed by an organized criminal group, and the accused is one of its members.

7- If the Crime is of a transnational nature.

### Article 15 (Repeated)

Whoever intentionally and with the aim of obtaining, directly or indirectly, a financial benefit or another material benefit, commits any of the migrant smuggling crimes stipulated in Article (4 bis) of this Law / Act shall be punished with imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years and a fine not exceeding (200,000) two hundred thousand riyals.

### Article 15 (Repeated 1)

The penalties stipulated in the previous article shall be doubled if the Crime is accompanied by any of the following circumstances:

1- If it is committed by an organized criminal group where one of the perpetrators has contributed to its formation, organization, management, assumed leadership in it, or joined it knowing its purposes.

2- If one of the perpetrators exploited his authority, influence, or the facilities granted to him by virtue of his job, professional, or social activity.

3- If the commission of the Crime involves endangering or likely endangering the life or safety of a migrant.

4- Inhumane or degrading treatment of migrants.

5- If one of the perpetrators carried a weapon.

### Article 16

Shall be punished by imprisonment for a period not exceeding five years and a fine not exceeding (200,000) two hundred thousand riyals, anyone who uses force or threats or offers a gift or benefit of any kind or promises thereof to induce another person to give false testimony or conceal a matter or provide false statements or information at any stage of evidence gathering, investigation, or trial regarding the commission of any Crime among the Crimes stipulated in this Law / Act.

### Article 17 Amended

Imprisonment for a period not exceeding three years and a fine not exceeding (200,000) two hundred thousand riyals shall be imposed on anyone who attempts to commit any of the crimes of human trafficking or the crimes of migrant smuggling as stipulated in Articles (2) and (4 bis) of this Law / Act.

### Article 18

Shall be punished by imprisonment for a period not exceeding three years and a fine not exceeding (150,000) one hundred fifty thousand Riyals, anyone who conceals a perpetrator or objects or funds obtained from any of the Crimes stipulated in this Law / Act, or conceals any traces of the Crime or its tools with knowledge thereof.

The court may exempt from the Penalty / Sentence if the person who concealed a perpetrator is his/her spouse or one of his/her ascendants or descendants up to the second degree.

### Article 19

A prison sentence not exceeding three years and a fine not exceeding (150,000) one hundred and fifty thousand riyals shall be imposed on anyone who is proven to have knowledge of the commission of any of the Crimes stipulated in this Law or of an attempt to commit them, and fails to report this to the competent authorities. The Penalty / Sentence shall be a prison term not exceeding five years and a fine not exceeding (200,000) two hundred thousand riyals if the offender is a public employee or assigned to a public service, and the Crime occurred as a result of his breach of his job duties or the tasks assigned to him.

The court may exempt from the Penalty / Sentence if the person who refrained from reporting is the spouse of the offender or one of his/her ascendants or descendants up to the second degree.

### Article 20

Anyone who discloses or reveals the identity of the victim or witness in a manner that exposes them to danger, causes them harm, facilitates contact with the perpetrators, or provides them with incorrect information about their legal rights with the intention of harming them or compromising their physical, psychological, or mental safety shall be punished by imprisonment for a period not exceeding two years and a fine not exceeding fifty thousand (50,000) riyals.

### Article 21 Amended

A penalty of imprisonment for a period not exceeding seven years and a fine not exceeding fifty thousand riyals (50,000) shall be imposed on anyone who incites, by any means, the commission of any crime of human trafficking stipulated in this Law / Act, even if the incitement does not result in any effect.

### Article 22

The Official responsible for the actual management of the legal entity shall be punished by imprisonment for a period not exceeding five years and by a fine not exceeding two hundred thousand (200,000) riyals, if any Crime of the Crimes stipulated in this Law is committed by one of the employees of the legal entity in its name and for its benefit, if it is proven that he was aware of it or if the Crime was committed due to his breach of his job duties.

The legal entity shall be jointly responsible for the payment of any financial penalties imposed by a Ruling if the Crime was committed by one of its employees in its name and for its benefit.

The court may decide to suspend the activity of the legal entity for a period not exceeding two years or to cancel or withdraw its license as the case may be.

### Article 23

A Ruling shall always be issued for the confiscation of money, luggage, means of transport, or tools acquired from any of the Crimes stipulated in this Law / Act, or that were used in their commission, without prejudice to the rights of bona fide third parties.

### Article 24

Anyone who takes the initiative from among the perpetrators to inform the competent authorities about the Crime and its perpetrators before the authorities become aware of it shall be exempted from the Penalty / Sentence stipulated in this Law / Act, if the report leads to the apprehension of the remaining perpetrators and the funds obtained from this Crime.

The court may grant exemption from the Penalty / Sentence if the report is made after the authorities become aware of the Crime and it leads to the apprehension of the remaining perpetrators and the funds obtained from it.

The provisions of this article do not apply if the Crime results in the death of the victim or causes permanent disability or an incurable disease.

### Article 25 Amended

Victims in human trafficking crimes and victims of migrant smuggling crimes are exempt from the penalties prescribed for violating the provisions of Law / Act No. (21) of 2015 referred to above.

### Article 26

It is not required to file a complaint or request from the victim or their representative to initiate the Lawsuit / Legal Action arising from any of the Crimes stipulated in this Law / Act.

### Article 26 (Repeated)

By way of exception from the provisions of the referred Criminal Procedure Law, the detention period for anyone accused of committing acts related to crimes of human trafficking and migrant smuggling shall be no more than four days before being brought before the Public Prosecution, and the order issued by the Public Prosecution for pretrial detention, after the interrogation of the accused, shall be for fourteen days, which may be extended for a similar period.

### Article 27

All provisions contrary to this Law shall be repealed.

### Article 28

All relevant authorities, each within their jurisdiction, must implement this Law / Act. It shall be published in the official gazette.

Hamad Bin Khalifa Al-Thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
