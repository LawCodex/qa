# Law / Act No. (9) of 2025 amending certain provisions of Law / Act No. (20) of 2018 regarding the regulation of tourism

**We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani**

**Amir of the State of Qatar**,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And Law / Act No. (21) of 2015 on the Regulation of Entry, Exit and Residence of Expatriates, and its amending laws,

And Law / Act No. (20) of 2018 regarding the Regulation of Tourism,

And Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (15) of 2021 on the Establishment of Qatar Tourism (QT),

And Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (69) of 2024 on the reorganization of the Supreme Committee for Delivery & Legacy,

And Council of Ministers Decision No. (12) of 2024 on the Establishment of the Permanent Committee for Managing Visitor Entry to the State of Qatar (Hayya Platform),

Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

And upon the approval of the Shura Council,

**We have decided to ratify the following Law / Act**:

## المواد (1-4)

### Article 1

The phrase “Qatar Tourism (QT)” shall replace the word “Council” and the phrase “National Tourism Council”, respectively, wherever they appear in Law / Act No. (20) of 2018 referred to above.

The phrase “Chairman / President of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers” shall replace the phrase “The Council of Ministers” wherever it appears in Articles (10) and (31) of Law / Act No. (20) of 2018 referred to above.

The phrase “places of accommodation or residence” shall be replaced with the phrase “Hospitality Establishments” wherever it appears in Law / Act No. (20) of 2018 referred to above.

The word “Chairman / President” shall be replaced with the phrase “Board of Directors of the Council” wherever it appears in Law / Act No. (20) of 2018 referred to above, and with the phrase “Secretary General”, wherever it appears in Articles (22), (23), (24), and (25) of the same Law / Act.

Likewise, the phrase “Director General” shall be replaced with “Secretary General” wherever it appears in Articles (10), (16), (29), (30), (38), and (42) of Law / Act No. (20) of 2018 referred to above.

### Article 2

The texts of Articles (1), (3), (4), (6), (8), (14), (17), (32), (33), (40) of Law / Act No. (20) of 2018 referred to above shall be replaced with the following texts:

**Article (3):**

«A license application shall be submitted to the Competent Authority using the prescribed form, containing relevant data and attached supporting documents, including the Competent Authority’s approval of the preliminary design for all tourism asset development projects.

The Executive Regulation of this Law / Act shall specify conditions, requirements, bank guarantee, and insurance requirements necessary for granting the license.»

**Article (4):**

«The Competent Authority shall decide on the license application and notify the applicant of its decision at their national address or by any means as evidence of service, within one week from the date of application submission. In case of rejection, reasons must be provided, and if this period expires without a response, the application is deemed implicitly rejected.»

**Article (6):**

«The license duration shall be a maximum of five years, renewable for like periods upon payment of the prescribed fee.

The Executive Regulation of this Law / Act shall set out the regulations for applying this Article.»

**Article (8):**

«The Chairman / President, upon the proposal of the Director General, may by decision coordinate seasons for tourism festivals and events in the State within a unified national plan.

Qatar Tourism (QT) shall coordinate with relevant entities to supervise public events through Qatar Events Calendar and, after coordination, may reject or defer public event license applications to coordinate dates, avoid overlap, and ensure quality.

Other State authorities responsible for planning or licensing any aspects of public events must coordinate with Qatar Tourism (QT) prior to issuance of such licenses.»

**Article (14):**

«The licensee must provide Qatar Tourism (QT) with pricing plans and promotional offers at least ten days before announcing them to obtain the Competent Authority’s approval. Such programs are confidential, and Qatar Tourism (QT) employees are prohibited from disclosing related information to others.

It is not required to obtain the approval of Qatar Tourism (QT) for marketing or tourism promotion programs unrelated to pricing.

If the licensee is a tourist establishment or accommodation venue, they must provide Qatar Tourism (QT) with copies of agreements and contracts concluded with any other hotel or tourist establishment, whether operating inside or outside the State.»

**Article (17):**

«The licensee shall provide suitable opportunities for training, developing, and qualifying human resources in the tourism and hospitality sector at all technical and managerial levels, and ensure compliance with the guidelines set by Qatar Tourism (QT) regarding training, in coordination with the relevant authorities in the State.»

**Article (32):**

«Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years and/or a fine not exceeding (500,000) five hundred thousand riyals shall be imposed on anyone who:

1- Violates any provision of Article (2) of this Law / Act, and in addition, judgment may include closure of the establishment or Office.

2- Violates any provision of Article (18) of this Law / Act, and in addition, judgment may include closure of the establishment or Office, and cancellation of the License.

The Judgment / Ruling shall be published in two daily newspapers at the expense of the convicted, and on the website of Qatar Tourism (QT).

The Chairman / President may, in case of violation of the provisions of the two Articles mentioned in the first paragraph of this Article, close the establishment or Office, or tourism festival or event, or suspend the activity administratively and at the violator’s expense temporarily until the non-compliance is rectified.

If the establishment or Office or activity or festival or tourism event is part of an integrated establishment, Office, or larger activity, festival, or event, the closure in this case shall apply to the part in violation.

In case of closure, the accommodation or tourist establishment or festival/event Office subject to closure, as applicable, must provide a licensed alternative of the same category and level to clients with pre-closure contracts. If they fail, Qatar Tourism (QT) may provide this alternative at the violator’s expense and collect the costs, deducting from the Bank Guarantee it holds; otherwise, it shall be collected administratively.

The closure decision shall be published on the website of Qatar Tourism (QT).»

**Article (33):**

«Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, a fine not exceeding (150,000) one hundred and fifty thousand riyals shall be imposed on anyone who violates any provision of Articles (11), (12), (13), (14), (15), (16) of this Law / Act.

The court may in addition impose closure of the establishment or Office.

The Chairman / President may also, in case of violation of the provisions of Articles (11), (12), (13/paragraphs 1, 7, 8, 9), (16) of this Law / Act, close the establishment or Office or suspend the activity or tourism festival/event administratively and at the violator’s expense temporarily, until the violation is corrected.

If the establishment or Office or activity or festival or tourism event is part of an integrated establishment, Office, or larger activity, festival, or event, the closure in this case shall apply to the violating part.

The closure decision shall be published on the website of Qatar Tourism (QT).»

**Article (40):**

«Any concerned party has the right to Raising a Grievance against decisions issued in application of the provisions of this Law / Act to the Chairman / President within thirty days of notification of the decision at their national address, or by any means as evidence of service, or from the expiry of the time limit for deciding on the license application without response.

The Chairman / President shall decide on the Grievance within fifteen working days from its submission, and if this period expires without a response, the Grievance is deemed implicitly rejected. The decision on the Grievance shall be final.»

### Article 3

The following texts are added to Law No. (20) of 2018 referred to:

**Article (13/Item 10):**

“10- Observing the instructions issued by Qatar Tourism (QT), based on joint coordination with the competent entities in the State, regarding the regulations for issuing tourist visas and visit visas for tourism purposes, and adhering to the system prepared for this purpose.”

**Article (14/Bis):**

“The licensee must adhere to the marketing principles which determine the requirements and directives related to the marketing and promotion of the State of Qatar. These principles shall be issued by a decision of the Chairman / President.”

**Article (30/Bis):**

“Promotion of services provided in accommodation or housing places shall be In accordance with the provisions of this Law, with prior approval from Qatar Tourism (QT), and shall not be subject to any other systems, in accordance with the controls determined by the Executive Regulation of this Law.

Qatar Tourism (QT) shall grant special approvals and access to capture the destination and its tourist offers through photographs, audio and video recordings, and other necessary media for content organization.”

**Article (31/Bis):**

“Qatar Tourism (QT), in coordination with the concerned entities in the State, regulates all activities, services, and entities operating in the tourism and hospitality sector within the State.

The Chairman / President shall grant advantages to tourism establishments and activities in the State, after the approval of other competent entities.

The determination of new tourist areas shall be by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the Chairman / President.”

**Article (35/Bis):**

“The Penalties / Sentences stipulated in this Law shall be doubled in the case of recurrence. The accused shall be considered a repeat offender if he commits a similar crime within five years from the end date of the execution of the Penalty / Sentence imposed or its expiration by lapse of time.

All Crimes stipulated in this Law shall be considered similar in cases of recurrence.”

**Article (36/Bis):**

“The Chairman / President may impose one or more of the financial Penalties / Sentences stipulated in the Schedule / Table attached to this Law on the licensee if he commits a violation of the provisions of this Law or its Executive Regulation or the decisions, Licenses, or instructions issued by Qatar Tourism (QT) in implementation of the provisions of this Law, other than the Crimes and Penalties / Sentences stipulated in Chapter Four thereof.

The decisions of the Chairman / President must be justified, and the competent Department / Directorate must notify the licensee, at their national address or by any means that confirms notification, of the attributed violation and the necessity to provide their remarks within fifteen days from the date of notification. If the licensee does not provide their remarks within this period, or provides them and the competent Department / Directorate finds them unacceptable, the Chairman / President may impose a financial Penalty / Sentence not exceeding the maximum stated in the Schedule / Table attached to this Law.

When determining the Penalty / Sentence, the seriousness of the violation and its effects must be considered. The decisions of the Chairman / President shall be final.

The competent Department / Directorate must notify the violator of the decision to impose the Penalty / Sentence and publish it by any means it sees appropriate.

The violator must pay the amount of the financial Penalty / Sentence within ninety days from the date of notification of the violation.

In case of persistence in the violation or its repetition, or failure to correct it, or failure to pay the financial Penalty / Sentence, the Chairman / President may revoke the License or take any of the following measures until the reasons for the violation are removed:

1- Suspension of the event or activity.

2- Suspension of the License or non-renewal thereof.

3- Temporary closure of the establishment.”

### Article 4

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, must implement this Law / Act. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
