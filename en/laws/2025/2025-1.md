# Law / Act No. (1) of 2025 amending certain provisions of Law / Act No. (16) of 2018 concerning the regulation of ownership and use of real estate by non-Qataris

**We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar**,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And Law No. (16) of 2018 regarding the regulation of non-Qatari ownership and use of real estate,

And Amiri Decision No. (28) of 2023 on the establishment of the General Authority for the Regulation of the Real Estate Sector,

Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers:

And upon the approval of the Shura Council,

**We have decided to ratify the following Law**:

## المواد (1-3)

### Article 1

The word "Authority" replaces the word "Ministry" as stated in Article (3) of Law No. (16) of 2018 referred to.

### Article 2

The text of Article (1) of Law No. (16) of 2018 referred to, shall be replaced with the following text:

**Article (1)** :

«For the application of the provisions of this Law, the following words shall have the meanings indicated opposite each of them, unless the context requires otherwise:

The Minister: Minister of Municipality.

Authority: The Public Authority for Regulating the Real Estate Sector.

Committee: Committee for Regulating the Ownership and Utilization of Real Estate by Non-Qataris, as stipulated in Article (3) of this Law.

Real Estate: Vacant lands, buildings and constructions, residential units, and units subdivided within residential complexes.

### Article 3

All relevant authorities, each within its area of competence, shall implement this law. It shall come into effect on the day following its publication in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
