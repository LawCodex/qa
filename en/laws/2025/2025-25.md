# Law No. (25) of 2025 amending certain provisions of the Civil Human Resources Law issued by Law No. (15) of 2016

**We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, Emir of the State of Qatar,**

After reviewing the Constitution,

And the Civil Service Law issued by Law No. (15) of 2016, as amended by Law No. (23) of 2020,

And the Social Insurance Law issued by Law No. (1) of 2022,

And Amiri Resolution No. (58) of 2021 on the establishment of the Civil Service and Government Development Bureau,

Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

And the approval of the Shura Council,

**We have decided to ratify the following law:**

## Materials (1-8)

### Article 1

The texts of Articles (3) and (4) of Law / Act No. (15) of 2016 promulgating the Civil Human Resources Law / Act shall be replaced with the following texts:

**Article (3):**

"Government entities subject to the provisions of the attached Law / Act and entities whose budgets are funded by the State must provide the Civil Service Bureau and Government Development with all human resources data requested by the Bureau."

**Article (4):**

"Government entities that regulate their employees’ affairs with functional systems or special regulations, as well as other entities whose budgets are funded by the State or to which the State contributes, must submit their applicable human resources regulations, drafts, and any amendments thereto to the Civil Service Bureau and Government Development for review and to express an opinion thereon to the Prime Minister, to ensure that such regulations are consistent with the State’s general policy in the field of human resources."

### Article 2

The definitions of "Ministry", "Minister", and "Department / Directorate" as mentioned respectively in Article (1) of the referred Civil Service Law shall be replaced by the following definitions:

Bureau: Civil Service and Government Development Bureau.

Bureau President: President of the Civil Service and Government Development Bureau.

Department / Directorate: The administrative Unit specialized in training, planning, organization and arrangement, or in implementing HR Policies at the Bureau, as the case may be.

The word "Bureau" and the phrase "Bureau President" shall replace "Ministry" and "Minister" respectively, wherever they appear in the remaining articles of the referred Civil Service Law.

### Article 3

The texts of Articles (9/Item 4), (10), (12), (14), (18), (19), (20), (23), (25), (26), (30), (31), (33), (35), (36), (39), (40), (41), (42), (44), (45), (46/First Paragraph), (47), (48), (57), (58), (60), (61), (62), (63), (65), (66), (69), (73), (78), (79/Item 3), (81), (84), (85), (89), (90), (92), (93), (96), (101), (106), (111), (118) of the referred Civil Human Resources Law are replaced by the following texts:

**Article (9/Item 4):**

"4- By a temporary contract with a fixed remuneration in accordance with the controls and conditions specified by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers."

**Article (10):**

"The Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the government entity and after consulting the State Audit Bureau, may establish employment systems for certain categories or positions of a special nature, under which rights, duties, salaries, and benefits granted to those categories or holders of those positions are defined."

**Article (12):**

"The power of appointment for positions shall be as follows:

1- By a decision of the Emir, for positions where appointment by this instrument is stipulated in special legislation.

2- By decree, for positions of Undersecretaries of Ministries and positions where appointment by decree is stipulated.

A decision from the Emir is issued for the employment system of Undersecretaries of Ministries.

3- By a decision of the Chairman / President of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, for positions of Assistant Undersecretaries of Ministries or positions where appointment by this instrument is stipulated.

4- By a decision of the Chairman / President, for exceptional grade positions and below, or by an employment contract drawn up with the Employee / Staff, taking into account the provisions of this Law and the approved organizational and employment structures of the entity, as well as the Public Job Description, Classification, and Arrangement Guide. The Department / Directorate is obliged to provide the Competent Authority with a copy of the appointment decision or employment Contract or any amendments to the Contract, along with all necessary documents and information within one week from the date of issuance of the Decision / Resolution or signing of the Contract. The Competent Authority may object to the Decision / Resolution or Contract if it violates applicable laws and regulations within sixty days from issuance or signing, and in such case, the Decision / Resolution or Contract shall be considered null and void.

5- By a decision of the Bureau President in accordance with the provision of Article (6 bis/1) of this Law."

**Article (14):**

"Government entities are obliged to provide positions allocated for persons with disabilities in accordance with the provisions of the regulating law, to supply them with all appropriate means to perform their job duties, and to prepare their workplaces with requirements suited to their needs."

**Article (18):**

"A non-Qatari Employee / Staff shall be appointed in accordance with the Public Job Description, Classification, and Arrangement Guide. The government entity may, with prior approval of the State Audit Bureau and as required by public interest, grant him an exceptional allowance, provided that the basic salary and exceptional allowance do not exceed the top limit of grade and salary table No. (2) attached to this Law."

**Article (19):**

"The Employee / Staff is entitled to the starting basic salary for the grade upon appointment.

It is permissible to appoint the Employee / Staff at a higher salary than the starting basic salary for his grade, based on practical experience and in accordance with the conditions and controls specified by the Executive Regulation of this Law.

Practical experience in the private sector shall be recognized in accordance with the conditions and controls issued by a Decision / Resolution from the Bureau President.

The Employee / Staff shall be entitled to the monthly salary starting from the date he actually assumes work and calculated according to hours spent at work."

**Article (20):**

"It is permissible to reappoint an Employee / Staff who was appointed without a qualification and obtained a qualification during service, or an Employee / Staff who obtained a higher qualification during service, to a vacant position in the government entity if the qualification obtained is required for that position, provided other necessary requirements are met, or to upgrade his job level, in accordance with the Public Job Description, Classification, and Arrangement Guide.

The Executive Regulation of this Law shall specify the conditions and controls for reappointment as provided in the previous paragraph."

**Article (23):**

"A Qatari Employee / Staff is entitled to the periodic allowance on the first of January following the completion of one year from entitlement to the previous allowance, until his salary reaches the top limit of the grade. The value of the allowance is calculated as a percentage of the periodic allowance categories specified in grade and salary table No. (1) and according to the level of Performance Appraisal for the eligible year, as shown in the following table:

**Article (25):**

"A monthly bonus shall be granted to the Qatari Employee / Staff whose basic salary reaches the top limit of his grade. This bonus is granted with the same percentages and controls specified in Article (23) of this Law, and continues to be paid until the next due periodic allowance."

**Article (26):**

"The Employee / Staff is entitled to the following allowances, bonuses, and incentives:

1- Social allowance.

2- Housing allowance.

3- Transportation allowance.

4- Representation allowance.

5- Nature of work allowance in accordance with controls issued by a Decision / Resolution from the Chairman / President of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

6- Special allowance.

7- Retention allowance.

8- Exceptional allowance.

9- Recruitment and retention allowance.

10- Telephone allowance.

11- Overtime allowance.

12- Personal car use allowance.

13- Supervision allowance.

14- Furniture allowance.

15- Cashier safe custody allowance.

16- Professional certificate allowance in accordance with controls issued by a Decision / Resolution from the Bureau President.

17- Performance incentive.

18- Supervisory performance incentive.

19- Annual marriage incentive.

It is permissible to amend the aforementioned allowances, bonuses, and incentives or add others by a Decision / Resolution from the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

The government entity may allocate housing to its Employees / Staff in accordance with controls issued by a Decision / Resolution from the Bureau President, and payment of the housing allowance referred to in the first paragraph of this Article shall be suspended from the date the Employee / Staff receives the housing.

Except for Items (5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 16) of this Article, the Executive Regulation of this Law specifies the categories of these allowances, bonuses, and incentives, and the conditions and controls for entitlement."

**Article (30):**

"The government entity, in coordination with the Competent Authority, must develop a Training and Development plan according to the career pathway to achieve its objectives within the framework of the public policy of the State, ensuring Employee / Staff performance is developed by providing them with appropriate Training and Development and qualification opportunities to enhance and develop their abilities, equip them with new skills for improved performance of their job tasks, and qualify them for higher-level positions, all in accordance with the conditions and controls specified by the Bureau President."

**Article (31):**

"The Department / Directorate implements the annual Training and Development plan after budget approval and may amend it according to work requirements, in coordination with the relevant departments, without exceeding the approved training budget.

Except for craft and labor positions, it is mandatory to offer Training and Development courses or alternatives for all Employees / Staff nominated for promotion, to acquire the skills and experience necessary for the position to which promotion will occur.

In the public interest, the Bureau President, upon request of the government entity, may exempt some Employees / Staff from the training courses or training alternatives mentioned in the previous paragraph."

**Article (33):**

"The State Audit Bureau determines the requirements for Government Training Programs to obtain university or higher qualifications according to the national workforce plan in coordination with government entities, and those entities must provide the State Audit Bureau with their actual needs for Government Training Programs in accordance with that plan."

**Article (35):**

"The Competent Authority sets the general guidelines for Performance Appraisal of Employees / Staff, including appraisal forms, and the Employee / Staff is appraised annually.

The Executive Regulation of this Law determines the controls, systems, and methods for Performance Appraisal."

**Article (36):**

Performance Appraisal levels are as follows:

**Article (39):**

"The Employee / Staff's Performance Appraisal shall obligatorily be at the 'Performance meets expectations' level in the following cases:

1- Nomination for a study mission or Training and Development course for more than eight months.

2- Obtaining a study leave, or leave to accompany a patient, leave to accompany the husband, leave for childcare, leave for parental or relative care, leave for statutory waiting period, exceptional leave, or Sick Leave as stipulated in the second paragraph of Article (69) of this Law, for more than eight months.

3- Secondment to work outside the State for more than eight months.

4- Membership of the Shura Council.

5- Membership of the Central Municipal Council."

**Article (40):**

"When an Employee / Staff is reappointed or transferred from one government entity to another, the Performance Appraisal for the year in question shall be made by the entity where the Employee / Staff spent the greater part of that year. If the time spent in the original entity and the new entity is equal, appraisal shall be made by the government entity to which he was transferred or reappointed, as the case may be.

If an Employee / Staff who was reappointed to another entity has no Performance Appraisal for the immediately preceding years because his previous workplace was abolished or for any other reason, the appraisal report from the first year at the new entity shall be considered for previous years with missing appraisals."

**Article (41):**

"It is not allowed to appraise the performance of Employees / Staff in the following categories at the 'Exceptional Performance' or 'Performance exceeds expectations' levels:

1- An Employee / Staff who was offered a training opportunity during the year for which the appraisal report is prepared and did not successfully complete it or was absent without valid excuse acceptable to his employer.

2- An Employee / Staff who received a Disciplinary Sanction of salary deduction or suspension of work for more than ten days, or who received sanctions totaling salary deduction or suspension of work for more than fifteen days during the year for which the appraisal report is prepared, or any other more severe sanction.

3- Employees / Staff occupying special and exceptional grades who received any sanction except for warning during the year for which the appraisal report is prepared."

**Article (42):**

"The Department / Directorate, after approving Performance Appraisal reports, shall notify every Employee / Staff of their appraisal report. The Employee / Staff may raise a grievance to the Chairman / President within fifteen days from notification. The Chairman / President shall decide on the grievance within thirty days from its submission. If the period expires without notification of the Employee / Staff concerning amendment of the report, this is considered a Decision / Resolution of rejection. The Chairman / President's Decision / Resolution on the grievance is final.

The report is only final after the grievance period expires or a Decision / Resolution is made.

Notification is by handing the Employee / Staff the notice in person at the workplace or by modern electronic means or on the national address or by registered letter to the address in his service file."

**Article (44):**

"The Chairman / President may grant an incentive bonus to an Employee / Staff who provides outstanding services, work, research, or proposals that help improve work methods, enhance efficiency, or reduce expenses, provided it does not exceed the Employee / Staff's total salary and is not granted more than once during the fiscal year.

The Decision / Resolution granting the bonus must state the reasons for granting it.

In all cases, this bonus may only be granted if financial allocation is available."

**Article (45):**

"The government entity may grant in-kind bonuses to outstanding Employees / Staff or outstanding departments provided financial allocation is available, and the value of the bonus does not exceed (5,000) five thousand Riyals for the Employee / Staff or the Department / Directorate, in accordance with controls specified by the Bureau President."

**Article (46/First Paragraph):**

"Promotion to grades from twelfth to exceptional shall be according to the interval period and Performance Evaluation."

**Article (47):**

"Subject to Article (31) of this Law, promotion to positions up to exceptional grade requires that the level of Performance Appraisal for the Employee / Staff for the last two years be at least 'Performance meets expectations', the interval period as defined by the Public Job Description, Classification, and Arrangement Guide be fulfilled, and successful completion of training courses or training alternatives required for promotion as specified by Decision / Resolution from the Bureau President."

**Article (48):**

"By Decision / Resolution from the Chairman / President, it is permissible to promote an Employee / Staff who wins one of the individual government excellence awards to the next grade up, not exceeding the exceptional grade, without adhering to the interval or qualification conditions. This promotion does not affect the date of entitlement to interval promotion whenever its conditions are met.

An Employee / Staff at the exceptional financial grade or appointed under a special employment Contract who wins one of the awards specified in the previous paragraph is granted a financial bonus equal to his total monthly salary."

**Article (57):**

"The entity to which the Employee / Staff is seconded shall bear the salary of the position to which he is seconded and all its allowances and other benefits, or the total salary, allowances, and benefits he was receiving from the entity from which he was seconded, whichever is higher. The Chairman / President of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers may approve granting the seconded Employee / Staff his total salary, allowances, and benefits from the entity from which he was seconded if his total salary is higher than the salary scale in grade and salary tables (1) and (2) attached to this Law.

In all cases, the Employee / Staff is entitled to the functional allowances and other benefits prescribed for the seconded position provided there is no dual payment for any allowance or benefit based on the same reason."

**Article (58):**

"By Decision / Resolution from the Chairman / President of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the Chairman / President, it is permissible to second an Employee / Staff, with his approval, to a government, authority, or Arab, foreign, or international organization.

The Decision / Resolution concerning secondment must provide for continued payment of the total salary and allowances for the Employee / Staff and the benefits of the position he occupied from the entity from which he was seconded, as required by public interest. If the total he receives from the entity from which he was seconded and the entity to which he is seconded is less than that received by diplomatic and consular staff of equivalent grade, the difference shall be paid to him by the entity from which he was seconded."

**Article (60):**

"By Decision / Resolution from the Emir, workdays per week, holiday and official occasions are determined. The Cabinet / Council of Ministers, upon a proposal from the Bureau President, determines weekly work timings and their regulation mechanism, and the system for remote work and flexible hours as required by public interest. The Employee / Staff may not be absent except as permitted under leave granted according to the provisions of this Law. If a single workday falls between two holidays, it is considered a holiday.

The Cabinet / Council of Ministers may, upon proposal from the government entity, exempt positions whose circumstances require non-compliance with the timings mentioned in the previous paragraph, and the Chairman / President shall specify controls and timings for those positions."

**Article (61):**

"Leaves are as follows:

1- Periodic leave.

2- Emergency leave.

3- Sick Leave.

4- Maternity leave.

5- Childcare leave.

6- Pilgrimage leave.

7- Marriage leave.

8- Statutory waiting period leave.

9- Bereavement leave.

10- Accompanying husband leave.

11- Mahram leave.

12- Accompanying patient leave.

13- Exceptional leave.

14- Leave to carry out training tasks at government training centers and institutes.

15- Study leave.

16- Examination leave.

17- Unpaid leave.

The Cabinet / Council of Ministers may specify other types of leave.

The leaves prescribed in this Article shall be counted as part of the Employee / Staff's actual service period. If the total unpaid leave periods exceed one year, the excess period is not counted within the actual service except for unpaid leave for accompanying the husband as determined by the Executive Regulation of this Law."

**Article (62):**

"The Employee / Staff is entitled annually to periodic leave with full pay as follows:

1- (45) days for positions of grade seven and above or their equivalents.

2- (40) days for positions of grades ten to eight or their equivalents.

3- (30) days for other grades or their equivalents.

The Employee / Staff is entitled to periodic leave for any part of the year after successfully completing the probation period until the end of the financial year, calculated as a proportion of the period worked during the year.

If the Employee / Staff's leave includes official holidays or Sick Leave, these days shall be added to his leave.

Exceptionally, the Cabinet / Council of Ministers shall issue a Decision / Resolution regulating periodic leave for government entities whose nature of work requires a special regime."

**Article (63):**

"The government entity shall encourage Employees / Staff to take their periodic leave annually. If the Employee / Staff cannot take all his periodic leave, he must take at least half of it, whether continuous or not, and carry over the remaining balance to be taken in the following year only. The Employee / Staff may not be granted a cash allowance for the remaining, unused periodic leave while in service.

Exceptionally, the Chief Executive Officer (CEO), considering work requirements and availability of financial allocation, may refuse the Employee / Staff taking all his periodic leave or recall him from leave before its end, and in such case, the Employee / Staff shall be compensated with a cash payment for the period of leave denied, calculated based on his total salary at time of entitlement, and the remaining balance may be carried over to the following year only. The Executive Regulation specifies the period of leave for which payment is made.

In all cases, no more than 10% of government entity Employees / Staff may be denied taking their periodic leave, and the percentage may be increased in exceptional circumstances by approval of the Chairman / President of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

Exceptionally, Employees / Staff in craft and labor positions may carry over their entire periodic leave to the following year only."

**Article (65):**

"The Employee / Staff is entitled to emergency leave for a sudden reason for not more than ten workdays in a year.

The Employee / Staff's right to this leave lapses at the end of the financial year for which it was due."

**Article (66):**

"An Employee / Staff who is absent due to illness must refer to the nearest medical entity for examination and the necessary Sick Leave report, and must notify his employer of the report.

Sick Leave for the Employee / Staff is granted by the medical entity for not more than three consecutive workdays at one time and a maximum of fifteen workdays in a year. If the medical condition requires more leave, the Sick Leave report is referred to the Competent Authority for approval and determination of the period of Sick Leave granted."

**Article (69):**

"Sick Leave is granted with full pay for not more than thirty days in a year, and at 75% of total salary for any period beyond that, and at 50% of total salary for any period beyond four months in a year.

The previously mentioned provision does not apply during hospital treatment in the State or treatment abroad at State expense or for Employees / Staff suffering from serious diseases requiring medical follow-up, as listed by the Competent Authority, by a Decision / Resolution issued by it.

Sick Leave for an Employee / Staff in the cases mentioned above shall be with full pay for not more than one year. If recovery does not occur in that period, the Competent Authority may grant another Sick Leave of not more than one year with full pay. If the Competent Authority determines no recovery or inability to work, the Employee / Staff is retired for medical unfitness if Qatari or service terminated if non-Qatari."

**Article (73):**

"A female Employee / Staff is granted maternity leave with full pay for three months not counted from other leaves, and the leave is six months in cases of twins or a child with disabilities.

The female Employee / Staff may determine the start of leave from the eighth month of pregnancy.

Based on request, a Qatari female Employee / Staff may extend maternity leave up to three more months, in which period the leave is with basic salary, social allowance, and housing allowance.

A Qatari female Employee / Staff may, on her request, work remotely from the seventh month of pregnancy until delivery, and remote work after maternity leave as per the controls and durations specified by Decision / Resolution from the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

The female Employee / Staff must submit evidence of delivery by a medical report or an official copy of the child's birth certificate.

In all cases, the female Employee / Staff may, upon request, use her periodic leave balance in addition to maternity leave."

**Article (78):**

"The Executive Regulation of this Law specifies other conditions and controls for granting leaves as provided in the preceding Articles of this section and defines the periods and conditions for granting the following leaves:

1- Bereavement leave.

2- Accompanying husband leave.

3- Mahram leave.

4- Accompanying patient leave.

5- Exceptional leave.

6- Examination leave.

7- Unpaid leave."

**Article (79/Item 3):**

"3- Commitment to official working hours and allocating work time to fulfill job duties.

The government entity may deduct from the Employee / Staff's total salary for lateness, early departure, or leaving during working hours without permission, based on controls approved by the Chairman / President. Without prejudice to disciplinary accountability under this Law.

The administrative unit head may permit an Employee / Staff to arrive late or depart early or leave during working hours for up to three hours at a time and up to ten hours a month. If permission exceeds this limit, it must be given in writing by the Chairman / President or Chief Executive Officer (CEO)."

**Article (81):**

"The Employee / Staff must avoid any action that would result in a conflict of interest between his private activities and those of the government entity and its projects or affect, directly or indirectly, his interest or the interest of a relative up to the second degree."

**Article (84):**

"No Disciplinary Sanction may be imposed on an Employee / Staff except after written investigation, hearing his statements, and ensuring his defense, in accordance with what is specified by the Executive Regulation of this Law. The Decision / Resolution imposing the sanction must state its reasons.

For warning, notification, or deduction of up to three days from the total salary on Employees / Staff in exceptional grades and below or equivalent, the investigation may be oral, provided its content is documented in the Decision / Resolution imposing the sanction. The decision in this case is issued by the Director of the Department / Directorate to which the Employee / Staff belongs.

The Employee / Staff may raise a grievance against this Decision / Resolution to the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) within thirty days from notification, and the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) will decide on the grievance within sixty days of submission. If this period passes without a Decision / Resolution, rejection is implied, and the Chief Executive Officer (CEO)'s Decision / Resolution is final."

**Article (85):**

"The Chief Executive Officer (CEO), after reviewing the investigation, may order the investigation to be closed or refer the investigated Employee / Staff to the Competent Authority for disciplinary accountability, or suffice with imposing a sanction as follows:

1- For Employees / Staff in grades one and below, or equivalent salary:

a- Notification.

b- Deduction from the total salary for not more than forty-five days in a year and not more than fifteen days at one time.

2- For Employees / Staff from Assistant Undersecretary grade to special grade or equivalent or above:

a- Warning.

b- Deduction from the total salary for not more than twenty days in a year and not more than three days at one time.

The Employee / Staff may raise a grievance regarding the Decision / Resolution imposing the Disciplinary Sanction to the Chairman / President within thirty days, who shall decide within sixty days; failure to decide is considered rejection, and the Chairman / President's Decision / Resolution is final."

**Article (89):**

"The disciplinary sanctions that the Competent Authority may impose on the Employee / Staff are:

First: For Employees / Staff in grade one and below:

1- Notification.

2- Deduction from total salary for not more than forty-five days a year and not more than fifteen days at one time.

3- Suspension from work with half salary deduction for not more than three months.

4- Deprivation of the periodic allowance for not more than six months.

5- Postponement of promotion for not more than one year.

6- Lowering of grade and salary to the starting level of the next lower grade.

7- Dismissal from employment with retention of rights to bonus and pension.

Second: For Employees / Staff from Assistant Undersecretary to special grade or equivalent or above:

1- Warning.

2- Deduction from total salary for not more than forty-five days a year and not more than fifteen days at one time.

3- Suspension from work with half salary deduction for not more than three months.

4- Deprivation of the periodic allowance for not more than six months.

5- Lowering of grade and salary to the starting level of the next lower grade.

6- Dismissal from employment with retention of rights to bonus and pension.

In all cases, no more than one sanction may be imposed for the same violation, or grade and salary lowering sanction imposed on newly appointed Employees / Staff occupying jobs at the lowest financial grades prescribed for their academic qualification."

**Article (90):**

"By Decision / Resolution from the Chairman / President, a committee called 'Disciplinary Committee' shall be formed in every government entity, consisting of a Chairman / President and two members whose grade is not less than grade one or who hold the position of Department / Directorate Director. One member represents the Department / Directorate, and the other represents the Competent Authority for legal affairs. The Committee is responsible for:

1- Disciplining Employees / Staff in grades one and below or equivalent salary, and imposing appropriate sanctions.

2- Considering suspension of Employees / Staff referred for disciplinary accountability and extending their suspension, whether issued by the Committee or the Chief Executive Officer (CEO).

If the Chairman / President or member of the Disciplinary Committee is the manager of the unit where the referred Employee / Staff works or is his direct supervisor, the Chairman / President shall assign another Employee / Staff of the government entity to serve as a temporary substitute in this case.

The Committee cannot convene except in the presence of the Chairman / President and both members. Decisions are issued by majority vote."

**Article (92):**

"A permanent disciplinary council shall be formed, chaired by the Bureau President, with the Director General of the State Audit Bureau as Deputy Chairman, and membership including:

1- A judge from the Court of Appeal selected by the President of the Supreme Judiciary Council.

2- A representative of the State Audit Bureau, grade not less than Director of Department / Directorate, selected by the Bureau President.

3- A representative of the Ministry of Justice, grade not less than Director of Department / Directorate, selected by the Minister of Justice.

The council may only convene with the Chairman / President or Deputy Chairman and members present.

The council issues its decisions by majority vote, and if votes are equal, the Chairman / President's side prevails.

The council's headquarters shall be at the State Audit Bureau, with a secretariat from its Employees / Staff assigned.

A Decision / Resolution from the Chairman / President of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, every three years, shall name the representatives and determine bonuses for the Chairman / President, Deputy Chairman, members, and secretariat."

**Article (93):**

"The permanent disciplinary council is responsible for:

1- Disciplining Employees / Staff from Assistant Undersecretary to special grade or equivalent or above, and imposing appropriate sanctions.

2- Considering suspension of Employees / Staff referred for disciplinary accountability and extending their suspension, whether issued by the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) or the council."

**Article (96):**

"The Competent Authority, sua sponte or at the request of the referred Employee / Staff, may conduct the investigation itself or entrust it to one of its members.

Whoever conducts the investigation may interrogate the Employee / Staff, review all documents deemed relevant, even if confidential, and hear witnesses, both Employees / Staff and others.

If the Employee / Staff witness fails to attend to give Testimony before the Competent Authority after postponement of proceedings and being notified again, or attends and refuses to give the Testimony without valid excuse or gives false Testimony, the Competent Authority, after an investigation and without prejudice to criminal liability, may impose one of the following sanctions:

First: For Employees / Staff from Assistant Undersecretary grade to special grade or equivalent or above:

1- Warning.

2- Deduction from total salary for not more than fifteen days.

Second: For Employees / Staff in grade one and below:

1- Notification.

2- Deduction from total salary for not more than fifteen days.

The violating Employee / Staff has the right to attend all investigation and accountability proceedings, unless the investigation or accountability requires doing it in his absence.

Nevertheless, he has the right to review all investigations and related documents and obtain copies thereof."

**Article (101):**

"An Employee / Staff may not be considered for promotion if one of the following Disciplinary Sanctions is imposed unless the following periods have elapsed:

1- Three months in case of deduction from total salary for more than eight days up to fifteen days.

2- Six months in case of deduction from total salary for more than fifteen days.

3- Duration of deprivation of the periodic allowance.

4- Two years in case of lowering grade and salary to the starting level of the next lower grade.

Seniority is recalculated, taking into account previous service in the lowered grade.

The postponement period specified in this Article is calculated from the date the sanction is imposed, even if it overlaps with another period resulting from a previous sanction.

An Employee / Staff may not be considered for promotion while a criminal Judgment / Ruling is being executed.

The period of executing a criminal Judgment / Ruling is not counted within the Employee / Staff's service period."

**Article (106):**

"Disciplinary Sanctions imposed on an Employee / Staff are automatically erased after the following periods:

1- Three months for warning, notification, or deduction from total salary for not more than eight days.

2- Six months for deduction from total salary for more than eight days.

3- One year for deprivation of the periodic allowance.

4- Two years for other sanctions except dismissal from the position.

Once erased, the sanction is considered as if it had not occurred for the future, without affecting rights and compensations resulting from it, and all sanction papers and references are removed from the Employee / Staff's service file."

**Article (111):**

"The Employee / Staff is considered to have submitted his resignation in the following cases:

1- If absent without permission for more than fifteen consecutive days, even if absence is after authorized leave, unless within the following fifteen days he proves his absence was for acceptable reason. In this case, the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) may refrain from depriving him of pay for the period of absence if he has a sufficient leave balance; otherwise, he must be deprived of pay for this period. If the Employee / Staff fails to provide reasons or the reasons are rejected, his service ends on the date of absence.

2- If absent without permission acceptable to employer for more than thirty non-consecutive days in a year; service ends the day after completion of this period.

3- If a seconded Employee / Staff does not return within fifteen days after the period defined by the Executive Regulation of this Law, unless within fifteen days he proves his non-return was for acceptable reason.

If no reason is presented or is rejected, service ends from the date secondment ends.

In the above three cases, Employee / Staff must be notified in writing of the legal consequence of absence following five days in the first case, ten days in the second and third.

In all cases, for provisions of this Article to apply, Employee / Staff must continue his absence after notification for at least ten days in the first case and twenty days in the second and third.

Notification is to the address in the Employee / Staff's service file or national address or by modern electronic means.

4- If he joins service at any foreign entity without license from the Competent Authority; in this case, service ends on the date of joining the foreign entity."

**Article (118):**

"A non-Qatari Employee / Staff who has served in the government entity for at least one year is entitled to End of Service Gratuity, based on one month's salary for each year of service up to ten months, for his entire service with the State whether continuous or not, and the gratuity is calculated on the last ten years of service, with basic salary in each year as the basis for calculation.

The provisions of the preceding paragraph do not apply to Employees / Staff subject to the unified system for extending insurance protection to GCC nationals working outside their countries in any member State, issued by Law No. (4) of 2007."

### Article 4

The following texts are added to the referenced Civil Human Resources Law:

**Article (6 bis):**

"The competent Department / Directorate shall set the general guidelines for localization and replacement policies in government entities in coordination with the relevant administrative units.

Each government entity shall prepare its annual localization and replacement plan in consideration of these guidelines, provided that the aforementioned annual plan is approved by the Bureau President."

**Article (6 bis/1):**

"Government entities are required to appoint the nominated Qatari candidates for replacement positions within no more than one month from the date of their nomination by the competent Department / Directorate.

If the government entity fails to appoint the nominated Qataris within the period mentioned in the previous paragraph without reasons acceptable to the competent Department / Directorate, the Bureau President, after approval by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, and in consideration of the job requirements, may appoint them in these entities instead of non-Qatari employees, within the scope of the localization and replacement plans."

**Article (11 bis):**

"A government entity may contract with Qatari students enrolled in one of the Higher Education institutions within the State to fill certain positions on a part-time basis during their study period in return for a fixed remuneration. The regulations of such a contract shall be issued by a decision from the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

The provisions of the Social Insurance Law referenced above do not apply to the student contracted according to this system."

**Article (13 bis):**

"It is not permissible to appoint, transfer, second, or loan an Employee / Staff to work in an administrative unit within the organizational structure of a government entity where his/her spouse or one of his/her relatives up to the second degree works."

**Article (26 bis):**

"A government entity may, with prior approval from the Bureau, grant a Qatari Employee / Staff a recruitment and retention allowance, provided that the total of the basic salary and the said allowance does not exceed the maximum salary stated in Payroll and Grades Table No. (1) attached to this Law / Act. This allowance is not granted to more than (5%) of those occupying specialist, supervisory, and assistant deputy minister positions in the entity, and with the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, this percentage may be exceeded up to a maximum of (10%) of the total persons occupying the aforementioned positions.

The Bureau President shall set the additional terms and controls regarding the allowance stipulated in the previous paragraph."

**Article (33 bis):**

"An Employee / Staff may be sent on a study mission for the period and under the conditions set out in the regulating law for study missions.

The Employee / Staff shall receive during the mission the salary of the position he/she holds and other prescribed allowances, except for work-related allowances."

**Article (39 bis):**

"Without prejudice to the provisions of Article (39) of this Law / Act, the Performance Evaluation of an Employee / Staff shall be judged as 'Below Expectations' if he/she has obtained leaves totaling more than eight months during the year subject to the Assessment."

**Article (45 bis):**

"The Chairman / President may grant a functional excellence bonus to an Employee / Staff who has provided services, works, research, or suggestions that contributed to the government entity where he/she works obtaining the government excellence award, provided that the bonus does not exceed the total monthly salary of the Employee / Staff. This bonus may not be granted more than once during the year the entity received the award, and the total bonuses disbursed for this purpose may not exceed (5%) of the total annual budget allocated to Chapter One for the entity.

It is required for the disbursement of the bonus that the Employee / Staff's Performance Evaluation is not less than 'Meets Expectations' for the year preceding the date the entity obtained the government excellence award, and that he/she has not taken leaves exceeding three months during the year preceding the award date, except for regular or emergency leaves.

In all cases, this bonus shall only be granted if financial allocation is available."

**Article (47 bis):**

"The requisite service period for promotion, as specified in the job description, classification, and ordering guide for public jobs, is reduced by one year in the following cases:

1- If the period is three years and the Employee / Staff has received two Performance Evaluations at the 'Exceptional Performance' level during the first and second years from the date of attaining the current grade.

2- If the period is four years and the Employee / Staff has received three Performance Evaluations at 'Exceptional Performance' during the first three years from the date of attaining the current grade.

3- If the period is five years and the Employee / Staff has received four Performance Evaluations at 'Exceptional Performance' during the first four years from the date of attaining the current grade.

The provisions of this Article do not apply to Employees / Staff working in government entities that have a special Performance Evaluation system different from that applicable In accordance with the provisions of this Law."

**Article (57 bis):**

"By way of exception to the provisions of Articles (56) and (57) of this Law / Act, and with the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal by the Chairman / President, an Employee / Staff may, after his/her approval, be seconded to associations, charitable institutions, and non-profit organizations. In this case, the secondment decision may include the continued disbursement of all or part of the Employee / Staff’s total salary and the allocations and benefits of the position he/she held at the seconding entity, according to the requirements of the public interest."

**Article (77 bis):**

"By decision of the Chairman / President, provided this does not conflict with work interests, an Employee / Staff may be granted full-paid leave to carry out tasks and activities for training at government training institutes and centers. The leave is granted upon the request of the institute or center and according to the periods they specify.

In all cases, the leave may not exceed fifteen days per month or two months per year."

**Article (77 bis/1):**

"A Qatari Employee / Staff may be granted a study leave for the period and under the conditions stipulated in the regulating law for study missions.

During such leave, the Employee / Staff shall receive his/her basic salary, the social allowance and the housing allowance."

**Article (107 item 10):**

"10- At the request of the Employee / Staff upon reaching the age of fifty-five, and a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers shall be issued setting the conditions, controls, and employment rights arising from the end of service in this case."

**Article (110 bis):**

"An Employee / Staff may withdraw the resignation request before the end date of service specified therein or within one month of submitting it, whichever is earlier.

The withdrawal request must be in writing and free from any restriction or condition.

An Employee / Staff may not withdraw the resignation request more than twice at the same entity."

**Article (118 bis):**

"It is permissible to establish an investment savings scheme for non-Qatari Employees / Staff, funded by the End of Service Gratuity amount and any other contributions, in accordance with rules and procedures issued by a decision from the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

The Cabinet / Council of Ministers may specify the mechanism for implementing an optional investment savings scheme for Qatari Employees / Staff."

**Article (120 bis):**

"A committee shall be established at the Bureau called the 'Committee for Reviewing Grievances and Complaints', specialized in reviewing the grievances and complaints submitted by the Employees / Staff subject to In accordance with the provisions of this Law and its executive regulations, and the Employees / Staff subject to special employment systems established by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers In accordance with the provisions of this Law, with respect to their employment entitlements. The Committee shall be chaired by the Bureau President and include three representatives of the Bureau selected by the Bureau President, one of whom shall be a member and rapporteur of the Committee, and a representative from each of the following entities:

1- Ministry of Justice.

2- One judge from the Court of First Instance or Court of Appeal selected by the President of the Supreme Judiciary Council.

3- Office of the Prime Minister.

4- General Secretariat of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

5- Ministry of Education and Higher Education.

6- Ministry of Public Health.

7- A person of expertise nominated by the Bureau President.

Each entity nominates its representative, and a decision by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers shall be issued to appoint the Committee members and define the remuneration for the Chairperson and Committee members.

The Committee shall set its work regulations, and its decisions are issued by majority; in the case of parity, the side on which the Chairperson is prevails. The Committee’s decisions are binding.

The secretariat of the Committee is assumed by one or more Employees / Staff of the Bureau, appointed and their remuneration determined by a decision of the Bureau President."

**Article (126 bis):**

"Any case/claim for pecuniary rights resulting from the implementation of In accordance with the provisions of this Law, its executive regulations, and their enforcing decisions, is time-barred after fifteen years."

### Article 5

Qatari employees who are in service on the date this Law / Act comes into effect shall be granted the periodic allowance on the first of January following the date of its implementation, in proportion to the period from the date they received the previous periodic allowance until the date they become entitled to the new allowance. The value of the allowance shall be calculated according to the same categories stipulated in the Grades and Salaries Table No. (1) attached to the Law / Act.

### Article 6

The Performance Evaluation system established In accordance with the provisions of this Law shall be applied starting from the 2026 Performance Evaluation.

### Article 7

Articles numbered (24), (34), (37) of the referenced Civil Human Resources Law are repealed.

### Article 8

All concerned authorities, each within its competence, shall implement this Law / Act. It shall be effective from the date of issuance. It shall be published in the official gazette.
