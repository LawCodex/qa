# Law No. (8) of 2025 regarding Support for Innovation and Scientific Research

**We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani**

**Emir of the State of Qatar,**

After reviewing the Constitution,

And the Decree Law No. (24) of 2008 regarding support and regulation of scientific research,

And the State’s Financial System Law issued by Law No. (2) of 2015,

And the Amiri Decision No. (19) of 2025 on establishing the Qatari Foundation for Supporting Innovation and Scientific Research,

Based on the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

And on the approval of the Shura Council,

**We have decided to ratify the following law:**

## المواد (1-5)

### Article 1

A percentage of the revenues listed in the state's general budget, determined annually, is allocated to support innovation and scientific research. The proceeds from this percentage serve as the main annual resource for the national institution for supporting innovation and scientific research.

### Article 2

The Qatar Foundation for the Support of Innovation and Scientific Research shall manage, develop, and invest the proceeds of the percentage referred to in the previous article, based on economic principles and standards. The areas of expenditure shall be determined for supporting innovation and scientific research activities and enhancing their level, in accordance with the provisions of this law.

### Article 3

The Minister of Finance shall issue the necessary resolutions to implement the provisions of this law.

### Article 4

Decree Law No. (24) of 2008 referred to above shall be repealed, and any provision that contradicts the provisions of this law shall also be repealed.

### Article 5

All relevant authorities, each in their field of competence, shall implement this law. It shall be published in the official gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
