# Law / Act No. (10) of 2025 amending some provisions of Law / Act No. (21) of 2018 regarding the regulation of business events

**We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani**

**Emir of the State of Qatar,**

After reviewing the Constitution,

And Law / Act No. (21) of 2015 on the Regulation of Entry, Exit, and Residency of Expatriates, and its amending laws,

And Law / Act No. (21) of 2018 regarding the Regulation of Business Events,

And Amiri Decision No. (15) of 2021 establishing Qatar Tourism (QT),

And Amiri Decision No. (69) of 2024 reorganizing the Supreme Committee for Delivery & Legacy,

And Council of Ministers Decision No. (12) of 2024 establishing the Standing Committee for Visitor Entry Management to the State of Qatar (Hayya Platform),

Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

And upon the approval of the Shura Council,

**We have decided to ratify the following Law / Act**:

## المواد (1-4)

### Article 1

The phrase "Qatar Tourism (QT)" shall replace the word "Council" and the phrase "National Tourism Council" respectively, wherever they appear in Law / Act No. (21) of 2018 referred to. The phrase "Chairman / President of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers" shall be replaced with the phrase "Cabinet / Council of Ministers", and the phrase "accommodation or lodging places" shall be replaced with "Hospitality Establishments" wherever they appear in Law / Act No. (21) of 2018 referred to. The word "Chairman / President" shall be replaced with the phrase "Board of Directors of the Council" wherever it appears in Law / Act No. (21) of 2018 referred to, and with the phrase "Secretary General" wherever it is mentioned in Articles (16), (17), (18), (19) of the same Law / Act. Also, the phrase "Director General" shall be replaced with the phrase "Secretary General" wherever it appears in Articles (11), (24), (28) of Law / Act No. (21) of 2018 referred to.

### Article 2

The texts of Articles (1), (3), (4), (6), (8), (11/paragraphs 1 and 6), (12), (20), (21), and (26) of Law / Act No. (21) of 2018 referred to above shall be replaced with the following texts:

**Article (1):**

«For the application of the provisions of this Law, the following words and expressions shall have the meanings indicated against each of them, unless the context requires otherwise:

**Article (3):**

«The application for the License shall be submitted to the competent Department / Directorate, on the form prepared for this purpose, including the required data, and accompanied by supporting documents, including the approval of the competent Department / Directorate on the initial design of all asset development projects related to business events.

The Executive Regulations of this Law shall specify the requirements, conditions, Bank Guarantee and Insurance Document necessary to grant the License.».

**Article (4):**

«The competent Department / Directorate shall decide on the License application, and notify the applicant of its decision at their national address or by any method that provides notice, within one week from the date of submission. If the application is rejected, the rejection must be justified, and if this period expires without a response, the application shall be considered implicitly rejected.».

**Article (6):**

«The duration of the License shall be a maximum of five years, and it may be renewed for an equal or other periods, after payment of the prescribed fee.

The Executive Regulations of this Law shall specify the rules for implementing this article.».

**Article (8):**

«No License to hold a business event in the State shall be granted unless the operator of the event is one of the licensed business event organization offices.

Qatar Tourism (QT) shall, after coordination with the relevant entities, manage business events through the Qatar Events Calendar, and may reject or postpone applications to obtain Licenses to hold business events for the purpose of coordinating dates, avoiding overlaps, and ensuring quality.

Other entities in the State involved in planning and licensing any aspect of business events must coordinate with Qatar Tourism (QT) prior to issuing such Licenses.».

**Article (11/paragraphs 1 and 6):**

«1- Commitment to the service excellence standards and requirements specified by Qatar Tourism (QT).

6- Provide Qatar Tourism (QT) with the pricing plans and promotional offers related to pricing, at least ten days before announcing them, to obtain the approval of the competent Department / Directorate. These programs shall be confidential, and it is prohibited for the relevant employees of Qatar Tourism (QT) to disclose any information related to them to others.

No approval from Qatar Tourism (QT) is required for marketing or tourism promotion programs not related to pricing.».

**Article (12):**

«The Licensee shall provide suitable opportunities for the training, development, and qualification of workforce at various levels related to business events organization and its relevant activities, and ensure compliance with the guidelines set by Qatar Tourism (QT) regarding training.».

**Article (20):**

«Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, imprisonment for a period not exceeding two years, and a fine not exceeding (500,000) five hundred thousand riyals, or either of these penalties, shall be imposed on anyone who:

1- Violates any provision of Article (2) of this Law, and in addition to that, the establishment shall be closed.

2- Violates any provision of Article (23) of this Law.

The Judgment / Ruling shall be published in two daily newspapers at the expense of the condemned, and on the website of Qatar Tourism (QT).

The Chairman / President may, in case of violation of any provision of Article (2) of this Law, close the establishment or business event or suspend the activity, administratively and at the expense of the offender, temporarily, until the violation is rectified.

If the establishment or business event is part of an integrated establishment or a larger event, the closure in this case shall be limited to the part where the violation occurred.

In case of closure, the offender must provide a licensed alternative, of the same category and level as committed to provide to customers with whom contracts were concluded prior to closure. In case of non-compliance with this, Qatar Tourism (QT) may provide this alternative at the expense of the offender, recover the expenses by deduction from the Bank Guarantee if available with Qatar Tourism (QT), otherwise, the expenses shall be recovered administratively.

The closure decision shall be published on the Qatar Tourism (QT) website.».

**Article (21):**

«Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, a fine not exceeding (150,000) one hundred and fifty thousand riyals, shall be imposed on anyone who violates any provision of Articles (9), (10), (11) of this Law.

The Chairman / President may, in addition, in case of violation of the provisions of Articles (9), (10), (11/paragraphs 1, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9) of this Law, close the establishment or suspend the event administratively and at the expense of the offender, temporarily, until the violation is rectified.

If the establishment or business event is part of an integrated establishment or a larger event, the closure in this case shall be for the part where the violation occurred.

The closure decision shall be published on the Qatar Tourism (QT) website.».

**Article (26):**

«Any interested party may submit a Grievance against the decisions issued in application of the provisions of this Law to the Chairman / President within thirty days from the date of being notified of the decision, at their national address, or by any method providing notice, or from the date the period for deciding the License application expires without a response.

The Chairman / President shall decide on the Grievance within fifteen working days from the date of its submission. If this period lapses without a response to the Grievance, it shall be considered implicitly rejected, and the decision on the Grievance shall be final».

### Article 3

The following texts are added to Law No. (21) of 2018 referred to: **Article (10/Items 4 and 5):** "4 - Adhering to the business event program in accordance with the granted license." "5 - Registering the event in the Qatar Events Schedule as determined by Qatar Tourism." **Article (11/Item 10):** "10- Observing the instructions issued by Qatar Tourism, based on joint coordination with the competent authorities in the State, regarding the regulations for issuing tourist visas and visit visas for the purposes of business events, and complying with the system prepared for this purpose." **Article (21/Repeated):** "The penalties stipulated in this Law shall be doubled in case of recurrence, and the defendant shall be considered a repeat offender if he commits a similar crime within five years from the date of completion of the execution of the imposed penalty or its expiry by limitation. All crimes stipulated in this Law shall be considered similar for cases of repeat offense." **Article (22/Repeated):** "The Chairman may impose one or more of the financial penalties stipulated in the Table attached to this Law on the licensee, in the event of violating the provisions of this Law, its executive bylaw, decisions, licenses, or the instructions issued by Qatar Tourism in implementation of the provisions of this Law, in cases other than the crimes and penalties stipulated in Chapter Four of this Law. The decisions of the Chairman must be justified, and the competent Department / Directorate must notify the licensee at his national address or by any means that ensures his awareness of the violation attributed to him, and the necessity of submitting his remarks regarding it within fifteen days from the date of notification. If the licensee does not submit his remarks within this period, or submits them and the competent Department / Directorate deems them unacceptable, the Chairman may impose a financial penalty on him not exceeding the maximum stipulated in the Table attached to this Law. The seriousness of the violation and its resulting effects must be taken into account when imposing the penalty, and the Chairman's decisions shall be final. The competent Department / Directorate must inform the violator of the decision imposing the penalty and publish it by whatever means it deems appropriate. The violator must pay the amount of the financial penalty within ninety days from the date of notification of the violation. In the case of persistence in the violation, recurrence, or failure to remedy it, or failure to pay the financial penalty amount, the Chairman may cancel the license or take any of the following measures until the reasons for the violation are removed: 1- Suspend the event or activity. 2- Suspend or not renew the license. 3- Temporarily close the establishment."

### Article 4

All relevant authorities, each within its area of competence, must implement this Law / Act. It shall be published in the official gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
