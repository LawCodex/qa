# Law No. 4 of 1998 Amending Certain Provisions of Law No. 10 of 1996 on the Promulgation of the Law of the Legal Profession

We, Hamad bin Khalifa Al-Thani, Emir of the State of Qatar,

Having reviewed the Amended Provisional Constitution, in particular Articles 23, 34 and 51 thereof,

Law No. 10 of 1996 promulgating the Law on the Legal Profession,

The proposal of the Minister of Justice,

The bill submitted by the Council of Ministers, and

After consulting the Shura Council,

Hereby promulgate the following law:

## Articles (1-2)

### Article 1

The text of Article (3) of Law / Act No. (10) of 1996 referred to shall be replaced by the following text:

Article (3):

“The Section of Record in the temporary Schedule / Table shall be closed as of the date this Law / Act comes into force, and no new lawyers shall be recorded therein, and the Record of the lawyers registered in that Schedule / Table shall be canceled after two years have elapsed from the date this Law / Act comes into force. This period may be extended by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers for one time only, in light of the justifications presented by the Minister of Justice, after which this Schedule / Table shall be permanently canceled.

Lawyers registered in this Schedule / Table may practice all legal work until the date of cancellation of their Record.

A lawyer registered in the temporary Schedule / Table shall, at an appropriate time before the cancellation of that Schedule / Table, appoint on his behalf one or more practicing lawyers to conduct any cases he was conducting until the date of cancellation of the Record, provided that this is with the approval of the Principal / Grantor.

In the event of failure to agree on the fees due to the new lawyer, they shall be decided in accordance with the provisions of Articles (36) et seq. of the accompanying Law / Act.”

### Article 2

All competent authorities, each within its jurisdiction, shall implement this Law / Act. It shall come into force as of the date of entry into force of Law / Act No. (10) of 1996 referred to. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Hamad Bin Khalifa Al-Thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
