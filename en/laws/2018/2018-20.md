# Law No. (20) of 2018 concerning the Regulation of Tourism

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And Law No. (6) of 2012 concerning the regulation of tourism,

And Amiri Resolution No. (74) of 2018 on the establishment of the National Tourism Council,

And the Draft Law submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

And after taking the opinion of the Shura Council,

We have made the following Law:

## الباب الثاني: تراخيص المنشآت الفندقية والسياحية والأنشطة السياحية والمهرجانات والفعاليات السياحية ومكاتب تنظيمها (2-29)

### الفصل الأول: شروط وإجراءات الترخيص (2-10)

#### Article 2 Amended

It is prohibited to establish, set up, operate, or manage a hotel or tourism establishment, or an Office for organizing festivals and tourism events, or practice any tourism activity, or hold a festival or tourism event without obtaining a License to do so from Qatar Tourism (QT), In accordance with the provisions of this Law and its executive regulations.

#### Article 3 Amended

The application for obtaining the license shall be submitted to the competent Department / Directorate, on the form prepared for this purpose, including the data, and accompanied by the supporting documents, including the approval of the competent Department / Directorate for the preliminary design of all asset development projects related to tourism.

The Executive Regulations of this Law / Act shall specify the conditions, controls, and requirements for the Bank Guarantee and the insurance document necessary for granting the license.

#### Article 4 Amended

The competent Department / Directorate shall decide on the license application and notify the applicant of its decision at his national address or by any means that ensure notification, within a week from the date of submission. In case of rejection, the refusal must be justified, and if this period expires without a response to the application, it shall be considered an implicit rejection.

#### Article 5

The competent Department / Directorate, in case of approval of the License application for the hotel or tourist establishment or the Office for organizing Events and Festivals, issues a preliminary approval for the period specified by the Executive Regulation of this Law / Act, in order to enable the applicant for the License to complete the construction and necessary preparations to commence the activity.

The applicant for the License, once the construction and necessary preparations to commence the activity are completed, must notify the competent Department / Directorate in writing, and the aforementioned Department / Directorate must verify the completion of the aforementioned construction and preparations within fifteen days from the date of notification, and the License is issued if it is proven that they are completed.

The competent Department / Directorate, in case the aforementioned construction and preparations are not completed, may grant the applicant for the License one or more additional periods, each not exceeding the period mentioned in the first paragraph of this article, and the application shall be rejected if the required works are not completed.

#### Article 6 Amended

The license period shall be five years at most, and the license may be renewed for one or more similar periods after payment of the prescribed fee.

The Executive Regulations of this Law / Act shall specify the rules for implementing this article.

#### Article 7

The Executive Regulations of this Law / Act specify the requirements that must be met in tourism festival and event programs and the locations where they are permitted to be held, as well as the types of tourism festivals and events allowed in the State, their number, and duration.

#### Article 8 Amended

By a decision of the Chairman / President, based on a proposal from the Director General, the seasons for holding festivals and tourism events in the State may be coordinated within a unified national plan.

Qatar Tourism (QT) shall coordinate with the relevant authorities in supervising public events through the Qatar Events Calendar, and shall have, after coordination with those authorities, the right to refuse or postpone public event License applications in order to coordinate dates, avoid overlaps, and ensure quality.

The other relevant authorities in the State involved in planning and Licensing any aspects of public events must coordinate with Qatar Tourism (QT) before issuing these Licenses.

#### Article 9

A License is not granted to hold a festival or tourism event in the State unless the organizer of the festival or tourism event is one of the licensed offices for organizing festivals and tourism events.

#### Article 10 Amended

It is permissible, by a Decision / Resolution of the Chairman / President, based on a proposal from the Director General, to suspend the issuance of licenses for accommodation or residential and tourist places and tourist activities for reasons of public interest, provided that the suspension period does not exceed two years from the date of issuing the Decision / Resolution.

The Chairman / President of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the Chairman / President, may extend the suspension for one or more similar periods.

### الفصل الثاني: التزامات المرخص له (11-18)

#### Article 11 Amended

The licensee of the office for organizing festivals and tourism events must comply with the following:

1- Providing all necessary services for preparing and organizing the festival or tourism event, as the case may be, and in particular the following:

a- Submitting the license application for holding the festival or tourism event on the designated form for this purpose, and following it up on behalf of the license applicant.

b- Holding the festival or tourism event and its related arrangements.

c- Supervising the operation and management of the licensed festival or tourism event In accordance with the provisions of this Law.

d- Providing public services and facilities to the participants and visitors of the festival or tourism event.

e- Organizing other activities related to the festival or tourism event after obtaining the approval of the Competent Authority and the necessary license for the related event from the Competent Authority.

2- Adhering to the conditions and instructions of the Competent Authority regarding organizing licensed festivals and tourism events.

3- Adhering to the festival or event program in accordance with the granted license.

4- Obtaining the Licenses and other necessary approvals for organizing the festival or tourism event from the Competent Authority in the State, and notifying Qatar Tourism (QT) of those Licenses and approvals before they are held.

#### Article 12

The licensee to organize a tourism festival or event in the State must adhere to the following:

1- Comply with the program of the festival or tourism event according to the granted license.

2- Comply with the duration of the festival or tourism event according to the granted license.

3- Comply with organizing the festival or tourism event in the specified location, according to the granted license.

#### Article 13 Amended

The licensee, as appropriate, must adhere to the following:

1- The controls and conditions stipulated in this Law / Act, its executive regulations, and the decisions issued for its implementation.

2- Subjecting the establishment or the licensed activity to a unified management.

3- Not making any modification to facilities and constructions except with the approval of the competent Department / Directorate.

4- Not making any modification to the trade name of the establishment or its management except after notifying Qatar Tourism (QT) in writing of this modification.

5- Not making any modification to the ownership of the establishment except after the approval of Qatar Tourism (QT).

6- Providing the client with an invoice listing the services provided and the value of each in Arabic and in other languages specified by the competent Department / Directorate.

7- Taking the necessary precautions to preserve the environment.

8- Obtaining the necessary Licenses and approvals from the competent authorities before holding any events in accommodation, residence, or tourist places, and notifying Qatar Tourism (QT) of those Licenses and approvals before holding them.

9- Complying with the instructions issued by Qatar Tourism (QT) based on joint coordination between Qatar Tourism (QT) and the competent authorities in the State regarding the controls of operating tourism activities or accommodation, residence, and tourist places, or organizing tourism festivals and events.

10- Complying with the instructions issued by Qatar Tourism (QT), based on joint coordination with the competent authorities in the State, regarding the controls for issuing tourist visas and visit visas for tourism purposes, and adhering to the system prepared for this purpose.

#### Article 14

The licensee must adhere to the marketing principles that define the requirements and guidelines related to marketing and promotion for the State of Qatar. These principles are issued by a decision from the Chairman / President.

#### Article 14 Amended

The licensee must provide Qatar Tourism (QT) with the pricing plans and promotional offers related to pricing, at least ten days prior to their announcement, in order to obtain the approval of the competent Department / Directorate. These programs shall have a Capacity of confidentiality, and it is prohibited for Qatar Tourism (QT) employees concerned to disclose any information about them to others.

It is not required to obtain the approval of Qatar Tourism (QT) for marketing or tourism promotion programs that are not related to pricing.

The licensee, as a tourism facility or a tourism and accommodation venue, must provide Qatar Tourism (QT) with copies of agreements and contracts concluded with any other hotel or tourism facility, whether operating within the State or outside it.

#### Article 15

The licensee must, as appropriate, place the license and the distinctive mark of the establishment’s grade, if any, in a visible location and in the manner determined by the Department / Directorate, and announce the prices of services and products offered in a visible location with clear lists in Arabic and in other languages as determined by the Department / Directorate.

#### Article 16 Amended

The licensee must, as applicable, retain the books, records, and documents related to the establishment's operation or activity, as well as statistics related thereto, in accordance with the regulations and periods specified by a decision issued by the Director General, based on a proposal from the Department / Directorate concerned.

He must also provide any information required by Qatar Tourism (QT) to assess the tourism experience, and any other data specified and the period for their submission determined by a decision from the Chairman / President, based on a proposal from the Department / Directorate concerned.

He is responsible for the accuracy of these data, and is obliged to provide them upon request from the Department / Directorate concerned and according to the mechanism specified for this purpose, and the licensee shall bear the requirements for preparation, electronic linkage, and any other requirements necessary to implement this obligation.

#### Article 17 Amended

The licensee must provide appropriate opportunities for training, development, and qualification of the workforce in the tourism and hospitality sector at various tourism and administrative levels, and ensure compliance with the guidelines set by Qatar Tourism (QT) regarding training, in coordination with the relevant authorities in the State.

#### Article 18

The licensee is prohibited from allowing, in the hotel or tourist establishment, festival, tourist event, or Office for the organization of festivals and tourist events, the commission of any acts that conflict with the Law / Act, public order, public morals, or that affect the safety of society or the security of the State.

### الفصل الثالث: التنازل عن الترخيص ونقله وإلغاؤه (19-22)

#### Article 19

The licensee may not assign the license granted to him except after obtaining written approval from the competent Department / Directorate, provided that the assignee meets the licensing conditions stipulated in this Law / Act, its executive regulations, and the decisions issued in implementation thereof.

#### Article 20 Amended

The licensee of a hotel or tourist establishment, tourist activity, or Office for organizing festivals and tourist events shall, upon ceasing to practice the licensed activity, or terminating it for any reason, announce this on its website, if any, and in one of the widely circulated local daily newspapers at its own expense, in accordance with the form specified by the competent Department / Directorate. If the licensee does not make such announcement, Qatar Tourism (QT) may carry out the announcement at the licensee's expense and recover the costs by deducting from the Bank Guarantee if available to Qatar Tourism (QT), otherwise they shall be collected administratively, in the event the licensee fails to pay within one week at most from the date of the announcement.

#### Article 21

The heirs of the deceased who was licensed to operate a hotel or tourist establishment, or a tourism activity, or an Office for organizing festivals and tourism events, must notify the competent Department / Directorate within three months from the date of death, with their names, places of residence, and the name of their representative. This representative shall be responsible for fulfilling the obligations imposed by the provisions of this Law / Act, its executive regulations, and the decisions issued for its implementation. He must take the necessary procedures to transfer the license in the name of the heirs or one of them, within the period determined by the competent Department / Directorate, otherwise the license will be considered canceled.

#### Article 22 Amended

The president, based on the recommendation of the competent Department / Directorate, may revoke the License for accommodation, housing, tourism places, tourism activity, the festival and event organizing Office, or the License for the festival or tourism event, as the case may be, in any of the following cases:

1- Conducting any activities or work in violation of the License.

2- Cessation of the activity for a continuous period of six months.

3- Loss of one of the conditions or requirements necessary to obtain the License, as stipulated in this Law / Act, its executive regulations, and decisions issued in implementation thereof.

4- Demolition of the building housing the establishment or Office, wholly or partially.

5- Changing the location of the establishment or Office without notifying Qatar Tourism (QT) thereof.

6- The License holder notifying Qatar Tourism (QT) of cessation of the licensed activity.

7- Violation of the Law / Act, public order, or public morals, or committing any act that harms the reputation, security, or relations of the country with brotherly or friendly states.

8- Issuance of a Final Judgment to close the establishment or Office, or revoke the License.

### الفصل الرابع: تصنيف المنشآت الفندقية والسياحية والأنشطة السياحية والمهرجانات والفعاليات السياحية ومكاتب تنظيمها (23-25)

#### Article 23 Amended

A classification guide for accommodation or housing and tourism places, and offices organizing festivals and tourism events into grades shall be issued by a decision from the Chairman / President, based on a proposal from the Department / Directorate concerned.

The Department / Directorate concerned shall classify accommodation or housing and tourism places, and offices organizing festivals and tourism events into one of the classification grades that correspond to their evaluation in accordance with the classification guide stipulated in the previous paragraph. The validity of the classification shall be linked to the license period unless its classification is amended by a decision from the Chairman / President, based on a recommendation from the Department / Directorate concerned, due to the decrease in the classification grade granted to it.

#### Article 23 Amended Canceled

يصدر بقرار من الأمين العام، بناءً على اقتراح الإدارة المختصة، دليل تصنيف المنشآت الفندقية والسياحية ومكاتب تنظيم المهرجانات والفعاليات السياحية إلى درجات.

وتُصنف الإدارة المختصة المنشآت الفندقية والسياحية ومكاتب تنظيم المهرجانات والفعاليات السياحية في إحدى درجات التصنيف التي تتناسب مع تقييمها وفقاً لدليل التصنيف المنصوص عليه في الفقرة السابقة، وتكون صلاحية التصنيف مرتبطة بمدة الترخيص ما لم يتم تعديل تصنيفها بقرار من الأمين العام، بناءً على توصية الإدارة المختصة، بسبب انخفاض درجة التصنيف الممنوحة لها.

#### Article 24 Amended

Accommodation, residence, and tourism site operators and those responsible for their management must request during the month of January each year a review of the classification grade of the accommodation, residence, or tourism sites in accordance with the rules and procedures determined by a decision issued by the Chairman / President, based on a proposal from the relevant Department / Directorate. The Chairman / President may, at any time, amend the establishment's classification grade if there are serious reasons that require this, based on a proposal from the relevant Department / Directorate.

#### Article 25 Amended

The guideline regarding the technical and administrative requirements and specifications that must be met in accommodation or housing venues, tourist establishments, tourism activities, tourism events and festivals, offices organizing festivals and tourism events, and the conditions that must be met by their employees shall be issued by a decision from the Chairman / President.

### الفصل الخامس: الترخيص السياحي للمجمعات التجارية (26)

#### Article 26 Amended

Commercial complexes are prohibited from engaging in tourism and entertainment activities and organizing or hosting tourism festivals and events before obtaining a License from Qatar Tourism (QT). The Executive Regulations specify the procedures for submitting the License application, its issuance and renewal, the conditions for granting the License and its duration, the rights and obligations of licensed commercial complexes, and the cases for suspension and termination of the License.

### الفصل السادس: الإرشاد السياحي (27-29)

#### Article 27 Amended

It is prohibited to practice the profession of tourist guiding except for those who have obtained a License from Qatar Tourism (QT) and are Recorded in the register of tourist guides there. The Executive Regulations determine the method of Record and the procedures for submitting the License application, issuing it, and renewing it.

#### Article 28

The Executive Regulations specify the conditions required for those working in the tourism guidance profession, the conditions for granting the license and its duration, the rights and obligations of tour guides, and the cases of suspension from practicing the profession, termination of the license, and its cancellation.

#### Article 29 Amended

The Chairman / President issues, based on a proposal from the Director General, a decision specifying the fee tariff for tourist guides, and determining the number of Licenses granted to tourist guides according to the requirements of the tourism sector in the State.

## الباب الثالث: تنشيط المهرجانات والفعاليات السياحية (30-31)

### Article 30

The promotion of services offered in accommodation or housing venues shall be In accordance with the provisions of this Law, with prior approval from Qatar Tourism (QT), and is not subject to any other regulations, in accordance with the controls specified by the Executive Regulations of this Law.

Qatar Tourism (QT) grants special approvals and access to capture the destination and its tourism offerings, through photographs, audio recordings, video and other media necessary for content organization.

### Article 30 Amended

By a decision of the Chairman / President, based on a proposal by the Director General and for the purpose of enhancing participation in festivals and tourism events organized by Qatar Tourism (QT) or other government entities during seasons that require boosting tourist attraction, participants in those festivals and tourism events may be granted a license to sell at discounted prices and to promote the sale of goods and the provision of services through advertisements, prize draws, or gift distribution, without being bound by the periods and regulations stipulated in the decisions governing this, and in coordination with the relevant entities.

### Article 31

Qatar Tourism (QT), in coordination with the relevant authorities in the State, organizes all activities, services, and entities operating in the tourism and hospitality sector within the State.

The Chairman / President grants benefits to establishments and tourism activities in the State, after the approval of other competent authorities.

The identification of new tourist areas shall be by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal by the Chairman / President.

### Article 31 Amended

The Chairman of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers may, upon the proposal of the Chairman / President, grant additional incentives to develop and encourage festivals and tourism events organized by Qatar Tourism (QT) or other government entities.

## الباب الرابع: العقوبات والجزاءات الإدارية (32-36)

### Article 32 Amended

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, imprisonment for a period not exceeding two years, and a fine not exceeding (500,000) five hundred thousand riyals, or either of these penalties, shall be imposed on anyone who:

1- Violates any of the provisions of Article (2) of this Law / Act, and it is additionally permitted to issue a Judgment / Ruling to close the establishment or Office.

2- Violates any of the provisions of Article (18) of this Law / Act, and it is additionally permitted to issue a Judgment / Ruling to close the establishment or Office, and to cancel the license.

The Judgment / Ruling shall be published in two daily newspapers at the expense of the convicted person, and on the Qatar Tourism website.

The president may, in case of violation of any of the provisions of the two articles referred to in the first paragraph of this article, temporarily close the establishment or Office, or the tourist festival or event, or suspend the activity administratively at the expense of the violator, until the violating conditions are corrected.

If the establishment, Office, activity, festival, or tourist event is part of an integrated establishment or Office or larger activity, festival, or event, the closure in this case shall be proportional to the part in which the violation occurred.

In the case of closure, the accommodation, residence, tourist establishment, or Office organizing festivals or tourist events against which the closure is issued, as applicable, must provide an alternative that is licensed and of the same category and level that was committed to providing to customers who have contracts made before the closure. If there is failure to comply with this, Qatar Tourism may provide this alternative at the expense of the violator, and collect the expenses by deducting from the Bank Guarantee available, or otherwise collect them administratively.

The closure decision shall be published on the Qatar Tourism website.

### Article 33 Amended

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, a fine not exceeding (150,000) one hundred and fifty thousand riyals shall be imposed on anyone who violates any provision of Articles (11), (12), (13), (14), (15), (16) of this Law / Act.

In addition, the court may issue a Judgment / Ruling to close the establishment or the Office.

In addition, the president may, in the event of a violation of the provisions of Articles (11),

(12), (13/items 1, 7, 8, 9), (16) of this Law / Act, close the establishment or the Office, or suspend the activity, festival, or tourism event administratively and at the violator’s expense, temporarily, until the violating situation is rectified.

If the establishment, Office, activity, festival, or tourism event is part of an integrated establishment or Office or a larger activity, festival, or event, the closure in this case shall apply to the part where the violation occurred.

The closure decision shall be published on the Qatar Tourism website.

### Article 34

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, a fine not exceeding (200,000) two hundred thousand riyals shall be imposed on anyone who violates the Ruling of Article (26) of this Law / Act.

### Article 35

The Penalties / Sentences stipulated in this Law / Act shall be doubled in case of recidivism, and the accused shall be considered a recidivist if he commits a similar Crime within five years from the date of completion of the execution of the Penalty / Sentence imposed or its expiration by lapse of time.

All Crimes stipulated in this Law / Act shall be considered similar in cases of recidivism.

### Article 35

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, a fine not exceeding (20,000) twenty thousand riyals shall be imposed on anyone who violates the Ruling of Article (27) of this Law / Act.

### Article 36

The Chairman may impose one or more of the financial penalties stipulated in the table attached to this Law on the licensee, in the event of committing a violation of the provisions of this Law, its Executive Regulations, the decisions, licenses, or instructions issued by Qatar Tourism (QT) in implementation of the provisions of this Law, provided that the violation does not fall under the crimes and penalties stipulated in Chapter Four thereof. The decisions of the Chairman must be justified, and the competent Department / Directorate shall notify the licensee, to his national address or by any means that indicates receipt, of the attributed violation and the need to submit his comments regarding it within fifteen days from the date of notification. If the licensee does not submit his comments within this period, or if he submits them and the competent Department / Directorate deems them unacceptable, the Chairman may impose a financial penalty not exceeding the maximum limit stipulated in the table attached to this Law. When determining the penalty, the seriousness of the violation and its resulting consequences must be taken into account. The decisions of the Chairman are final. The competent Department / Directorate shall notify the violator of the decision to impose the penalty and publish it by any means it deems appropriate. The violator must pay the monetary penalty within ninety days from the date of notification of the violation. In the event of continued violation, repetition, failure to correct, or failure to pay the monetary penalty, the Chairman may cancel the license or take any of the following measures until the causes of the violation are removed: 1- Suspend the event or activity. 2- Suspend or not renew the license. 3- Temporarily close the establishment.

### Article 36

The president, or whoever he authorizes, may reconcile in the crimes stipulated in this Law / Act, before initiating the Lawsuit / Legal Action, or during its consideration, and before a Final Judgment is issued, in exchange for paying half of the maximum prescribed fine.

The reconciliation results in the Lawsuit / Legal Action not being permissible to be initiated or terminated, as the case may be.

### Article 37 Amended

It is permissible, by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the Chairman / President, to grant investors in tourist areas privileges to develop and invest in those areas in a manner that achieves their development and sustainability.

### Article 38 Amended

A decision shall be issued by the Chairman / President, based on the proposal of the Director General, to determine the fees for issuing and renewing the licenses stipulated in this Law / Act.

### Article 39

The competent Department / Directorate shall keep a Record in which it documents the violations committed by those licensed under this Law / Act, and the procedures taken regarding them.

The competent Department / Directorate may, in the event of repeated violations or the commission of a serious violation, issue a decision to prohibit the licensed persons from engaging in activities related to tourism and organizing tourism festivals and events, for the period specified in the prohibition decision.

The Executive Regulations of this Law / Act shall specify the Specifications of the Record, the prohibition and unprohibition lists, and the procedures for recording therein.

### Article 40 Amended

The concerned party may raise a grievance against the decisions issued in implementation of the provisions of this Law / Act to the Chairman / President, within thirty days from the date of being notified of the decision at their national address, or by any means that indicates acknowledgment, or from the expiry date of the period set for deciding on the license application without a response.

The Chairman / President shall decide on the grievance within fifteen working days from the date of its submission. If this period passes without a response to the grievance, it shall be considered implicitly rejected. The decision on the grievance shall be final.

### Article 41 Amended

Employees of Qatar Tourism (QT) who are granted the Capacity of Law enforcement Officer by a decision of the Attorney General in agreement with the Board of Directors of the Council, shall have the authority to detect and establish Crimes and violations committed in contravention of the provisions of this Law / Act, its executive regulations, and the decisions issued in implementation thereof.

### Article 42 Amended

All those addressed by the provisions of this Law / Act must adjust their situations in accordance with its provisions, within six months from the date it comes into effect.

It is permissible, by a decision of the Chairman / President, based on a proposal from the Director General, to extend this period for one or more similar terms.

### Article 43

The Board of Directors of the Council shall issue the Executive Regulations for this Law / Act and the necessary decisions for its implementation. Until such Executive Regulations and decisions come into force, the current regulations and decisions in effect shall continue to apply, provided they do not conflict with its provisions.

### Article 44

يُلغى القانون رقم (6) لسنة 2012 المشار إليه، كما يُلغى كل حكم يخالف أحكام هذا القانون.

### Article 45

All competent authorities, each within its competence, shall implement this Law / Act. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.
