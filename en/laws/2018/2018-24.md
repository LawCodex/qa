# Law No. (24) of 2018 issuing the Income Tax Law

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And the Income Tax Law issued by Law No. (21) of 2009,

And Law No. (17) of 2014 regarding the exemption of the shares of non-Qatari investors in the profits of certain companies and investment funds from Income Tax,

And the State Financial System Law issued by Law No. (2) of 2015,

And the Amiri Resolution No. (77) of 2018 on the establishment of the General Tax Authority (GTA),

And based on the proposal of the Minister of Finance,

And upon the Draft Law submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

And after taking the opinion of the Shura Council,

We have decided the following Law:

## Part Two: Scope of Tax Application (2-6)

### Chapter One: Subject to Tax (2-3)

#### Article 2 Amended

An annual tax is imposed on the total taxable income of the taxpayer, arising from sources in the State during the previous tax year.

#### Article 2

The income of a Qatari project from real estate located abroad, and the income resulting from the direct use, leasing, or use in any other form of immovable property, is subject to taxation, taking into consideration the following:

1. The Qatari project beneficiary of the income from immovable property must not conduct business in the foreign country where the property generating the income is located through a permanent establishment there.

2. The immovable property for which income is actually paid must not be associated with this permanent establishment.

#### Article 2

Dividends paid by a foreign company residing abroad to a Qatari project are subject to tax, as well as interest and royalties arising abroad and paid to a Qatari project, taking into consideration the following:

1- The Qatari project benefiting from the distributed profits, interest, or royalties must not conduct business in the foreign country in which the company paying the dividends is resident, or in which the interest or royalties have arisen, through a permanent establishment located there.

2- The Equities / Stocks, shares, or other rights for which dividends were paid, or the debt claims for which interest was paid, or the rights or properties for which royalties were paid, must not be actually connected with such permanent establishment.

#### Article 2

Technical service fees arising abroad and paid to a Qatari project are subject to tax, taking into account the following:

1- The Qatari project benefiting from the technical service fees must not conduct business in the foreign State where the technical service fees arise through a permanent establishment located in that foreign State.

2- The technical service fees must not actually be related to that permanent establishment.

#### Article 2

"Subject to tax, the profits resulting from the disposal of assets abroad for any Qatari project."

#### Article 2

If a Qatari enterprise carries out activities in a foreign State through a permanent establishment located there, the profits attributable to the permanent establishment shall not be subject to tax, provided that they are subject to tax in that foreign State.

#### Article 2

Taxable income includes income derived from abroad from the following services:

1- Provision of rights to distribute products or services.

2- Payments for the provision of marketing, purchasing, financial Mediation, agency, and other Mediation services.

3- Fees paid to obtain guarantees or similar financial support.

4- Provision of Telecommunications Services and broadcasting services

#### Article 3

The income accrued in the State includes the following:

1- The gross income arising from an activity carried out in the State.

2- The gross income arising from contracts executed wholly or partially in the State.

3- The gross income arising from properties located in the State, and capital gains arising from their disposal.

4- The gross income arising from shares or ownership interests in companies resident in the State or listed in its financial markets, and the capital gains arising from their disposal.

5- The consideration for services paid to headquarters or main offices or branches or to related companies.

6- The interest on loans obtained in the State.

7- The gross income arising from the exploration, extraction, or exploitation of natural resources located in the State.

8- The gross income subject to taxation in the State based on a tax treaty. All in accordance with the provisions specified in the Regulation.

### Chapter Two: Tax Exemptions (4)

#### Article 4 Amended

Without prejudice to the tax exemptions prescribed under special Laws or international agreements, or those determined in accordance with the provisions of Article (35) of this Law / Act, the following incomes shall be exempt from tax:

1- Incomes of associations and private institutions, charitable associations and private institutions, and private institutions of public benefit established in accordance with the provisions of the Regulating Law of any of them.

2- Interest and banking returns due to natural persons who do not engage in a taxable activity in the State, whether they are residents or non-residents.

3- Interest and returns on Public Debt securities and Islamic securities, issued in accordance with the provisions of the financial system Law of the State, and bonds of public authorities and institutions.

4- Capital gains arising from the disposal of real estate or securities, achieved by natural persons, provided that the disposed real estate or securities are not assets of a taxable activity.

5- Capital gains arising from the revaluation of company assets when contributed as an in-kind share in return for participation in the capital of a joint stock company resident in the State, provided that the shares corresponding to the in-kind share are nominal, and are not disposed of before five years have passed.

6- Capital gains realized by a Qatari project from the disposal of the following:

a. Immovable property located abroad.

b. Movable property forming part of the assets of a permanent establishment owned by the Qatari project abroad, including capital gains arising from the transfer of ownership of that permanent establishment, by itself or with the entire project.

c. Foreign shares or quotas or other rights.

7- Fees of Board of Directors’ members and other similar payments received by a Qatari project as a member of the Board of Directors of a company resident abroad.

8- Profits from equities / stocks and other related income, if the amounts distributed during the tax year are deducted from the following:

a. Profits that were subject to tax under the provisions of this Law / Act.

b. Profits distributed by a company whose profits are exempt from tax under the provisions of this Law / Act or other Laws.

9- The gross income arising from artisanal activities that do not use machinery, where their gross income does not exceed (200,000) two hundred thousand riyals per year, and the average number of workers during the tax year does not exceed (3) three workers, and is practiced through a single establishment.

The Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on the proposal of the minister, may issue a decision to amend the exemption conditions specified in this item.

10- The gross income arising from agricultural or marine fishing activities.

11- The gross income earned by non-Qatari companies engaged in air or maritime navigation in the State, provided there is reciprocity of treatment.

12- The gross income of Qatari natural persons residing in the State.

13- The gross income of juridical persons residing in the State and wholly owned by Qataris.

14- The profits of juridical persons residing in the State in proportion to the profit shares of the following persons therein:

a. Qatari natural persons.

b. Juridical persons wholly owned by Qataris.

c. Juridical persons partially owned by Qataris in proportion to their profit shares therein.

The provisions of this item shall not apply to the profit shares of juridical persons owned by the State, wholly or partially, directly or indirectly, and operating in the field of petroleum operations and petrochemical industries.

15- The gross income arising from activities authorized for private bodies registered in the State, or registered in another State and licensed to operate in the State, within the limits of their non-profit primary activities.

16- The share of the non-Qatari investor in the profits of companies whose equities / stocks are offered for trading in financial markets.

17- The share of the non-Qatari investor in the profits of investment funds whose units are offered for trading in financial markets.

18- The share of the non-Qatari investor in profits resulting from the trading of all securities, including the units of investment funds listed for trading in financial markets.

The regulations shall specify the conditions and controls for the exemption of incomes mentioned in this Article from tax.

### Chapter Three: The Accounting Period (5-6)

#### Article 5

The accounting period for the taxpayer who conducts an activity is the tax year.

However, the taxpayer may, after obtaining the approval of the Authority, adopt an accounting period different from the tax year, in accordance with what is specified in the regulation.

#### Article 6

The taxpayer determines their taxable income based on accrual accounting applied in commercial accounting, in accordance with international accounting standards, taking into consideration the provisions of this Law / Act and the regulation.

The taxpayer may not use other accounting methods except after obtaining approval from the Authority.

## الباب الثالث: حساب الضريبة (7-9)

### Chapter One: Taxable Income (7-8)

#### Article 7

The taxable income is determined based on the gross income arising from all transactions executed by the taxpayer, after deducting the allowable deductions and losses stipulated in this article.

The allowable deductions refer to expenses and costs incurred by the taxpayer, which meet the following conditions:

1- They must be necessary to achieve the gross income.

2- They must have been actually incurred and supported by documentation.

3- They must not increase the value of the fixed assets used in the activity.

4- They must be related to the tax year.

The taxpayer may deduct losses incurred during the tax year from the net income of subsequent years.

All of this shall be as determined by the regulations.

#### Article 8

The following expenses and costs may not be deducted:

1- Expenses and costs incurred to achieve income exempt from tax.

2- Amounts paid in violation of the laws of the State.

3- Fines and penalties imposed due to violation of the laws of the State.

4- Expenses or losses related to Compensation that have been recovered or are recoverable, if those Compensation amounts have not been accounted for within the taxpayer’s gross income.

5- The portion of total expenses spent on entertainment, hospitality, restaurant food, vacations, club subscriptions, and customer gifts, in accordance with the situations, conditions, and percentages determined by the regulations.

6- Salaries and Wages, bonuses and their equivalents, including in-kind benefits, paid to the owner and his spouse and children, to the partners in a general partnership or limited partnership, to members of the Board of Directors, and to the manager of a limited liability company who directly or indirectly owns the majority of the shares in the company.

7- The Branch’s share of the administrative and general expenses of the center or head office that exceed the percentage determined by the regulations.

8- Commissions of foreign company agents that exceed the percentages specified by the regulations.

9- Any other deductions not permitted, In accordance with the provisions of this Law or the regulation.

### Chapter Two: Tax Rate (9)

#### Article 9

The tax rate (10%) shall be ten percent of the taxable income of the taxpayer during the tax year.

By way of exception to the Ruling of the previous paragraph, the tax rate shall be as follows:

1- The tax rate and all other tax conditions stipulated in the Agreements related to petrochemical industries, as well as those related to petroleum operations according to the definition specified in Law / Act No. (3) of 2007 regarding the exploitation of natural resources and their assets, shall apply, provided that the tax rate in all cases is not less than (35%) thirty-five percent.

2- Taking into account the provisions of the tax Agreements, a final withholding at source at a rate of (5%) five percent of the total amount shall be applied to royalties, interest, commissions, and fees for services performed wholly or partially in the State, and paid to non-residents for activities not related to a permanent establishment in the State, all in accordance with what is determined by the regulation.

3- The tax rate stipulated in the Agreements to which the government, ministries, other governmental bodies, agencies, public institutions, or those representing the government are a party, concluded prior to the entry into force of this Law / Act, shall apply. If the Agreement does not specify a tax rate, tax shall be imposed at the rate of (35%) thirty-five percent.

## الباب الرابع: الالتزامات الضريبية (10-13)

### Chapter One: Registration and Notification (10)

#### Article 10

Each taxpayer engaged in an activity or earning income subject to tax is required to:

1- Complete Registration with the Authority.

2- Notify the Authority of any change that may affect their tax obligations.

3- Submit an application to the Authority to obtain their own tax number.

The regulations specify the necessary conditions, controls, deadlines, and procedures for this.

### Chapter Two: Submission of Tax Returns (11)

#### Article 11 (Duplicate)

The entity that meets the criteria specified by the regulation must submit to the Authority a Report on the minimum indicators of its substantial activities, in accordance with the manner and within the period determined by a decision of the Minister.

The entity, regarding any reported financial year, is considered not to be conducting substantial activity in the State in the following cases:

1- If it does not submit the Report stipulated in the first paragraph of this Article.

2- If it does not meet one of the minimum indicators of substantial activity as specified by the regulation.

3- If it does not provide the Authority, upon its request, with documentary evidence regarding those indicators.

The Authority may, if the entity does not have the minimum indicators of substantial activity, refuse to issue a tax residency Testimony for it.

#### Article 11

The taxpayer, even if benefiting from a tax exemption, is obligated to submit a declaration to the Authority on the form prepared for this purpose, indicating the taxable income and the amount of tax due.

Without prejudice to the provisions regarding tax assessment, financial penalties, and statute of limitations stipulated in this Law / Act and the regulations, the taxpayer may, upon the Authority's approval, submit an amended declaration to correct errors contained in the declaration related to a previous tax year or to add any omission.

The regulations shall determine the necessary conditions, controls, deadlines, and procedures for this.

### Chapter Three: Accounting Obligations (12-13)

#### Article 12

The taxpayer who conducts an activity in the State is required to keep accounting books, records, and documents in accordance with the laws of the State and international accounting standards, and to retain them. The Authority may exempt certain taxpayers from retaining them, according to the cases, conditions, and procedures specified by the regulation.

#### Article 13 (Duplicate)

The Authority may obtain information and documents for the purpose of conducting a tax audit, or for the purpose of exchanging them with the competent foreign authorities, in accordance with agreements related to administrative assistance and the exchange of information for tax purposes, from any person or entity that possesses or controls such information and documents.

It is not required, when obtaining information to be exchanged with the competent foreign authorities, that the Authority needs it for its own tax purposes, or that the Authority conducts an audit on the taxpayer who possesses or controls such information, or who is concerned with that information, or that the acts for which information is to be obtained are criminalized in the State.

The Authority has the right to obtain information for the purposes specified in the first paragraph of this Article, in all tax matters, including information related to criminal tax matters.

The Authority also has the right to obtain information for the purposes specified in the first paragraph of this Article, from any person in the State who possesses or controls such information, regardless of any legal obligation upon that person to maintain the confidentiality of the information.

Information is under the control of a person if he has the legal right, authority, or ability to obtain the information or documents held by another person.

#### Article 13

Companies and other entities whose headquarters or actual place of management is in the State must provide the Authority, if requested, with information that identifies their legal owners and beneficial owners, including information about persons on whose behalf legal owners act as Deputy / Assistant, or under any similar arrangement, and information about persons present in a chain of intermediary entities between the company or other entity concerned and its beneficial owner.

Partnerships established under the laws of the State or operating in the State must provide the Authority, if requested, with information that identifies their partners and beneficial owners.

Trust funds established under the laws of the State or managed in the State or in which the trustee or Attorney / Agent resides in the State must provide the Authority, if requested, with information about the identity of their beneficial owners, including the grantor or founder, the trustee or Attorney / Agent, the protector or guarantor, as the case may be, all beneficiaries or classes of beneficiaries, and any other natural person who ultimately exercises actual control over the trust fund.

Non-profit organizations established under the laws of the State must provide the Authority with information that identifies the founders, board members, present beneficiaries, any beneficial owners thereof, or persons with the authority to represent it.

The information provided by legal entities and arrangements regarding the identity of their legal owners and beneficial owners must also be sufficient, accurate, and up to date.

#### Article 13 Amended

Government entities, companies, associations, private institutions, charitable private associations and institutions, private institutions of public benefit, sole proprietorships, and any other entity specified by the regulation must notify the Authority of contracts, Agreements, and transactions they conclude according to the controls and periods specified by the regulation.

With due regard to the regional and international tax Agreements to which the State is a party, the entities referred to in the previous paragraph must provide the Authority, upon its request, with any information relevant to tax purposes.

Any person residing in the State must provide the Authority, upon its request, with a detailed statement of their foreign financial assets or the rights they have in foreign financial assets, if they are suspected during the tax examination of committing or participating in the acts stipulated in Article (26) of this Law / Act.

For the purposes of the previous paragraph, foreign financial assets include the following assets:

1- Financial accounts held with a foreign financial institution.

2- Equities / Stocks and shares in companies, partnerships, and foreign trusts.

3- Government Securities, bonds, foreign debt securities, and any other form of indebtedness.

4- Real estate or real property rights.

The person required to submit the aforementioned statement must report their foreign financial assets or the rights they have in foreign financial assets even if none of those assets or rights affect that person's tax liability for the reported fiscal year.

The aforementioned statement shall be submitted according to the controls and within the time specified by the regulation.

## Chapter Five: Authority's Powers and Duties (14-16)

### Chapter One: Tax Assessment (14-15)

#### Article 14

The tax is assessed based on the taxable income indicated in the declaration, and the declaration is considered an assessment of the tax and an obligation to pay it on the same day it is submitted.

The Authority may amend the assessment based on the information provided in the declaration and the supporting documents, in accordance with the provisions of this Law / Act and the regulations.

The Authority may also conduct an estimated assessment based on any available information in case the taxpayer does not submit their tax declaration or fails to provide the information and documents supporting the declaration.

The Authority, in the two cases specified in the previous paragraphs, must notify the taxpayer of the elements of tax assessment and its value using the form prepared for this purpose, via registered Book or by any means that provides acknowledgement of receipt.

The liquidator is considered a taxpayer, and the assessment procedures are carried out against them.

This shall be according to the terms and conditions specified by the regulations.

#### Article 15

Subject to the provisions of the statute of limitations stipulated in this Law / Act, the Authority may not reassess the tax due on the taxpayer for a tax year for which the tax has already been assessed, except if new information emerges to the Authority that affects the Tax Obligations of the taxpayer and was not taken into account during the previous tax assessment.

The decision to reassess the tax is subject to the same rules applied to the initial tax assessment decision.

### Chapter Two: Confidentiality Commitment (16)

#### Article 16 Amended

Employees of the Authority must maintain the confidentiality of the information and documents that come to their knowledge or are in their possession during or on the occasion of performing their duties. Employees of the Authority are exempted from this obligation when disclosing such information and documents in the following cases:

1- To the taxpayer or their agent, or to any government entity provided that the taxpayer approves, unless the disclosure is prohibited by a provision in another Law / Act.

2- Upon the request of any judicial entity.

3- In the context of Information Exchange procedures under an international or regional tax agreement to which the State is a party.

Any information obtained by the Authority from a foreign Competent Authority pursuant to an agreement related to administrative assistance and Information Exchange for tax purposes shall be treated as confidential and may not be disclosed except to persons or authorities, including courts and administrative bodies responsible for assessment or collection of taxes, or for law enforcement or prosecution related to such taxes, or for deciding on appeals related thereto, or for supervising such duties. Such persons or authorities are not permitted to use that information except for the purposes mentioned above. They may disclose or reveal such information in public court proceedings or in judicial rulings. Information obtained by a contracting State may be used for other purposes if the laws of both States permit its use for such other purposes and the Competent Authority of the supplying State allows such use.

## Chapter Six: Objections and Complaints (17-19)

### Chapter One: Objections (17-18)

#### Article 17

The taxpayer may object, by registered letter or by any means deemed appropriate, to the tax assessment decision within thirty days from the date of being notified.

The objection is submitted to the Authority, and submitting it results in the suspension of the implementation of the tax assessment decision.

If the taxpayer does not submit an objection within the period specified in the first paragraph of this article, the tax assessment decision becomes final, and the tax becomes due and payable.

#### Article 18

The Authority considers the objection and notifies the taxpayer or the Official / Officer of its decision in any manner that provides knowledge, within sixty days from the date of submitting the objection.

Sixty days passing without a response to the objection is considered an implicit rejection of it.

In case the taxpayer agrees with the Authority's decision issued regarding the objection he submitted, the tax shall be assessed definitively based on that.

### Chapter Two: Complaints (19)

#### Article 19

One or more Committees shall be established at the Authority, called the "Tax Grievance Committee," which shall be formed under the chairmanship of one of the judges of the Court of Appeal chosen by the Supreme Judiciary Council.

The formation of the Committee, the Regulation of its work, the procedures for Raising a Grievance before it, and the determination of its remunerations shall be issued by a Decision / Resolution from the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on the proposal of the Minister.

The nomination of the Chairperson and members of the Committee shall be issued by a Decision / Resolution from the Minister.

The Committee shall be competent to adjudicate applications submitted by the taxpayer as a Legal Challenge / Appeal against Decisions of the Authority, and any other competencies determined by the Decision / Resolution issued on the Regulation of its work. The Committee has the authority to reduce the financial penalties stipulated in this Law / Act.

The Committee is obliged to observe the established procedures and general principles of litigation procedures.

The taxpayer and the Authority may file a Legal Challenge / Appeal against the Decision / Resolution of the Committee before the Administrative Circuit of Appeal within sixty days from the date of notification of the Decision / Resolution, and the filing of the Legal Challenge / Appeal does not result in suspension of the execution of the Committee's Decision / Resolution unless the Court decides otherwise.

## الباب السابع: تحصيل واسترداد الضريبة (20-23)

### Chapter One: Tax Collection (20)

#### Article 20 Amended

The taxpayer pays the tax due based on the declaration submitted by him, on the same day the declaration is submitted. In case he is notified of a Decision / Resolution of the Authority regarding adjustment or estimation, and the objection period stipulated in Article (17) of this Law / Act expires without submitting an objection, the taxpayer is obligated to pay the tax and the related financial penalties within thirty (30) days from the expiry date.

Of the period referred to.

In case the taxpayer agrees to the Decision / Resolution of the Authority issued regarding the objection, the due tax is paid within thirty (30) days from the date of notification of this Decision / Resolution.

In cases other than those stipulated in the previous two paragraphs, the tax and the related financial penalties are collected in a single payment within thirty (30) days from the expiry date of the period stipulated in Article (18) of this Law / Act without a response, or from the date the taxpayer or the Official / Officer is notified of the Authority's reply to the objection.

The Authority may, upon the request of the concerned party, approve the payment of the due tax and related financial penalties in installments, as specified by the regulation. If the taxpayer fails to pay any installment on the specified date, all remaining installments become immediately due.

The taxpayer may, if he pays a foreign tax on income subject to tax in the State, deduct that foreign tax within the limit of the tax amount due in the State, taking into account the following:

1- The foreign tax must be a tax on income imposed by a foreign State or one of its political divisions or local Authorities.

2- The foreign tax must be actually paid.

3- The foreign tax must be reduced by any amounts recovered by the taxpayer from the foreign State.

It is not permitted to claim a deduction for a foreign tax on income exempt from tax in the State.

### Chapter Two: Seizure of the Taxpayer's Assets (21-22)

#### Article 21

The president, in cases where it appears that the collection of the tax is threatened with loss, may obtain an order from the summary matters judge to impose a precautionary seizure on the taxpayer’s assets necessary for the collection of the tax and related financial penalties, whether these assets are in the possession of the taxpayer or another party.

These assets are considered under precautionary seizure from the date the taxpayer is notified of the summary matters judge’s order, and the taxpayer may not dispose of them except in the case that the precautionary seizure is lifted by order of the summary matters judge.

The taxpayer and interested parties may file a Legal Challenge / Appeal against the order of precautionary seizure before the competent Court within thirty days from the date of notification.

#### Article 22

If a decision to assess the tax and related financial penalties becomes final, and they are not paid on the due date, the Chairman / President shall take executive seizure measures on the taxpayer’s assets necessary for tax collection, whether these assets are in the possession of the taxpayer or others.

The Authority may request, by registered letter, any person to submit to it within thirty days from the date of receipt of this letter, a statement of the amounts they owe to the account of the taxpayer. The statement shall include the following:

a) The amounts owed by the person to the taxpayer and their due dates.

b) The amounts in their possession and due to the taxpayer before a third party, and whether the person is authorized to pay the taxpayer on behalf of the third party.

The aforementioned person in the previous paragraph must pay the amounts due from the taxpayer to the Authority, within the limit of the tax amount and its related financial penalties, within thirty days from their due date. Amounts that have become due at the date of submitting the statement to the Authority must be paid within thirty days from this date.

If the person does not submit the statement within the specified period, or if the amounts are not paid to the Authority in accordance with the provisions of the previous paragraph, the Authority shall take executive seizure measures on that person’s assets.

For the purposes of applying the provisions of paragraphs one and four of this Article, the Authority shall notify the debtor, and the seizure shall be executed by the Authority in accordance with the provisions of the Law / Act.

The provisions of paragraphs two, three, and four of this Article shall not apply to Banks, except by order of the Court.

### Chapter Three: Refund of Tax Amount and Financial Penalties Collected Without Justification (23)

#### Article 23

Subject to the provisions of the statute of limitations stipulated in this Law / Act, the taxpayer may reclaim amounts of tax and financial penalties collected from them without justification, by submitting a request to the Authority.

The Authority shall notify the taxpayer of its decision regarding the refund request within sixty days from the date of submission.

The taxpayer may raise a grievance before the Tax Grievance Committee in the event the Authority rejects the refund request or fails to notify the taxpayer of its decision within the specified period.

In the event of a delay by the Authority in refunding the amounts collected without justification within the specified period, the taxpayer has the right to compensation, calculated in accordance with the provisions of the regulation.

### Chapter Two: Penalties (26-32)

#### Article 26

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, the penalty of imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year and a fine not exceeding three times the due tax, or either of these penalties, shall be imposed on any taxpayer or person responsible:

1- Who submits forged or fictitious books, records, or documents.

2- Who uses fraudulent methods, including submitting forged, fictitious, or incorrect data or documents, with the intention of obtaining a tax deduction, exemption, or refund of previously paid tax.

3- Who deliberately fails to complete Registration for tax purposes, or conceals real income or any activity subject to tax.

4- Who commits any act intended to prevent Authority employees from performing their duties.

#### Article 27

The person who intentionally participated in violating any of the obligations stipulated in this Law shall be jointly liable with the taxpayer or the المسؤول for the payment of any amounts due as a result of the violation.

Each of the assignor and assignee, partners in partnerships, the representative of the non-resident person, and their agent shall be jointly liable for the payment of taxes and financial penalties due to the Authority, in accordance with the regulations determined by the Authority.

#### Article 28

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, shall be punished by imprisonment for a period not exceeding six months and a fine not exceeding (50,000) fifty thousand riyals, or by either of these penalties, anyone who violates the provisions of Article (16) of this Law / Act.

#### Article 29

The penalties stipulated in this Law shall be doubled in case of repeated offense, and the accused shall be considered a repeat offender if they commit a similar crime within five years from the date of complete execution of the Penalty / Sentence issued against them or its expiration due to lapse of time.

#### Article 30

Criminal Lawsuit / Legal Action may not be initiated regarding the Crimes stipulated in Articles (26) and (27) of this Law / Act, except based on a written request from the Chairman / President.

#### Article 32

Employees of the Authority, who are granted the Capacity of Law enforcement Officer by a decision from the Attorney General in agreement with the Minister, shall have the right to detect and document any violations of the provisions of this Law / Act and the implementing decisions thereof.
