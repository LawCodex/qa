# Law No. (21) of 2018 concerning the Regulation of Business Events

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And Law No. (17) of 2013 regarding the regulation of exhibitions,

And Emiri Decision No. (74) of 2018 on the establishment of the National Tourism Council,

And the Draft Law submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

And after consulting the Shura Council,

We have decided the following Law:

For the application of the provisions of this Law, the following words and expressions shall have the meanings ascribed to each of them, unless the context requires otherwise:

## Chapter Two: Business Events Licenses, Organizing Offices, and Venues (2-16)

### Chapter One: Licensing Terms and Procedures (2-8)

#### Article 2 Amended

It is prohibited to establish, operate, or manage venues designated for hosting business events or offices for organizing such events, or to hold a business event in the State, except after obtaining a License from Qatar Tourism (QT) in accordance with the provisions of this Law and its executive regulations.

The Ruling of the previous paragraph does not apply to venues for holding business events located within accommodation or residential places and commercial complexes licensed by Qatar Tourism (QT), provided that these venues comply with the other provisions and obligations imposed on License holders under this Law and its executive regulations.

#### Article 3 Amended

The license application is submitted to the competent Department / Directorate, using the designated form for this purpose, including the required data and accompanied by supporting documents, including the approval of the competent Department / Directorate on the preliminary design for all asset development projects related to business activities.

The Executive Regulations of this Law / Act specify the conditions, controls, Bank Guarantee requirements, and insurance policy necessary for granting the license.

#### Article 4 Amended

The competent Department / Directorate shall decide on the license application and notify the applicant of its decision at their national address or by any means that provides notice, within a week from the date of submission. If the application is rejected, the rejection must be justified. The expiration of this period without a response to the application shall be considered an implicit rejection.

#### Article 5

The competent Department / Directorate, in the event of approval of a License application for a venue to host business events, or an Office organizing business events, issues a preliminary approval for the period specified by the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act, according to the type of establishment to be licensed, to enable the License applicant to complete the construction and necessary preparations to start the activity.

The License applicant must, once the construction and preparations necessary to start the activity are completed, notify the competent Department / Directorate in writing, and the said Department / Directorate must verify the completion of the construction and the preparations referred to within fifteen days from the date of notification, and the License is issued if completion is confirmed.

The competent Department / Directorate, in the event that the referred construction and preparations are not completed, may grant the License applicant one or more additional extensions, each not to exceed the period referred to in the first paragraph of this article, and the application is rejected if the required works are not completed.

#### Article 6 Amended

The license period shall be a maximum of five years, and the license may be renewed for one or more similar periods, after payment of the prescribed fee.

The Executive Regulations of this Law / Act specify the controls for the application of this article.

#### Article 7

The Executive Regulation of this Law / Act shall determine the following:

1- The requirements that must be met in the business event program and the places where business events are permitted to be held, according to the nature of each.

2- The types of business events permitted in the State, their duration, and the entities from which approval or a License must be obtained regarding such events.

3- The exhibitions in which indirect sale of exhibits and contracting for them is permitted, the limits of that, and the obligations arising in case of sale.

4- The necessary terms and conditions for granting Licenses to hold business events, organizer offices, and the places where business events are permitted to be held.

#### Article 8 Amended

No License to hold a business event in the State shall be granted unless the event operator is one of the licensed business event organizing offices.

Qatar Tourism (QT), after coordinating with the relevant authorities, manages business events through the Qatar Events Calendar and may reject or postpone applications for Licenses to hold business events in order to coordinate dates, avoid overlaps, and ensure quality.

Other relevant authorities in the State responsible for planning and Licensing any aspect of business events must coordinate with Qatar Tourism (QT) before issuing these Licenses.

### Chapter Two: Obligations of the Licensee (9-12)

#### Article 9 Amended

The licensee of a venue for hosting business events, or a business event organizing office, must comply with the following:

1- Providing all necessary services to prepare and organize the business event, especially the following:

a- Submitting the license application for holding the business event on the form prepared for this purpose, and following it up, on behalf of the license applicant.

b- Holding the business event and its associated preparations.

c- Supervising the operation and management of the licensed business event in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

d- Providing general services and facilities to participants and visitors of the business event.

e- Holding other events related to the business event after obtaining the approval of the Department / Directorate concerned and the necessary license to hold the related event from the Competent Authority.

2- Adhering to the conditions and instructions of the Competent Authorities regarding the holding of the licensed business event.

3- Obtaining the Licenses and other approvals necessary to hold the business event from the Competent Authorities in the State, and notifying Qatar Tourism (QT) of these Licenses and approvals before holding the event.

4- Not making any changes to the commercial name of the establishment or its management except after notifying Qatar Tourism (QT) in writing of this change.

5- Not making any change in the ownership of the establishment except after the approval of Qatar Tourism (QT).

#### Article 10 Amended

The licensee for holding a business event in the State must comply with the following:

1- Adhere to the duration of the business event specified in the issued license.

2- Adhere to holding the business event at the location specified according to the granted license.

3- Observe the requirements that must be met for exhibits participating in the exhibition, and the indirect display and sales instructions specified according to the granted license.

4- Adhere to the business event program according to the granted license.

5- Register the event in the Qatar Events Calendar as determined by Qatar Tourism (QT).

#### Article 11 Amended

The licensee must, as applicable, comply with the following:

1- Comply with the standards and requirements for service excellence set by Qatar Tourism (QT).

2- Display the license and the distinguishing mark of the classification grade, if any, in a visible place and in the manner determined by the competent Department / Directorate.

3- Provide the client with an invoice listing the services provided and the value of each, in Arabic and in other languages specified by the competent Department / Directorate.

4- Take necessary precautions to protect the environment.

5- Adhere to the controls and conditions of licensing stipulated in this Law / Act, its executive regulations, and the decisions issued in implementation thereof.

6- Provide Qatar Tourism (QT) with pricing plans and promotional offers related to pricing, at least ten days before they are announced, to obtain the approval of the competent Department / Directorate. These programs shall be confidential, and the employees responsible at Qatar Tourism (QT) are prohibited from disclosing any information about them to others.

Qatar Tourism (QT) approval is not required for marketing or tourism promotion programs that are not related to pricing.

7- Keep the books, records, and documents related to the operation of the establishment, the practice of the activity, or the organization of business events, as well as the special statistics determined by the Director General and for the period specified by the competent Department / Directorate.

8- Provide any information required by Qatar Tourism (QT) to evaluate the tourism experience, and any statistics and data determined by the Director General for the period specified by the competent Department / Directorate. The licensee is responsible for the accuracy of this data and must submit it as soon as it is requested by the competent Department / Directorate, in accordance with the mechanism determined for this purpose. The licensee shall bear the requirements for equipment, electronic linkage, and any other requirements necessary to fulfill this obligation.

9- Comply with the instructions issued by Qatar Tourism (QT), based on joint coordination between Qatar Tourism (QT) and the competent authorities in the State, regarding the regulations for organizing and holding business events in licensed venues.

10- Observe the instructions issued by Qatar Tourism (QT), based on joint coordination with the competent authorities in the State, regarding the regulations for issuing tourist visas and visit visas for the purposes of business events, and comply with the system prepared for this purpose.

#### Article 12 Amended

The licensee must provide suitable opportunities for training, development, and qualification of the workforce involved in organizing business events at various levels and related activities, and ensure compliance with the guidelines set by Qatar Tourism (QT) regarding training.

### Chapter Three: Waiver, Transfer, and Revocation of the License (13-16)

#### Article 13

The licensee may not assign the granted license except after obtaining written approval from the competent Department / Directorate, provided that the assignee meets the licensing conditions stipulated in this Law / Act, its executive regulations, and the decisions issued in implementation thereof.

#### Article 14

The licensee of the business events location, or the Office for organizing business events, is obligated, when ceasing the licensed activity or terminating it for any reason, to announce this on their website, if available, and in one of the widely circulated local daily newspapers at their own expense, in accordance with the template specified by the competent Department / Directorate. If the licensee does not do so, the Council may make the announcement at their expense and collect the costs by deducting from the Bank Guarantee if available to the Council; otherwise, they are collected administratively, in the event the licensee does not pay them in cash within one week at most from the date the announcement is made.

#### Article 15

The heirs of the deceased who is licensed for a place to hold business events, or an Office for organizing business events, must notify the competent Department / Directorate, within three months from the date of death, of their names and places of residence, and the name of their representative. This representative shall be responsible for implementing the provisions of this Law / Act, its executive regulations, and the decisions issued in implementation thereof. He must take the necessary procedures to transfer the license to the name of the heirs or one of them, within the period specified by the competent Department / Directorate, otherwise the license shall be considered cancelled.

#### Article 16 Amended

The Secretary General, based on the recommendation of the competent Department / Directorate, may cancel the License for a business event venue, the License for an Office organizing business events, or the License to hold a business event, as the case may be, in any of the following cases:

1- Engaging in any activities or works in violation of the License.

2- Suspension of activity for six consecutive months.

3- Loss of one of the requirements to obtain the License stipulated in this Law / Act, its executive regulations, or the decisions issued for its implementation.

4- Changing the location of the activity without notifying Qatar Tourism (QT).

5- The Licensee notifies Qatar Tourism (QT) of discontinuing the licensed activity.

6- Violation of the Law / Act, public order, or public morals, or committing any act that harms the country’s reputation, security, or its relations with brotherly or friendly countries.

7- Issuance of a Final Judgment to close the establishment or the Office, or to cancel the License.

## Chapter Three: Classification of Business Event Organizing Offices (17-19)

### Article 17 Amended

A guide for classifying business event organizing offices into grades is issued by a decision of the Chairman / President, based on a proposal from the relevant Department / Directorate.

The relevant Department / Directorate classifies business event organizing offices into one of the grades that correspond to their evaluation, according to the classification guide referred to in the previous paragraph. The validity of the classification is linked to the license period unless the classification is modified by the relevant Department / Directorate due to a decrease in the grade awarded.

### Article 18 Amended

Business event organization Office owners and those responsible for managing them may request, during the month of January each year, a review of the Office classification level, in accordance with the rules and procedures determined by a decision of the Chairman / President based on a proposal from the relevant Department / Directorate. The Chairman / President may, at any time, amend the Office classification level if there are serious reasons justifying this, based on a proposal from the relevant Department / Directorate.

### Article 19 Amended

The guide detailing the technical and administrative requirements and specifications that must be met in both business activities and the establishment, as well as the conditions required for its employees, shall be issued by a decision of the Chairman / President.

## الباب الرابع: العقوبات والجزاءات الإدارية (20-22)

### Article 20 Amended

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, the punishment of imprisonment for a period not exceeding two years and a fine not exceeding (500,000) five hundred thousand riyals, or either of these two penalties, shall be imposed on anyone who:

1- Violates any of the provisions of Article (2) of this Law / Act, and in addition, a ruling shall be issued to close the establishment.

2- Violates any of the provisions of Article (23) of this Law / Act.

And the Judgment / Ruling shall be published in two daily newspapers at the expense of the convicted person, and on the Qatar Tourism (QT) website.

The Chairman may, in case of violation of any of the provisions of Article (2) of this Law / Act, administratively close the establishment or the business event or suspend the activity, at the expense of the violator, temporarily, until the violating conditions are rectified.

If the establishment or the business event is part of an integrated establishment or a larger event, the closure in this case shall be in proportion to the part in which the violation occurred.

In the case of closure, the violator must provide a licensed alternative of the same category and standard that he was obliged to provide to the clients with whom he had contracts before the closure, and in the event of failure to do so, Qatar Tourism (QT) may provide this alternative at the expense of the violator, and recover the costs by deduction from the Bank Guarantee if available to Qatar Tourism (QT), otherwise, it shall be recovered administratively.

The closure decision shall be published on the Qatar Tourism (QT) website.

### Article 21

The Penalties / Sentences stipulated in this Law / Act shall be doubled in case of recidivism, and the accused shall be considered a recidivist if he commits a similar crime within five years from the date of completion of the implementation of the Penalty / Sentence imposed on him or its expiration by the passage of time.

All Crimes stipulated in this Law / Act are considered similar with regard to cases of recidivism.

### Article 21 Amended

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, a fine not exceeding (150,000) one hundred and fifty thousand Riyals shall be imposed on anyone who violates any of the provisions of Articles (9), (10), (11) of this Law / Act.

In addition, the president may, in case of violation of the provisions of Articles (9), (10), (11/sections 1, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9), of this Law / Act, close the establishment or suspend the activity administratively and at the expense of the violator, temporarily, until the irregular situation is rectified.

If the establishment or business activity is part of an integrated establishment or a larger activity, the closure in this case shall be for the part where the violation occurred.

The closure decision shall be published on the Qatar Tourism website.

### Article 22

The President, or whomever he delegates, may reconcile in the Crimes stipulated in the two previous articles, before initiating the Lawsuit / Legal Action, during its consideration, and before the issuance of a Final Judgment therein, in exchange for payment of half the maximum prescribed fine.

The reconciliation results in the Lawsuit / Legal Action not being initiated or its extinction, as the case may be.

### Article 22 Amended

The Chairman / President may impose one or more of the financial penalties stipulated in the Schedule attached to this Law / Act, on the licensee, in case of violating the provisions of this Law / Act, its executive regulations, decisions, Licenses, or instructions issued by Qatar Tourism (QT) in implementation of the provisions of this Law / Act, and this applies other than the Crimes and penalties stipulated in its fourth chapter.

The Chairman / President’s decisions must be justified, and the competent Department / Directorate must notify the licensee at his national address or by any means that ensures notification, of the attributed violation and the need to submit his observations regarding it within fifteen days from the date of notification. If the licensee does not submit his observations within this period, or if he submits them and the competent Department / Directorate deems them unacceptable, the Chairman / President may impose a financial penalty not exceeding the maximum stipulated in the Schedule attached to this Law / Act.

When determining the penalty, the seriousness of the violation and its resulting consequences must be taken into account, and the Chairman / President’s decisions shall be final.

The competent Department / Directorate must notify the violator of the decision imposing the penalty, and publish it by the means it deems appropriate.

The violator must pay the financial penalty amount within ninety days from the date of notification of the violation.

In the event of persistence in the violation or its recurrence, or failure to correct it, or failure to pay the financial penalty amount, the Chairman / President may revoke the License

or take any of the following measures, until the causes of the violation are removed:

1- Suspension of the event or activity.

2- Suspension of the License or non-renewal thereof.

3- Temporary closure of the establishment.

## الباب الخامس: الأحكام العامة والختامية (23-31)

### Article 23 Amended

No person may organize or participate in a business event abroad in the name of the State or its flag without obtaining approval from the Prime Minister.

Similarly, no person or entity may submit a request to any foreign body or organization to attract or organize a business event in the State without obtaining approval from Qatar Tourism (QT).

### Article 24 Amended

A decision from the Chairman / President, based on a proposal from the Director General, shall be issued to determine the fees for issuing and renewing the Licenses stipulated in this Law / Act.

### Article 25

The competent Department / Directorate shall maintain a Record in which violations committed by licensees and the measures taken regarding them are recorded.

In the event of repeated violations or the commission of a serious violation, the Secretary-General may issue a decision to prohibit the licensees from engaging in activities related to the Regulation of business events, for the period specified by the prohibition decision.

The Executive Regulations of this Law / Act specify the serious violations, the specifications of the Record, and the procedures for recording in it.

### Article 26 Amended

The concerned party may Raise a Grievance against decisions issued in implementation of the provisions of this Law / Act to the Chairman / President, within thirty days from the date of notification of the decision, to their national address, or by any means that signifies awareness, or from the date of expiry of the period prescribed to decide on the licensing application without response.

The Chairman / President shall decide on the grievance within fifteen working days from the date of its submission. The lapse of this period without a response to the grievance shall be considered an implicit rejection of it, and the decision to decide on the grievance shall be final.

### Article 27

Council employees who are granted the Capacity of Law enforcement Officer by a decision from the Attorney General, in agreement with the Chairman / President, shall have the power to detect and record Crimes and violations committed in contravention of the provisions of this Law / Act, its executive regulations, and the decisions issued in implementation thereof.

### Article 28 Amended

All those addressed by the provisions of this Law / Act must adjust their status in accordance with its provisions, within six months from the date it comes into force.

It is permissible, by a decision of the Chairman / President, based on a proposal from the Director General, to extend this period for one or more similar periods.

### Article 29 Amended

The Chairman / President shall issue the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act and the necessary decisions for its implementation. Until these Executive Regulations and decisions come into effect, the currently applicable regulations and decisions shall remain in force, provided they do not conflict with its provisions.

### Article 30

The Law No. (17) of 2013 referred to is hereby repealed, and all provisions contrary to this Law shall be repealed.

### Article 31

All relevant authorities, each within its area of competence, shall implement this Law / Act. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
