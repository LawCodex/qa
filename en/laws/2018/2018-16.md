# Law No. (16) of 2018 Regarding the Regulation of Ownership and Use of Real Estate by Non-Qataris

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

Having reviewed the Constitution,

And Law No. (5) of 1963 on the prohibition of foreigners acquiring ownership of immovable property in Qatar,

And Law No. (14) of 1964 on the real estate Registration system, and its amending laws,

And Law No. (1) of 1980 concerning the regulation of the ownership of real estate by foreign missions in Qatar,

And Law No. (13) of 2000 on the Regulation of non-Qatari capital investment in economic activity, and its amending laws,

And Law No. (2) of 2002 concerning the regulation of property ownership for citizens of the Gulf Cooperation Council States, amended by Law No. (25) of 2008,

And Law No. (25) of 2002 regarding investment funds,

And Law No. (17) of 2004 on the Regulation of ownership and usufruct of real estate and housing units by non-Qataris,

And Commercial Companies Law issued by Law No. (11) of 2015,

And the Draft Law submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

And after taking the opinion of the Shura Council,

We have decided the following Law:

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
