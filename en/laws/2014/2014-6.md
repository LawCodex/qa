# Law No. (6) of 2014 on the Regulation of real estate development

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And Law No. (14) of 1964 on the real estate registration system, and its amending laws,

And Law No. (4) of 1985 on the Regulation of buildings, and its amending laws,

And Law No. (13) of 2000 on the Regulation of investment of non-Qatari capital in economic activity, and its amending laws,

And Law No. (2) of 2002 on the Regulation of real estate ownership by citizens of the Gulf Cooperation Council States, as amended by Law No. (25) of 2008,

And Commercial Companies Law promulgated by Law No. (5) of 2002, and its amending laws,

And Law No. (17) of 2004 on the Regulation of ownership and usufruct of real estate and residential units by non-Qataris,

And The Civil Law promulgated by Law No. (22) of 2004,

And Law No. (19) of 2005 on the Regulation of practicing engineering professions,

And Law No. (29) of 2006 on the supervision of buildings,

And The Law of Qatar Central Bank and Regulation of Financial Institutions promulgated by Law No. (13) of 2012,

And Amiri Resolution No. (18) of 2014 on the organizational structure of the Ministry of Municipality and Urban Planning,

And Amiri Resolution No. (20) of 2014 on the organizational structure of the Ministry of Economy and Commerce,

And Amiri Resolution No. (25) of 2014 on the organizational structure of the Ministry of Justice,

And upon the proposal of the Ministers of Justice and Economy and Commerce,

And upon the Draft Law submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

And after seeking the opinion of the Shura Council,

We have decided the following Law:

## Chapter One: Definitions (1)

### Article 1 Amended

For the application of the provisions of this Law, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings assigned to each of them, unless the context requires another meaning:

Ministry: Ministry of Municipality.

Minister: Minister of Municipality.

Authority: The General Authority for Regulating the Real Estate Sector, established pursuant to the Decision / Resolution of the Emir No. (28) of 2023 referred to.

Chairman / President: Chairman / President of the Authority.

Real estate development: Activities carried out by the developer with the aim of disposing of real estate units, including subdivision of plots and buildings, infrastructure development, designing, construction, advertising, marketing, Registration of the mentioned units.

Developer: Any natural or legal person licensed to undertake real estate development activities.

Contractor: Any natural or legal person assigned to execute all or Part of the real estate development project.

Owner: Any natural or legal person who owns the plot intended for real estate development activities.

Map: Detailed engineering plan of the real estate units subject to disposal, prepared by the developer.

Subdivision of plots on the map: Activities for subdividing plots in accordance with the approved planning consent, with the aim of disposing of them on the map by any type of disposal.

Subdivision of buildings on the map: Activities for subdividing buildings according to the approved building License, with the purpose of disposing of the subdivided units on the map by any type of disposal.

Project consultant: Any engineering consulting Office, whether local or a branch of an international Office, registered with the Engineers Acceptance Committee and classified as not less than category two.

Register: Register of real estate developers, established pursuant to the provisions of Article (6) of this Law.

Real Estate Register: The register Regulating pursuant to the provisions of Law / Act No. (14) of 1964 concerning the real estate Registration system and its executive regulations.

Initial Real Estate Register: A set of real estate sheets and supplementary registers, in which, provisionally, the nature and description of the units subdivided on the map and the legal transactions concerning them are recorded.

Bank: Qatar Central Bank.

Bank: Any national or foreign bank, or one of their branches, licensed by the Bank to open the account.

Account: Real estate development escrow account with the bank, in which amounts of buyers of units disposed of on the map or amounts of project financiers are deposited.

Contract: An agreement concluded between the developer and the buyer, under which the rights and mutual obligations between them are determined.

Committee: Committee for settlement of real estate development disputes, established pursuant to Article (32) of this Law.

Licenses Committee: The Committee established pursuant to Article (3 bis) of this Law

## Chapter Two: Licensing (2-9)

### Article 2 Amended

Real estate development activities may not be carried out before obtaining a License from the Department / Directorate In accordance with the provisions of this Law.

"Companies may not carry out activities related to the management and maintenance of facilities in real estate development projects except after obtaining a License from the Licenses Committee."

### Article 3

It is required that those who are licensed to practice real estate development activities fulfill the following conditions:

First: For a natural person:

1- Must be of Qatari nationality.

2- Must not have had a Final Judgment issued against them for a crime involving dishonor or breach of trust, or have been declared bankrupt, unless they have been rehabilitated.

3- Must have previous experience in the management of construction and building projects for no less than three years prior to submitting the application.

4- Must be registered in the Commercial Register.

Second: For a legal entity:

1- Must be a Qatari company established in accordance with the provisions of the Commercial Companies Law referred to above, with the purpose of engaging in real estate development activities among its objectives.

2- Must have previous experience in construction and building activities for no less than three years prior to submitting the application.

3- Must not have previously been declared bankrupt by a Final Judgment.

By way of exception to the provisions of Law No. (13) of 2000 referred to above, non-Qatari companies wishing to be licensed to practice real estate development activities must meet the following conditions:

1- Activities must be within the areas where non-Qataris are permitted to own property, and in accordance with the permitted ownership sizes.

2- Must be a company established abroad in accordance with the applicable legislations, with the purpose of engaging in real estate development activities among its objectives, and the incorporation documents must be certified by the Diplomatic Mission or Qatari Consulate in the country of incorporation or its equivalent, or by whoever is entrusted with safeguarding its interests in the State of the main office.

3- Must have previous experience in construction and building activities for no less than ten years prior to submitting the application, having executed and completed such projects and enjoying a good reputation and fame.

4- Must have a branch in the State, or in one of the Gulf Cooperation Council States, and must be registered in the Commercial Register.

### Article 3 (Duplicate)

A Committee called the "Licenses Committee" shall be established in the Authority, specializing in issuing development licenses, developer licenses, and licenses for companies operating in the management and maintenance of properties under development.

A decision from the Cabinet / Council of Ministers shall be issued to form the Committee, determine its working system and procedures to be followed before it, and specify its remuneration.

A decision from the Minister shall be issued for Nominating the Chairman and members of the Committee.

### Article 4

The application for obtaining or renewing a License to practice real estate development activities shall be submitted, on the form prepared for this purpose, to the Department / Directorate, accompanied by the documents supporting the application.

The Department / Directorate shall consider the application and notify the concerned party of its Decision / Resolution within thirty days from the date of submission, by registered letter or any other method that ensures acknowledgment. In case the application is rejected, the Decision / Resolution must be justified, and the lapse of this period without response shall be considered an implied rejection of the application.

The applicant whose request has been rejected may raise a Grievance to the minister within sixty days from the date of being notified of the Decision / Resolution, or from the date the application is considered rejected.

The minister shall decide on the Raising a Grievance within thirty days from the date of submission, and the lapse of this period without rejection shall be considered an implied acceptance of the Raising a Grievance, and the Decision / Resolution on the Raising a Grievance shall be final.

### Article 5

After payment of the prescribed fee, the Department / Directorate issues a license to practice real estate development activities, valid for three years and renewable for one or more similar periods.

The renewal application must be submitted during the month preceding the license expiration date.

The Department / Directorate may accept the application if it is submitted within three months following the expiration of the license, provided that the applicant presents an excuse accepted by the Department / Directorate.

### Article 6 Amended

"A special register shall be created at the Licenses Committee called the 'Real Estate Developers Register' for registering those licensed to practice real estate development activities, and a special register called the 'Register of Real Estate Development Project Management and Maintenance Companies' for registering those licensed to practice these activities."

"A decision from the Chairman / President shall be issued on the Regulation of the two aforementioned registers, specifying their data and the criteria for classifying developers and companies."

### Article 7 Amended

"The developer must comply with the following:

1 - Commence and complete the works at the date specified in the Contract.

2 - Use the funds deposited in the account for their designated purposes.

3 - Follow up and supervise the contractor's execution of the works assigned to him, according to the building permit, and in accordance with the approved technical specifications, through the project consultant and its units.

4 - Prepare and submit the reports requested by the competent authorities, according to the regulations set by the Authority.

5 - Achieve the approved technical specifications in accordance with the general designs of the project.

6 - Deliver the buyers their reserved units, on the date specified in the Contract, and with the approved technical specifications on which the Contract was based, as well as take the necessary procedures to register them in their names.

7 - Not carry out more than three (3) real estate development projects at the same time, except after obtaining the approval of the Chairman / President.

In case of rejection, the developer may Raising a Grievance to the minister within fourteen (14) days from the date of being notified of the rejection decision at his national address or by any means indicating acknowledgment, or after the lapse of fourteen (14) days without a response to his request. The minister shall decide on the grievance within thirty (30) days from the date of its submission. Failure to decide within this period is considered an implicit rejection of the grievance.

8 - Comply with the regulations and conditions set by the Authority when studying the development project for real estate units.

9 - Provide a consultant for the project and a legal office to audit the stages of the project according to the requirements of the Authority.".

### Article 8

The developer may not assign to the subcontractor the execution of some of its works beyond 50% (fifty percent) of the project, except after obtaining the approval of the Department / Directorate, and any agreement to the contrary shall not be considered.

### Article 9 Amended

Without prejudice to the rights of others with the real estate developer, the Department / Directorate may revoke the License in any of the following cases:

1- If the real estate developer loses one of the necessary conditions for granting the License, as stipulated in Article (3) of this Law / Act.

2- If the developer does not commence the implementation of the project within six months from the date of obtaining approval for off-plan sale without an acceptable excuse.

3- If units are offered for sale off-plan through fraud or deception.

4- If the account payments are used for purposes other than those for which they were allocated.

5- If the developer submits a request to the Department / Directorate to cease his activity or to terminate his License.

Anyone whose License has been revoked in the cases stipulated in clauses (1), (2), (3), (4) of this article may Raise a Grievance to the minister within sixty days from the date of being notified of the decision by registered Book or any other method that proves notification.

The provisions and procedures stipulated in Article (4) of this Law / Act shall apply to the aforementioned Raising a Grievance.

The decision to revoke the License shall be published in two daily newspapers, one of which is in English, at the expense of the party whose License has been revoked.

"In all cases, the Authority must notify the relevant entities of the decision to revoke the License, and the owner must appoint another developer within a maximum period of three (3) months from the date of License revocation issued for the developer."

## Chapter Three: Off-Plan Sale Procedures (10-17)

### Article 10

To offer the units outlined on the map for sale, the developer must submit an application to the Department / Directorate, accompanied by the following documents:

1- Testimony of the account opening In accordance with the provisions of this Law.

2- Title deed for the project land, as well as proof of completion of the map-based subdivision, specifying all details of the outlined units.

3- The estimated project budget indicating construction costs and expected revenues, approved by a state-certified auditor.

4- Draft announcement for the sale of units on the map, in accordance with the provision of Article (11) of this Law.

5- Contract template.

6- Architectural designs and engineering plans for the project, approved by the competent authorities.

7- A copy of the Contract concluded between the developer and the subcontractor, if any.

The Department / Directorate issues its approval, including the endorsement of the draft announcement and the Contract template.

In all cases, it is not permissible to amend the draft announcement or the terms and conditions stated in the Contract template.

The same provisions and procedures stipulated in Article (4) of this Law shall apply to the referred application.

### Article 11

The announcement for sale or participation in exhibitions must include, in addition to the approval of the Department / Directorate, the trade name of the developer, their headquarters, a statement of the project's location, its nature, and a precise description, including its total area, the number of floors, the available spaces for its units and their specifications, the specified sale price for each type of unit, the payment method, the location for viewing project and unit samples, account number, bank name, and any other data determined by the Department / Directorate.

### Article 12

The developer must submit an application to the relevant Department / Directorate at the Ministry of Justice for the subdivision of the real estate units of the project, accompanied by all architectural designs and engineering plans, and a certified copy of the building permit.

The said Department / Directorate is responsible for reviewing the application and attached documents, and referring it to the relevant Department / Directorate at the Ministry of Municipality and Urban Planning to take the prescribed actions in this regard.

### Article 13

A preliminary real estate Register shall be established at the competent administration of the Ministry of Justice, in which all data related to off-plan partitioned real estate units, as well as all transactions and real rights pertaining thereto, are to be recorded, including those that establish ownership or any other real right, transfer it, amend it, or terminate it, as well as final court judgments that affirm such rights.

In the event of non-Registration, the said rights shall not be established, transferred, amended, or extinguished, neither between concerned parties nor with respect to others, and unregistered transactions shall have no effect except as personal obligations between the concerned parties.

The Regulation of the Register, the specification of its data, procedures, and the fees for Record therein, shall be issued by a decision from the Minister of Justice.

### Article 14

The competent Department / Directorate at the Ministry of Justice issues a preliminary title deed for each individual real estate unit on the map, based on the preliminary data of the Real Estate Register, detailing all information related to it, with a notation made in the original record of the project land to indicate this.

### Article 15

It is permissible to dispose of real estate units sold off-plan and registered in the preliminary Real Estate Register, by sale, mortgage, or other legal transactions.

Disposal of real estate units subdivided on a plan that has not been approved by the competent authorities is prohibited, and any contract concluded in violation thereof shall be null and void.

### Article 16

The developer must take the procedures for the Registration of the partitioned units, in accordance with the provisions of the Real Estate Register Law referred to, for the project that has been completed, in the Real Estate Register at the competent Department / Directorate at the Ministry of Justice, within sixty days from the date of receiving the Testimony of Completion of Construction. This includes the Registration of the sold units in the names of the purchasers who have fulfilled their contractual obligations.

### Article 17

The area of the real estate Unit registered in the preliminary Real Estate Register is considered the contracted area. If, upon delivery of the Unit to its owner, it is found that its actual area is less than the contracted area, the price of the Unit shall be reduced in proportion to its actual area, based on the price per meter recorded in the preliminary Real Estate Register.

In all cases, any person who has suffered damage, whether due to an increase or decrease in the area of the real estate Unit, may claim compensation if justified.

## Chapter Four: Escrow Account (18-28)

### Article 18

Each real estate development project shall have a separate dedicated account. If the real estate developer undertakes multiple projects, it is required to open a separate account for each of them, based on a request submitted to the Department / Directorate, accompanied by the documents specified.

### Article 19 Amended

"The account is opened pursuant to an agreement between the developer and the bank, which determines the rights and obligations of each party, provided that this agreement includes the following:

1 - The name of the developer and the agreement contract concluded between them and the owner in case the developer is acting on behalf of the owner.

2 - The standards and controls that must be met before any amounts are paid to the developer from the account.

3 - The amount of disbursements to be paid to the developer during the execution of the project, accompanied by a report from the project consultant stating its eligibility, after being approved by the Authority, according to the stages of execution.

4 - Linking the payments deposited by purchasers in the account to the actual completion rates of the construction works, according to a report prepared by the developer and approved by the Authority."

### Article 20 Amended

No withdrawal of any amounts from the account may be made, subject to the terms and conditions of the special agreement for the Real Estate Developer Escrow Account, unless at least 20% (twenty percent) of the construction works in the project have been completed.

The completion ratio of such works shall be estimated by comparing their cost relative to the total estimated cost of the project, excluding the value of the land under development and the project marketing expenses from that cost.

The bank must request the developer to submit a financial statement certified by the Authority of the amounts that have been spent on the project.

### Article 21

With due regard to the Ruling of Articles (20), (22) of this Law, the payment from the account to the developer shall be according to the approved installments, in accordance with the stages of project execution, and based on a report prepared by the project consultant and approved by the relevant Department / Directorate at the Ministry of Municipality and Urban Planning.

### Article 22

With due regard to the Ruling of Article (711) of the Civil Law / Act referred to, the bank shall retain 10% (ten percent) of the total project value, or against a bank guarantee accepted by the Competent Authority, to ensure the rectification of any defects that may appear in its units.

The value of the percentage referred to, or any remaining part of it, shall not be disbursed except after the expiry of the period specified by a resolution from the minister.

### Article 23

The account data and its accounting records are confidential, and neither the real estate developer nor the competent administrative authorities may access them or obtain a copy, except by order or Ruling of the court.

However, concerned parties may review the project’s accounts and obtain a copy with the approval of the Department / Directorate.

### Article 24

The purchaser contracting to buy one of the units offered for sale off-plan must deposit the agreed payments for the reserved unit in the account by the specified deadlines according to the contract. Direct payment to the developer or any of its representatives is not recognized. Lending financial institutions, regardless of their type, are obligated to deposit in the account the amounts borrowed by the real estate developer, secured by the project. In all cases, borrowing against the account or seizing the amounts deposited therein to settle the real estate developer's debts, regardless of their source, is not permitted.

### Article 25

Subject to the provisions of Article (24) of this Law / Act, the developer may borrow against the project, after obtaining approval from the Department / Directorate, according to the following regulations:

1- Not all units offered for sale off-plan must be reserved, according to a Testimony issued by the competent Department / Directorate at the Ministry of Justice.

2- The actual total value of what has been accomplished from the project must be equal to or greater than the total amounts deposited in the account, according to a technical report submitted by the project consultant and approved by the competent Department / Directorate at the Ministry of Municipality and Urban Planning.

3- The value of the loan must not exceed the total value of the units that have not been reserved, pursuant to the instructions issued by the bank.

4- The developer must submit a Testimony from the bank regarding the status of the account.

### Article 26

Subject to the provisions of Article (10) of this Law / Act, it is permissible to grant a license for off-plan sales to anyone who has obtained a loan secured by the project, provided that the actual total value of the completed works of the project is equal to or greater than the value of the loan. This must be proven through the technical Report of the project's consultant, after its approval by the competent Department / Directorate at the Ministry of Municipality and Urban Planning.

### Article 27

In the event of any emergency circumstance that prevents the developer from completing the project, the bank, after coordination with the Department / Directorate, takes the necessary measures to preserve the rights of depositors.

In this case, it is permissible to assign the project to another developer, in accordance with the regulations specified by the bank.

### Article 28

The bank issues instructions and regulatory controls for account management and deposit procedures, as well as any other regulatory provisions related to it.

## Chapter Five: Penalties (29-31)

### Article 29

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, a punishment of imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year, and a fine not exceeding (50,000) fifty thousand riyals, or either of these penalties, shall be imposed on anyone who: 1- Practices real estate development activities without being licensed. 2- Announces or offers units for sale off-plan to the public by any means in violation of the provisions of Article (10) of this Law. 3- Obtains or facilitates obtaining approval for the sale of subdivided units off-plan by means of fraud or deception. 4- Uses, or facilitates the use of, account payments for purposes other than those for which they are allocated. 5- Obtains, or facilitates obtaining, a loan secured by the project without the approval of the Department / Directorate. 6- Fails to deliver the units to the buyers at the time specified in the Contract without a valid excuse, or delivers units that do not conform to the technical specifications agreed upon in the Contract, or for which building permits were issued based on, taking into account the ruling of Article (17) of this Law.

### Article 30

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, a fine not exceeding (200,000) two hundred thousand riyals shall be imposed on the developer who does not begin implementation of the project within six months from the date of obtaining approval for off-plan sales without an acceptable excuse.

### Article 31

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, a fine not exceeding one hundred thousand (100,000) riyals shall be imposed on anyone who violates any provision of Articles (7/ Item 3, 4, 5), (16) of this Law / Act.

## Chapter Six: Final Provisions (32-40)

### Article 32

One or more Committees shall be established at the ministry called the “Real Estate Development Dispute Settlement Committee,” chaired by a judge at the rank of President in the Primary Court selected by the Supreme Judiciary Council, and two other members chosen by the minister, one of whom shall be from the Ministry of Justice. The formation of the Committee and the determination of its remuneration shall be decided by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers within three months from the date of entry into force of the provisions of this Law / Act.

The Committee shall have a secretary, assisted by a sufficient number of employees, who shall be appointed and their remuneration determined by a decision from the minister. The secretariat shall be responsible for monitoring and implementing the decisions issued by the Committee and all matters related to its affairs.

### Article 33

The Committee is responsible for the urgent adjudication of all disputes arising from real estate development In accordance with the provisions of this Law.

The procedures for considering applications, the rules and procedures to be followed before the Committee, the mechanism for implementing its decisions, and the fees to be collected in this regard shall be determined by a decision from the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the minister.

### Article 34

The decisions issued by the Committee shall have the enforceable power stipulated in Article (362) of the Civil and Commercial Procedures Law.

### Article 35

The concerned parties may initiate a Legal Challenge / Appeal against the decisions issued by the Committee before the Civil Circuit of the Appeal to Higher Court within fifteen days, starting from the date the Committee’s decision is issued if it is issued in the presence of the parties, or from the day following the notification if the decision was issued in their absence.

### Article 36

The decisions of the Committee are issued with expedited enforcement, and only the competent Civil Circuit of the Appeal to Higher Court may order the suspension of their execution.

### Article 37

Those engaged in construction and real estate development activities who offer units for sale off-plan at the time this Law / Act comes into effect must regularize their status in accordance with its provisions within six months from the date it comes into effect. The minister may extend the aforementioned deadline for a similar period or periods.

### Article 38 Amended

"Employees of the Authority who are granted the Capacity of Law enforcement Officer by a decision of the Attorney General in agreement with the Minister, shall have the authority to detect and document the Crimes and violations committed in contravention of the provisions of this Law / Act and the decisions issued in implementation thereof."

### Article 39 Amended

"The minister and the Minister of Justice, each within their jurisdiction, and after coordination between them, shall issue the necessary decisions to implement the provisions of this Law / Act."

### Article 40

All competent authorities, each within its own jurisdiction, shall implement this Law / Act. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
