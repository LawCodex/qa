# Law No. (24) of 2015 Issuing the Tenders and Bids Regulation Law

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And Law / Act of Regulating Tenders and Bids issued by Law / Act No. (26) of 2005, and its amendments,

And Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (21) of 2014 regarding the organizational structure of the Ministry of Finance,

And upon the proposal of the Minister of Finance,

And on Draft Law submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

And after taking the opinion of the Shura Council,

We have enacted the following Law / Act:

## الفصل الأول: تعاريف (1)

### Article 1

For the application of the provisions of this Law, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings indicated opposite each of them, unless the context requires otherwise:

Ministry: Ministry of Finance.

Minister: Minister of Finance.

Department / Directorate: the Department / Directorate concerned with the Regulating of Government Procurement at the Ministry.

Government entity: the competent Ministry or Body / Organ or Authority or public institution, as the case may be.

Chairman / President: the competent minister or Chairman / President of the governmental Body / Organ or Chairman / President of the Authority or public institution, as the case may be.

Committee: the Tenders and Bids Committee referred to in Article (9) of this Law / Act.

Competent department: the administrative Unit concerned at the government entity.

Items: all materials, goods, equipment, machines and other commodities and other necessary supplies, and similar.

Works contracting: construction, building and other engineering contracting works, installations, manufacturing, operation, maintenance, repair and similar.

Services: technical, training and consulting works, hiring of equipment and machinery, as well as cleaning, insurance, guarding, transportation services, and similar.

Value for price: an assessment made by the Committee between the value or the expected return from the required items, works or services versus the price borne by the government entity.

Emergency case: any serious and unexpected threat to safety or security, or any serious breach indicating potential loss of life or property or production, or risk of environmental pollution, which cannot be dealt with through regular tender procedures.

Urgent case: any situation where completing the works or providing the services or items in a short time is essential and necessary to ensure the safety and efficiency of the workflow at the government entity.

Executive Regulation: the Executive Regulation of this Law / Act.

## Chapter Two: Methods of Contracting and Its Provisions (2-8)

### Article 2

Contracting for the purchase of goods, construction works, or services shall be conducted through a public tender.

By way of exception, a decision by the Chairman, based on a justified recommendation from the Committee, may permit contracting through one of the following methods:

1- Two-stage tender.

2- Limited tender.

3- Practice.

4- Competition.

5- Direct agreement.

After the tender has been announced and bids have been submitted, it is not permitted to convert it to a practice or direct agreement.

All provisions regulating the public tender shall apply to the two-stage tender, limited tender, practice, competition, and direct agreement, except where a specific provision is made for each.

In all cases, contracting shall be carried out in accordance with the rules and procedures set forth in this Law and the regulation.

It is permissible, with the approval of the Chairman of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, and based on a proposal from the minister, to exempt a certain transaction, service, or supply of a particular item from being subject to the provisions of this Law.

### Article 3

The public tender is the set of procedures announced according to the conditions specified in this Law / Act and the regulation, with the aim of selecting the bidder who submits the best offer.

The public tender is subject to the principles of justice, transparency, equality, equal opportunities, freedom of competition, and value for money.

The announcement of the public tender shall be made in accordance with the rules and procedures specified by the regulation.

### Article 4

The contracting shall be conducted by means of a two-stage tendering process in cases where the government entity is unable to prepare detailed Specifications for the items, construction works, or services, in order to obtain the solutions that best meet the government entity’s needs, in accordance with the regulations and procedures specified by the bylaws.

### Article 5

Contracting is done through a limited tender in cases where the nature of the matter requires restricting participation to specific suppliers, contractors, service providers, consultants, or technicians listed in the rosters prepared by the government entity for this purpose and approved by the Committee, or those who have previously qualified to participate in the tender, provided they possess technical and financial competence and good reputation.

The regulations specify the rules and criteria for the aforementioned qualification, as well as the procedures and provisions for inviting participation in the limited tender.

### Article 6

Contracting shall be conducted by way of negotiation in urgent cases that do not permit the holding of a tender, to select from at least three suppliers, contractors, or service providers who possess the required items, or are capable of executing the required works or performing the required services, all in accordance with the rules and procedures prescribed by the regulations.

### Article 7

The contracting shall be conducted by means of a competition announced for the purpose of conducting studies or preparing designs, plans, models, or other artistic works necessary for a specific project, provided that the objectives, scope, and specifications of the project are determined prior to the announcement, in accordance with the controls and procedures specified by the regulations.

The provisions related to temporary and final insurance stipulated in this Law / Act shall not apply to the competition.

### Article 8

The provisions related to temporary insurance stipulated in this Law / Act do not apply to direct practice and agreement.

### Article 8 Amended

The contract is made through direct agreement with a supplier, contractor, consultant, or service provider to purchase specific items, execute works, or provide defined services. This applies in emergency or urgent cases where tender or competitive procedures cannot be followed, or due to the nature and uniqueness of the required items, works, or services, in accordance with the controls and procedures specified by the regulations.

## Chapter Three: Tenders and Bids Committee (9)

### Article 9 Amended

A Committee shall be established within the government entity, specialized in conducting Tenders and Bids, practices, competitions, and direct agreements. It shall be formed of a number of members from the government entity’s employees, not less than five and not more than seven, including the Chairperson and their deputy. It is permissible to include in the formation of the Committee a member from outside the government entity.

It is permissible, with the approval of the Chairman / President of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal by the Chairman / President, and a justified recommendation from the minister, to establish more than one Committee within the government entity.

It is a condition for the validity of the Committee’s meetings that a representative from both the State Audit Bureau and administration be present as an observer member.

The regulation shall specify the other competencies of the Committee, its working system, procedures to be followed before it, the mechanism for approving its recommendations, and the rules of Membership therein.

A decision shall be issued by the Chairman / President to nominate the members of the Committee.

A decision shall be issued by the minister to determine the rewards for the members of the Committee and the secretariat, and the rules of eligibility and disbursement thereof.

It is permissible, by a decision of the Chairman / President of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal by the minister, to establish one or more Committees, each specialized in conducting Tenders and Bids, practices, competitions, and direct agreements for more than one government entity. In this case, the Committee shall be formed of a number of members from the employees of these entities, under the same rules stipulated in this article.

## Chapter Four: Tendering and Submission of Bids (10-12)

### Article 10

The tendering shall be within the actual needs estimated by the government entity, and within the allocated Financial Appropriations, and based on precise and detailed technical Specifications for the items, works, or services required.

It is permissible to conclude supply and periodic service contracts for a period exceeding the financial year, provided that this does not result in an increase in commitments in any of the subsequent financial years beyond what is stipulated in the financial year in which the contract was concluded.

The regulations specify the controls and procedures related to tendering, preparing its documents, and submitting bids, whether by regular or electronic means, as well as those related to determining the estimated value of the tender.

### Article 11

The submitted bids remain valid for the period specified in the tender announcement, and the bidder may not withdraw their bid during its validity period; otherwise, they shall be considered withdrawn.

Similarly, the bidder to whom the award has been made will also be considered withdrawn if they abstain from providing the Performance Bond and signing the Contract within the period specified by the government entity.

### Article 12

By a decision of the Chairman / President, based on the recommendation of the Committee, one or more of the following penalties may be imposed on the withdrawing bidder, after hearing their statements before the Committee or their failure to appear before it despite being notified:

1- Confiscation of the Tender Bond.

2- Confiscation of the Performance Bond.

3- Suspension of dealing with the bidder for a specific period or permanently, after obtaining the opinion of the Department / Directorate, with it being notified of the decision issued in this regard.

By a decision of the Chairman / President, based on the recommendation of the Committee, in the event of withdrawal by the successful bidder, the tender may be cancelled and re-tendered, or awarded to the next preferred bidder.

The concerned party has the right of Raising a Grievance against the aforementioned decisions to the Chairman / President, in accordance with the rules and procedures specified in the regulations.

In all cases, taking any of the foregoing measures does not prejudice the government agency's right to pursue any of its rights against the withdrawing bidder or to claim compensation if necessary.

## Chapter Five: Insurance (13-14)

### Article 13 Amended

A provisional security must be submitted with each bid, and the successful bidder must provide a final security. The regulations specify the rules, procedures, and deadlines related to the provisional and final securities, as well as their value, method of payment and refund, and cases of exemption.

The government entity may retain a percentage of the total value of certain contracts to ensure their execution, in accordance with the rules and procedures specified in the regulations.

### Article 14

By a decision of the Chairman / President, based on an undertaking from Qatar Development Bank, small and medium-sized enterprises may be exempted, in whole or in part, from providing the Tender Bond, final guarantee, and advance payment guarantee.

## Chapter Six: Evaluation and Decision of Bids (15-17)

### Article 15

The opening of envelopes, the evaluation of submitted bids from both technical and financial perspectives, and their adjudication shall be carried out in accordance with the rules and procedures specified by the regulations.

### Article 16 Amended

The Committee may not negotiate with bidders regarding the modification of their bids after the envelopes have been opened, except in the following cases:

1 - If the bids' validity period expires due to exceptional circumstances, for the purpose of extending this period.

2 - If the best bid is accompanied by reservations, in order for the bidder to waive his reservations; if he refuses, the Committee may negotiate with the bidder next in order of preference.

3 - If the best bid exceeds the estimated value, in order to lower its price, whether total or individual, to the estimated value or the limit accepted by the Committee; if he refuses, the Committee may negotiate with the bidder next in order of preference.

If the limit accepted by the Committee is higher than the estimated value, it is required to have the financial allocation available to cover the difference.

4 - If the price of the best bid is within the estimated value, and market prices drop before awarding the tender, in order to lower its price to the limit accepted by the Committee; if he refuses, it is permissible to negotiate with the bidder next in order of preference, or cancel the tender.

### Article 17 (Repeated)

Each bidder has the right to Raising a Grievance against the award decision, before the Committee for dispute resolution, within five working days from the day following the date of announcing the award decision on the unified electronic website for State purchases.

The Committee is responsible for deciding on the Raising a Grievance within five working days from the date its secretariat receives the request for resolution.

The relevant department may not proceed with the award procedures except after this period ends without any Raising a Grievance, or a decision is issued by the Committee for dispute resolution confirming the validity of the award decision.

In all cases, the bidder to whom the award is made may not start implementing the required works before entering into the Contract, and they are not entitled to claim any compensation if they proceed otherwise, even if the implementation is based on a request from the government entity.

### Article 17

The tender is canceled before it is decided upon, by a decision of the Chairman / President, if it is no longer needed or if public interest requires it.

It is permissible, by a justified decision from the Chairman / President, based on a recommendation from the Committee, to cancel the tender and relaunch it in the following cases:

1- If only a single bid is submitted, or if only one bid remains after the excluded bids.

2- If the value of the best bid exceeds the estimated value.

3- If all or most bids are accompanied by reservations.

## Chapter Seven: Conclusion and Execution of the Contract (18-25)

### Article 18 Amended

The President, based on the recommendation of the Committee and in accordance with the requirements of the public interest, may modify the quantities or scope of the contracts concluded In accordance with the provisions of this Law and the regulation, by increase or decrease under the same terms and prices, without the contractor with the government entity having the right to claim any compensation for that, in accordance with the controls and within the limits specified by the regulation.

It is also permissible to modify the scope of works or services in accordance with the controls specified by the regulation.

### Article 19

It is permissible, with the approval of the Chairman / President, based on a request from the relevant Department / Directorate, to disburse advance payments on account to the contractor with the government entity, in exchange for an approved bank guarantee of the same value and currency or their equivalent, not subject to any conditions and valid until the actual due date of those amounts, in accordance with the regulations and procedures specified by the bylaw.

### Article 20

The contractor may not Assign the Contract or any amounts due to him, in whole or in part, to others, except after the approval of the Chairman / President.

In the case of Assign the Contract, the contractor remains jointly liable with the assignee for the execution of the Contract, and the acceptance of the contractor's assignment of the amounts due to him does not affect the rights of the government entity against him.

In the event of the contractor's death, his heirs may continue to execute the Contract, provided that the heirs have sufficient technical and financial guarantees, unless the personality of the deceased was a consideration in concluding the Contract.

### Article 21

The contractor must execute the Contract within the specified time. If the contractor fails to do so, the government entity may, for reasons of public interest, grant an additional period to complete the execution, provided a Fine is imposed for the period of delay, according to the controls, principles, and percentages determined by the regulations.

The contractor may be exempted from the Fine or Part of it, by a decision of the Chairman, after taking the opinion of the Committee, if it is proven that the delay was due to reasons beyond their control.

The Chairman, based on a reasoned recommendation from the Committee and after Reconnaissance of the opinion of the competent Department / Directorate, may exempt the contractor from the Fine if no harm resulted from the delay.

Imposing the Fine does not affect the right of the government entity to claim the full Compensation / Indemnity due for damages suffered as a result of the delay.

### Article 22 Amended

If the contractor breaches any of their contractual obligations and does not remedy the impact within the period specified by the government entity, the latter, after notifying them at their national address or by any other means that ensures awareness, has the right to take one of the following two actions, in accordance with the requirements of the public interest:

1 - Contract Rescission.

2 - Execution at the contractor's expense under the same terms and specifications agreed upon.

In all cases, the rescission or execution at the expense is based on a decision by the Chairman / President based on the recommendation of the Committee.

### Article 23

Without prejudice to any of the liabilities prescribed by law, the Contract shall be considered terminated in either of the following cases:

1- If it is proven that the contractor has used fraud or manipulation in the execution of the Contract or in obtaining it.

2- If it is proven that the contractor has attempted, directly or indirectly, personally or through others, to bribe any State employee or to collude with him to the detriment of the contracting government entity.

### Article 24 Amended

In all cases of Contract Rescission or its execution at the expense of the contractor, the Performance Bond becomes the right of the government entity. The government entity also has the right to deduct any fines or losses incurred from any amounts due or to become due to the contractor with it or with any other government entity, without the need to take any further action.

With the approval of the Prime Minister, based on a justified recommendation from the minister and after the opinion of the Committee is sought, and except in cases where the Contract is considered rescinded according to the provision of Article (23) of this Law, it is permissible to exempt the contractor from all or some of the penalties resulting from cases of rescission or execution at expense.

With the approval of the minister, based on the proposal of the Department / Directorate, it is permissible to suspend dealings with the bidder or contractor in all government entities for a specified period or permanently in the following cases:

1 - If dealings with him are suspended in more than one government entity due to his withdrawal in accordance with the provisions of Article (11) of this Law.

2 - If dealings with him are suspended in any government entity, according to any of the cases stipulated in Articles (22), (23) of this Law.

### Article 25

The government entity has the right to Contract Termination in any of the following cases:

1- Death of the contractor when their personality is a consideration in the Contract.

2- Bankruptcy or insolvency of the contractor.

3- Dissolution of the contracting company or institution in accordance with the provisions of the Law / Act.

4- If the public interest so requires, by a justified decision from the Chairman / President, based on the recommendation of the Committee.

## Chapter Eight: Auctions (26-30)

### Article 26 Amended

The sale and lease of items and the lease of real estate shall be conducted through a public auction or sealed bids, after the approval of the President, and in accordance with the controls and procedures specified by the regulations.

It is permissible, by a reasoned decision from the President, based on the recommendation of the Committee, to contract by way of negotiation or direct agreement, in accordance with the controls specified by the regulations.

The sale of items shall be in the following cases:

1- If they are unfit for use.

2- If they are likely to be damaged.

3- If their use has become obsolete.

4- If they are surplus to requirements.

5- If their storage cost exceeds their value.

6- If they are damaged or beyond repair, or if their repair is not economically viable.

And subject to the Ruling of Article (29/ second Paragraph) of this Law, the sale or lease shall be in accordance with the basic value determined by the inspection and valuation Committee formed in the ministry for this purpose, and according to the controls and procedures specified by the regulations.

A decision shall be issued by the minister regarding the formation of the aforementioned Committee, its work system, and the determination of its remuneration.

As an exception to the provisions of this article, and with the approval of the Chairman of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal by the minister, auctions may be conducted for all or some government entities through one or more companies, provided that the approval includes a statement of the mechanism for their selection and the method of their operation.

### Article 27

The successful bidder must pay the full value of the items or the due installment of the rental value, as applicable, in accordance with the procedures specified by the regulation.

If the successful bidder delays payment without an acceptable excuse, the president may, based on the Committee's recommendation, cancel the awarding, and confiscate the Tender Bond or the final guarantee as applicable, without prejudice to the government entity's right to claim any other compensation.

### Article 28

A government entity may sell the items without adhering to the base value, if it is impossible to sell them despite having previously offered them in a public auction, or if their base value is less than the cost of offering.

In this case, the government entity, after the minister's approval, may make a Donation of the aforementioned items to any entity or destroy them.

### Article 29 Amended

It is permissible, by a justified decision from the Chairman / President, to cancel the auction after it has been announced and before a recommendation has been made regarding it, if it is no longer needed permanently, or if public interest requires it.

It is also permissible for the committee to cancel the auction and re-offer it in any of the following cases:

1- If only one bid fulfilling the requirements is submitted for the auction.

2- If the result of the auction does not reach the basic value.

3- If the bidder to whom the bid was awarded withdraws, or does not pay the value of the items, or the due installment of the lease value, or refrains from Contract Signing within the specified period.

In the latter case, the committee may award the auction to the next highest bidder in price or lease value, taking into account the basic value and the validity period of the bid.

### Article 30

Where no specific provision is made in this chapter or the regulations, all rules governing public tender shall apply to the auction.

## Chapter Nine: General Provisions (31-38)

### Article 31

Without prejudice to the principle of value for money, government entities may contract with each other without adhering to the procedures stipulated in this Law and the regulation.

They may also represent each other in procuring their needs, provided that the Chairman / President in each entity grants approval.

### Article 32

The president may delegate any of his authorities, stipulated in this Law / Act and the regulation, to the immediate subordinate in the government entity or to the next in line, and to no others.

### Article 33

Government employees who undertake Functions and Competencies related to the contracts concluded by their entity are prohibited from having any direct or indirect interest in those contracts. Furthermore, none of them may be a partner of any of the bidders or auctioneers, nor act as their agent, nor be a member of their board of directors or an employee thereof. In such cases, they must disclose any interest they may have that might overlap with the nature of their aforementioned Functions and Competencies.

An exception is made, in cases of necessity, for the purchase of books authored by them or for assigning them to carry out artistic tasks related to relevant business activities, provided that they do not participate in any manner in the purchasing or assignment procedures, and that the required work does not fall within the employee’s job Functions and Competencies nor is considered an extension thereof.

### Article 34

The parties to the Contract may, in case of a dispute arising from the Contract, agree to settle it through Arbitration, with the approval of the Minister, based on a proposal from the Chairman / President, with each party undertaking to continue fulfilling its obligations arising from the Contract.

### Article 35 Amended

The government entity must announce the final Decisions issued In accordance with the provisions of this Law and the regulation, including Decisions related to the acceptance, exclusion, awarding of bids, or cancellation of the tender. The announcement shall be made on the unified electronic website for State purchases within two working days from the date the Decision is approved by the Committee or Chairman / President, as the case may be, in accordance with the guidelines specified by the regulation.

### Article 36 Amended

The regulation defines the rules, controls, and procedures for classifying contractors, suppliers, and service providers into various categories and specializations, according to their financial and technical capabilities, experience, competence, and previous work, as well as evaluating the performance of contractors, suppliers, and service providers.

The administration may announce the results of the classification and evaluation of contractors, suppliers, and service providers on the unified electronic website for the State's procurement.

### Article 36 (Duplicate 1)

The participation of companies and entities established and registered with the Free Zones Authority and the Qatar Financial Centre, or with other bodies legally authorized to do so, in Tenders and Bids, practices, competitions, and direct agreements, shall be in accordance with the terms and regulations issued by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on the proposal of the minister.

### Article 36 (Duplicate)

It is permissible, by a decision from the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the minister, to specify the types that government entities are obliged to provide from national products.

### Article 36 (Repeated)

Small, medium, and micro enterprises, as well as environmentally friendly projects, may be granted benefits, exemptions, and facilities, in accordance with the provisions of the regulations.

### Article 37

One or more Committees shall be established at the Ministry, called the "Dispute Resolution Committee", formed under the chairmanship of a judge at the rank of President of the Court of First Instance selected by the Supreme Judiciary Council, and with the membership of two experts nominated by the Minister.

The appointment of the chairman and members of the Committee and the determination of their remuneration shall be made by a Decision / Resolution of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, which shall also specify the procedures for considering applications, the rules and procedures to be followed before the Committee, the mechanism for implementing its Decisions / Resolutions, and the fees to be collected for applications submitted to it.

The Committee shall have a secretariat from the employees of the Department / Directorate, whose secondment, duties and remuneration shall be decided by a Decision / Resolution of the Minister.

### Article 38 Amended

The Dispute Resolution Committee is responsible for adjudicating, by a reasoned Decision / Resolution, on an expedited basis, all administrative disputes preceding the Contract, arising from the application of the provisions of this Law / Act and the regulation. The Committee may suspend the tender procedures until the Raising a Grievance is resolved.

Concerned parties may file a Legal Challenge / Appeal against the Decision / Resolution issued by the aforementioned Committee before the competent Administrative Circuit at the Court of Appeal to Higher Court. The Legal Challenge / Appeal does not result in suspending the execution of the Decision / Resolution unless the competent Division decides otherwise.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
