# Law No. (5) of 2015 concerning commercial, industrial, and similar public establishments and street vendors

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

after reviewing the Constitution,

and Decree Law No. (9) of 1969 regarding street vendors, as amended by Law No. (18) of 1987,

and Law No. (3) of 1975 regarding similar commercial, industrial, and public shops, and the laws amending it,

and upon the proposal of the Minister of Economy and Commerce,

and upon the Draft Law submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

and after seeking the opinion of the Shura Council,

we have enacted the following Law:

## Chapter One: Definitions (1)

### Article 1 Amended

Ministry: Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

Minister: Minister of Commerce and Industry.

Competent administrative Department: the Competent administrative Unit at the Ministry.

Competent Authority: the Ministry or the Body / Organ or the Authority or the public institution, as the case may be.

Peddler: Anyone who moves from one place to another, or in any road or public place, to sell goods or merchandise, or display them for sale or rent, or practice a craft or trade, or provide a service to the public for a fee. Anyone who sells their agricultural products is not considered a peddler.

## Chapter Two: Scope of application of the Law / Act (2)

### Article 2

The provisions of this Law / Act apply to the following establishments:

1- Commercial and industrial establishments, whether they operate in commercial activity, industrial activity, or both.

2- Similar public establishments, including restaurants, cafés, hotels, clubs, and similar venues.

3- Establishments practicing the liberal professions, including clinics, offices, and similar entities.

4- Establishments that are disturbing to comfort, harmful to health, or dangerous.

This applies whether these establishments are located on land or any fixed place, or on any means of land or sea transportation.

The schedules detailing each type of establishment subject to the provisions of this Law / Act shall be issued by a decision of the Minister.

The provisions of this Law / Act also apply to street vendors, according to the terms and conditions specified therein.

## Chapter Three: Commercial, Industrial and Similar Public Premises (3-20)

### Chapter One: Licenses (3-15)

#### Article 3

No premises subject to the provisions of this Law / Act may be opened or operated except with a license from the competent Department / Directorate.

No additional activity may be carried out, nor may any modification be made to the licensed premises or the location changed, except with the approval of the competent Department / Directorate.

#### Article 4

The conditions and regulations for granting Licenses to open stores subject to the provisions of this Law / Act, in residential areas, and the activities permitted to be carried out, shall be determined by a decision from the minister.

#### Article 5 Amended

The general and specific requirements that are determined by a decision of the minister, in coordination with the competent authorities, must be met in establishments subject to the provisions of this Law / Act.

The minister may exempt some of these requirements if the public interest so requires.

"The special requirements for non-medical public health, which must be met in some establishments subject to the provisions of this Law / Act, are determined by a decision of the Minister of Municipality and Environment, in coordination with the minister. The applicant for the license or the licensee, as the case may be, must comply with those requirements."

#### Article 6

The application for obtaining the license shall be submitted by the concerned party to the Department / Directorate, using the form prepared by the Department / Directorate for this purpose, accompanied by the documents and information requested by the Department / Directorate.

#### Article 7

The competent Department / Directorate shall decide on the license application and notify the applicant of its Decision / Resolution on the same day of submission, provided that the application meets the documents and data required by the competent Department / Directorate.

The applicant is obliged to fulfill all general and specific requirements according to the type of licensed activity, and not to practice the activity except after obtaining all approvals required by the Law / Act from the competent authorities, and must submit these approvals upon renewal of the license.

If the Decision / Resolution is issued with a rejection, it must be reasoned. The expiration of the specified period for deciding on the application without a response shall be considered an implicit rejection.

The person whose application was rejected may submit Raising a Grievance to the Minister within thirty days from the expiration of the specified period for deciding on the license application. The Minister shall decide on the Raising a Grievance within fifteen days from the date of its submission. The expiration of this period without a response to Raising a Grievance shall be considered an implicit rejection. The Minister's Decision / Resolution on Raising a Grievance shall be final.

#### Article 8 Amended

The duration of the license shall be determined by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the Minister, according to the type of establishment being licensed, and this period shall be renewable for one or more similar periods after payment of the prescribed fee. The competent Department / Directorate, at the request of the concerned party, shall renew the license accordingly.

The application for renewal of the license must be submitted at least thirty days before its expiration. In case of delay in renewing the license after its expiration, a Fine for delay of (500) five hundred riyals shall be charged for each month of delay, up to a maximum of three months, otherwise the license shall be considered canceled.

The competent Department / Directorate may, based on the reasons provided by the licensee, extend the mentioned three-month period for one or more similar periods, and in this case, the Fine stipulated in the previous paragraph shall apply to the entire period during which the license was not renewed.

#### Article 9

By way of exception to the provisions of the previous article, temporary licenses may be granted for a period of less than one year for certain places or parts of places where commercial, industrial, or similar public activities are conducted, or for conducting commercial activities in homes, in accordance with the terms, conditions, and procedures specified by a decision issued by the Minister.

#### Article 10

The conditions and regulations for practicing any commercial, industrial, or similar public activities through electronic websites, which do not require a physical location, shall be specified by a decision issued by the Minister.

#### Article 11

The licensee must display the license in a visible location and present it to staff of the relevant authorities upon request.

#### Article 12

Subject to the provisions of Law / Act No. (1) of 2012 on the Regulation of and oversight of placing advertisements, the licensee must place an identification sign on the front of the licensed premises, in accordance with the controls determined by the competent Department / Directorate. The licensee is obliged to remove this sign in the event of cancellation of the license or cessation of activity.

#### Article 13

A decision shall be issued بتنظيم the working hours in establishments subject to the provisions of this Law / Act, by the Minister, based on the proposal of the competent Department / Directorate.

And the establishments specified by a decision of the Minister, based on the proposal of the competent Department / Directorate, shall close their doors and cease all activities during Friday prayers for a period of one and a half hours starting from the first call to prayer.

#### Article 14

It is not permitted to advertise by any means for the shops subject to the provisions of this Law / Act, unless the licensee provides the advertising entity with a valid copy of the shop license.

#### Article 15

The store manager or supervisor responsible for its management, together with the licensee, shall be liable for any violation of the provisions of this Law / Act and the implementing decisions.

### Chapter Two: License Assignment, Transfer, and Revocation (16-20)

#### Article 16

The licensee may not assign the license, except after obtaining written approval from the Department / Directorate concerned.

#### Article 17

If the licensee dies, the heirs must notify the competent Department / Directorate within ninety days from the date of death, stating their names and places of residence, and the person chosen as Deputy / Assistant on their behalf to temporarily manage the establishment until the License is transferred. This Deputy / Assistant shall be responsible for implementing the provisions of this Law / Act and the decisions issued under it.

The Deputy / Assistant must take the necessary steps to transfer the License of the establishment in the name of the heirs, one of them, or another party within ninety days from the expiry of the period specified in the previous paragraph. This period may be extended for a similar term with the approval of the competent Department / Directorate based on the justification provided by the Deputy / Assistant; otherwise, the License shall be considered canceled.

#### Article 18

The Department / Directorate concerned, in coordination with the relevant authorities, may issue a justified Decision / Resolution to close the shop for a period not exceeding fifteen days if any of the general or specific requirements referred to in Article (5) of this Law / Act are violated.

The violator shall be notified to remove the causes of the violation within the period specified in the previous paragraph. If the causes of the violation are not removed, the closure shall continue for another similar period or periods as determined by the Department / Directorate concerned.

The interested party may Raise a Grievance against this Decision / Resolution to the Minister within ten days from the date of notification, and the Minister shall decide on the grievance within fifteen days from its submission. If this period elapses without a response to the grievance, it shall be considered an implied rejection.

#### Article 19

The license shall be revoked, by a decision of the competent Department / Directorate, in the following cases:

1- If the licensee notifies the competent Department / Directorate of the cessation of activity at the premises.

2- If the licensee, without the approval of the competent Department / Directorate, makes modifications to the premises or carries out any activity in the premises contrary to the license.

3- If the premises become inoperable or fail to meet the conditions stated in the license.

4- If there is an imminent danger to Public Health or Public Security, or if acts contrary to public morals or public order occur in the premises.

5- If the new owner does not notify the competent Department / Directorate of the transfer of ownership of the premises to him, In accordance with the provisions of this Law and the decisions issued in implementation thereof.

6- If a decision or Final Judgment is issued to revoke the license.

7- If the premises cease to operate for one hundred and twenty days without reasonable justification acceptable to the competent Department / Directorate.

8- If the property where the premises is located is demolished and is not moved to a new location, with the approval of the competent Department / Directorate, within one hundred and twenty days from the date of demolition.

9- If it is proven that the licensee obtained the license based on false or fictitious information.

#### Article 20

The Minister, based on the proposal of the competent Department / Directorate, may revoke the license in cases other than those stipulated in the previous article, if the public interest so requires.

The concerned party has the right to Raising a Grievance to the Minister against the revocation decision within thirty days from the date of being notified thereof.

The same provisions stipulated in Article (7) of this Law / Act shall apply to the Raising a Grievance.

## Chapter Four: Street Vendors (21-25)

### Article 21

It is not permitted to practice the activity of a street vendor except after obtaining a License from the competent Department / Directorate.

The rules and requirements necessary for the License are determined by a decision from the minister.

### Article 22

The provisions of Articles (6), (7), (8), and (19) of this Law / Act shall apply to the license for practicing the activity of itinerant vendor, in accordance with the nature of the license.

### Article 23

The licensed street vendor must carry the license and display the identification card given to him by the Department / Directorate concerned in a visible manner during the practice of his activity. He must present the license to any employee of the competent authorities upon request and notify the Department / Directorate concerned in case of loss or damage of the license or identification card.

He must also return the identification card to the Department / Directorate concerned in case the license is cancelled or he stops practicing his activity.

### Article 24

It is prohibited for the street vendor to do the following:

1- Pursue the public to display his product, goods, conduct his activity, or provide his service.

2- Practice his activity near schools, educational centers, hospitals, or Health Centers according to what is determined by the competent Department / Directorate, or in places where he is not licensed to operate, or where the minister or the Police forbid his presence, or next to stores that engage in similar licensed activities, or inside means of transportation.

3- Sell or display fireworks, and other toys whose sale is prohibited by Law / Act.

4- Practice any of the licensed activities during the time referred to in Article (13/ Paragraph Two) of this Law / Act.

5- Advertise his activity by calling out, using bells or megaphones, or any other method that causes disturbance, or conduct his activity outside the times specified for that.

### Article 25

The License of the street vendor shall be canceled by a Decision from the competent Department / Directorate in the following cases:

1- If any of the required conditions for obtaining the License are no longer met.

2- If false documents or information are provided to obtain the License.

3- If an activity is practiced contrary to the granted License.

4- If the License or the identification card obtained is allowed to be used by another person.

The concerned party has the right to submit a Grievance against this Decision to the minister within ten days from the date of notification, and the minister shall decide on the Grievance within fifteen days from the date of its submission. The expiration of this period without a response to the Grievance shall be considered an implicit rejection thereof.

## Chapter Five: Penalties (26-29)

### Article 26

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, whoever violates any of the provisions of Articles (3 / Paragraph One), (7 / Paragraph Two) of this Law / Act shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year, a fine not exceeding fifty thousand (50,000) riyals, or either of these penalties.

### Article 27 Amended

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, a fine not exceeding (10,000) ten thousand riyals shall be imposed on anyone who violates any of the provisions of Articles (3/Paragraph Two), (5/Paragraph Three), (11), (12), (13), (14), (16), (21), (23), (24) of this Law / Act.

### Article 28

The court may, in addition to the Judgment / Ruling of the prescribed penalty in accordance with the previous two articles, order the closure of the establishment for a period not exceeding one year or the cancellation of the license, and the Judgment / Ruling shall be published in two daily newspapers at the expense of the convicted person.

### Article 29 Amended

The Minister and the Minister of Municipality and Environment, each within their jurisdiction, or whomever either of them delegates, may settle in Crimes committed in violation of the provisions of this Law, before initiating Legal Action or during its consideration and before issuing a final judgment, in exchange for paying half of the maximum fine and removing the causes of the violation.

## Chapter Six: General Provisions (30-34)

### Article 30 Amended

"Employees of the Ministry and the Ministry of Municipality and Environment, each within their respective jurisdiction, who are granted the Capacity of Law enforcement Officer by a decision from the Attorney General in agreement with the Minister and the Minister of Municipality and Environment, as the case may be, shall have the power to detect and prove Crimes committed in violation of the provisions of this Law / Act."

### Article 31

All establishments subject to the provisions of this Law / Act and street vendors who are practicing their activity at the date of entry into force of this Law / Act must regularize their status in accordance with its provisions within six months from the date it comes into effect.

It is permissible, by a decision of the minister, based on a proposal from the competent Department / Directorate, to extend this period for one or more similar terms.

### Article 32

The Minister issues the necessary decisions to implement the provisions of this Law / Act. Until these decisions are issued, the regulations and decisions currently in force shall continue to be applied, provided that they do not conflict with its provisions.

### Article 33

Decree 34 No. (9) of 1969, and Law / Act No. (3) of 1975, referred to above, are hereby repealed, as well as All provisions contrary to this Law shall be repealed.

### Article 34

All relevant entities, each in its own domain, must implement this Law / Act. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
