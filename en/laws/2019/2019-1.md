# Law No. (1) of 2019 on the Regulation of Non-Qatari Capital Investment in Economic Activity

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

after reviewing the Constitution,

and Law No. (13) of 2000 on the Regulation of the Investment of Non-Qatari Capital in Economic Activity, and its amending laws,

and Law No. (8) of 2002 concerning the Regulation of the Work of Commercial Agents, as amended by Law No. (2) of 2016,

and the Customs Law issued by Law No. (40) of 2002,

and the Income Tax Law issued by Law No. (24) of 2018,

and the Commercial Companies Law issued by Law No. (11) of 2015,

based on the proposal of the Minister of Commerce and Industry,

and on the Draft Law submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

and after seeking the opinion of the Shura Council,

we have decided as follows:

## Chapter One: Definitions (1)

### Article 1

For the application of the provisions of this Law, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings indicated opposite each of them, unless the context requires another meaning:

Ministry: Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

Minister: Minister of Commerce and Industry.

Competent Department / Directorate: The administrative Unit of the Ministry.

Non-Qatari investor: The person who invests his funds in one of the projects authorized for direct investment in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Non-Qatari capital: What is invested by a non-Qatari in cash or in-kind funds or rights of financial value in the State of Qatar, including:

1- The cash transferred to the State through licensed banks and financial companies.

2- Tangible assets imported for investment purposes in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

3- Profits, returns, and reserves resulting from the investment of non-Qatari capital in the project, if such are used to increase the capital of this project, or if they are invested in one of the permitted projects in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

4- Intangible rights, such as licenses, patents, and trademarks registered in the State.

Company: Any company established in accordance with the provisions of the Commercial Companies Law referred to.

## Chapter Two: Regulations for Investment of Non-Qatari Capital (2-7)

### Article 2

Without prejudice to the legislations **on the Regulation of** the practice of commercial activities and professions by non-Qataris, and the provisions of Article (4) of this Law / Act, a non-Qatari investor may invest in all economic sectors up to (100%) of the capital, in accordance with what is specified in the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act.

### Article 3

Applications for approval to exceed the contribution of non-Qataris of 49% of the capital of companies, in accordance with the provisions of Article (2) of this Law / Act, shall be submitted to the competent Department / Directorate on the form prepared for this purpose, accompanied by the supporting documents as specified by the Department / Directorate, after payment of the prescribed fees.

The competent Department / Directorate shall decide on the application within fifteen days from the date of completion of the required documents, and shall notify the concerned party thereof by registered letter or by any other means that provides notification, and the expiration of this period without a response shall be considered an implicit rejection of the application.

Anyone whose application has been rejected may submit a Raising a Grievance against the decision of the Department / Directorate to the Minister within fifteen days from the date of being informed of the rejection decision, or from the date the application is deemed rejected.

The Minister shall decide on the Raising a Grievance within thirty days from the date of its submission, and the expiry of this period without a decision on the Raising a Grievance shall be considered an implicit rejection, and the Minister's decision on the Raising a Grievance shall be final.

The Executive Regulations of this Law / Act shall specify the mechanism for deciding on investors’ applications in accordance with the provisions of this article.

### Article 4

Investment by a non-Qatari investor is prohibited in the following fields:

A- Banks and insurance companies, except those excluded by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

B- Commercial Agencies.

C- Any other fields specified by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

### Article 5

Non-Qatari companies involved in the execution of Contracts of works in the State are obliged to adhere to the following regulations:

1- The Contract must be executed through the branch of the company in the State.

2- The Contract must be with one of the ministries, government agencies, public bodies, public institutions, or companies and institutions in which the State participates.

3- Registration in the commercial register and obtaining the Commercial License for the branch of the company through which the Contract is executed in the State, after the awarding of the work and before Contract Signing.

4- Fulfillment of all requirements of the competent authorities in the State according to the regulations implemented by these authorities throughout the duration of the Contract.

5- Renewal of the registration in the commercial register as well as renewal of the Licenses issued to the branch of the company throughout the duration of the Contract.

The Cabinet / Council of Ministers may, upon the proposal of the Minister, amend these regulations by addition, deletion, or merging.

### Article 6

In matters for which there is no specific provision in this Law / Act, the obtaining of the necessary Licenses by the non-Qatari investor to practice any of the activities permitted for investment shall be subject to the provisions of the Laws / Acts in force in the State regarding this activity.

### Article 7

A non-Qatari investor may own up to (49%) of the capital of Qatari joint-stock companies listed on the Qatar Stock Exchange, after obtaining approval from the Ministry for the proposed percentage in the company’s articles of incorporation and bylaws. He may also own a percentage exceeding the aforementioned limit, with the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the minister.

## Chapter Three: Investment Incentives (8-16)

### Article 8

It is permissible to allocate the necessary land to the non-Qatari investor for establishing their investment project, either through rental or by granting usufruct rights, in accordance with the applicable legislation in this regard.

### Article 9

A non-Qatari investor may import for his investment project whatever is necessary for establishing, operating, or expanding the project, in accordance with the provisions of the laws in force in the State.

### Article 10

Non-Qatari investment projects may be exempted from Income Tax according to the regulations, procedures, and periods stipulated in the Income Tax Law referred to.

### Article 11

Non-Qatari investment projects are exempt from Customs Duties on their imports of machinery and equipment necessary for their establishment. Non-Qatari investment projects in the industrial sector are exempt from Customs Duties on their imports of raw materials and semi-manufactured materials necessary for production that are not available in local markets.

### Article 12

The Council of Ministers may, based on the proposal of the Minister, grant investment projects incentives and advantages in addition to those stipulated in this Law / Act.

### Article 13

Non-Qatari investments, whether direct or indirect, shall not be subject to expropriation or any action of similar effect, unless it is for public benefit, in a non-discriminatory manner, and in exchange for fair and appropriate compensation, in accordance with the same procedures applied to citizens.

### Article 14

1- The non-Qatari investor enjoys the freedom to make all transfers related to their investments to and from abroad without delay. These transfers include:

a- Investment returns.

b- Proceeds from the sale or liquidation of all or part of the investment.

c- Proceeds of amounts resulting from the settlement of investment disputes.

d- The Compensation / Indemnity stipulated in Article (13) of this Law / Act.

2- Transfers shall be carried out in any convertible currency, at the exchange rate in effect on the date of transfer.

### Article 15

A non-Qatari investor may transfer ownership of his investment to any other investor or relinquish it to his national partner in the case of partnership, provided that this is done in accordance with the applicable laws and legislation.

In this case, the investment continues to be subject to the provisions of this Law / Act, and the new investor shall continue to operate the project and replace the previous investor in rights and obligations.

### Article 16

Except for labor disputes, a non-Qatari investor may agree to resolve any dispute arising between him and others through Arbitration or any other prescribed dispute resolution method, in accordance with the law.

## Chapter Four: General Provisions (17-18)

### Article 17

The non-Qatari investor is obligated to preserve the environment from pollution, to comply with the laws, regulations, and instructions related to security and public health, and to refrain from any actions that might affect the public order of the state or public morals.

### Article 18

The provisions of this Law / Act shall not affect the advantages, tax exemptions, or other guarantees and incentives granted to companies and establishments existing at the time it comes into effect. These companies and establishments shall retain those advantages, exemptions, guarantees, and incentives in accordance with the legislations, agreements, and contracts from which they are derived.

## Chapter Five: Penalties and Final Provisions (19-27)

### Article 19

The competent Department / Directorate shall notify the non-Qatari investor when he violates any Ruling of the provisions of this Law / Act, to rectify the violation within a period not exceeding three months from the date of notification. In the event of failure to rectify the violation, the competent Department / Directorate shall cancel the license issued for the project and erase the registration of the company or Branch / Subdivision, as the case may be, from the commercial register, with notification to the relevant government authorities concerned with the investment project to take action regarding it.

The concerned party may Raise a Grievance to the Minister against the decision to cancel the license and erase the registration of the company or Branch / Subdivision, and the same provisions and procedures stipulated in Article (3) of this Law / Act shall apply regarding the Raising a Grievance.

### Article 20

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law / Act, a fine not exceeding (500,000) five hundred thousand riyals shall be imposed on anyone who practices or participates in an economic activity in violation of the provisions of this Law / Act.

### Article 21

The legal entity shall be punished with the penalty stipulated in the previous article for acts committed in violation of the provisions of this Law / Act, if the Crime is committed by one of its employees in its name or on its behalf, without prejudice to the criminal responsibility of the natural person affiliated with it.

### Article 22

Employees of the Ministry, who are delegated the Capacity of Law enforcement Officer by a decision from the Attorney General in agreement with the Minister, shall have the authority to detect and record Crimes committed in violation of the provisions of this Law / Act.

### Article 23

The fees for services provided In accordance with the provisions of this Law shall be determined by a decision from the Minister.

### Article 24

The Minister issues the Executive Regulations and all regulations and decisions necessary to implement the provisions of this Law / Act. Until they are issued, the currently applicable decisions shall remain in effect, provided they do not conflict with its provisions.

### Article 25

The provisions of this Law do not apply to:

1- Companies and individuals to whom the State assigns the extraction, exploitation, or management of natural resources, by virtue of a concession or special agreement, except to the extent that does not conflict with the provisions of the concession contract or special agreement.

2- Companies established or participated in by the government and other public institutions and bodies, and companies in which the State participates with not less than (51%) or with a lesser percentage provided the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, in partnership with non-Qatari investors, in accordance with the provisions of the Commercial Companies Law referred to.

3- Companies and individuals licensed by Qatar Petroleum to conduct any of the petroleum operations, or that aim to invest in the oil, gas, and petrochemicals sector.

### Article 26

Law / Act No. (13) of 2000 referred to is hereby cancelled.

### Article 27

All relevant authorities, each within their competence, shall implement this Law / Act. It shall be published in the official gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
