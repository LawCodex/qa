# Law No 24 of 2002 on Retirement and Pensions

We Jassim bin Hamad Al T-hani, Deputy Emir of the State of Qatar;

Having perused the Amended Provisional Constitution, in particular Articles 22, 23, 34, and 51 thereof;

Law No. 2 of 1962 regulating the General Fiscal Policy in Qatar, as amended by Decree-Law No. 19 of 1996;

Decree Law No. 7 of 1984 concerning the retirement of civil servants,

Law No. 8 of 1984 deferring the application of Decree Law No. 6 of 1984 and Decree Law No. 7 of 1984 on military retirement and retirement of civil servants;

Law No. 5 of 1989 on the State General Budget;

Law No. 7 of 1999 regulating the Ministry of Civil Service Affairs and Housing and functions thereof;

The Civil Service Law promulgated by Law No. 1 of 2001;

The proposal of the Minister of Civil Service Affairs and Housing;

The draft law submitted by the Council of Ministers, and

Having consulted the Shura Council,

Hereby promulgate the following law:

## Chapter One (1)

### Definitions (1)

#### Article 1 Amended

في تطبيق أحكام هذا القانون، تكون للكلمات والعبارات التالية، المعاني الموضحة قرين كل منها، ما لم يقتض السياق معنى آخر:

الراتب الأساسي: الراتب الشهري الأخير الذي يتقاضاه الموظف أو العامل ولا يشمل البدلات والمخصصات والتعويضات أيا كان نوعها.

الراتب: الراتب الأساسي مضافاً إليه العلاوة الاجتماعية المقررة بموجب قانون الخدمة المدنية أو غيره من التشريعات المماثلة.

مدة الخدمة: المدة مدفوعة الاشتراك سواء كانت فعلية أو اعتبارية.

المكافأة: مكافأة نهاية الخدمة.

المعاش: المبلغ المستحق شهرياً لصاحب المعاش أو المستحقين عنه وفقاً لأحكام هذا القانون.

صاحب المعاش: من انتهت خدمته وتقرر له معاش بموجب أحكام هذا القانون.

المستحق: القطري الذي تقرر له معاش أو نصيب فيه عن صاحب المعاش وفقاً لأحكام هذا القانون.

العجز: كل مرض أو إصابة جسدية أو عقلية تحول كلية وبصفة دائمة دون القدرة على العمل.

المفقود: الذى يصدر باعتباره مفقوداً حكم نهائي من محكمة مختصة.

الهيئة: الهيئة العامة للتقاعد والمعاشات.

المجلس: مجلس إدارة الهيئة.

اللجنة الطبية: الجهة الطبية التي يعتمدها المجلس.

الصندوق: صندوق المعاشات المنشأ بمقتضي أحكام هذا القانون.

## Chapter Two (2-4)

### In personam Jurisdiction (2-4)

#### Article 2 Amended Canceled

The provisions of this Law / Act shall apply to:

1- Qatari employees subject to the provisions of the Civil Service Law and occupying permanent positions.

2- Qatari employees and workers in public authorities and institutions, joint-stock companies, and others as determined by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

#### Article 2 Amended

تسري أحكام هذا القانون على:

1- الموظفين القطريين الخاضعين لأحكام قانون الخدمة المدنية ويشغلون وظائف دائمة.

2- الموظفين والعاملين القطريين في الهيئات والمؤسسات العامة والشركات المساهمة، وغيرها من الجهات التي يصدر بتحديدها قرار من مجلس الوزراء.

#### Article 3 Amended

The provisions of this Law / Act shall apply to anyone whose service ended before the date it came into force and who received the gratuity, if the following two conditions are met in respect of him:

1- He must submit an application to that effect to the Authority within the period specified by the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act.

2- He must pay the prescribed contributions for the previous service period. They shall be paid in accordance with the provision of Article (20) of this Law / Act.

The provisions of this Article shall apply to the beneficiaries of the pensioner.

#### Article 4

The provisions of this Law / Act shall not apply to the following two categories:

1- Those referred to Retirement before the date it enters into force.

2- Employees and workers subject to special Retirement and pension schemes that provide them with better benefits.

## Chapter Three (5-23)

### Pension Annuity (5-23)

#### Article 5 Amended

A percentage of (5%) five percent shall be deducted from the salary of the Employee / Staff or Employee / Worker, and the employer shall bear double this percentage, and shall be obligated to pay these amounts to the Authority by no later than the fifth day of the following month. A late-payment Fine of (2%) per month shall be imposed on the overdue amounts for the period from the date they became due until the date of payment.

As for Employee / Staff and Employee / Worker who are in service on the date this Law / Act comes into force, the contributions due from them for their previous periods of service shall be deducted from the gratuity due to each of them, or the remainder thereof after deducting the loans granted against its guarantee, and the remaining amount shall be paid to them, and they shall pay the difference, if any.

#### Article 6 Amended

The pension shall be due if the service of the Employee / Staff or Employee / Worker ends for any of the following reasons:

1- Death.

2- Reaching the prescribed age for termination of service or termination of service through disciplinary means, provided that the period of service is not less than fifteen years.

3- Resignation, provided that the period of service is not less than fifteen years and the age of the Employee / Staff or Employee / Worker is not less than forty years.

4- Medical unfitness, in accordance with what is determined by the medical Committee.

5- Abolition of the position or termination of service other than through disciplinary means for employees or workers in ministries, other government agencies, public authorities, and public institutions, provided that the period of service is not less than five years.

Companies subject to the provisions of this Law / Act and other entities to which Cabinet / Council of Ministers decides that its provisions shall apply may apply this Judgment / Ruling, provided that they bear all Financial Obligations arising therefrom, after the approval of Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

6- Termination of service for reasons other than those stipulated in the preceding clauses, provided that the period of service is not less than fifteen years.

#### Article 7 Amended

In addition to the benefits that may be due in accordance with the Law / Act, the pension shall be settled according to the following bases:

1- The actual service period or a notional service period of twenty years, whichever is longer, if the service ends due to death or medical unfitness caused by or during service in either case.

2- The actual service period or a notional service period of fifteen years, whichever is longer, if the service ends due to death, medical unfitness, abolition of the position, or otherwise than by disciplinary means.

3- The actual service period if the service ends for reasons other than those stipulated in the preceding clauses.

In all cases, the employer shall be obligated to pay all contributions for the difference between the actual service period and the notional service period, as well as the value of the additional amounts in the cases of abolition of the position or termination of service otherwise than by disciplinary means, in accordance with what is determined by the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act.

#### Article 8

In calculating the period of service, any fraction of a month shall be deemed a full month, and any period exceeding six months shall be deemed a full year.

#### Article 9 Amended

The length of service shall include periods of leave of all types, periods of secondment, study missions, official assignments, and training courses, provided that the prescribed contributions for such periods are paid under this Law / Act, in accordance with what is determined by the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act.

Periods of Endowment (Waqf) or absence from work shall not be included in the calculation of the length of service whenever it is decided to deprive the Employee / Staff of his entire salary for them.

#### Article 10 Amended

The monthly pension for employees or workers in ministries, other government agencies, public authorities, and public institutions shall be calculated on the basis of (5%) five percent of the last salary, multiplied by the number of years of service, provided that the pension does not exceed the amount of the salary.

The pension for employees or workers in entities other than those referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be calculated on the basis of (5%) of the average salary during the last five years of the contribution period, multiplied by the number of years of service, provided that the pension does not exceed that average.

#### Article 11 Amended

If service ends due to resignation or pursuant to the provision of clause (6) of Article (6) of this Law / Act, the pension shall be reduced by (2%) two percent. If service ends through disciplinary action, the pension shall be reduced by (2.5%) two and a half percent for each year of the difference between the age upon termination of service and the age of sixty for males and fifty-five for females.

#### Article 12

The pension becomes payable from the day following the end of service, and is paid at the end of the month for which it is due.

In the event that the Pension Settlement is not completed in the month following the end of service, for any reason, the pension determined by the Authority shall be paid until the final settlement is completed.

The remaining amount of the due pension shall be paid after the final settlement in one lump sum, and any excess amounts paid shall be recovered in monthly installments not exceeding one year from the date of the final settlement.

#### Article 12

An employee or Employee / Worker may request to purchase a notional service period to be added to his/her actual service period, provided the following conditions are met:

1- The age on the date of submitting the request must not be less than (50) fifty years for males and (45) forty-five years for females.

2- His/her actual service period must not be less than ten years.

3- He/she must pay the contributions due for the purchased period at the rate of (15%) of the salary, and the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act shall determine the method of paying these contributions to the Authority.

#### Article 13 Amended Canceled

If the pensioner dies, the right to the pension shall pass to his eligible beneficiaries, in accordance with the shares specified in Table No. (1) attached to this Law / Act.

If payment of the share of any eligible beneficiary is suspended, it shall be distributed among the remaining eligible beneficiaries, and if the suspended share is reinstated to its owner, the shares of the eligible beneficiaries shall be reduced by the amount of the increase.

#### Article 13 Amended

إذا توفى صاحب المعاش انتقل الحق في المعاش إلى المستحقين عنه، وفقاً للأنصبة المحددة بالجدول رقم (1) المرفق بهذا القانون.

وفي حالة وفاة الموظفة أو العاملة أو صاحبة المعاش القطرية ولها أولاد غير قطريين، يتم معاملتهم معاملة المستحقين القطريين وفقاً لأحكام هذا القانون.

وإذا أوقف صرف نصيب أحد المستحقين وزع على باقي المستحقين، وإذا أعيد النصيب الموقوف إلى صاحبه، خفضت أنصبة المستحقين بمقدار الزيادة.

#### Article 14 Amended

If the Employee / Staff or Employee / Worker is considered missing, his pension shall be settled on the assumption that his service ended due to death, and it shall be granted to his beneficiaries. If he returns, he shall be treated in accordance with the provisions of this Law / Act.

If the pensioner or beneficiary is considered missing, his pension or share shall be distributed among the beneficiaries in accordance with the provision of the preceding article.

#### Article 15 Amended

إذا عُين أو أُعيد صاحب المعاش للعمل في إحدى الجهات الخاضعة لأحكام هذا القانون، أوقف صرف المعاش مدة عمله، فإذا كان راتبه أقل من معاشه صرف له الفرق من الهيئة، ويجوز له الاشتراك عن مدة الخدمة الجديدة، ويسوى معاشه عند انتهاء خدمته، على أساس راتبه مضافاً إليه الفرق عن المدة الإضافية التي لا تقل عن ثماني سنوات، بحساب معاش عن هذه المدة، وفقاً لأحكام المادتين (10)، (11) من هذا القانون، وإضافته إلى معاشه السابق.

وإذا قلت مدة خدمته الإضافية عن ثماني سنوات أُعيد له معاشه الموقوف وصرفت له اشتراكاته التي سددها عن هذه المدة وفقاً لما تحدده اللائحة التنفيذية لهذا القانون.

#### Article 16

If the pension is payable to the widow of the pensioner, she shall have the right to combine her salary from work or her pension with the pension payable to her for her Husband without being restricted by a maximum limit, and the daughter may combine her pension with the pension payable to her for her mother, father, or both.

The Husband shall be entitled to a pension in the event of the death of his wife, whether an employee or a pensioner, and the pension shall be reduced by half if he is entitled to another pension or salary from any entity subject to the provisions of this Law / Act. If such other pension or salary is less than half of the pension, the difference shall be paid to him.

#### Article 17 Amended

The pension of an eligible male beneficiary among the children, brothers, and sons of a son shall cease when he reaches twenty-one years of age, with the following two cases excluded:

1- The beneficiary’s inability to earn and work, and his right to the pension shall end upon his death or the cessation of the state of disability.

2- The beneficiary’s study at one of the schools, Institutes, or Universities, in which case his share of the pension shall be paid to him until he completes his studies or reaches the age of twenty-seven, whichever comes first.

#### Article 18

Payment of the widow’s pension due to her on behalf of her deceased husband shall be suspended if she marries, and shall be reinstated to her if she divorces.

Payment of the mother’s pension due to her on behalf of her deceased son shall be suspended if she marries someone other than his father, and shall be reinstated to her if she divorces.

Payment of the pension due to the daughter, sister, or son’s daughter shall be suspended if she marries or takes up a permanent position with one of the entities subject to the provisions of this Law / Act. If her salary from her work is less than the pension, the difference shall be paid to her, and her full pension shall be reinstated to her if she divorces, is widowed, or her service ends.

In all cases, if a pension beneficiary is widowed again and becomes entitled to another pension, the larger pension shall be paid to her.

#### Article 19 Amended

If Qatari Nationality is withdrawn from, revoked from, or renounced by the pensioner, the pension shall be paid to his beneficiaries, and the pension shall be restored to him if Qatari Nationality is restored to him.

#### Article 20 Amended

The employee or Employee / Worker may request that all or some of his previous periods of service be counted toward the pension, provided that he pays the contributions due for them on the basis of the salary as of the date of the inclusion request.

The employee or Employee / Worker may pay the prescribed contributions for previous periods of service in one lump sum, or in monthly installments over five years, at the equivalent of twenty-one riyals for every one thousand riyals.

#### Article 21

If the non-Qatari Employee / Staff or Employee / Worker acquires Qatari Nationality, they may request that all or some of their periods of service prior to acquiring nationality be counted as part of their period of service, provided that the contributions stipulated in the previous Article are paid for them.

#### Article 22 Canceled

In all cases where the Employee / Staff or Employee / Worker pays his contribution percentage to the Fund for a previous period of service, the employer or the Employee / Staff or Employee / Worker, as the case may be, shall pay to the Fund an amount equivalent to double it, in accordance with what is specified by the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act.

#### Article 23

#### Article 23 Amended Canceled

If the Employee / Staff or Employee / Worker does not meet the conditions for entitlement to a pension, the contributions he paid for his period of service shall be refunded to him in accordance with what is determined by the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act, in addition to the employer bearing the End of Service Gratuity prescribed for him.

#### Article 23

The Employee / Staff or Employee / Worker may not combine the pension due pursuant to this Law / Act with the End of Service Gratuity stipulated in the laws or regulations of the entities subject to the provisions of this Law / Act. The Employee / Staff or Employee / Worker whose actual period of service exceeds twenty years shall be entitled to an End of Service Gratuity borne by his employer, in accordance with what is determined by the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act.

## Chapter Four (24-25)

### Commutation (24-25)

#### Article 24 Amended

An Employee / Staff or Employee / Worker entitled to a pension, or a pensioner, may request to commute into a cash amount a portion not exceeding half of the pension, in accordance with Table No. (2) attached to this Law / Act.

The deduction of the commuted portion shall cease upon the death of the Employee / Staff, Employee / Worker, or pensioner, and in such case the beneficiaries’ shares shall be paid in full. The beneficiaries may not commute their shares.

#### Article 25 Amended

An employee or Employee / Worker entitled to a pension, or a pensioner, may make more than one commutation within the limits of half of the pension due to him at the time of requesting the commutation, in accordance with what is specified by the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act.

## Chapter Five (26-48)

### The General Retirement and Pension Authority (26-48)

#### Article 26 Canceled

A public Authority shall be established called the “General Authority for Retirement and Pensions” and shall have legal personality and an independent budget.

#### Article 27 Canceled

The Authority shall be affiliated with the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, and its headquarters shall be in the city of Doha.

#### Article 28 Canceled

The Authority aims to ensure a decent life for pensioners and their eligible beneficiaries in accordance with the provisions of this Law / Act, and undertakes the management and investment of the Fund's assets.

#### Article 29 Amended Canceled

The Authority shall be managed by a Board of Directors composed of a Chairperson, a Vice-Chairperson, and a number of members not fewer than (5) five and not exceeding (9) nine, whose appointment and remuneration shall be determined by an Amiri Decision. The Council shall have a secretary selected by the Council, which shall determine his powers and remuneration.

#### Article 30 Canceled

The term of membership in the Council shall be three years, renewable for another similar term or terms.

#### Article 31 Amended Canceled

The Council shall have the competencies and powers necessary to manage the affairs of the Authority and achieve its objectives, in particular the following:

1- Establish the general policies of the Authority and supervise their implementation.

2- Approve the Authority’s plans, programs, and projects and follow up on their implementation.

3- Establish the system for investing the Fund’s assets.

4- Approve pension disbursement procedures.

5- Approve procedures for addressing deficits in the Fund’s obligations.

6- Propose increases to pensions.

7- Establish the Organizational Structure of the Authority, the administrative, financial, and technical regulations, and the personnel affairs regulations.

8- Conclude loans.

9- Approve the annual budget of the Authority and its final account.

10- Propose legislation related to pensions.

11- Review Periodic Reports and follow-up reports related to the progress of work.

12- The decisions of the Council stipulated in items (3, 5, 6, 8, 9) shall not be effective until approved by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

#### Article 32 Canceled

The Chairman of the Council represents the Authority before the judiciary and in its relations with third parties.

#### Article 33 Amended Canceled

يجتمع المجلس بدعوة من رئيسه، مرة كل ثلاثة أشهر على الأقل، وكلما دعت الحاجة.

ولا يكون اجتماع المجلس صحيحاً إلا بحضور أغلبية الأعضاء على أن يكون من بينهم الرئيس أو نائبه.

#### Article 34 Canceled

Council sessions shall be confidential, and proxy attendance or Voting is not permitted.

Resolutions shall be issued by a majority of the votes of the members present, and in the event of a tie, the side on which the Chairman / President votes shall prevail.

#### Article 35 Canceled

The minutes of the Council meetings and its decisions shall be recorded in a special register with numbered pages, signed by the Chairperson of the Council and the Secretary.

#### Article 36 Canceled

للمجلس أن يدعو لحضور اجتماعاته من يرى الاستعانة بهم من موظفي الهيئة أو غيرهم من ذوي الخبرة، دون أن يكون لهم حق التصويت.

#### Article 37 Canceled

The Chairman of the Council has the right to sign on behalf of the Authority, and the Council may authorize the Director General or other employees of the Authority to sign, individually or jointly, in the matters determined by the Council.

#### Article 38 Canceled

The Authority's seal on its documents shall not be valid unless accompanied by the signature of the Chairman of the Council or the Delegate / Authorized Person authorized to sign.

#### Article 39 Canceled

The Chairman of the Council, any of its members, or any employee of the Authority may not have a direct or indirect personal interest in contracts concluded with the Authority or on its behalf.

#### Article 40 Canceled

The Authority shall have a Director General appointed by a resolution of the Council. Under the supervision of the Council and within the framework of the general policy of the Authority, he shall manage all its technical, administrative, and financial affairs, in accordance with the prescribed regulations and systems, and within the limits of the annual budget. In particular, he shall have the following duties:

1- Proposing plans and programs for the Authority’s projects, the draft Organizational Structure, and draft regulations of the Authority.

2- Preparing an annual report on the achievements and work programs of the Authority and presenting it to the Council at the end of each year.

3- Implementing the decisions of the Council.

4- Preparing the draft annual estimated budget of the Authority and its final account.

5- Any other work assigned to him by the Council In accordance with the provisions of this Law.

#### Article 41 Canceled

The resources of the Authority consist of the following:

1- The funds and financial appropriations allocated to it by the State.

2- Loans.

#### Article 42 Canceled

The Authority shall have an estimated annual budget prepared in accordance with the rules determined by the Authority’s regulations.

The financial year shall begin on the first of January and end on the last day of December of each year, provided that the first financial year shall begin from the date this Law / Act comes into force until the end of December of the following year.

#### Article 43 Canceled

The Council shall submit to the Cabinet / Council of Ministers a detailed report on the Authority’s activities, projects, and the Fund’s financial position, within a period not exceeding three months from the end of the fiscal year. The report shall be accompanied by a copy of the auditor’s report and a statement of the Council’s proposals and recommendations.

#### Article 44 Canceled

The Council of Ministers may request the Authority to submit reports on its financial, administrative, and technical conditions, or on its activities.

It may issue general directives to it regarding what it must follow in matters related to the general policy for pensions, and the Council shall comply with these directives.

#### Article 45

A fund called «Pension Fund» shall be established, to which the contributions stipulated in this Law / Act and any other amounts allocated to it in the State budget shall be paid.

#### Article 46

The Fund’s financial resources shall consist of the following:

1- Contributions of employees and workers at a rate of (5%) five percent of the salary.

2- Contributions of the employer equivalent to twice the contributions of employees and workers.

3- Funds and appropriations allocated to it by the State.

4- Returns on the investment of the Fund’s assets.

5- Any other resources accepted by the Council.

#### Article 47

The financial position of the Fund shall be examined at least once every three years by one or more actuaries appointed by the Council. If a deficit in the Fund’s assets is found, it must be settled in accordance with the method determined by the Council.

#### Article 48

The Board may appoint one or more auditors. The auditor shall at all times have the right to inspect the Fund’s books, records, and documents, and to request such data as he deems necessary to properly perform his duties. He may verify the Fund’s assets and liabilities and shall submit his report to the Board, accompanied by his recommendations.

## Chapter Six (49)

### Sanctions (49)

#### Article 49

مع عدم الإخلال بأية عقوبة أشد ينص عليها قانون آخر، يعاقب بالحبس مدة لا تجاوز ثلاثة أشهر وبغرامة لا تزيد على (2000) ألفي ريال، أو بإحدى هاتين العقوبتين، كل من أعطى بسوء نية بيانات غير صحيحة أو امتنع عن إعطاء البيانات المنصوص عليها في هذا القانون أو لائحته التنفيذية، إذا ترتب على ذلك الحصول على مبالغ من الصندوق بغير وجه حق.

## Chapter Seven (50-56)

### General Provisions (50-56)

#### Article 50

تنشأ لجنة لفحص المنازعات الناشئة عن تطبيق أحكام هذا القانون، ويصدر بتشكيلها ونظام عملها قرار من مجلس الوزراء.

ولا تقبل دعاوى جهات العمل والموظفين والعاملين وأصحاب المعاشات والمستحقين وغيرهم من المستفيدين أمام القضاء، إلا بعد تقديم طلباتهم إلى اللجنة المشار إليها والبت فيها أو مضى ستين يوماً على تقديمها دون البت فيها.

#### Article 50

The amounts due to the Authority pursuant to this Law / Act shall have a preferential right over all the debtor’s assets, and shall take precedence over all debts after judicial expenses and maintenance debt. The Authority shall have the right to collect them in accordance with the regulations in force for the collection of government funds, and all or part of them may be paid in installments in accordance with the conditions determined by the Council.

#### Article 51

The pension may not be garnished except to fulfill adjudicated alimony or to settle any amounts due to the employer or the Authority.

In all cases, the amount garnished may not exceed one quarter of the pension, and in the event of competing claims, priority shall be given to the alimony debt.

#### Article 51

Lawsuits / Legal Actions and Legal Challenges / Appeals filed by the Authority or the pensioner or the beneficiaries thereof pursuant to this Law / Act shall be exempt from judicial fees at all Levels of Litigation / Degrees of Jurisdiction, and shall be heard on an expedited basis. In all cases, the court may order that the Judgment / Ruling be subject to expedited enforcement without bond, and in the event of rejection of the Lawsuit / Legal Action or Legal Challenge / Appeal, it may order the party who filed it to pay all or part of the costs.

#### Article 52

The Fund’s assets and returns are exempt from taxes.

#### Article 53

Exceptional pensions may be granted, or all or some of the payable pensions may be increased, in accordance with the conditions and procedures determined by the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act.

#### Article 54

The Cabinet / Council of Ministers shall issue, based on a proposal by the Authority, the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act.

#### Article 55

Decree 34 by Law No. (7) of 1984 referred to above is repealed.

#### Article 56

All competent authorities, each within its respective jurisdiction, shall implement this Law / Act, and it shall come into force five months after the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
