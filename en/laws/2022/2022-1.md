# Law No. (1) of 2022 Issuing the Social Insurance Law

We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, Emir of the State of Qatar,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And Law No. (24) of 2002 regarding Retirement and pensions, and the laws amending it,

And the Labour Law issued by Law No. (14) of 2004, and the laws amending it,

And Law No. (13) of 2006 regarding the Retirement and pensions of military personnel,

And Law No. (4) of 2007 issuing the unified system for extending insurance protection to citizens of the Gulf Cooperation Council States working outside their own countries in any member state of the Council, as amended by Law No. (5) of 2021,

And the State Financial System Law issued by Law No. (2) of 2015, and the Civil Human Resources Law issued by Law No. (15) of 2016, as amended by Law No. (23) of 2020,

Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

And after the approval of the Shura Council,

We have decided to ratify the following Law

## الفصل الأول - تعاريف (1)

### Article 1

For the application of the provisions of this Law and the Executive Regulation, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings indicated alongside each, unless the context requires otherwise:

Minister: Minister of Finance.

Authority: General Retirement and Social Insurance Authority (GRSIA).

Chairman / President: Chairman / President of the General Retirement and Social Insurance Authority (GRSIA).

Fund: Pension Fund referred to in Article (59) of this Law.

Employer:

1- Government Sector: Ministries, other government agencies, authorities, and public institutions.

2- Private Sector: Any natural or legal person who regularly employs one or more Qatari workers for remuneration.

Contribution: The amount paid monthly to the account of the Fund, in accordance with the cases and rates stipulated in this Law.

Insured Person: Any Qatari employee in the government sector or worker in the private sector or self-employed person, for whom the contribution is paid or paid by themselves in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Salary for Contribution Calculation: The salary or Basic Salary of the insured person, in addition to the social allowance and housing allowance in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Income Bracket: The income bracket stipulated in accordance with Article (4) of this Law.

Salary for Pension Calculation: The salary for pension calculation for the insured person in accordance with Article (29) of this Law.

Lump-Sum Compensation: The amount due to the insured person if the pension eligibility conditions are not met, in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Contribution Period: The period of service for which the contribution is paid, whether actual or deemed.

Actual Service Period: The period of service served with an employer subject to the provisions of this Law, or any period that this Law stipulates to be included within the actual service period.

Deemed Service Period: The period of service that this Law stipulates to be counted by law in Pension Settlement.

Pension: The amount due monthly to the pensioner or their beneficiaries, in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Pensioner: The insured person who is granted a pension by virtue of the provisions of this Law.

Beneficiary: The person to whom a share of the pension is granted from the insured person or the pensioner, in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Excess Service Bonus: The bonus due to the pensioner in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Actuarial Cost: The present value of the pension payments in Qatari Riyal that the pensioner will receive for life and to the beneficiaries after his death.

Medical Committee: The General Medical Committee at the Ministry of Public Health.

Disability: Any disease or physical or mental injury which totally and permanently prevents the ability to work.

Missing Person: A person for whom a final judgment is issued declaring him missing by a competent court.

Executive Regulation: Executive Regulation of this Law.

## Chapter Two - Persons Subject to the Provisions of the Law / Act (2-6)

### Article 2

The provisions of this Law / Act apply to Qataris of the following categories:

First: The government sector:

1- Employees in ministries, other government agencies, and public bodies and institutions.

2- Employees subject to special employment laws, systems, and regulations.

Second: The private sector:

1- Workers subject to the provisions of Labour Law.

2- Workers in companies and institutions exempted from being subject to Labour Law and who have their own employment systems, except for members of the Employer's family; "family members" means spouses, ascendants, and descendants.

### Article 3

The application of the provisions of this Law / Act to Qatari employees and workers at a workplace in either the government or private sector is conditioned upon the availability of the following two requirements:

1 - The insured's age must not be less than eighteen (18) years.

2 - The employment relationship must be regular in a permanent position, with a duration of no less than one year, whether continuous or intermittent.

### Article 4

The provisions of this Law / Act shall apply optionally to Qataris who are self-employed and subject to the income bracket system.

The income bracket system means the social insurance system selected by the insured, which is suitable for their estimated income level.

The categories of this system, and the conditions, controls, procedures for participation, pension settlement in accordance with it, and cases of cancellation of participation, shall be specified by a Council of Ministers Decision.

The participation is calculated based on the income bracket participated in, and is paid at a rate of twenty-one percent (21%).

The Authority shall undertake the enumeration of insured persons from the private sector who fall within the categories subject to optional participation in accordance with the income bracket system, and their continued subjection to the provisions of this Law / Act according to their previous situations, provided that the salary for the participation calculation is fixed on the basis of the last salary before the implementation of the provisions of this Law / Act, until the issuance of the Council of Ministers Decision referred to in the third paragraph of this Article, and their transfer to the income bracket system and regularization of their situation in compliance with the prescribed conditions and controls, and payment of any financial differences in the participation calculation salary that may be due from them.

### Article 5

The provisions of this Law / Act do not apply to the following categories:

1- Military personnel subject to the Law / Act Regulating military retirement.

2 - Employees and workers subject to retirement or special insurance systems that grant them better benefits.

### Article 6

The Ruling of Article (69) of this Law applies to pension holders under the provisions of Law No. (24) of 2002 referred to.

It also applies to beneficiaries under the provisions of Law No. (24) of 2002 referred to, Schedule / Table No. (1) attached to this Law, regarding any better benefits it provides.

## Chapter Three - Registration (7-10)

### Article 7

All employers subject to Law / Act No. (24) of 2002 referred to, must update their Registration data with the Authority within fourteen (14) days from the date of entry into force of the provisions of this Law / Act, and must provide the Authority with the Registration data of current employees and workers subject to the provisions of this Law / Act within ninety (90) days from the date of entry into force of the provisions of this Law / Act.

Other employers mentioned in Article (2) of this Law / Act, not registered with the Authority, must apply for their Registration with the Authority within thirty (30) days from the date of entry into force of the provisions of this Law / Act, and must register their current employees with the Authority within ninety (90) days from the date of entry into force of the provisions of this Law / Act.

The Registration and submission of the data mentioned in this Article must be in accordance with the forms determined by the Authority.

The regulations specify the rules for applying this Article.

### Article 8

The employer must provide the Authority with the data of any employee or worker appointed within it within thirty (30) days from the date of joining work, and it must also notify the Authority in case the service of any employee or worker ends within thirty (30) days from the date of termination of their service.

The employer that is established or acquires legal personality after the implementation of this Law / Act must submit an application for Registration with the Authority within fourteen (14) days from the date of starting its activity.

The regulation shall determine the Controls and Procedures and rules for Registration and notification stipulated in this article.

### Article 9

The Authority may register the employees or workers of the employer, or correct or amend their data, if it is proven that the employer has not fulfilled the obligations stipulated in the previous article, in accordance with the regulations specified by the bylaws.

### Article 10

If the insured person works for more than one employer subject to the provisions of this Law / Act, or another employer subject to any of the Retirement and social insurance laws, his participation shall be limited to his employment in one job or position only, according to the conditions and regulations specified by the bylaws.

## Chapter Four - Subscriptions (11-23)

### Article 11

The subscription for the insured is calculated based on the salary used for subscription calculation, not exceeding (100,000) one hundred thousand riyals, and the subscriptions are paid at a rate of (21%) twenty-one percent, as follows:

1- (7%) seven percent, deducted from the salary used for subscription calculation from the insured.

2- (14%) fourteen percent, paid by the employer on the salary used for subscription calculation.

It is paid to the fund no later than the fifth day of the month following the month for which these subscriptions are due.

It is permitted, with the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, for the Public Treasury of the state to bear a percentage of the subscription prescribed for private sector employers, in accordance with the regulations it specifies.

### Article 12

The insured person's subscription from the government and private sectors for the addition of the housing allowance to the salary for subscription calculation is determined according to the following rules:

1- The housing allowance must not exceed (6,000) six thousand riyals per month.

2- The subscription period for the housing allowance at the end of service must not be less than (15) fifteen years.

3- The subscription for the housing allowance that is suspended for the insured person due to benefiting from government housing or housing provided at the expense of the employer, as the case may be, is calculated as if it were paid in cash.

4- The employer bears the payment of the subscription due for the housing allowance for the service period that completes (15) fifteen years when the insured person’s service ends for any of the reasons stated in items (1), (2), (3) of Article (24) of this Law / Act. Otherwise, the insured person bears the payment of the subscription for the housing allowance or the contributions paid for that allowance are refunded to him.

In accordance with the provisions of Articles (38) and (39) of this Law / Act, the subscription for those returning to work is calculated so that the subscription period for the housing allowance at the end of service is not less than (8) eight years. The employer bears the payment of the subscription due for the housing allowance for the service period that completes (8) eight years when the insured person returning to work ends service for either of the two reasons stated in items (1), (2) of Article (24) of this Law / Act. Otherwise, the insured person bears the payment of the subscription for the housing allowance when the pension becomes due, or the contributions paid for that allowance are refunded to him.

The regulation specifies the Controls and Procedures for implementing this article.

### Article 13

As an exception to the Ruling of Article (11/First Paragraph) of this Law, the insured person whose subscription calculation salary exceeds the amount of (100,000) one hundred thousand riyals before the effective date of this Law may continue his subscription, provided that the salary he receives is established as the subscription calculation salary.

The subscription is paid for him at a rate of (21%) twenty-one percent In accordance with the provisions of this Law, until the actual date of the end of his service.

### Article 14

The private sector employer must submit to the Authority, in the month of January of each year, the salary data for the subscription calculation for their employees for that month and their monthly contributions, and must provide the Authority with any changes in their number or salaries.

If the employer does not submit the data stipulated in the previous paragraph, the contributions due shall be calculated temporarily based on the last data submitted to the Authority, until the due contributions are calculated, in accordance with the rules, regulations, and procedures specified by the bylaw.

### Article 15

The period spent by the insured person performing national service, leave periods, secondments, study missions, official assignments, and training courses is included in the calculation of the subscription period. The subscriptions due under this Law / Act for these periods shall be payable according to the rules and procedures specified by the regulation.

The period of Endowment (Waqf) or interruption from work is not included in the calculation of the subscription period if it has been decided to deprive the Employee / Staff or Employee / Worker of their full salary or wage for it, and it is not counted among the periods required for pension entitlement.

If the decision to dismiss the Employee / Staff or Employee / Worker is canceled by a final court ruling, or withdrawn by a decision from the employer within sixty (60) days from the date of issuance, the period of dismissal is included in the actual service period of the insured person, and the payment of subscriptions in this case shall be in accordance with what is specified by the regulation.

### Article 16

The insured person may request to add all or part of their previous service period(s) to their current service period, according to the following terms and conditions:

1- The service period must be actual service with an employer subject to the provisions of this Law / Act, in a permanent position, according to the rules specified by the regulation.

2- The service period must be after the age of eighteen (18) years.

3- The period must not have already resulted in entitlement to a pension or retirement salary under any other system.

4- It is permissible to add a previous service period in the government sector, public-benefit private institutions, public shareholding companies, or companies in which the State participates, regardless of the duration of the previous service.

5- It is not permitted to add more than five (5) years of previous service at any entity in the private sector, except for the entities mentioned in the previous clause.

6- Commitment to pay the contribution due for it at a rate of twenty-one percent (21%), based on the salary used to calculate the contribution at the date the addition is requested, or the salary used to calculate the pension, whichever is higher.

Private sector employers may request payment of the value of Monthly Contributions due for the service period of the Employee / Worker prior to the effective date of this Law / Act, in accordance with the rules specified by the regulation.

The Ruling of this article applies to those who have acquired Qatari Nationality, subject to the terms and conditions referred to in this article.

### Article 17

A person whose service has ended may request to purchase a notional service period to be added to their actual service period, in order to qualify for a pension, in accordance with the following terms and conditions:

1 - Their age at the time of application must not be less than fifty (50) years.

2 - Their actual service period must not be less than twenty (20) years.

3 - The purchased period shall be limited to the period required to qualify for a pension.

4 - They must pay the total contributions due for the purchased period, calculated at twenty-one percent (21%) of either the last salary considered for subscription at the time of the purchase request, or the pension calculation salary, whichever is higher.

5 - They must submit the purchase request within a period not exceeding one year from the date of notification to their national address or any means of being informed of the decision to end their service.

### Article 18

The due contribution for purchasing notional service period or adding a previous service period may be paid either in a lump sum within thirty (30) days from the date of notification of acceptance of the application at the national address, or in monthly installments according to Table No. (2) attached to this Law / Act. The regulations specify the rules for applying this article.

### Article 19

In the event of the death of the pension holder, before completing the payment of all installment contributions in accordance with Articles (16) and (17) of this Law / Act, the remaining installments are waived, the pension is calculated for the entire period, and the Public Treasury bears the remaining amounts.

In cases other than that stipulated in the previous paragraph, only the period for which installment contributions have been paid is calculated in the pension, unless the remaining installments are paid in a lump sum within thirty (30) days from the date of service termination. If at least half of the contributions are paid, the remainder is deducted from the pension due to him, and the Pension Settlement is recalculated upon payment of all due installments.

### Article 20

When calculating the subscription period, a Part of the month is considered as a full month, and any period exceeding six (6) months is considered a full year.

### Article 21

A Fine is imposed on those who are late in paying the contributions and any other amounts due to the fund within the deadlines stipulated in this Law / Act and the regulation, at a delay rate of (2%) two percent monthly of the amounts delayed in payment for the period from the due date until the date of payment.

### Article 22

The employer who has not registered or enrolled all or some of its employees or workers, or has not paid contributions based on the actual salaries or wages, shall be required to pay an additional amount of (10%) ten percent of the total value of the actual contributions due.

### Article 23

The Council of Ministers, in cases it deems appropriate, may exempt from the Fine for delay stipulated in Article (21) of this Law / Act and the additional amount stipulated in Article (22) of this Law / Act

## Chapter Five - Settlement and Payment of Pension (24-45)

### Article 24

The pension is payable if the insured person's service ends for one of the following reasons:

1- Death.

2- Disability according to what is decided by the medical Committee.

3- Reaching the age of Retirement according to the age specified in the employment regulations to which the insured person is subject, and not less than (60) sixty years old.

4- Resignation.

5- Dismissal by a disciplinary decision, or based on the issuance of a Final Judgment against the Employee / Staff in a crime involving dishonor or breach of trust.

6- Termination of service for reasons other than those specified in the previous clauses.

Except for items (1) and (2) of this article, it is required for pension eligibility that the subscription period shall not be less than (25) twenty-five years, including at least (20) twenty years of actual service, and the age shall not be less than (50) fifty years in the case of resignation. If the age is less than (50) fifty years, the pension shall not be paid until this age is reached and after the date the application is submitted to the authority.

The service period of the insured person whose service ended for any of the reasons contained in items (4), (5), (6) of this article, and who is eligible for a pension, shall be considered continuous if he joins an entity subject to the provisions of this Law / Act within the period specified in the regulations, provided that a request not to pay the pension is submitted within (30) thirty days from the date of the end of his service.

### Article 25

For the application of items (1), (2) stipulated in the first paragraph of the previous article to insured persons in the private sector, it is required that the insured person is registered with the Authority or that the existence of an employment relationship before the date of death or disability is proven, in accordance with what is determined by the regulation.

### Article 26

The pension is settled according to the following principles:

1- The subscription period or a period of (30) thirty years, whichever is longer, if the termination of service is due to death or disability resulting from a work injury.

2- The subscription period or a period of (25) twenty-five years, whichever is longer, if the termination of service is due to death or disability.

3- The subscription period, if the termination of service is for any other reason stated in Article (24) of this Law / Act.

In the case of items (1) and (2) of the previous paragraph, the employer is obligated to pay the amounts for the difference between the subscription period and the period on which the pension is settled.

### Article 27

The pension of the insured person who performs national service and dies or suffers a disability as a result of it or as a result of Military Operations or security operations shall be settled in accordance with the Judgment / Ruling of item (1) of the previous article, and the Public Treasury of the state shall bear the payment of the amounts prescribed for the difference between the period of contribution and the period of service on which the pension is settled.

### Article 28

By way of exception from the Ruling of Article (24/Second Paragraph) of this Law / Act, the pension of the insured woman shall be settled upon termination of her service due to resignation for the reason of caring for one or more children with disabilities, on the basis of a participation period of (25) twenty-five years. The pension reduction stipulated in Article (36) of this Law / Act shall not apply to her, provided that the actual service period is not less than (20) twenty years, and the Public Treasury of the State shall bear the value of the actuarial cost resulting from the application of this Article.

The regulations shall determine the rules for applying this Article.

### Article 29

The Retirement account salary for the insured is as follows:

1 - Government sector:

The last subscription account salary.

2 - Private sector:

The average subscription account salary during the last three years of the subscription period, provided that the Retirement account salary does not exceed (130%) one hundred and thirty percent of the subscription account salary in the first year of the last three years before Retirement or the actual subscription period, whichever is less.

3 - Self-employed workers under the income bracket system:

The average subscription account salary for the entire subscription period

### Article 30

Except in the cases of termination of service stipulated in clauses (4), (5), (6) of Article (24) of this Law / Act, the pension must not be less than (15,000) fifteen thousand riyals for the pensioner from the government sector, and the Public Treasury of the State shall bear the actuarial cost of settling the pension to reach the minimum.

It may be permitted, with the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on the proposal of the minister, to apply the Ruling of the previous paragraph to the pensioner from the private sector.

The executive regulations shall specify the controls for the application of this article.

### Article 31

If the actual service period of the insured person exceeds thirty (30) years, the pension holder or their beneficiaries, as the case may be, shall be granted a bonus for the excess period, paid from the fund in accordance with what is stipulated by the regulations.

The benefit from the bonus stated in the previous paragraph does not prevent benefitting from any bonuses granted by the laws and employment regulations specific to the workplace.

### Article 32

If the Qatari Nationality is withdrawn or revoked from the insured person, or if he renounces it, and his actual service period exceeds one year, he is entitled to Lump-Sum Compensation.

If the Qatari Nationality is restored to the insured person and he returns to work at an employer subject to the provisions of this Law / Act, he may return the Lump-Sum Compensation paid to him within sixty (60) days from the date of his return to work, and complete his previous service period.

If the Qatari Nationality is withdrawn or revoked from the pensioner or if he renounces it, the pension is paid to his beneficiaries, and the pension is restored to him if the Qatari Nationality is returned to him in accordance with what is stipulated in the regulation.

If the Qatari Nationality is withdrawn or revoked from the beneficiary or if he renounces it, the payment of the pension to him is suspended, and his share is restored if the Qatari Nationality is returned to him.

The Ruling of this article applies to those referred to Retirement or their beneficiaries under Law / Act No. (24) of 2002 mentioned above.

### Article 33

If the insured is considered missing, the pension shall be settled on the assumption that his service ended by death In accordance with the provisions of this Law, and it shall be paid to the eligible beneficiaries. If he returns, he shall be treated In accordance with the provisions of this Law.

If the pensioner or the eligible beneficiary is considered missing, his pension or share shall be distributed to the eligible beneficiaries In accordance with the provisions of this Law.

The Ruling of this article shall apply to those referred to Retirement or their eligible beneficiaries under the Law No. (24) of 2002 referred to above.

The regulations shall specify the cases, conditions, controls, and procedures for implementing this article.

### Article 34

The pension is calculated based on (1/30) Part of thirty of the pension account salary multiplied by the number of years of the subscription period, provided that the pension does not exceed the amount of the pension account salary.

### Article 35

In calculating the age for pension settlement, a fraction of a month is considered a full month, and any period exceeding six (6) months is considered a full year.

### Article 36

In the event of termination of the insured person's service due to resignation or in accordance with the Ruling of Article (24/Item 6) of this Law / Act, the pension will be reduced by (2.5%) two and a half percent; and if their service is terminated by a disciplinary decision or due to the issuance of a Final Judgment against them in a crime involving dishonor or breach of trust, the pension will be reduced by (3%) three percent, for each year of the difference between the age at the end of service and the age of (60) sixty for males and (55) fifty-five for females.

### Article 37

The pension is paid at the end of the month in which it became due, whenever the conditions stipulated by law are met.

In the event that the Pension Settlement is not completed in the month following the end of service, for any reason, the pension determined by the Authority shall be paid until the final settlement is completed.

The remainder of the pension due after the final settlement shall be paid in a single payment, and any excess paid shall be recovered in monthly installments not exceeding one year from the date of the final settlement.

The regulations shall specify the conditions and controls necessary for the settlement and payment of the pension.

### Article 38

If the pension holder is appointed or reappointed in one of the government sector entities subject to the provisions of this Law, Suspension of Pension Payment applies to him during the period of his employment, and participation for his new period of service is mandatory. If his salary is less than his pension, the difference will be paid to him from the fund.

The pension of the person appointed or reappointed in accordance with the ruling of the first paragraph of this Article will be calculated, upon the end of his new service, based on the pension calculation salary for that period, and he will be paid this in addition to his suspended pension, provided that this period is not less than eight (8) years in cases other than death or disability. If the actual new service period is less than this period, only his suspended pension will be paid to him, and he will be entitled to Lump-Sum Compensation for that period in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

The ruling of this Article applies to the pension holder returning to work in accordance with Law No. (24) of 2002 referred to.

The regulation specifies the procedures and controls for applying this Article.

### Article 39

If the pension beneficiary is appointed or re-appointed in a private sector entity subject to the provisions of this Law / Act, he may choose between continuing to receive the pension and not being subject to the provisions of this Law / Act, or Suspension of Pension Payment and being subject to the provisions of this Law / Act. In the case of choosing to be subject to the provisions of this Law / Act, the provisions of the second paragraph of the previous article shall apply. In all cases, the subscription starts from the date of submitting the request to be subject to the provisions of this Law / Act, and he cannot add the period prior to submitting the request.

If the pension beneficiary from the private sector is re-appointed in the same entity where he worked before the end of his service, Suspension of Pension Payment will occur during his employment period, and the subscription for his new service period shall be mandatory. If his salary is less than his pension, the difference shall be paid to him from the fund, and his pension shall be adjusted in accordance with the provisions of the second paragraph of the previous article.

The Ruling of this article applies to the pension beneficiary returning to work in accordance with Law / Act No. (24) of 2002 referred to above.

The regulation sets forth the procedures and controls for implementing this article.

### Article 40

An insured person returning to work under the provisions of the previous two articles is not permitted to add any period prior to their referral to Retirement or to purchase notional service periods, even if they have not received a pension for them.

### Article 41

If the pension holder is appointed in one of the entities subject to the provisions of the Regulating Law / Act for the retirement and pensions of military personnel, Suspension of Pension Payment shall occur, and he shall receive the salary determined for him from the military entity.

### Article 42

If the insured does not meet the requirements for pension entitlement upon the end of their service, they have the right to request Lump-Sum Compensation if their contribution period exceeds one year, in accordance with the regulations.

If the contribution period is less than one year, the contributions paid by the insured will be refunded.

In all cases, the contributions paid by the employer are transferred to the fund.

The regulations specify the cases, controls, and procedures for the application of this article.

### Article 43

It is permissible, by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, to grant a periodic bonus, to all or some of the pensions, and the Public Treasury of the state shall bear the actuarial cost of the increase in pensions determined by the Authority, and it shall be paid to the Authority in a single payment.

### Article 44

The Council of Ministers may grant exceptional pensions to Qataris who are not entitled to a pension.

The Public Treasury of the State shall bear the value of the actuarial cost of the exceptional pensions in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

### Article 45

The right to claim the rights stipulated by this Law / Act expires after two years from the date of notification of the final Pension Settlement at the national address, or from the date of payment regarding other rights, except in cases of a request to recalculate these rights for an increase resulting from a settlement based on a Law / Act or a final Ruling, as well as material errors in the calculation during the Pension Settlement.

## Chapter Six - Eligible Persons (46-58)

### Article 46

If the insured person dies, the right to the pension is transferred to the eligible beneficiaries from the day following the date of the end of service, and it is paid at the end of the month in which it became due.

If the pensioner dies, the right to the pension is transferred to the eligible beneficiaries from the first month following the month in which the death occurred.

The pension is distributed to the beneficiaries according to the shares specified in Schedule / Table No. (1) attached to this Law / Act.

The pension is redistributed when new beneficiaries appear, and payment shall begin from the month following the date the Authority is notified.

### Article 47

If all or Part of a beneficiary's share is suspended or ends, redistribution is done among the remaining beneficiaries of the pension, in accordance with Table No. (1) attached to this Law / Act. If the reason for suspending the share ceases, the share is restored to the beneficiary starting from the month following the cessation of the reason.

In the case of divorce or widowhood of a daughter, sister, or mother, or in the case of a son or brother becoming unable to earn an income after the death of the insured person or the pension owner, and if none of them had previously been entitled to a share of the pension, the pension due to any of them shall be determined in accordance with what is specified by the regulation.

### Article 48

The right to the pension is transferred to eligible non-Qatari beneficiaries upon the death of the insured or pension holder from the following categories:

1- The non-Qatari widow.

2- Non-Qatari children.

3- Non-Qatari parents.

4- Non-Qatari brother or sister. The regulations specify the conditions and rules for the application of this article.

### Article 49

It is required for the Husband to be entitled to a share in the Retirement pension as follows:

1 - He must not be insured, a pension holder, or subject to any of the Retirement and social insurance laws.

2 - He must prove his inability to work by a report from the Committee medical, and his condition is reviewed every two years unless the Committee decides his permanent inability and the impossibility of recovery.

### Article 50

The widow is entitled to her share of her deceased husband's pension, and its payment is suspended if she remarries. She regains her right to it if she gets divorced or becomes widowed again. She is granted the larger share if she becomes eligible for a share in the pension of another husband.

### Article 51

To be eligible for the son's share in the pension, the following conditions must be met:

1 - He must not be insured or a pension holder, or subject to any of the Retirement and social insurance laws.

2 - He must not have reached the age of (21) twenty-one years, except in the following cases:

a- Those incapable of working, with a report from the medical Committee, and his condition is checked every two years, unless the Committee decides he is permanently incapable and his recovery is impossible.

b- A student in any educational stage beyond secondary education until completion of university studies, or reaching the age of (27) twenty-seven, whichever comes first.

### Article 52

The daughter is entitled to a share in the pension provided that:

1 - She is not married, and her right to the pension is reinstated if she gets divorced or widowed and her husband was neither insured nor a pensioner.

2 - She is not insured herself or a pensioner, and if her salary or pension is less than her share of the pension, the difference will be paid to her from the fund. In case she is entitled to a share in two pensions, she will be paid the difference from her larger share.

### Article 53

It is required for the parents to be eligible for a share in the pension that neither of them is insured, a pensioner, or entitled to a share in another pension subject to any of the Retirement and social insurance laws.

### Article 54

The brother or sister is entitled to a share in the pension provided that the following conditions are met:

1- They must meet the conditions specified in Articles (51), (52) of this Law / Act.

2- The father of the brother or sister must be deceased.

### Article 55

The children of the deceased son are entitled to their father's share in the pension if the following conditions are met:

1- They must fulfill the conditions outlined in Articles (51), (52) of this Law / Act.

2- They must not be entitled to the pension of the deceased son under any Retirement or social insurance Laws / Acts.

### Article 56

If any eligible person meets the entitlement conditions for more than one share in a pension, from the fund or from any other fund, only the largest share will be paid to them. If all shares are equal, the priority shall be as follows:

1- The pension due from the Husband or Wife

2- The pension due from the parents.

3- The pension due from the children.

4- The pension due from the brothers or sisters

### Article 57

By way of exception to the Ruling of the preceding article, more than one pension or salary may be combined, provided that the total amount does not exceed (100,000) one hundred thousand riyals, in the following cases:

1- The widow entitled to her share of her Husband's pension and her salary or the pension she is entitled to.

2- Children entitled to two pensions from their parents.

3- The Husband or son who is unable to work and is entitled to a share in a pension, and any other pension he is entitled to pursuant to the Act Regulating Persons with Disabilities.

4- Cases of combining two pensions or combining a pension and a salary, prior to the implementation of the provisions of this Act.

The maximum total shares in cases of combining more than one share for the same beneficiary may be amended by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

### Article 58

If the insured person or the pension holder dies without any eligible beneficiaries, the pension and the excess period bonus revert to the fund.

If eligible beneficiaries appear, Pension Settlement will be recalculated and redistributed among them, in accordance with the provisions of this Law / Act.

## Chapter Seven - Civil Pension Fund (59-69)

### Article 59

A fund called the "Civil Pension Fund" is established by the Authority to which the contributions stipulated in this Law / Act are paid, and the Authority manages it.

The rights and obligations of the Pension Fund established in accordance with the provisions of Law / Act No. (24) of 2002 referred to shall devolve to the Pension Fund.

The financial year of the Pension Fund begins on the first of January and ends on the last of December each year. The first financial year begins from the date this Law / Act comes into effect and continues until the end of December of the following year.

### Article 60

The fund is responsible for disbursing the financial rights established under the provisions of this Law / Act to the insured persons, pensioners, or beneficiaries.

### Article 61

The financial resources of the fund consist of the following:

1- The prescribed contributions in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

2- The amounts borne by the Public Treasury of the State, in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

3- Delay fines or any other amounts due to the fund, in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

4- The funds and allocations designated by the State.

5- The proceeds from the investment of the fund’s assets.

6- Any other resources approved by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

### Article 62

Disbursement of the fund's money shall be in accordance with the principles and rules specified by the regulations.

### Article 63

A decision is issued on the investment system of the fund's assets by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the Authority.

### Article 64

The fund’s assets and revenues are exempt from taxes and fees.

### Article 65

The amounts due to the Fund under the provisions of this Law / Act shall have a lien over all the debtor’s assets, and shall have priority over all debts after judicial expenses and alimony debts. The Authority has the right to collect them according to the rules in force for the collection of public funds, and it is permissible to pay them in installments, in accordance with the regulations and procedures specified by the bylaw.

### Article 66

The financial status of the fund shall be examined once every three years, and whenever necessary, by one or more external actuarial experts appointed by the Chairman / President. This examination must include an assessment of the value of existing obligations, and the results of the examination shall be submitted to the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

In the event of a deficit in the fund's resources, it shall be compensated from the Public Treasury of the state, according to the method proposed by the minister and approved by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers. In this case, the expert must explain the reasons for the deficit and the means to avoid it.

### Article 67

The minister may appoint one or more auditors. The auditor has the right, at any time, to review the fund’s books, records, and documents, as well as to request any information he deems necessary to properly perform his duties. He may verify the fund’s assets and liabilities, and shall submit his report to the minister accompanied by his recommendations.

### Article 68

In case the insured person transfers from a Retirement and social insurance system affiliated with one of the Retirement and pension funds to a system subject to another fund, the period of his service shall not be considered terminated even if he is entitled to a pension for the first period, and his rights shall be settled upon the end of his service, as if his entire subscription period was under one system.

The fund to which the insured person belongs at the end of his service shall be obligated to pay his entitlements for all periods of his service.

The regulation defines the rules for implementing this article.

### Article 69

A sum may be allocated annually from the fund to grant advances to pensioners, with the approval of the Minister, based on a proposal from the Authority.

The regulations specify the amount of these advances, the rules for their disbursement, the repayment of their installments, and cases of exemption from repayment.

## Chapter Eight - Dispute Resolution Committee (70-73)

### Article 70

One or more committees shall be established within the Authority, called the “Dispute Resolution Committee,” each of which is formed under the chairmanship of a judge of the rank of President at the Primary Court, and a Vice-Chairman of the rank of judge at the Primary Court, both appointed by the Supreme Judiciary Council, along with a number of members. The formation of the committees, determination of their other competencies, their operating system, and the rules and procedures to be followed before them shall be issued by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

### Article 71

Claims filed by concerned parties before the courts in disputes under the jurisdiction of the Committee for Dispute Resolution will not be accepted unless their applications have been submitted to the Committee and decided upon.

### Article 72

The Committee, to the exclusion of others, is competent to rule urgently on all disputes arising from the application of the provisions of this Law / Act.

The decisions issued by the Committee shall have the force of an executive instrument in accordance with the Law / Act.

### Article 73

The concerned parties have the right to Legal Challenge / Appeal against the decisions issued by the Committee before the competent Division of the Appeal to Higher Court, within sixty (60) days from the date of the Committee’s decision if the decision is issued in presence, or from the day following their notification at the national address of the Committee’s decision if the decision is issued in absentia. The court may suspend the implementation of the Committee’s decisions.

The competent Division of the Appeal to Higher Court reviews the Legal Challenge / Appeal expeditiously, and must decide on it within forty-five (45) days from the date of the first session before it. In all cases, it may rule for the Judgment / Ruling to be immediately enforceable without security.

## Chapter Nine - Penalties (74-81)

### Article 74

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, the penalties stipulated in the following articles shall be applied to the violations specified in each of them.

The Fine penalty shall be multiplied according to the number of insured persons affected by the violation.

### Article 75

He shall be punished by imprisonment for a period not exceeding six months, and by a fine not exceeding (30,000) thirty thousand riyals, or by either of these penalties, anyone who:

1 - Has not enrolled any of his workers subject to the provisions of this Law / Act in the Authority.

2 - Has not enrolled his insured workers based on their actual wages and periods of service.

3 - Has required his insured workers to bear the value of the contributions or part thereof, in violation of the provisions of this Law / Act.

The Court shall, Sua sponte / Motu Proprio, oblige the perpetrator of the violation mentioned in item (3) of this article to pay the insured the value of the contributions he has borne.

### Article 76

He shall be punished with imprisonment for a period not exceeding three months, and with a fine not exceeding (50,000) fifty thousand riyals, or with either of these two penalties, anyone who, in bad faith, submits incorrect data or refrains from providing the data requested by the Authority In accordance with the provisions of this Law or the regulation.

### Article 77

Shall be punished by imprisonment for a period not exceeding three months, and by a fine not exceeding (10,000) ten thousand riyals, or by either of these two penalties, anyone who prevents the employees of the authority who have Capacity of judicial enforcement from entering the workplace, or who does not allow them to inspect the records, books, documents, and papers required in implementation of the provisions of this Law / Act and the regulation.

### Article 78

The Penalty / Sentence stipulated in this Law / Act is doubled in the case of recidivism, and a person is considered recidivist, For the application of the provisions of this Law, if they commit a crime similar to one for which they were previously punished with one of the Penalty / Sentence prescribed for violation of its provisions, before four years have passed since the date of completion of the Penalty / Sentence imposed or its expiration by lapse of time.

### Article 79

The Official responsible for the actual Department / Directorate of the legal person shall be punished with the same penalties prescribed for acts committed in violation of the provisions of this law, if it is proven that he was aware of the violation, or if the violation occurred due to his failure to fulfill his job duties.

### Article 80

Employees of the Authority who are granted the capacity of Law enforcement Officer by a decision issued by the Attorney General in agreement with the Minister, shall have the authority to detect and record the Crimes that occur in violation of the provisions of this Law / Act.

They shall have the right to enter and inspect the workplaces where the insured person carries out his work or activity during official or customary working hours, as the case may be, to review records, books, and documents and obtain copies thereof, to record violations and prepare reports, as well as to request the necessary data from the Employer or the insured person, and to conduct Inspection and investigations necessary to implement the provisions of this Law / Act. For this purpose, the Authority may seek assistance from and coordinate with the competent authorities in the implementation of these procedures.

### Article 81

The minister, or whomever he delegates, may carry out Conciliation in the Crimes stipulated in this Law / Act, before initiating the Lawsuit / Legal Action or during its consideration and before a final judgment is issued, in exchange for paying half of the maximum prescribed fine for each, and removing the causes of the violation.

Conciliation results in the Lawsuit / Legal Action not being initiated or its termination, as the case may be.

## Chapter Ten - Transitional Provisions (82-83)

### Article 82

The requirement for the subscription period stipulated in Article (24/ Paragraph Two) of this Law is waived for those subject to the provisions of Law No. (24) of 2002 referred to, from the following two categories:

1 - Those whose subscription period upon reaching the Retirement age is (20) twenty years or more, and in this case, their pension is calculated based on a subscription period of (30) thirty years.

2 - Those whose subscription period upon reaching the Retirement age is (15) fifteen years and less than (20) twenty years, and in this case, their pension is calculated based on a subscription period ranging from (25) twenty-five years to (29) twenty-nine years, depending on their subscription period.

The Public Treasury of the state is obligated to pay the amounts covering the difference between the actual subscription period and the period upon which the pension is calculated.

### Article 83

By way of exception from the Ruling of Article (24) of this Law / Act, the conditions for entitlement to early retirement pension shall be determined in accordance with the table issued by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers

## Chapter Eleven - General Provisions (84-92)

### Article 84

It is permissible, by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the Authority, to amend the maximum salary for the contribution account, amend the prescribed contribution for adding the housing allowance, the contribution rates, and to increase the minimum and maximum pension amounts as stipulated in this Law / Act.

### Article 85

Exempt from court fees at all Levels of Litigation / Degrees of Jurisdiction are the lawsuits and appeals filed by the Authority, the insured person, the pensioner, or their beneficiaries, in accordance with the provisions of this Law / Act.

### Article 86

The pension may not be seized except to pay court-ordered alimony or to settle amounts owed to the employer or the fund.

In all cases, the amount that may be seized may not exceed one-quarter of the pension, after deducting the installment for any advance granted against the pension, and in cases of conflict, priority is given to alimony debt.

### Article 87

Pensions due shall be paid without being restricted by the registration of the Employee / Staff or the Employee / Worker, or by the payment of the due contributions that should have been made, provided that the existence of the relationship between the insured and the employer is proven. In this case, the employer is obliged to pay all due contributions and the resulting fines stipulated in this Law / Act, in accordance with the controls, conditions, and procedures specified by the regulations.

### Article 88

The employer is obligated to retain the files, records, books, and financial and administrative documents related to the application of the provisions of this Law / Act and the regulation, as determined by the regulation.

These files serve as the basis for paying contributions, settling pensions, and all insurance benefits.

The employer is also obligated to appoint a liaison officer with the authority, as determined by the regulation.

### Article 89

The periods and deadlines stipulated in this Law / Act and the regulation are calculated according to the Gregorian calendar, and a month means (30) thirty days, and a year means (365) three hundred and sixty-five days, starting from the first of January and ending at the end of December each year.

### Article 90

Subscriptions and any other amounts shall be paid to the fund in Qatari Riyal, through one of the Banks licensed in the State, which are specified by the Authority, and the fund shall not bear any administrative fees or foreign currency exchange rate differences for the Qatari Riyal.

### Article 91

The calculation of the actuarial cost stipulated in this Law shall be in accordance with the regulations and tables issued by a decision of the Chairman / President based on the actuarial assessment.

### Article 92

The tables attached to this Law / Act may be amended by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.
