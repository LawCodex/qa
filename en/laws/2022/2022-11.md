# Law No. (11) of 2022 amending certain provisions of the Income Tax Law issued by Law No. (24) of 2018

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

After perusal of the Constitution,

And the Income Tax Law issued by Law No. (24) of 2018,

Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

And the approval of the Shura Council,

We have decided to ratify the following Law:

## المواد (1-4)

### Article 1

The text of Article (2) of Law / Act No. (24) of 2018 referred to above is replaced with the following text:

Article (2):

"With due regard to the provisions of Articles (9 Paragraph Two/Section 2), (13) of the attached Law / Act, the provisions of the attached Income Tax Law / Act shall not apply to the following:

1- Ministries, other government agencies, and public authorities and institutions.

2- International Organizations, their offices, and their branches operating in the state.

3- Salaries and Wages, allowances, and anything similar.

4- Inheritances, estates, and capital gains resulting from the sale of an inheritance, a share thereof, or any right included therein."

### Article 2

The texts of Articles (1), (2), (4), (13), (16), (20), (24), (33), (34) of the Income Tax Law referred to shall be replaced with the following texts:

Article (1):

"For the application of the provisions of this Law and its Executive Regulation, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings set forth opposite each of them, unless the context requires otherwise:

Minister: Minister of Finance.

Authority: General Tax Authority (GTA).

Chairman: Chairman of the Authority.

Tax: Income Tax.

Person: Any individual, company, or any other body of persons.

Entity: Any legal person, or any legal arrangement that maintains separate financial accounts.

Activity: Profession or craft, or trade, or industry, or speculation, or any undertaking aimed at profit or income, including the exploitation of movable and immovable property.

Official: Chairman of the Board of Directors, or the delegated member, or the authorized Director / Head, or any person representing the company, entity, or establishment or managing its business, as the case may be.

Non-profit organization: Any entity that meets the following criteria:

1- It is established and operates in the State or in a foreign country where it is resident for tax purposes, in the following two cases:

(a) Exclusively for religious, Charitable Activities, scientific, artistic, cultural, sports, educational, or other similar purposes.

(b) As a professional organization, business association, chamber of commerce, labor organization, or agricultural organization, or a civic association operating exclusively to promote Social Care.

2- It is exempt in the State or in the foreign country where it is resident for tax purposes from Income Tax arising from the activities mentioned in the previous clause.

3- It does not have shareholders or members who have ownership or benefit rights in its income or assets.

4- No private person or profit-making entity may claim the income or assets of the entity, nor may the entity distribute its income or assets to a private person or profit-making entity, unless this is for the management of Charitable Activities of the entity, or as reasonable compensation for services provided, or for the use of property or capital of the entity, or as payment reflecting the fair market value of property purchased by the entity.

5- All assets of the entity, upon its dissolution, liquidation, or termination, are distributed or returned to a non-profit organization, the government, any political division (administrative or regional), or a governmental entity, as the case may be, in the State or in the foreign country where it is resident for tax purposes, and not distributed or returned to any entity that engages in activities not directly related to the purposes for which the entity was established. This includes associations and private institutions, Charitable Activities, and private institutions of public benefit.

Project: Carrying out any activity that generates income or profit.

Qatari Project: A project managed by a resident in the State.

Foreign Project: A project managed by a resident in a foreign country.

Business: Providing professional services and other independent activities.

Permanent Establishment: A fixed place of business through which the project wholly or partially carries out its business.

Immovable Property: Real estate, allocated real estate, and real property assets, including rights in payments versus exploitation of natural resources and their associated rights.

Distributed Profits:

1- Income from Equities / Stocks and usufruct shares or usufruct rights or mining shares or founders’ shares or other rights to participate in profits, other than claims on debts.

2- Income from other rights in companies that are subject to the same tax treatment as income from Equities / Stocks under the laws of the country where the distributing company is resident.

Interest:

1- Income from claims on debts of any kind, whether secured by mortgage or not, whether carrying the right to participate in the debtor’s profits or not, and in particular, income from government Securities, income from Bonds, including installments and prizes related to such Securities or Bonds or debt bonds.

2- Income from Islamic financial instruments where the essence of the Contract is akin to a loan, similar to Murabaha, Istisna’, Mudaraba, Musharaka, Ijara, and Sukuk Contracts.

Royalties:

Payments of any kind received in consideration for:

1- Use or right to use any copyright relating to literary, artistic, or scientific works including cinematographic films or tapes or disks used for radio or television broadcasting, or any patent, trademark, design, model, secret formula, or process, or for the use or right to use industrial, commercial, or scientific equipment.

2- Use or the right to use information related to industrial, commercial, or scientific expertise.

Fees for technical services: Any payments for any service of an administrative, technical, or consulting nature.

Resident: Any individual who has a permanent residence available to him in the State, or has resided in the State consecutively or intermittently for more than (183) one hundred eighty-three days in the year, or holds Qatari Nationality, and any entity whose place of establishment is in the State, provided that the main and actual place of its management and leadership is in the State.

Tax return: A statement of income, expenses, exemptions, and due tax submitted by the person according to the form approved by the Authority.

Full competition: A principle according to which transactions between related entities are recorded based on the terms that could have been obtained between independent businesses in similar transactions under similar circumstances.

Foreign Tax: Tax actually paid to a foreign country.

Financial year: The accounting period for which the project prepares its financial statements; it is the calendar year or twelve (12) consecutive months ending on the last day of any month except December, by the permission of the Chairman.

Gross income: The total income of the taxpayer during the financial year.

Net income: The gross income after deducting the allowances permitted by this Law.

Taxable income: The net income after deducting losses according to this Law.

Actual place of management: The place where executive officials and senior management employees of the entity make strategic, commercial, administrative, financial, and operational decisions, or exercise their daily business, and where employees and others employed undertake the daily activities necessary for operating the entity.

Taxpayer: Any natural or legal person or any other entity, obliged to pay the tax, or to comply with the other tax duties stipulated in this Law and the relevant Executive Regulations, and Decision / Resolution.

Regulation: The Executive Regulation of this Law."

Article (2):

"An annual tax shall be imposed on the total taxable income of the taxpayer, arising from sources in the State during the previous tax year."

Article (4):

"Without prejudice to tax exemptions prescribed by special Laws / Act or international Agreements, or which are decided according to the provisions of Article (35) of this Law, the following incomes shall be exempted from tax:

1- Incomes of associations and private institutions, associations and private charitable institutions, and private institutions of public benefit established according to the provisions of the regulating Law / Act for any of them.

2- Interest and banking returns due to natural persons, who do not engage in taxable activities in the State, whether resident or non-resident.

3- Interest and returns of Public Debt instruments and Islamic Securities, issued under the provisions of the State’s financial system Law / Act, and Bonds of authorities and public institutions.

4- Capital gains arising from the disposal of real estate or Securities, realized by natural persons, provided such real estate or Securities disposed of do not belong to the assets of a taxable activity.

5- Capital gains resulting from valuation of company assets when offered as an in-kind share in return for contribution in the capital of a joint-stock company resident in the State, provided that the Equities / Stocks corresponding to the in-kind share are nominative and not disposed of before five years have elapsed.

6- Capital gains realized by a Qatari Project from the disposal of the following:

a. Immovable property located abroad.

b. Movable property forming part of a Permanent Establishment owned by the Qatari Project abroad, including capital gains arising from the transfer of ownership of that Permanent Establishment, alone or with the whole project.

c. Foreign Equities / Stocks, shares, or other rights.

7- Fees of Board of Directors members and other similar payments received by a Qatari Project in its capacity as a member of the Board of Directors of a company resident abroad.

8- Profits from Equities / Stocks and other incomes arising from them, if the amounts distributed during the tax year are deducted from the following:

a. Profits subjected to tax under the provisions of this Law.

b. Profits distributed by a company whose profits are exempt from tax under the provisions of this Law or other Laws / Act.

9- Gross income arising from craft activities that do not use machinery, whose gross income does not exceed (200,000) two hundred thousand riyals in the year, and whose average number of workers does not exceed (3) three workers in the tax year, provided the activity is carried out through one establishment.

The Cabinet / Council of Ministers, upon the proposal of the Minister, may, by Decision / Resolution, amend the exemption conditions provided for in this clause.

10- Gross income arising from agricultural or sea fishing activities.

11- Gross income earned by non-Qatari companies engaged in air or sea navigation in the State, provided reciprocity applies.

12- Gross income of Qatari Nationality natural persons residing in the State.

13- Gross income of entities resident in the State and fully owned by Qataris.

14- Profits of entities resident in the State in proportion to the shares of the following persons’ profits therein:

a. Qatari Nationality natural persons.

b. Entities fully owned by Qataris.

c. Entities partially owned by Qataris in proportion to their shares of profits therein.

The provisions of this clause shall not apply to share profits of entities owned by the State, wholly or partially, directly or indirectly, and operating in petroleum operations and petrochemical industries.

15- Gross income arising from activities authorized for private entities registered in the State, or registered in another country and licensed to operate in the State, insofar as they conduct activities that are not primarily aimed at achieving profit.

16- The share of the non-Qatari investor in the profits of companies whose Equities / Stocks are traded in the financial markets.

17- The share of the non-Qatari investor in the profits of investment funds whose units are traded in the financial markets.

18- The share of the non-Qatari investor in profits arising from the trading of all Securities, including units of investment funds listed for trading in financial markets.

The Executive Regulation shall specify the terms and controls for exempting the incomes mentioned in this Article from tax."

Article (13):

"Government agencies, companies, associations and private institutions, associations and private charitable institutions, private institutions of public benefit, sole establishments, and any other entity specified in the Executive Regulation shall be required to notify the Authority of contracts, Agreements, and transactions they conclude, in accordance with the controls and periods specified by the Executive Regulation.

Without prejudice to regional and international tax Agreements in which the State is a party, the entities referred to in the previous paragraph shall provide the Authority, upon request, any information relevant to tax purposes.

Any person resident in the State must, upon the Authority’s request, submit a detailed statement of his foreign financial assets or his rights in foreign financial assets, if suspected, during tax inspection, of committing or participating in the acts specified in Article (26) of this Law.

For the purposes of the previous paragraph, foreign financial assets include the following assets:

1- Financial accounts maintained with a foreign financial institution.

2- Equities / Stocks, shares in foreign companies, partnerships, and trusts.

3- Foreign government Securities, Bonds, debt bonds, and any other form of indebtedness.

4- Real estate or real property rights.

The person required to submit the statement shall report his foreign financial assets or his rights in foreign financial assets even if none of those assets or rights affect the tax liability of that person for the reported financial year.

The statement referred to shall be submitted according to the controls and at the time specified in the Executive Regulation."

Article (16):

"The employees of the Authority shall maintain the confidentiality of information and documents that come to their knowledge or are in their possession during or on the occasion of performing their duties.

The employees of the Authority are exempt from this obligation when disclosing such information and documents in the following cases:

1- To the taxpayer or his agent, or to any government agency provided the taxpayer agrees, unless disclosure is prohibited by a provision of another Law / Act.

2- Upon request by any judicial body.

3- In the context of Information Exchange procedures under an international or regional tax Agreement in which the State is a party.

Any information obtained by the Authority from a foreign Competent Authority under an Agreement relating to administrative assistance and Information Exchange for tax purposes shall be treated as confidential and may only be disclosed to persons or authorities, including courts, administrative bodies responsible for assessment or collection of taxes, law enforcement, or prosecution related to them, or deciding appeals related thereto, or supervising these competences.

Those persons or authorities shall only use such information for the stated purposes; they may disclose this information in public court proceedings or in judicial decisions, and information obtained by a contracting State may be used for other purposes, if the laws of both states permit its use for such other purposes, and the Competent Authority of the supplying State allows such use."

Article (20):

"The taxpayer shall pay the due tax based on the return he submits, on the same day the return is submitted. In the event of receiving a Decision / Resolution by the Authority regarding assessment by amendment or estimation, and the lapse of the objection period stipulated in Article (17) of this Law without submitting an objection, the taxpayer shall be obliged to pay the tax and related financial penalties within thirty (30) days from the expiry of the period referred to.

In the event the taxpayer agrees to the Authority’s Decision / Resolution on resolving the objection, the due tax shall be paid within thirty (30) days from the date of notification of such Decision / Resolution.

In cases other than those provided in the two previous paragraphs, the tax and related financial penalties shall be collected in one payment within thirty (30) days from the expiry of the period specified in Article (18) of this Law without response, or from the date the taxpayer or Official is notified of the Authority’s response to the objection.

The Authority may, upon request by the concerned party, approve payment of the due tax and related financial penalties in installments as clarified by the Executive Regulation. If the taxpayer fails to pay any installment on the due date, all remaining installments become immediately payable.

The taxpayer may, if he paid Foreign Tax on income taxable in the State, deduct such Foreign Tax within the limits of the tax due in the State, subject to the following:

1- The Foreign Tax must be an Income Tax imposed by a foreign country or its political subdivisions or local authorities.

2- The Foreign Tax must be actually paid.

3- The Foreign Tax shall be reduced by any amounts reimbursed to the taxpayer by the foreign country.

No deduction may be claimed for Foreign Tax on income exempted from tax in the State."

Article (24):

"Except for acts constituting a crime pursuant to the provisions of Article (26) of this Law, the Chairman or his delegate shall impose a financial penalty in the following cases:

1- Any taxpayer who fails to submit the return within the period prescribed by this Law and the Executive Regulation shall be subject to a financial penalty of (500) five hundred riyals for each day of delay, up to a maximum of (180,000) one hundred eighty thousand riyals.

2- Any taxpayer who fails to pay tax within the period prescribed by this Law and the Executive Regulation, and any natural or legal person who fails to remit the amount of tax withheld at source within the specified dates, shall be subject to a financial penalty of (2%) two percent of the due tax amount for each month of delay or Part thereof, not exceeding the amount of tax due.

3- Any taxpayer who violates the Registration and notification provisions stipulated by this Law and the Executive Regulation shall be subject to a financial penalty of (20,000) twenty thousand riyals.

4- Any taxpayer benefiting from a tax exemption who fails to submit the tax return and the documents required to be attached to it according to the provisions of this Law and the Executive Regulation shall be subject to a financial penalty of (10,000) ten thousand riyals.

5- Any taxpayer who violates the provisions for submitting audited final accounts and keeping and retaining accounting records stipulated by this Law and the Executive Regulation shall be subject to a financial penalty of (30,000) thirty thousand riyals.

6- Except for government agencies, any entity that fails to notify the Authority of contracts, Agreements, and transactions it concludes according to Article (13) of this Law shall be subject to a financial penalty of (10,000) ten thousand riyals.

7- Any person who fails to withhold tax at source according to Article (9, second Paragraph / clause 2) of this Law shall be subject to a financial penalty equal to the amount of tax not withheld, in addition to paying the due tax amount.

8- Anyone who violates the provisions of Decision / Resolution issued according to Article (34, second Paragraph) of this Law shall be subject to a financial penalty not exceeding (500,000) five hundred thousand riyals.

9- Projects that fail to meet the requirements of physical presence and substantial activity as stipulated by this Law or the Executive Regulation shall be subject to a financial penalty of (15%) fifteen percent of their net income.

For the application of the provisions of clauses (1) and (2) of this Article, the delay period starts the day following the expiry of the last deadline to submit the return and ends on the date of submission of the return or payment of tax, as the case may be.

The concerned party shall be notified of the financial penalties imposed, as specified by the Executive Regulation."

Article (33):

"If the taxpayer enters into Agreements, transactions, or operations of which one of the main objectives is to avoid payment of due tax, the Authority may revoke the tax benefit obtained because of these Agreements, transactions, or operations as per the Executive Regulation.

The Authority may, in any of the cases referred to in the previous Paragraph, take any or all of the following actions:

1- Reclassify the transaction if its form does not reflect its actual substance.

2- Adjust the amount of tax due on the taxpayer or any other person related to the Agreements, transactions, or operations mentioned in the first Paragraph of this Article.

If a Qatari Project directly or indirectly participates in the management of a Foreign Project, or in its control or capital, or if a Foreign Project directly or indirectly participates in the management of a Qatari Project or in its control or capital, or if the same persons directly or indirectly participate in the management of a Qatari Project and a Foreign Project, or in their control or capital, and conditions are set or imposed in any of these cases between the two projects in their commercial or financial relations that differ from those that might be set between independent projects, any profits that could have accrued to either project, had those conditions not been applied, may be included in the profits of the Qatari Project and subjected to tax accordingly.

No entity shall be entitled to any tax exemption or benefit unless its actual place of management is in the State."

Article (34):

"For the application of the provisions of this Law, the implementation of this Law shall not affect any obligations imposed under international Agreements or arrangements in which the State is a party, in the field of Information Exchange for tax purposes or combating international tax avoidance.

The Executive Regulation clarifies the necessary provisions to address requirements arising from the digitization of the economy and sets a minimum tax on entities in the State based on their excess profits determined in a manner equivalent to global rules for combating base erosion, provided it is not less than (15%) fifteen percent, and determines the scope, terms, and procedures for its application.

The Minister shall issue the necessary Decision / Resolution to implement those obligations, and his Decision / Resolution in this regard is binding on all entities and bodies in the State, including those that apply special tax systems under the regulating Laws / Act."

### Article 3

The following provisions are added to the Income Tax Law referred to:

Article (2 bis):

"Subject to tax is the income of a Qatari project from real estate located abroad and the income generated from the direct use of immovable property or its leasing or use in any other form, taking into account the following:

1- That the Qatari project benefiting from the income from immovable property does not engage in business in a foreign State where the real estate generating such income is located, through a permanent establishment located therein.

2- That the immovable property for which the income is actually paid is not actually connected to that permanent establishment."

Article (2 bis/1):

"Subject to tax are dividends distributed by a foreign company resident abroad to a Qatari project, as well as interest and royalties arising abroad and paid to a Qatari project, with consideration of the following:

1- That the Qatari project benefiting from the distributed dividends or interest or royalties does not conduct business in a foreign State where the company paying the distributed dividends is resident or where the interest or royalties originated, through a permanent establishment located therein.

2- That the equities or shares or other rights in respect of which the dividends were paid, or the debt claims in respect of which the interest was paid, or the rights or property in respect of which the royalties were paid, are not actually connected to that permanent establishment."

Article (2 bis/2):

"Subject to tax are the fees for technical services that arise abroad and are paid to a Qatari project, taking into account the following:

1- That the Qatari project benefiting from the fees for technical services does not conduct business in the foreign State where the fees for technical services arise through a permanent establishment located in that foreign State.

2- That the fees for technical services are not actually connected to that permanent establishment."

Article (2 bis/3):

"Subject to tax are the profits arising from the disposal of property abroad by any Qatari project."

Article (2 bis/4):

"If a Qatari project conducts business in a foreign State through a permanent establishment located therein, the profits attributable to the permanent establishment shall not be subject to tax, provided that they are subject to tax in that foreign State."

Article (2 bis/5):

"Taxable income includes income derived from abroad from the following services:

1- Granting distribution rights of products or services.

2- Payments for providing marketing services, procurement, financial mediation, agency, and other mediation services.

3- Fees paid for obtaining guarantees or similar financial support.

4- Provision of Telecommunications Services and broadcasting services."

Article (11 bis):

"An entity that meets the criteria determined by the regulations must submit to the Authority a Report on the minimum indicators of its substantial activities, according to the method and within the period determined by a ministerial decision.

The entity, in relation to any financial year reported, shall be deemed not to conduct substantial activity in the State in the following cases:

1- If it does not submit the Report referred to in the first paragraph of this Article.

2- If it does not satisfy one of the minimum indicators of substantial activity determined by the regulations.

3- If it does not submit to the Authority, upon request, documentary evidence of those indicators.

The Authority may, if the entity does not have the minimum indicators of substantial activity, refuse to issue it a tax residence certificate."

Article (13 bis):

"The Authority may obtain information and documents for the purpose of conducting a tax audit, or for the purpose of exchanging them with competent foreign authorities, in accordance with the agreements related to administrative assistance and the exchange of information for tax purposes, from any person or entity that has or controls such information and documents.

It is not required, for the information to be obtained for exchange with competent foreign authorities, that the Authority needs it for its own tax purposes, or that the Authority perform an audit on the taxpayer who possesses or controls such information, or who is concerned with such information, or that the acts for which information is sought be criminalized in the State.

The Authority has the right to obtain information for the purposes specified in the first paragraph of this Article in all tax matters, including information concerning criminal tax matters.

The Authority also has the right to obtain information for the purposes specified in the first paragraph of this Article from any person in the State who possesses or controls such information, regardless of any legal obligation on that person to maintain the confidentiality of the information.

Information shall be deemed to be under the control of a person if he has the legal right or power or ability to obtain the information or documents in the possession of another person."

Article (13 bis/1):

"Companies and other entities whose head office or place of effective management is in the State must, if requested by the Authority, provide the information determining their legal owners and their beneficial owners, including information about persons for whom the legal owners act as proxies, or under any similar arrangement, and information about persons in a chain of intermediary entities between the company or other concerned entity and the beneficial owner.

Partnerships established in accordance with the laws of the State or conducting their activity in the State must, if requested by the Authority, provide information specifying their partners and beneficial owners.

Trusts established according to the laws of the State, or managed in the State, or with a trustee or agent residing in the State, must, if requested by the Authority, provide information on the identity of their beneficial owners, including the grantor or settlor, trustee or agent, protector or guarantor, as the case may be, and all beneficiaries or classes of beneficiaries, and any other natural person who ultimately exercises effective control over the trust.

Non-profit organizations established in accordance with the laws of the State must provide the Authority with information specifying the founders, board members, existing beneficiaries, any of its beneficial owners, or persons having the power to represent it.

The information provided by entities and legal arrangements on the identity of their legal owners and beneficial owners must also be sufficient, accurate, and up-to-date."

### Article 4

All competent authorities, each within its area of responsibility, shall implement this Law / Act. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
