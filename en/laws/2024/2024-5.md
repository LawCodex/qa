# Law No. (5) of 2024 on the Regulation of Real Estate Registration

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

After perusing the Constitution,

And Law No. (14) of 1964 on the Real Estate Registration System and the laws amending it,

And Law No. (10) of 1987 concerning Public and Private State Property, and the laws amending it,

And the Civil and Commercial Procedures Law issued by Law No. (13) of 1990, and the laws amending it,

And the Civil Code issued by Law No. (22) of 2004,

And the Electronic Transactions and Commerce Law issued by Decree-Law No. (16) of 2010,

And Law No. (6) of 2014 on the Regulation of Real Estate Development, as amended by Law No. (5) of 2023,

And Law No. (16) of 2018 concerning the Regulation of Non-Qataris’ Ownership and Use of Real Estate,

And Law No. (8) of 2022 concerning the Expropriation and Temporary Appropriation of Real Estate for Public Benefit,

And Law No. (4) of 2023 concerning Inheritance Division Procedures,

Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

And upon the approval of the Shura Council,

We have decided to approve the following Law

## Chapter One - Definitions (1)

### Article 1

For the application of the provisions of this Law, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings specified opposite each of them, unless the context requires otherwise:

**Ministry**: Ministry of Justice.

**Minister**: Minister of Justice.

**Department / Directorate**: The administrative Unit concerned with Real Estate Register at the Ministry, or its Reporting to offices established by a decision of the Minister and entrusted to carry out all or some of its powers, as appropriate.

**Committee**: The Real Estate Register Committee stipulated in Article (44) of this Law.

**Real Estate Register**: The collection of documents and records dedicated to properties or real estate units, which indicate the descriptions of each property and its legal status, the rights associated with or upon it, as well as the transactions and amendments that occur to this status, and which the Law requires to be Registered or Recorded.

**Real estate Unit**: Any divided Part of a property that includes a group of real estate units and is issued an independent title deed.

**Unification of properties**: The merging of two or more properties into a single property, with a new title deed issued.

**Registration**: Evidence of dispositions or final judgments related to ownership right or any original real real property right in the Real Estate Register.

**Record**: Entering a summary of contracts or final judgments related to accessory real rights in the Real Estate Register.

## Chapter Two - Real Estate Ownership (2-3)

### Article 2

Without prejudice to the provisions of the laws regulating the ownership and use of real estate by non-Qataris, the right to own real estate is restricted to Qataris.

### Article 3

Without prejudice to the powers of the Committee stated in Article (45) of this Law / Act, the provisions regarding acquisitive possession leading to ownership by prescription shall not apply to registered real estate properties.

## Chapter Three - Documents Required to Be Registered and Recorded (4-13)

### Article 4

All actions that may create, transfer, change, or terminate ownership rights, or other original real property rights, as well as the final provisions confirming these rights, must be registered.

All actions that may create, acknowledge, transfer, change, or terminate a dependent real property right, as well as the final provisions confirming it, must also be recorded. If a mortgage is recorded on the property, it remains valid without the need for renewal.

The failure to register or record results in the aforementioned rights not being created, transferred, changed, or terminated, neither between the concerned parties nor in relation to others, and these rights have no effect except as personal obligations between the concerned parties.

### Article 5

No Case/Lawsuit regarding real estate ownership or other real rights attached to unregistered properties shall be accepted, except if it is an appeal against final decisions issued by the Committee in this regard.

### Article 6

Lawsuits / Legal Action related to a real estate right in rem, or the validity or enforceability of a disposition required to be registered or recorded, must include among the claims a request to change the data in the real estate register. The Lawsuit / Legal Action will not be accepted before the Court except after submitting an official copy of the Statement of Claim to the administration for annotation in the register with the contents of these claims, and delivering to the Plaintiff/Claimant/Complainant a Testimony thereof.

### Article 7

The operative part of the final Judgment / Ruling in the Lawsuit / Legal Action referred to in the previous article shall be indicated at the bottom of the annotation in the Real Estate Register on the Lawsuit / Legal Action record. The competent Court must notify the Department / Directorate of the Judgments / Rulings issued in those Lawsuits / Legal Actions.

### Article 8

The entry in the Real Estate Register regarding the claim records requires that the right of the Plaintiff/Claimant/Complainant, established by a final judgment and registered in accordance with the law, shall be binding on those in whose favor real rights have been established, starting from the date of this entry.

### Article 9

Anyone with an interest may request from the Department / Directorate the removal of the annotation referred to in Article (6) of this Law / Act, if the Lawsuit / Legal Action is rejected by a final judgment, or the Lawsuit / Legal Action is considered as if it did not exist, or in any case where the effect of the Lawsuit / Legal Action ceases, provided that an official Testimony is submitted from the clerk of the competent Court.

### Article 10

The proven right of inheritance must be registered whenever it includes real rights in real estate, and no disposition issued by the Heir regarding any of these rights may be registered before the right of inheritance is registered. The registration of the right of inheritance may be limited to a Part of the real estate of the Estate of the Deceased.

### Article 11

Any of the heirs, or their legal representative, submits a request to register the right of inheritance. The request must include the full name of the deceased, their nationality and place of residence, the date and place of death, the names of their heirs, the place of residence of each of them, the data related to the property and the real rights established on it, and any other relevant information required by the Department / Directorate.

### Article 12

The application for registration of inheritance rights must be accompanied by the Heirship Certificate, and other documents specified by the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act.

### Article 13

Taking into account the provisions of the laws organizing the establishment and registration of legal entities and related transactions, any transactions that create, establish, transfer, modify, or terminate a right of original or subsidiary real estate rights, as well as the Final Provisions confirming them, must be registered or recorded if they pertain to property included among the financial assets of the legal entity or in any case where the owner of the legal entity changes, with the exception of transactions involving Equities / Stocks and shares listed on the financial market, and in accordance with the procedures specified by the Executive Regulation of this Law.

## Chapter Four Registration Procedures, Entry, and Signature Authentication (14-33)

### Article 14

A register is created in the administration in which registration requests are recorded with serial numbers, according to the order in which they are submitted. Requests for obtaining documents and certificates are also recorded in it.

### Article 15

The procedures for Registration or Record or authentication of signatures are carried out upon the request of the concerned parties, or their representatives, in accordance with the forms prepared for this purpose, and in accordance with the procedures and conditions stipulated in this Law / Act and its executive regulations.

### Article 16

Each property is assigned an independent sheet in the Real Estate Register, indicating its physical status and all transactions pertaining to it.

### Article 17

The documents to be registered must include the following:

1- The data identifying the identity of each party, especially their full name, age, nationality, personal number, and place of residence.

2- Statement of the Capacity of anyone acting on behalf of another, and the authorities granted to them.

3- The information necessary to identify the property, especially its number, location, area, type, and designated purpose.

4- The subject of the document to be registered, and a statement of the consideration or debt, if any.

5- Data regarding the origin of the ownership right or the real right which is the subject of the transaction.

6- Statement of the established real rights on the property transacted, if any.

7- An acknowledgment from the party in whose favor the transaction is made, confirming that they have inspected the subject property and accepted it in its current condition.

8- Any other data relating to the document to be registered as requested by the Department / Directorate.

### Article 18

The Department / Directorate is responsible for collecting the data related to the property description and its type, based on the documents submitted by the concerned parties, and the Department / Directorate must verify the authenticity of these documents in any way it deems appropriate.

The Department / Directorate is also responsible, for the purpose of determining the prescribed fees, for assessing the value of the property if its value stated in the Contract differs from the market value.

### Article 19

The responsible Employee / Staff must, before certifying the signatures of the concerned parties, verify their identities and legal capacities using official documents, and read to them the content of the document and the consequences thereof, without influencing their will.

### Article 20

If a person acts on behalf of another in any transaction through representation, agency, authorization, Legal Guardianship, trusteeship, or supervision over the Endowment (Waqf), it is necessary to verify the Capacity of this person, the powers granted to them, and the official supporting documents.

### Article 21

The deed of the Legal Representative for the disposer must be specific and explicit in defining the powers of disposition granted to them, especially with regard to conducting sales, mortgages, donations, settlements, and acknowledgments.

### Article 22

Transactions submitted to the Department / Directorate for registration or recording shall be prepared in Arabic.

The Department / Directorate issues property ownership deeds in Arabic, and they may be issued, at the request of interested parties, in any other language.

### Article 23

The documents to be registered must include the following:

1- The name of the creditor, nationality, place of residence, personal number or proof of identity.

2- The name of the owner who established the right on his property, nationality, place of residence, personal number or proof of identity.

3- The date and number of the document verifying the debt.

4- The source of the secured debt, its full amount, accessories, and maturity date.

5- In the case of real estate possessory mortgage, a special statement regarding the lease to the mortgagor must be mentioned if stipulated in the mortgage contract.

6- Any other information required by the Department / Directorate regarding the registration of the document to be registered.

### Article 24

Only the Title Deed or the real estate record is accepted as Evidence / Proof of the origin of ownership or Right in rem of registered properties.

### Article 25

No right may be registered in the Real Estate Register unless its holder has acquired it from the holder of the previous Registration right. If successive actions have taken place, the last action shall not be registered except after registering the actions that preceded it in successive order, and after fulfilling the specific Registration requirements for each of them individually.

### Article 26

If more than one application is submitted to the administration regarding a single property, these applications must be examined according to the order in which they are recorded in the applications register.

### Article 27

If the procedures for the previous application are not completed due to a deficiency or error in the data or documents, the concerned party must remedy this deficiency or error within fifteen days from the date of being notified at their national address.

The expiration of the period mentioned results in the loss of the application's priority or the suspension of the procedures for subsequent applications, as appropriate.

### Article 28

The concerned party, upon being notified at his national address to complete a statement for which he sees no justification, and for whom the priority of his request is decided to be revoked because of this, may raise a Grievance to the Committee within ten days from the date of notification of the Decision / Resolution of completion or revocation.

The Committee must decide on the grievance within fifteen days from the date of its submission. If the Decision / Resolution is issued with rejection, it must be justified, and the Committee’s Decision / Resolution on the grievance is final.

### Article 29

The Department / Directorate issues the Title Deed based on what is recorded in the real estate register, according to the model prepared for this purpose.

### Article 30

The minister, or whom he authorizes, certifies the validity of the Title Deed and its conformity with the register before delivering it to the owner.

The deed is delivered to its owner, or to their legal representative, with Evidence / Proof of receipt. If there are multiple owners, each of them is given a deed including their share in the property, if requested, after payment of the prescribed fee.

Owners of other real or subsidiary rights on the property, based on their request, are given a Testimony containing their related data in the register, after payment of the prescribed fee.

### Article 31

Another copy of the Title Deed may not be issued, except in the case of damage or proven loss of the first copy, after payment of the prescribed fee, and in accordance with the procedures specified by the Executive Regulations.

### Article 32

The owner of the property and the holder of the Right in rem over it have the right to obtain certified copies of the title deed from the Department / Directorate after paying the prescribed fee.

### Article 33

The originals of the documents, Records, books, or documents related to registration or Record may not be transferred from the Department / Directorate.

The Court or the Competent Public Prosecution may review any of them and return them to the Department / Directorate.

Concerned parties shall be granted, based on a decision from the Court or the Competent Public Prosecution, or with the permission of the minister based on a substantiated request from one of the government entities, an official copy of the originals of the documents, records, books, and documents referred to. The appointed expert must also be enabled to review the originals.

## Chapter Five - Dividing and Consolidating Properties (34-36)

### Article 34

Applications for the segregation of properties into floors and apartments, or into several plots, are submitted to the Department / Directorate.

The Department / Directorate must ensure that there are no legal obstacles preventing the requested segregation and refer the application to the specialized Department / Directorate at the relevant entity to take the necessary procedures regarding it, provided that it is returned to the Department / Directorate to complete the required Registration procedures, In accordance with the provisions of this Law.

### Article 35

A real estate record is dedicated to each subdivided property, including information about its physical condition and ownership rights, and an independent ownership deed is issued for it.

Rights attached to the original property are transferred to all properties subdivided from it.

It is permissible to limit this to one or more of the subdivided properties, based on the approval of the rights holder.

### Article 36

Properties may be consolidated at the request of the owner, provided that the properties are contiguous. If the property is encumbered by a registered right for another party, the approval of the right holder must be obtained.

## Chapter Six - Real Estate Register (37-43)

### Article 37

The Real Estate Register includes the data for each property as follows:

1 - The name of the real estate area, the number, area, boundaries, descriptions, type of the property, and its designated purpose.

2 - The real rights and their documents, the name of the owner or co-owners in common, and the share of each of them.

The other data of the Real Estate Register, the rules for Registration and entry therein, are determined by a decision issued by the minister.

### Article 38

The engineering plan of the property and its cadastral number issued by the Competent Authority for Urban Planning affairs shall be the basis for determining its location, clarifying its boundaries, and specifying its area

### Article 39

The data of the Real Estate Register is evidence against all parties.

### Article 40

No change may be made to the data of the Real Estate Register, or deletion, registration, or entry in it, except by means of authenticated documents issued by those who have the authority to dispose of the rights established in the register, or by virtue of a Final Judgment.

### Article 41

The administration, on its own initiative or upon the request of concerned parties, may correct material and administrative errors in the real estate register.

The correction may only be made after notifying or announcing the concerned parties at their national address, in cases where required, and a report must be prepared clarifying the reasons for the error and how it was discovered.

The concerned parties have the right to Raising a Grievance against this correction by submitting a request to the Committee within fifteen days from the date of their notification.

The Committee must decide on the Raising a Grievance within fifteen days from the date of submission; if the Decision / Resolution is issued to reject it, it must be justified. The Decision / Resolution of the Committee regarding the Raising a Grievance is final.

### Article 42

The Department / Directorate must, when making any update to the real estate registry data, notify the concerned parties at their national address, in cases where necessary, within thirty days from the date of the update.

The concerned parties have the right to Raising a Grievance regarding this procedure, by submitting a request to the Committee within fifteen days from the date of receiving the notification or the publication of the update.

The Committee must decide on the Raising a Grievance within fifteen days from the date of its submission, and if the Decision / Resolution is issued to reject it, it must be justified. The Committee's Decision / Resolution regarding the Raising a Grievance shall be final.

### Article 43

Owners of real estate must notify the Department / Directorate of any changes in their descriptions. The notification must be accompanied by the relevant certificates, and the data of the Real Estate Register shall be amended accordingly, in accordance with the controls and procedures specified by the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act.

## Chapter Seven - Real Estate Registration Committee (44-47)

### Article 44

A committee called "Real Estate Registration Committee" shall be established in the Ministry. Its formation, regulations, and compensations shall be issued by a decision from the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

### Article 45

The Committee is responsible for the following:

1- Deciding on the applications submitted by interested parties for the Registration of unregistered properties within the boundaries of cities and villages, and the objections submitted regarding them.

2- Deciding on the objections submitted by interested parties concerning the Department / Directorate’s assessment of the value of properties for the purpose of determining the applicable fees upon Registration or Record.

3- Deciding on ownership rights of unregistered properties, and those expropriated for public benefit.

4- Considering objections submitted by interested parties related to properties registered in the name of the State.

5- Any other responsibilities determined by the Executive Regulations of this Law.

The Executive Regulations of this Law set out the procedures necessary to carry out the responsibilities of the Committee In accordance with the provisions of this Law.

### Article 46

The Committee is not permitted to rule on inheritance rights or the validity of lineage, and it must require the applicant to submit a legal inheritance inventory that includes the names of the heirs seeking Registration and the share of each one of them.

A request may not be submitted in the name of the heirs except by someone who is legally authorized to represent them.

The Committee notifies the concerned parties of its decisions at each one's national address.

### Article 47

Concerned parties have the right to a Legal Challenge / Appeal against the decisions of the Committee issued In accordance with the provisions of this Law before the competent Court within fifteen days from the date of their notification thereof.

## Chapter Eight - Penalties and Final Provisions (48-55)

### Article 48

Without prejudice to any more severe Penalty stipulated by another law, imprisonment for a period not exceeding three years and a fine not exceeding (500,000) five hundred thousand riyals, or either of these Penalties, shall be imposed on anyone who manages to register or record a document for a property, or to arrange, transfer, change, or cancel any real right to it in an unlawful manner, with knowledge of that.

Attempting to commit this Crime shall be punished with the same Penalty prescribed for the completed Crime.

In all cases, the Court shall order the cancellation of the procedures that have been taken.

### Article 49

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, a fine not exceeding (10,000) ten thousand riyals shall be imposed on anyone who violates the provision of Article (43) of this Law / Act.

The Minister or whomever he authorizes may conduct Conciliation in the Crime referred to in the preceding paragraph, before initiating the Lawsuit / Legal Action, or during its consideration and before it is decided by a Final Judgment, in exchange for paying half of the maximum prescribed fine and removing the cause of the violation. Conciliation entails that the Lawsuit / Legal Action may not be initiated, or is extinguished, as the case may be.

### Article 50

Without prejudice to the provisions of the Law / Act on Electronic Transactions and Commerce referred to, it is permissible to carry out the real estate Registration procedures stipulated in this Law / Act electronically, in accordance with the controls and procedures issued by a decision of the minister.

The copies, procedures, applications, and Electronic Transactions referred to in the previous paragraph shall have the same legal validity as original paper documents, and in the event of a discrepancy between them, the original paper documents shall prevail.

### Article 51

The organization and preservation of records, documents, and indexes related to real estate registration, as well as the disposal of items that no longer have value, shall be carried out in coordination with the relevant authorities in accordance with the regulations and procedures issued by a decision of the minister.

### Article 52

A decision by the minister is issued to determine the fees due for the services provided by the Department / Directorate in application of the provisions of this Law / Act, and the cases of exemption from them, as well as the forms stipulated therein.

### Article 53

The Minister shall issue the Executive Regulations of this Law / Act, and the decisions necessary to implement its provisions. Until such time as the Executive Regulations and the decisions referred to are put into effect, the provisions of the Executive Regulations and the decisions issued in implementation of the Law / Act No. (14) of 1964 referred to shall continue to apply, insofar as they do not conflict with the provisions of this Law / Act.

### Article 54

Law / Act No. (14) of 1964 referred to herein is repealed, and the exemptions stipulated in the aforementioned Law / Act shall continue until the necessary decisions to implement the provisions of this Law / Act are put into effect.

### Article 55

All relevant authorities, each within their jurisdiction, must implement this Law / Act. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.
