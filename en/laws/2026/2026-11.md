# Law No. (11) of 2026 regarding Owners' Association

**We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, Emir of the State of Qatar,**

After having reviewed the Constitution,

And Law No. (4) of 1985 on the Regulation of Buildings, and its amending laws,

And the Civil Law issued by Law No. (22) of 2004,

And Law No. (29) of 2006 on Building Control,

And Law No. (6) of 2014 on the Regulation of Real Estate Development, as amended by Law No. (5) of 2023,

And Law No. (24) of 2017 regarding the National Address,

And Amiri Decision No. (40) of 2022 on the organizational structure of the Ministry of Municipality,

And Amiri Decision No. (28) of 2023 establishing the Public Authority for Regulating the Real Estate Sector,

Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

And upon the approval of the Shura Council,

**We have decided to ratify the following Law:**

#### Article 2

The provisions of this Law / Act apply to all real estate development projects and jointly owned properties, owned in shares as determined according to unit entitlements, whether built, under construction, or undeveloped.

The provisions of this Law / Act do not apply to the following:

1. Buildings used entirely for administrative purposes by government entities.

2. Hospitality Establishments and tourist facilities subject to the Law / Act Regulating tourism.

3. Any other buildings or establishments determined by a decision issued by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

## الفصل الرابع (26)

### General Assembly (26)

#### Article 26

The General Assembly of the Owners’ Association is formed from all owners, in accordance with the provisions of this Law / Act and the regulations.

The General Assembly convenes at least once a year, or upon the request of the Board of Directors whenever the interest so requires. It may also be convened by an invitation signed by at least a quarter of the members of the General Assembly, or upon the request of the competent Department / Directorate if it deems it necessary.

The meeting of the General Assembly is considered valid with the attendance of more than (50%) of its members who have the right to attend the meeting. If the required number is not met, the meeting shall be postponed to another session held after one hour or within at most fifteen days from the date of the postponed meeting, and in this case, the meeting shall be valid with the attendance of any number of members.

Decisions of the General Assembly are issued with the approval of the majority of the members present. The majority is calculated according to the number of real estate units concerned by the association, provided that no owner’s share exceeds (50%) of the total number of votes, regardless of the number of units owned.

The regulations specify the controls for implementing this article.

## Chapter Seven (43-44)

### Administrative Official / Officer (43-44)

#### Article 43

The competent authority may, according to the regulations, appoint an administrative officer to manage the Owners Association for a specified period when necessary, and as required by the public interest, or if the Owners Association breaches its obligations stated in this Law / Act and the regulations.

#### Article 44

The appointment of the Official / Officer results in all positions of the Board of Directors becoming vacant, and the Official / Officer shall have the authority to exercise all the powers and duties of the Board of Directors.

The regulations specify other provisions governing the work of the Official / Officer.

## Chapter Three (16-39)

### Owners' association management (16-39)

#### Article 16

The management of the Owners’ Union is carried out by a Board of Directors composed of a Chairman / President, a Vice Chairman / President, a Treasurer, and one or more members, depending on the number of members of the General Assembly. The General Assembly elects them from among its members. If the number of unit owners does not exceed three, the Vice Chairman / President or the third member shall undertake the duties of the Treasurer.

The Authority appoints a temporary Board of Directors for the Owners’ Union of any shared property whose owners have not submitted a request to establish the Union according to the provisions of Article (4) of this Law / Act. All unit owners in this shared property are members of the Union.

In all cases, the Chairman / President of the Board of Directors or their deputy shall preside over the meetings of the Board of Directors and follow up its decisions, and for this purpose, may take appropriate Executive Decisions. The Board of Directors issues its decisions by majority vote.

The regulation determines the procedures and requirements for implementing this article, including the duration of Board of Directors membership and the cases in which it ends.

#### Article 17

If the owner is a legal entity, they may nominate one person as their representative in the elections of the Board of Directors. If the Unit is owned by more than one owner, one of them may be nominated in the elections of the Board of Directors.

The developer who owns more than one Unit in the joint property may nominate one person as their representative in the elections of the Board of Directors.

#### Article 18

The Board of Directors is responsible for implementing the decisions of the General Assembly.

The Chairman of the Board of Directors is responsible for managing the affairs of the union, convening meetings of the Board of Directors and the General Assembly, and handling the registration procedures of the union in the records of the competent Department / Directorate. He must notify the competent Department / Directorate of any amendments to the members’ data, in accordance with what is specified by the regulations. The Board of Directors may delegate others in this regard.

The Chairman of the Board of Directors also represents the union before the judiciary, competent authorities, and in dealings with others.

The Vice Chairman of the Board of Directors assumes the duties of the Chairman of the Board of Directors in his absence.

#### Article 19

The Board of Directors must work to achieve the interest of the Owners Union by exercising the necessary diligence to ensure that the Owners Union fulfills its obligations under the provisions of this Law / Act and the regulation, and it may, for this purpose, contract with individuals with sufficient expertise and knowledge.

#### Article 20

A member of the Board of Directors must disclose to the Board of Directors any conflict of interest, and the member of the Board of Directors who has a conflicting interest must refrain from Voting on any matter related to the conflict of interest.

#### Article 21

The Board of Directors is responsible for preparing the annual budget of the Owners' Association related to the costs of Department / Directorate of common properties and the maintenance of shared areas.

The financial year of the Owners' Association is determined in its bylaws as twelve months.

As an exception, the first financial year may be set for not less than six months and not more than eighteen months, starting from the date of the first meeting of the General Assembly.

#### Article 22

Disbursement from the operating account shall be made by bank transfer from the Chairman of the Board of Directors or his delegate for this purpose,

or by checks in the name of the Owners' Union, signed by both the Chairman of the Board of Directors or his delegate for this purpose, and the Treasurer, or by any other approved disbursement method.

#### Article 23

The Board of Directors may, by a majority of its members, grant Authorization / Delegation to the Chairman of the Board of Directors to carry out certain tasks, without prejudice to their responsibilities prescribed under this Law / Act or any other laws.

#### Article 24

A member of the Board of Directors may resign during their term of membership, without the Owners' Association being obliged to appoint a new member, provided that the remaining number is not less than three members; otherwise, a general assembly must be convened to elect the remaining members of the Council.

#### Article 25

The owners' association is dissolved if the number of owners falls below the required number for its establishment, and in the event of dissolution, its general assembly and board of directors are also dissolved.

In all cases of association dissolution, legal procedures specified by the regulation must be taken to settle its rights and obligations.

#### Article 27

A member of the General Assembly, if they are a legal entity or an owner of a property who does not occupy it, or multiple occupants of a single unit, must notify the Chairman of the Board of Directors of the name of the person they choose to represent them in the Membership.

In all cases, any member, even if he is the representative of a legal entity or an owner in the property, or multiple occupants of a single unit, may delegate whomever he deems fit to attend meetings of the General Assembly, in accordance with what is stipulated in the regulations.

#### Article 28

The General Assembly is responsible for the following:

1. Electing and dismissing the Board of Directors, in accordance with the procedures specified in the regulation.

2. Approving the Articles of Association of the Owners’ Union and any amendments thereto.

3. Appointing an auditor for the Union’s accounts.

4. Discussing and approving the Union’s annual budget.

5. Considering the discharge of the Chairman of the Board of Directors and the auditor.

6. Any other responsibilities necessary for the management of the Owners’ Union’s affairs.

Members must be provided with all documents, records, and data related to the agenda of the General Assembly well in advance of the meeting.

The regulation specifies the rules for applying this article.

#### Article 29

The invitation to attend the General Assembly of the federation is addressed to all its members at least fifteen days before the scheduled date of the meeting, and the invitation is conducted according to what is specified by the regulations.

#### Article 30

The General Assembly elects from among its members a Chairman at each meeting to conduct its Schedule of Work, and the Chairman appoints a secretary to prepare the Report / Official Record of the meeting. The topics listed in the Schedule of Work are discussed after it has been approved.

No meeting of the General Assembly may be chaired by the Chairman / President of the Board of Directors, the Vice Chairman, or the Treasurer, whether in person or through another person authorized by any of them.

#### Article 31

يجب أن يمتنع المطور أو مالك القسيمة عن التصويت على أي عقد للسلع أو الخدمات ، متى كانت له مصلحة في ذلك .

كما لا يحق له التصويت على قرار لاتخاذ إجراء في مواجهته لإخلاله بالتزاماته .

#### Article 32

The bylaws specify the other provisions regulating the work of the General Assembly, and the Voting mechanism within it.

#### Article 33

Owners must notify the Board of Directors of their place of residence or national address.

The owner is responsible for ensuring that the homeowners association records are updated whenever there is a change in their address.

#### Article 34

The owners' association member is committed to the following:

1. Paying the annual subscriptions and due maintenance expenses.

2. Complying with the decisions of the Board of Directors and the General Assembly, in accordance with the provisions of this Law / Act, the regulation, and other applicable laws.

3. Notifying the owners' association of any modification that occurs to the property.

4. Not causing harm to the building or to the common areas.

#### Article 35

يكون مالك القسيمة مسؤولًا أمام مجلس الإدارة عن سداد الاشتراكات السنوية المحددة وفقًا لأحكام هذا القانون ، عن جميع الوحدات المتبقية والالتزامات الأخرى .

ولا يجوز توثيق أو تسجيل عقد بيع هذه الوحدات أو أي تصرف عليها ، إلا بتقديم ما يفيد سداد المتأخرات مع الاشتراكات السنوية .

#### Article 36

The owner is obligated to carry out internal repairs for the unit they occupy and for all other specified parts they own or possess, whenever failure to do so may harm any of the owners or cause damage to the building.

In the event that the owner does not carry out these repairs, the competent Department / Directorate will execute them at the expense of the owner, adding (25%) to the actual cost of the repair works, without prejudice to their criminal liability for this matter.

#### Article 37

The owner is obligated to compensate the Owners' Association for any costs or financial obligations arising from the owner's, or his unit tenants' or occupants', non-compliance with the provisions of this Law / Act and the regulation.

#### Article 38

The member of the Owners' Association must notify the Chairman of the Board of Directors in writing if the unit is disposed of in any form.

The new member is not accepted in the Owners' Association until all Financial Obligations related to the unit have been settled.

#### Article 39

The general or specific successor of the owner is obligated towards the owners’ union with the same duties that were imposed on the predecessor, in accordance with the provisions of this Law / Act and the regulations.

## الفصل الثاني (3-15)

### Owners' Association (3-15)

#### Article 3

An owners' association is established if the number of owners of the separately owned real estate units in a joint property reaches three or more, including the developer or plot owner with regard to unsold units, provided their number is not less than three units.

An owners' association is also established for real estate complexes, whether all or some of them are residential or non-residential, owned or under usufruct rights.

An owners' association may be established to include more than one property, In accordance with the provisions of this Law and the regulation.

#### Article 4

The application for registration of the Owners Union is submitted to the competent Department / Directorate, either in paper or electronic form, using the form prepared for this purpose, attached with all the supporting documents, in accordance with what is specified by the regulations.

The competent Department / Directorate shall decide on the application for registration of the Owners Union and notify the applicant of its Decision, at his national address or by any means that provides proof of notification, within fifteen days from the date of submission. If the Decision is to reject, it must be justified. The lapse of this period without a response is considered an implicit rejection of the application.

Anyone whose application is rejected may raise a grievance to the minister within thirty days from the date of being notified of the Decision, or from the date of implicit rejection of his application. The minister shall decide on the grievance within thirty days from the date of submission. The lapse of this period without a response is considered an implicit rejection of the grievance, and the minister's Decision on the grievance is final.

#### Article 5

The competent Department / Directorate shall register the owners' association in its paper and electronic records, after verifying compliance with the regulations set forth in this Law / Act and the bylaw, and after payment of the prescribed fee.

#### Article 6

The Owners' Association has legal personality and an independent financial liability.

The Owners' Association acquires legal personality as soon as it is registered in the records referred to in Article (5) of this Law / Act, and in accordance with what is specified by the regulation.

#### Article 7

The Membership of the Unit owner in the homeowners association begins from the date the Unit is registered in his name or the right of usufruct is registered for him based on a legal document, whether he is a natural or legal person, and the Membership expires upon sale and transfer of ownership to another person or persons, or cancellation of the right of usufruct for any reason.

#### Article 8

The financial resources of the Owners’ Union consist of the following:

1. Proceeds of the Financial Obligations decided by the General Assembly for all owners.

2. Returns on investment of the Union’s resources, according to what is specified by the regulation, and such resources shall be invested in accordance with the law.

3. Donations received by the Union from members or others, as specified by the regulation.

4. Other resources determined by the General Assembly that arise from exploitation of the property.

The Treasurer is responsible for collecting the Financial Obligations decided by the General Assembly, preparing the estimated budget and final account, and presenting them to the General Assembly, according to what is specified by the regulation.

#### Article 9

The Owners Union, after obtaining the approval of the Authority, may include in the Articles of Association a mechanism to impose financial obligations on the owner in the event that he commits any act that leads to an increase in costs for the Owners Union. This is to cover these costs and in accordance with the controls and procedures specified by the Executive Regulations.

#### Article 10

The Owners' Association is responsible for maintaining the safety of the property and its parts and shared elements, ensuring its maintenance, restoration, and strengthening, preserving its architectural character, and providing the required services for the property. For this purpose, it is entitled to contract with building maintenance companies and demand appropriate legal compensation from anyone causing damage to the property or shared areas, in accordance with the regulations.

#### Article 11

The owners' association is responsible for supervising the shared properties and common areas, insuring and maintaining them, maintaining assets and replacing them when necessary, providing services related to the collective benefit of the owners, and keeping records.

#### Article 12

The Board of Directors, in order to collect the subscriptions and financial obligations determined by the General Assembly from a member of the owners' association, shall notify the member or the occupant of the Unit to pay within a period of not less than one week from the date of notification, and this shall be done twice provided that the period between both notifications is not less than fifteen days. If payment is not made within the specified periods, a late fee shall be incurred according to the rates specified in the regulations, and this fee shall be deposited into the operating account.

If payment is not made for three consecutive months, the Board of Directors may request the competent Department / Directorate to take measures to disconnect the electricity and water from the Unit.

A member of the owners' association or the occupant of the Unit may raise a grievance against this action to the competent Department / Directorate within fifteen days from the date of notification, and the competent Department / Directorate shall decide on the grievance within fifteen days from the date of its submission. If this period passes without a decision on the grievance, it shall be considered rejected, and the decision to resolve the grievance shall be final.

In all cases, it is not permissible to disconnect the electricity and water except after the grievance has been resolved, or after the expiry of the period prescribed for its submission or its resolution, as the case may be.

The disconnection of electricity and water shall be done in accordance with the provision of the previous paragraph, except in the cases, times, and according to the controls specified by the regulations.

#### Article 13

Subject to the Ruling of Article (12) of this Law / Act, the Board of Directors, in order to collect the subscriptions and financial obligations determined by the General Assembly on the member of the Owners’ Union, shall have Leasehold rights over the Unit and its appurtenances and its undivided share in the land and common areas of the property and what it contains.

The priority rank of the Leasehold rights shall be calculated from the date it is registered, and it shall be exempt from the prescribed fees and expenses for registering the Leasehold rights, or its renewal, or cancellation, or revocation of cancellation, all as determined by the regulation.

The Chairman of the Board of Directors or his deputy, as the case may be, after requiring the member of the Owners’ Union who has not fulfilled his subscriptions or financial obligations to do so, may request from the competent summary judge an order for enforcement, and the properties and movables subject to the Leasehold rights shall guarantee execution of the order or Judgment / Ruling.

#### Article 14

The developer or the plot owner, before delivering the Unit, may collect two years' subscriptions, provided that they are deposited directly in the name of the owners' association in the operating account, according to what is specified by the regulations.

#### Article 15

The owner's notification of any of his obligations or matters related to the owners' association shall be valid and effective if it is sent to his address registered with the Department / Directorate of the association.

The notification of the owners' association shall also be valid if it is sent to the association's headquarters, if any, or to the national address of the Chairman of the Board of Directors or the Treasurer.

## Chapter Eight (45-52)

### Sanctions and Final Provisions (45-52)

#### Article 45

Without prejudice to any more severe penalty prescribed by another Law, a fine not exceeding (50,000) fifty thousand riyals shall be imposed on anyone who violates the Ruling of Article (20) of this Law / Act.

#### Article 46

The Penalty of the Fine stipulated in this Law shall be doubled in the case of repeat offense, and for the application of the provisions of this Law, any person who commits an offense similar to an offense for which he has previously been punished with the Penalty prescribed in this Law, before the lapse of one year from the execution of the sentenced Penalty or its expiration by prescription, shall be considered a repeat offender.

#### Article 47

للوزير ، أو من يفوضه ، الصلح في الجريمة المنصوص عليها في هذا القانون ، قبل تحريك الدعوى الجنائية أو أثناء نظرها وقبل الفصل فيها بحكم نهائي ، وذلك مقابل سداد نصف الحد الأقصى للغرامة المقررة ، وإزالة أسباب المخالفة .

ويترتب على الصلح عدم جواز تحريك الدعوى الجنائية أو انقضاؤها ، بحسب الأحوال .

#### Article 48

The employees of the Authority, who are granted the Capacity of Law enforcement Officer by a decision from the Attorney General, in agreement with the Minister, shall have the power to detect and establish the Crime committed in violation of the provisions of this Law / Act.

#### Article 49

The persons addressed by the provisions of this Law / Act must adjust their situations in accordance with its provisions within six months from the date of its implementation. The minister may extend this period for a similar duration or durations.

#### Article 50

Where no specific provision is mentioned in this Law / Act, the provisions of the referred Civil Law / Act shall apply.

#### Article 51

The Cabinet / Council of Ministers shall issue the Executive Regulation of this Law / Act.

#### Article 52

All relevant authorities, each within its own jurisdiction, shall implement this Law / Act. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

## Chapter Six (40-42)

### Common areas (40-42)

#### Article 40

The share of each owner in the common parts shall be determined according to the mechanism and regulations specified by the bylaws.

#### Article 41

The common parts and the rights Reporting to them may not be subject to partition or seizure among all or some of the co-owners, or offered for sale by auction, apart from the separate parts.

Likewise, no owner may dispose of his separate share, rent it, or mortgage it without considering the specific common part Reporting to him.

#### Article 42

Each property or number of joint properties shall have Articles of Association in accordance with the template prepared by the competent Department / Directorate, which is published on the Authority's website.

The developer or the plot owner may, when preparing the Articles of Association for the joint property or for the owners' association after its establishment, amend or add specific provisions, provided that they are consistent with the provisions of this Law / Act and the regulations.

In all cases, no amendment, addition, or deletion is permitted except after obtaining the approval of the competent Department / Directorate.
