# Law No. (12) of 2026 regarding Animal Health

**We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, Emir of the State of Qatar,**

After having reviewed the Constitution,

And Law No. (9) of 1974 regarding neglected animals, as amended by Law No. (3) of 1994,

And Law No. (1) of 1985 concerning animal health, and its amending laws,

And Law No. (8) of 1995 regarding the practice of veterinary medicine, as amended by Law No. (5) of 1999,

And Law No. (14) of 2003 on the Regulation of veterinary quarantine, as amended by Law No. (9) of 2008,

And Law No. (18) of 2017 regarding public hygiene, as amended by Law No. (6) of 2023,

And Law No. (10) of 2019 on the Regulation of the possession of dangerous animals and organisms,

And Amiri Decision No. (40) of 2022 on the organizational structure of the Ministry of Municipality,

And the Agreement of the Office International des Epizooties signed in Paris on 25/1/1924, for which the approval of the State of Qatar’s accession was issued by Decree 34 No. (37) of 1994,

Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

And the approval of the Shura Council,

**We have decided to ratify the following Law:**

### Article 2

The Ministry, in cooperation with the Competent Authority, as appropriate, shall undertake the following:

1. Conduct epidemiological surveillance and the necessary tests to diagnose diseases and apply the required health measures.

2. Raise awareness about animal diseases and methods of their prevention and control.

3. Develop a program that includes a list of diseases that require vaccination and the required Preventive Measures and health measures.

4. Establish a database dedicated to livestock and their identification.

5. Vaccinate animals against diseases, and permit others to do so, provided there is medical supervision.

### Article 3

The animal owner, if he suspects or is notified of the animal being infected with a disease, must notify the Competent Authority within twenty-four hours, and promptly present it to a veterinarian for examination. Both the owner and the veterinarian must notify the Competent Authority of any disease listed in the table attached to this Law.

The Competent Authority, upon receiving the aforementioned notification, must verify its accuracy, identify the location of the reported animals, and the owner's data.

The owner of sick animals, or those suspected of being infected with a contagious, epidemic, or zoonotic disease, must isolate them in the places designated by the Ministry or the Competent Authority, as the case may be. The owner is not permitted to dispose of the animals or their products except with the joint approval of the Ministry and the Competent Authority. The animal owner bears the cost of feeding them during the quarantine period.

### Article 4

If it is determined that the animals referred to in Article (3) of this Law / Act are healthy, after the period specified by the Ministry or the Competent Authority for observation, as the case may be, their owners must receive them within two weeks from the date they are notified at the national address or by any means that indicates acknowledgment. Otherwise, it is permissible to sell them and deduct the necessary costs from their price.

### Article 5

The veterinarian, upon suspicion or confirmation of the presence of a contagious, epidemic, or zoonotic disease, must take all or some of the following measures, as appropriate:

1. Detain the sick or suspected animal, its waste, and the tools and materials used for its care.

2. Take measures to prevent the sale, transfer, or circulation of any products or waste from the infected or suspected animal.

3. Examine any animal, vaccinate it, or take a sample for testing as deemed appropriate; the veterinarian is permitted to enter any animal facility for this purpose.

4. Apply health procedures to the animal facility where the infected or suspected animal is located.

5. Ensure the effectiveness of the cleaning and disinfection operations.

6. Any other measures specified by the minister.

### Article 6

The minister issues a decision declaring any area as infected, and the area is not considered disease-free except after a decision is issued by the minister to that effect.

### Article 7

The Ministry, in cooperation with the Competent Authority, upon detecting an infected area, animal deaths, or notification of an animal being infected with a contagious, epidemic, or zoonotic disease, shall take the necessary health measures and procedures to control the disease and prevent its spread, which shall be decided by the Minister, after coordination with the Minister of Public Health.

### Article 8

It is prohibited to send pathological samples outside the State or to import them or conduct any scientific experiments on animals without obtaining prior permission from the competent Department / Directorate.

### Article 9

It is prohibited to trade or dispose of animals that are infected or suspected of being infected with a contagious, epidemic, or zoonotic disease, or their products or waste, or dead animals. It is also prohibited to transport any of these animals except with the approval of the Department / Directorate concerned.

### Article 10

If the animal dies as a result of contracting a contagious, epidemic, or zoonotic disease, the veterinarian must issue the necessary instructions for the safe disposal of the dead animal, its remains, or any tools or materials that were associated with that animal. The number of dead animals shall be recorded in the Report / Official Record, which is then submitted to the Ministry and the competent authority, in accordance with the regulations and timeframes issued by a decision of the Minister.

### Article 11

The competent authority has the power to order the safe disposal of any animal proven to be infected with a contagious, epidemic, or zoonotic disease. The animal shall not be returned to its owner unless its meat is suitable for human consumption.

### Article 12

The slaughter of animals, or the disposal of dead or slaughtered animals or any parts thereof, is prohibited except in locations designated by a decision from the minister specifying such places and the mechanism for disposing of those animals.

### Article 13

It is prohibited, without the approval of the Ministry, to disclose any information or data related to an animal confirmed to be infected with a contagious or epidemic disease

or shared disease, or to announce the health status of animals in the media or by any other means.

### Article 14

A committee called the "Animal Health Compensation Committee" shall be established in the ministry. It is responsible for reviewing Compensation requests submitted by owners of deceased animals or those disposed of based on a decision by the competent Department, estimating the appropriate Compensation for each case, and its formation, operating system, procedures to be followed before it, and its reward shall be issued by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

Rules for obtaining the Compensation referred to in the previous paragraph shall be issued by a decision of the Minister.

### Article 15

### Article 16

The private legal entity shall be punished with the Fine prescribed for any of the Crimes stipulated in this Law, if they are committed in its name or for its benefit.

The Official responsible for the actual management of the violating private legal entity shall be punished with the same penalties prescribed for acts committed in violation of the provisions of this Law, if it is proven that he was aware of the violation, or if his breach of the duties imposed on him by the Department contributed to its occurrence.

In all cases, the private legal entity shall be jointly liable for any compensations adjudicated by the Ruling.

### Article 17

The Penalty / Sentence stipulated in this Law / Act shall be doubled in the case of recidivism. Anyone who commits a similar crime within three years from the date of completion of the Penalty / Sentence imposed on him, or its expiration due to the passage of time, as the case may be, shall be considered a recidivist. All Crimes stipulated in this Law / Act shall be considered similar with regard to cases of recidivism.

### Article 18

Without prejudice to the penalties stipulated in this Law / Act, the Minister may, upon any violation of the provisions of this Law / Act, take any of the following measures, as appropriate:

1. Requiring the animal owner to attend at the specified time and place for injection or testing procedures.

2. Suspending the disbursement of allocations associated with the livestock ownership card, for a period not exceeding three months.

The concerned party may submit a Raising a Grievance to the Minister against the decision to suspend the aforementioned allocations within ten days from the date of notification at the national address or by any means by which knowledge is established. The Minister shall decide on the Raising a Grievance within fifteen days from the date of its submission. The lapse of this period without a decision shall be considered an implicit rejection of the Raising a Grievance. The Minister's decision regarding the Raising a Grievance shall be final.

### Article 19

Government veterinarians and employees of the Ministry and the competent authority, who are granted the Capacity of Law enforcement Officer by a decision from the Attorney General, in agreement with the Minister, the Minister of Public Health, and the heads of other relevant authorities, as the case may be, shall have the authority to take action regarding and prove Crimes committed in violation of the provisions of this Law / Act, each within their respective jurisdictions.

### Article 20

The minister shall issue the decisions that the public interest requires to be issued in order to preserve, sustain, and promote livestock wealth, and in particular in the following matters:

1. Designating infectious, epidemic, and zoonotic diseases to which the provisions of this Law / Act apply, methods of prevention and treatment, precautions to be taken to prevent their spread, and the procedures to be followed regarding sick, suspected, exposed, or healthy animals.

2. Procedures to be followed when herds of animals are moved from one place to another, monitoring animal gathering places such as markets and others, and the precautions or measures to be taken regarding them when any disease appears among them.

3. Defining the data to be included in the records prepared for injection and testing operations.

4. Setting the period for holding injected animals in barns and the procedures to be followed regarding those that die or yield a positive result after testing.

5. Procedures that may be taken to seize, treat, slaughter, or destroy infected or sick animals, with expenses collected administratively from the owner of the animal.

6. Measures to be taken regarding dogs and rabies, monitoring ferocious and vicious animals, and the cases in which these animals may be seized or destroyed and disposed of without compensation paid to their owners.

### Article 21

The minister may, by decision, amend the Schedule / Table attached to this Law / Act.

### Article 22

The Law / Act No. (1) of 1985 referred to is cancelled.
