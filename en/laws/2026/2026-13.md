# Law No. (13) of 2026 Amending Certain Provisions of Law No. (22) of 2017 on the Regulation of Real Estate Mediation Activities

**We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, Emir of the State of Qatar,**

After having reviewed the Constitution,

And Law No. (6) of 2014 on the Regulation of Real Estate Development, as amended by Law No. (5) of 2023,

And Law No. (22) of 2017 on the Regulation of Real Estate Mediation Activities,

And Amiri Decision No. (28) of 2023 on the Establishment of the Public Authority for the Regulation of the Real Estate Sector,

Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

And the approval of the Shura Council,

**We have decided to approve the following law:**

## Almawad (1-3)

### Article 1

The word "Ministry" shall be substituted for the word "Authority" wherever it appears in Law / Act No. (22) of 2017 referred to.

### Article 2

The texts of Articles (1), (3/First Item 7), (14/Item 3) of Law No. (22) of 2017 referred to, shall be replaced with the following texts:

**Article (1):**

« For the application of the provisions of this Law, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings set out opposite each of them, unless the context requires otherwise:

**Article (3/First Item 7):**

« 7-The individual must not have previously worked at the Authority, or at any of the administrative units related to real estate affairs and urban planning in governmental entities, unless three years at least have passed since leaving such employment.».

**Article (14/Item 3):**

« 3- A testimony from the competent Department / Directorate for real estate Registration at the Ministry of Justice, specifying the status of the property and including the restrictions, transactions, and all other data that the other contracting party should be aware of and which may affect their Decision / Resolution.».

### Article 3

All relevant authorities, each within its area of competence, shall implement this Law / Act. It shall be effective as of 01/01/2027 and shall be published in the Official Gazette.
