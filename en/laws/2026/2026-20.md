# Executive Regulations for the Organization of Personal Data Protection

This regulation aims to organize the mechanisms of Personal Data Protection, its processing and storage, to ensure the preservation of individuals' privacy and rights. Entities subject to the provisions of this regulation are obligated to take appropriate technical and organizational measures for Data Protection against unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or destruction.

The entity must obtain explicit consent from the data subject before collecting their personal data or using it for specified purposes. It is also required to provide the necessary means to update, correct, or request deletion of the data in accordance with applicable regulations.

The entity may share data with other parties if there is a legal basis or explicit consent from the data subject, with a commitment to the safeguards of protection and confidentiality stipulated in this regulation.

The provisions of this regulation apply to all public and private entities that process personal data within the State and specify the penalties and corrective actions resulting from violations of its provisions.

Summary

The regulation organizes Personal Data Protection and the mechanisms for its collection, processing, storage, and sharing, while defining the rights of data subjects, the obligations of processing entities, and the penalties for violations.

### اقسم الاول (1-3)

#### Article 1 (Mada 2) Amended

The collection of personal data must be for a legitimate, specific, and clear purpose.

Personal data may not be collected or processed except after obtaining the data subject’s consent or in the presence of a legal basis permitting it.

The entity is obligated to collect only the minimum data necessary to achieve the specified purpose.

#### Article 3 (ماده 3)

Entities must take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data from loss, damage, or unauthorized access.

The confidentiality of personal data must be maintained and not disclosed except in accordance with the applicable regulations and laws.

The entity is obligated to notify the Competent Authority and data subjects in the event of any security incident that may affect the confidentiality or integrity of the data whenever required by regulations.
