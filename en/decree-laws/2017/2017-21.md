# Decree by Law / Act No. (21) of 2017 amending certain provisions of Law / Act No. (34) of 2005 concerning investment Free Zones

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And Law No. (10) of 1987 regarding public and private property of the State, and its amending laws,

And Law No. (13) of 2000 on the Regulation of non-Qatari capital investment in economic activities, and its amending laws,

And Labour Law issued by Law No. (14) of 2004, and its amending laws,

And Law No. (34) of 2005 regarding investment Free Zones,

And Law of Qatar Central Bank and Regulating Financial Institutions issued by Law No. (13) of 2012,

And Law on the Financial System of the State issued by Law No. (2) of 2015,

And Law No. (21) of 2015 on the Regulation of Entry, Exit, and Residence of Expatriates, as amended by Law No. (1) of 2017,

And Law Regulating Tenders and Bids issued by Law No. (24) of 2015,

And Decree No. (64) of 2012 licensing the Economic Zones Company to benefit from certain lands,

And Amiri Decision No. (21) of 2006 establishing the Steering Committee for the Project to create investment Free Zones,

And the Draft Law submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

We have decided the following Law / Act:

## المواد (1-6)

### Article 1

The phrases "Free Zones" and "registered companies" and "Free Zone" replace the phrases and word "Investment Free Zones" and "companies, projects, establishments, and other entities" and "Zone", respectively, wherever they appear in the title and articles of Law No. (34) of 2005 referred to.

### Article 2

The texts of Articles (1), (2), (4), (10), (11), (14), (16), (17), (19), (20 items/3,4), (21), (30), (32 item/2), (33), (42), (44), (45), (48), are replaced with the following texts:

Article (1):

"For the application of the provisions of this Law, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings assigned to each of them, unless the context otherwise requires:

Free Zone: A geographical area within the territory of the State with defined area and boundaries, enclosed by a separating barrier, designated for practicing Economic Activities, and considered outside the customs scope of the State.

Authority: Free Zones Authority.

Council: Board of Directors of the Authority.

Chairman: Chairman of the Council.

Chief Executive Officer (CEO): Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of the Authority.

Economic Activity: Any commercial, industrial, agricultural, tourism, media, professional, craft, logistical, technical, service, or goods storage activity.

Registered Companies: The companies and other entities registered with the Authority, to practice an Economic Activity in the Free Zone in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the decisions issued in execution thereof.

Regulations: The regulations issued by the Council."

Article (2):

"Free Zones shall be established by a decree, based on a proposal by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, and their boundaries and coordinates shall be determined according to the maps and cadastral and planning schemes attached to the decree."

Article (4):

"In accordance with the Regulations, it is permissible to establish or create in the Free Zone limited liability companies, partnership contracts, or any other types of companies or entities, whether owned by one or more natural or legal persons, whether citizens, residents, or others, without being bound by the percentage of non-Qatari capital in the companies or increasing their authorized capital, as stipulated in the relevant laws.

Registered companies in the Free Zones shall practice the licensed Economic Activity in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the Regulations, and the contracts concluded between them and the Authority."

Article (10):

"Registered Companies are permitted to bring in and transfer foreign currency without restrictions through the banks operating in the Free Zone, in accordance with the decisions and instructions issued by Qatar Central Bank in this regard."

Article (11):

"Subject to the provisions of International Agreements and the Regulations issued regarding employees in the Free Zone, Registered Companies shall have the freedom to appoint their employees and organize their rights and statuses, provided that such rights are not less than those stipulated in the Labour Law referred to."

Article (14):

"Licenses of Registered Companies may not be cancelled or suspended except in the case of violation of the licensing conditions and by a decision of the Chief Executive Officer (CEO).

The registered company may raise a grievance to the Council within (15) fifteen days of being notified of the decision to cancel or suspend the License, and the Council shall decide on the grievance by a substantiated decision within one month of its submission. The lapse of this period without a response to the grievance shall be deemed acceptance thereof. The company shall have the right, within three months from the date of rejection of the grievance, to resort to the Dispute Resolution Center provided for in Article (44) of this Law."

Article (16):

"The following goods are prohibited from entering the Free Zone:

1- Flammable materials except for operational fuels permitted by the Authority and according to the conditions it determines.

2- Radioactive materials, except as exempted under the provisions of the Law.

3- Weapons of war, ammunition, and explosives of any kind, except by prior license from the competent authorities.

4- Goods in violation of the provisions of legislation and International Agreements related to the protection of Intellectual Property.

5- Hazardous psychotropic substances and all types of narcotic substances and their derivatives, except those required for the manufacture of medicines and pharmaceuticals.

6- Goods prohibited from import into the State."

Article (17):

"A public Authority called “Free Zones Authority” shall be established, with legal personality, an independent budget, and enjoying administrative and financial independence."

Article (19):

"The Authority aims to manage and develop the Free Zones in accordance with the best International Standards, and to encourage and attract investments in the fields of Scientific Research, technology, production, export, and others, and for that purpose may carry out the following functions and powers:

1- Conducting studies and determining proposed sites for establishing Free Zones and the activities that may be practiced therein and methods of supporting them, and submitting the necessary Recommendations to the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

2- Preparing the infrastructure, headquarters, facilities, administrative services, and any other services necessary to achieve the Authority’s objectives.

3- Supervising and controlling the management of Free Zones, following up on its affairs, and developing plans and programs necessary for their development.

4- Issuing Licenses, approvals, and permits to companies and other entities wishing to carry out Economic Activities in or through the Free Zone, as specified by the Regulations.

5- Determining and collecting fees and service charges it provides.

6- Collecting any taxes due to the State.

7- Facilitating work procedures between Registered Companies operating within the Free Zone and other entities outside it.

8- Entering into arrangements with suppliers to provide Information Technology (IT) and Telecommunications Services and equipment.

9- Establishing companies and entering into agreements necessary for developing the infrastructure of any Free Zone.

10- Setting the Regulations for the Authority and for the establishment, management, development, and operation of Free Zones and organizing the work therein.

11- Undertaking any permitted Economic Activities by establishing companies or contributing to some Registered Companies.

12- Appointing an advisory council composed of persons experienced in similar authorities’ activities to advise the Authority on strategy to achieve its objectives.

13- Appointing employees, consultants, and other service providers.

14- Cooperating and entering into arrangements with authorities, entities, bodies, and organizations carrying out similar activities.

15- Carrying out any tasks or duties entrusted to it by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers related to achieving its objectives."

Article (20 items/3&4):

"3- Regulations for granting Licenses and usufruct rights to Free Zone lands and facilities or leasing them to Registered Companies.

4- Prohibition of money laundering and illicit financial transactions, in coordination with Qatar Central Bank."

Article (21):

"The Authority shall be managed by a Board of Directors formed from a Chairman, a Vice-Chairman, and members with experience and competence from the various relevant sectors, appointed by an Amiri decision based on a proposal by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

The term of the Council shall be four years, renewable for one or more similar periods.

The remuneration of the Chairman, Vice-Chairman, and members of the Council shall be determined by a decision from the Amir.

The Council shall have a Secretary chosen by the Council, whose duties and financial remuneration are determined."

Article (30):

"The Authority shall have a Chief Executive Officer (CEO), appointed by a decision of the Council, who, under its supervision and within the general policy framework of the Authority, shall manage its administrative, financial, and technical affairs in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the prescribed Regulations and rules, and within the limits of its annual budget, and execute the decisions of the Council, and shall in particular have the following:

1- Proposing the plans and programs of the Authority.

2- Proposing the Organizational Structure of the Authority, and Regulations necessary for its administration and operating the Free Zones.

3- Registering companies and other entities wishing to carry out Economic Activities in the Free Zones.

4- Issuing Licenses and certificates relating to the practice of Economic Activities in the Free Zones.

5- Proposing the annual budget project of the Authority and submitting it to the Council for approval.

6- Preparing draft legislations and decisions necessary for the Authority’s work and submitting them to the Council for appropriate action.

7- Preparing an annual report on the achievements and work programs of the Authority and submitting it to the Council.

8- Preparing the financial statements of the Authority for the ended financial year and submitting them to the Council.

9- Signing contracts and agreements with third parties as mandated in accordance with this Law and the Regulations.

10- Proposing the fees and service charges provided by the Authority, administrative and financial penalties, and procedures for their collection.

11- Any other tasks assigned by the Council."

Article (32 item/2):

"2- Fees and service charges it provides."

Article (33):

"The Authority may borrow, including in a currency other than the State’s currency, to achieve its objectives, after the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

Any of the revenues, funds, or properties of the Authority may be provided as security for such loans or their interests."

Article (42):

"The regulation of Residence Licenses of workers in the Free Zones and members of their families shall be by a decision of the Minister of Interior, and shall include the following:

1- Entry visas for non-Qataris including temporary visas granted at the direct border entry points.

2- Recruitment of Workers and regulation of their residence in the Free Zone."

Article (44):

"A Dispute Resolution Center shall be established, exclusively specialized in resolving disputes related to the Free Zones by conciliation and arbitration.

The Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the Minister of Justice, shall issue a decision regulating the Center, its competencies, and the manner of Enforcement of Judgments and decisions it issues."

Article (45):

"The Authority shall be exempt from the application of Law No. (2) of 2015 and Law No. (24) of 2015, referred to."

Article (48):

"The Council shall issue the Regulations and decisions necessary for the implementation of the provisions of this Law."

### Article 3

The following Articles are added to Law No. (34) of 2005 referred to above:

Article (15) Bis:

"By a resolution of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the Chairman / President, additional incentives and benefits may be granted to registered companies that invest in sectors or economic projects considered strategic, or that work on increasing the proportion of the local component in their products."

Article (15) Bis/1:

"Registered companies shall, from the date of their registration and with respect to the licensed economic activity within the free zone, enjoy the following exemptions:

1- Exemption of profits from transit trade (transit) of goods in the free zone from all types of taxes, provided that transactions related to such trade are managed and executed in the public warehouses established in the free zone.

2- Exemption from the restrictions contained in the Regulating Law for commercial agencies."

Article (42) Bis:

"Qatar Central Bank shall undertake the development and monitoring of policies related to the regulation, licensing, supervision, and oversight of all activities of banks and financial institutions operating in the free zone."

### Article 4

Articles (3), (5), (22), (31/ second فقرة), of القانون No. (34) of 2005 referred to, are hereby repealed.

Also repealed is القرار Amiri No. (21) of 2006 referred to.

### Article 5

The first and third economic zones, the boundaries and landmarks of which are shown on the maps attached to Law / Act No. (34) of 2005 referred to, shall be considered a free zone according to the provisions of Decree No. (64) of 2012 referred to.

### Article 6

All concerned authorities, each within its jurisdiction, shall implement this Law / Act. It shall come into force from the date of its issuance. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
