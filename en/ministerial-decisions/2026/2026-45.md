# Decision of the Minister of Public Health No. (45) of 2026 regarding the determination of fees for Public Health research licenses

**Minister of Public Health,**

After reviewing Law / Act No. (6) of 2025 on the Regulation of health research,

And Law / Act No. (12) of 2025 regarding ministers,

And the Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (29) of 1996 regarding the Decisions / Resolutions of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers submitted to the Amir for ratification and issuance,

And the Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (36) of 2022 on the organizational structure of the Ministry of Public Health,

And the Executive Regulation of Law / Act No. (6) of 2025 on the Regulation of health research issued by Cabinet / Council of Ministers Decision / Resolution No. (12) of 2026,

And the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers for the draft of this Decision / Resolution in its regular meeting (19) for the year 2026 held on 20/5/2026,

**has decided as follows:**

## Almawad (1-2)

### Article 1

The fees for health research licenses stipulated in Law No. (6) of 2025 referred to, shall be determined according to the table attached to this Decision / Resolution.

### Article 2

All relevant authorities, each within their area of responsibility, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into effect on the day following its publication in the Official Gazette.
