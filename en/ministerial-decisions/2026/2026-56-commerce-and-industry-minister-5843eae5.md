# (Corrigendum) Decision of the Minister of Commerce and Industry No. (56) of 2026 issuing the Executive Regulation of Law No. (8) of 2020 on the Regulation of the Auditing Profession

**Minister of Commerce and Industry,**

After reviewing Law No. (8) of 2020 on the Regulation of the Auditing Profession,

And Amiri Decision No. (29) of 1996 regarding the Cabinet / Council of Ministers’ Decisions submitted to the Amir for ratification and issuance,

And the Executive Regulation of Law No. (30) of 2004 on the Regulation of the Auditing Profession issued by Decision / Resolution of the Minister of Business and Trade No. (18) of 2012,

And the Cabinet / Council of Ministers’ approval of the draft of this Decision / Resolution in its regular meeting (27) for the year 2025 held on 27/08/2025.

**Has decided as follows:**

## Almawad (1-3)

### Article 1

The provisions of the Executive Regulations of Law No. (8) of 2020, referred to and attached to this Decision / Resolution, shall be applied.

### Article 2

The Decision of the Minister of Business and Trade No. (18) of 2012 referred to above is hereby repealed.

### Article 3

All relevant authorities, each within their competence, must implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the day following its publication in the official gazette.
