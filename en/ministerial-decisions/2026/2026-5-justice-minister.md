# Minister of Justice Decision No. (5) of 2026 regarding the determination of fees for certain services provided by the Ministry of Justice

**Minister of Justice,**

After reviewing Law No. (6) of 2014 on the Regulation of Real Estate Development, as amended by Law No. (5) of 2023,

And Law No. (1) of 2023 concerning authentication,

And Law No. (5) of 2024 on the Regulation of Real Estate Registration,

And Law No. (12) of 2025 concerning ministers,

And Amiri Decision No. (29) of 1996 regarding the Cabinet / Council of Ministers' Decisions submitted to the Amir for ratification and issuance,

And Amiri Decision No. (3) of 2025 on the organizational structure of the Ministry of Justice,

And Minister of Justice Decision No. (29) of 2018 specifying the fees for some services provided by the Ministry of Justice,

And the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers for the draft of this Decision in its regular meeting (13) for the year 2025 held on 26/3/2025,

**Has decided as follows:**

## Almawad (1-6)

### Article 1

The fees for some services provided by the Ministry of Justice are determined, in accordance with tables number (1), (2) attached to this Decision / Resolution.

### Article 2

The same fees specified in Table No. (2) attached to this Decision shall apply to transactions of Registration and entry in the preliminary Real Estate Register.

### Article 3

Exemption from payment of the fees specified in Schedule No. (1) attached to this Decision / Resolution shall apply in the following cases:

1.

Transactions issued for the benefit of ministries, government agencies, and public authorities and institutions.

2.

Donations and bequests for charitable and philanthropic purposes.

3.

The transfer of an Employee to the location of the applicant for the transaction from among persons with disabilities and beneficiaries of social security.

4.

Transactions for notarizing contracts or incorporation documents of companies, or private institutions, or the Articles of Association of companies, or for private institutions for the first time.

### Article 4

Exemption from payment of the fees specified in Schedule No. (2) attached to this Decision / Resolution is granted in the following cases:

1. Transactions issued for the benefit of ministries, government bodies, and public authorities and institutions.

2. Donations, bequests, and endowments for charitable and benevolent purposes.

3. Contracts, documents, and papers related to real estate allocated by the State to citizens in accordance with Law / Act No. (1) of 1964 for the establishment of a public housing system, or Decree 34 No. (7) of 1977 on the Regulation of housing for senior Qatari employees, or Law / Act No. (2) of 2007 on the housing system, or any laws, regulations, decisions, or systems in force in this regard.

4. Inheritance documents with withdrawal, waiver, settlement, and specialization among heirs, based on a Judgment / Ruling or Decision / Resolution from the Family Court.

5. Judgment / Ruling of nullity, dissolution, restitution, or invalidation of a previously registered instrument, as well as a Judgment / Ruling to cancel or annul a previously registered Ruling.

6. Transfer of the Employee / Staff to the headquarters of the applicant with disabilities, and beneficiaries of social security.

### Article 5

The Decision / Resolution No. (29) of 2018 referred to is hereby cancelled.

### Article 6

All concerned authorities, each within its competence, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the day following its publication in the Official Gazette.
