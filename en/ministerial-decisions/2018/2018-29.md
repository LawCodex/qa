# Resolution of the Minister of Justice No. (29) of 2018 Determining the fees for Certain Services Provided by the Ministry of Justice

Minister of Justice,

Having reviewed Law / Act No. (14) of 1964 concerning the Real Estate Registration System, and the laws amending it,

And Law / Act No. (9) of 1979 concerning notarization, as amended by Law / Act No. (2) of 1996,

And Law / Act No. (21) of 2004 concerning ministers, as amended by Law / Act No. (12) of 2010,

And Emiri Decision / Resolution No. (29) of 1996 concerning the decisions of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers submitted to the Emir for ratification and issuance,

And Emiri Decision / Resolution No. (25) of 2014 on the organizational structure of the Ministry of Justice,

And Council of Ministers Decision No. (24) of 2003 amending certain notarization fees,

And the approval by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers of the draft of this Decision / Resolution at its regular (eighth) meeting of 2016, held on 24/2/2016,

And the memorandum of the General Secretariat of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers No.: Q M W /425/2017 dated 8/10/2017,

Has decided as follows:

## المواد (1-2)

### Article 1

The fees for certain services provided by the Ministry of Justice shall be determined in accordance with Schedules Nos. (1), (2) attached to this Decision / Resolution.

### Article 2

All competent authorities, each within its respective jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into effect one month after the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
