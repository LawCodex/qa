# Decision of the Minister of Transport and Communications No. (20) of 2018 regarding the regulations for operating the maritime intermediary

Minister of Transport and Communications,

After reviewing Law No. (8) of 2016 regarding marine vessels,

And Emiri Resolution No. (29) of 1996 regarding the Decisions of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers submitted to the Emir for ratification and issuance,

And Emiri Resolution No. (8) of 2016 on the organizational structure of the Ministry of Transport and Communications,

And the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers for this draft Decision in its regular seventh meeting of 2017 held on 15/2/2017,

has decided as follows:

## المواد (1-3)

### Article 1

To operate a marine intermediary, the owner or user must comply with the following regulations:

1- The marine intermediary must be technically seaworthy.

2- The captain of the marine intermediary must notify one of the Reporting to centers of the General Directorate of Coastal and Border Security at the Ministry of Interior of its destination when sailing.

3- The marine intermediary operating within the boundaries of the Corniche waters and docking there must be of the Sambuk or Shio'i type, made of teak wood, and must meet the requirements and conditions of traditional Qatari wooden vessels.

4- The number of passengers authorized for the marine intermediary is as follows:

a- Marine intermediaries less than (50) feet long are permitted to carry (25) passengers.

b- Marine intermediaries that are (50) feet or longer are permitted to carry (70) passengers.

A ministerial decision may increase the number of passengers allowed on the marine intermediary within the permitted limits, according to its manufacturing certificate and maritime safety requirements.

5- The weight of luggage on the marine intermediary must comply with what is specified in its manufacturing certificate; if this cannot be determined, the weight is set according to the results of the technical inspection.

6- The minimum number of sailors required to be present on board the marine intermediary when sailing is as follows:

a- (2) sailors for marine intermediaries sailing a distance not exceeding (5) nautical miles.

b- (4) sailors for marine intermediaries sailing a distance exceeding (5) nautical miles.

### Article 2

The competent department at the Ministry of Transportation and Communications may prevent any marine vessel from sailing in case of non-compliance with any of the regulations stipulated in this Decision / Resolution.

### Article 3

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into effect the day following its publication in the Official Gazette.
