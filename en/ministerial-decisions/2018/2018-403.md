# Decision of the Minister of Commerce and Industry No. (403) of 2018 extending the period for adjusting the conditions related to the application of the provisions of the Commercial Companies Law issued by Law No. (11) of 2015

Minister of Commerce and Industry,

After reviewing Law No. (21) of 2004 concerning Ministers, as amended by Law No. (12) of 2010,

And on Law No. (11) of 2015 issuing the Commercial Companies Law, and in particular Article (2) thereof,

And on Amiri Decision No. (20) of 2014 on the organizational structure of the Ministry of Economy and Commerce,

And on the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers in its ordinary meeting (36) for the year 2018 held on 12/12/2018 to extend the period for rectification of situations related to the application of the provisions of Law No. (11) of 2015 referred to, for a period of six months, as stated in the memorandum of the General Secretariat of the Council of Ministers No.: Q M W/489/2018 dated 20/12/2018,

Has decided as follows:

## المواد (1-2)

### Article 1

The period for rectifying the status of those subject to the provisions of the Commercial Companies Law referred to shall be extended for a period of six months starting from 07/02/2019.

### Article 2

All relevant authorities, each within its area of responsibility, must implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall take effect from the date of its issuance. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.
