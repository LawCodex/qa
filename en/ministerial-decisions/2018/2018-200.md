# Decision of the Minister of Culture and Sports No. (200) of 2018 to dissolve the Moater Center

Minister of Culture and Sports,

after reviewing Law / Act No. (21) of 2004 regarding ministers, as amended by Law / Act No. (12) of 2010,

and Decree 34 by Law / Act No. (5) of 1984 on the Regulation of clubs, and its amendments,

and Amiri Resolution No. (16) of 2014 assigning the competencies of ministries, as amended by Amiri Resolution No. (4) of 2016,

and Amiri Resolution No. (7) of 2016 on the organizational structure of the Ministry of Culture and Sports,

and Resolution of the Minister of Youth and Sports No. (45) of 2014 on the establishment of the Mowater Center and its attached articles of association,

and based on the proposal of the Department of Youth Affairs,

and for the public interest,

has decided as follows:

## المواد (1-3)

### Article 1

The previously established Mwatir Center is dissolved by Minister of Youth and Sports Decision No. (45) of 2014 as referred to.

All fixed and movable assets owned by the center, as well as all rights and obligations assigned to or against it, within the limits of the assets and rights transferred, shall be devolved to the Ministry of Culture and Sports.

### Article 2

The Department of Youth Affairs at the Ministry of Culture and Sports is responsible for directly undertaking the procedures for dissolving the aforementioned center and supervising the Enforcement of Judgments stated in the previous article.

### Article 3

All relevant authorities, each within its own jurisdiction, must implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the date of issuance. Every Ruling that contradicts its provisions shall be revoked, and it shall be published in the official gazette.
