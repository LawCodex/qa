# Decision No. (67) of 2018 granting some employees of the Ministry of Economy and Trade the Capacity of Law enforcement Officer

Attorney General,

After reviewing Law No. (12) of 1972 concerning compulsory pricing and determination of profits, and its amendments,

and Law No. (23) of 2004 issuing the Law of Criminal Procedures, in particular Article No. (27) thereof,

and Law No. (8) of 2008 regarding Consumer Protection, and its amendments,

and Law No. (5) of 2017 on the Regulation of dealing in subsidized goods,

and upon the proposal of the Minister of Economy and Commerce,

has decided as follows:-

## المواد (1-2)

### Article 1

Maryam Abdullah Rashid Al-Sheikh Al-Kuwari, an employee of the Ministry of Economy and Commerce—Second Market Controller—shall have the Capacity of a Law enforcement Officer for detecting and establishing Crimes committed in violation of the aforementioned laws and their implementing decisions.

### Article 2

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, must implement this Decision / Resolution, which shall be effective from its issuance date and published in the Official Gazette.

Dr Ali Ben Foutis Al Mirri — public prosecutor
