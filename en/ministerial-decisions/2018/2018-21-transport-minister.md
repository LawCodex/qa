# Decision of the Minister of Transport and Communications No. (21) of 2018 regarding the registration of marine vessels

Minister of Transport and Communications,

After reviewing Law No. (8) of 2016 regarding marine vessels,

And Amiri Resolution No. (29) of 1996 regarding the Decisions of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers that are submitted to the Emir for ratification and issuance,

And Amiri Resolution No. (8) of 2016 on the organizational structure of the Ministry of Transport and Communications,

And the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers for the draft of this Decision / Resolution in its seventh regular meeting of 2017 held on 15/2/2017,

has decided as follows:

## المواد (1-5)

### Article 1

The application for the registration of the marine vessel shall be submitted by the owner or their legal representative on the prescribed form from the Competent Authority at the Ministry of Transportation and Communications, accompanied by the following documents:

1- Title Deed.

2- Proof of identity of the owner of the marine vessel or their legal representative.

3- Proof of identity of the captain of the marine vessel, and evidence of his relationship with the owner.

4- Insurance policy issued by one of the national Insurance Companies.

5- Testimony of the manufacturing of the marine vessel.

6- Testimony of passing the technical inspection.

7- Evidence of payment of the prescribed fees.

8- Testimony issued by one of the accredited classification bodies for vessels operating in State projects.

9- Testimony issued by the Competent Authority regarding approval to operate in the field of tourism or fishing, as applicable.

### Article 2

To register the marine vessel, it must be equipped with navigational devices, Telecommunications devices, colors and lights, distress signals, and the necessary equipment and tools to meet security and safety requirements, as follows:

First: For marine vessels less than (20) feet in length:

1- Life jackets equal to the number of passengers the marine vessel is authorized to carry.

2- Waterproof handheld flashlight.

3- Compass or location determination device.

4- First aid kit.

5- Engine maintenance kit.

6- Navigational lights.

7- (Powder) fire extinguisher weighing at least (6) kilograms.

8- (Foam) fire extinguisher with a capacity of at least (2) liters.

9- (2) anchors with a rope not less than (25) meters in length.

10- Drinking water of not less than (6) liters.

11- Marine wireless radio.

12- Water pumping device.

13- Life ring.

Second: For marine vessels with a length of (20) feet or more:

1- The devices, equipment, and tools listed in item (First) of this article.

2- Two rocket flares and two handheld flares.

3- Additional (powder) fire extinguisher weighing at least (6) kilograms.

4- Additional (foam) fire extinguisher with a capacity of at least (2) liters.

Third: For marine vessels used in tourism or passenger transport in Qatari waters:

1- The devices, equipment, and tools listed in item (First) of this article.

2- Life raft with a capacity not less than the number of passengers and crew.

3- Side metal or wooden barriers.

4- Fixed, non-plastic chairs.

5- Passenger boarding ladder.

### Article 3

Marine vessels must be equipped with the devices, equipment, and tools specified in the previous article while present in the state's territorial waters and economic zone, and they must be in working condition, in accordance with the manual attached to this Decision / Resolution.

### Article 4

The competent department at the Ministry of Transport and Communications may prevent any marine vessel that is not equipped with the devices, equipment, and tools specified in this Decision / Resolution from sailing.

### Article 5

All relevant authorities, each within its area of responsibility, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the day following its publication in the official gazette.
