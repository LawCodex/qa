# Decision of the Minister of Commerce and Industry No. (44) of 2020 regarding the Executive Regulations of Law No. (1) of 2019 on the Regulation of Non-Qatari Capital Investment in Economic Activity

Minister of Commerce and Industry,

After reviewing Law No. (1) of 2019 on the Regulation of Non-Qatari Capital Investment in Economic Activity,

And Emiri Resolution No. (29) of 1996 regarding resolutions of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers submitted to the Emir for ratification and issuance,

And Emiri Resolution No. (12) of 2019 on the organizational structure of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry,

And the approval by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers of the draft of this Decision / Resolution in its regular meeting (19) for the year 2020, held on 6/5/2020,

has decided as follows:

## المواد (1-4)

### Article 1

In the application of the provisions of this Decision, the following two words and phrase shall have the meanings indicated beside each of them, unless the context requires otherwise:

Ministry: Minister of Commerce and Industry.

Minister: Minister of Commerce and Industry.

Competent Department / Directorate: the administrative Unit concerned within the Ministry.

### Article 2

Without prejudice to the legislation on the Regulation of non-Qatari practice of commercial activities and professions, and the provisions of Article (4) of Law / Act No. (1) of 2019 referred to, a non-Qatari investor may invest in all economic sectors up to (100%) of the capital, according to the following:

First: For a natural person:

He must not have been issued a Final Judgment in a felony or a crime involving dishonor or breach of trust, unless his status has been reinstated.

Second: For a legal entity:

1- It must be a company established in accordance with the law of the country where its headquarters is located.

2- The activity requested for investment must be consistent with its objectives.

In all cases, the non-Qatari investor must provide proof of fulfillment of the above conditions by means of documented and authenticated documents from the Competent Authority of the country of the main center and from the Qatari Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Third: For the non-Qatari investment project, the following must be available:

1- The project activity must be among the list of activities approved by the minister, based on the proposal of the Department / Directorate concerned.

2- The non-Qatari investor must submit a description of the activity, the business plan, and the financial plan for the project.

3- The non-Qatari investor must provide a written undertaking to bear all obligations arising from the project, and to commence his project within the period specified by the Department / Directorate concerned; otherwise, the decision to approve the project shall be deemed canceled.

### Article 3

The mechanism for deciding on requests from non-Qatari investors to exceed the shareholding percentage (49%) of the capital is determined as follows:

1- The investor submits the request to the competent Department / Directorate, using the form prepared for this purpose, accompanied by the supporting documents as specified by the competent Department / Directorate to meet the requirements of the Ministry and other relevant authorities, after payment of the prescribed fees.

2- The competent Department / Directorate registers the request in a special record prepared for this purpose, noting the date of the request, its serial number, the name of the applicant, the subject of the request, and a statement of the attached documents.

3- The request is referred to the relevant authorities to issue their approvals in accordance with the legislation applied by these authorities.

4- The relevant authorities must respond to the request within the specified period according to the requirements of the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for the service agreed upon between the competent Department / Directorate and the relevant authorities.

5- The competent Department / Directorate must issue its decision regarding the request within fifteen days from the date the required documents are completed and notify the concerned party by registered letter or any other means that confirms receipt. The lapse of this period without a response is considered an implicit rejection of the request.

6- In case of approval of the request, the competent Department / Directorate proceeds as follows:

A- Register the approval decision in the record referred to in item (2) of this article.

B- Notify the administrative Unit concerned with the Commercial Register in the Ministry of the approval decision, accompanied by the relevant authorities’ approvals of the request.

C- Notify the concerned party of the approval decision in accordance with the provisions of item (5) of this article, to undertake the procedures for registering the company that will execute the project in the Commercial Register.

### Article 4

All relevant authorities, each within its remit, must implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.
