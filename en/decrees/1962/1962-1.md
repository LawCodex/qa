# Decree No. (1) of 1962 on the Appointment of the Legal Adviser to the Government

**This Law / Act was repealed pursuant to Law / Act No. 5 of 1967**

We, Ahmed bin Ali Al Thani, Ruler of Qatar

After reviewing Article (5) of Law / Act No. (1) of 1962 on the Regulation of the higher administration of the government apparatus.

And based on what was presented to us by the Deputy Ruler

We have decreed as follows:

## Materials (1-3)

### Article 1

Dr. Hassan Kamel, the legal advisor to the government, is responsible for managing its legal affairs.

### Article 2

This Decree 34 shall come into effect as of the date of its issuance.

### Article 3

The Deputy Ruler shall execute this Decree 34, and it shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Ahmad Ben Ali Al thani — Ruler of Qatar
