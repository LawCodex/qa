# Decree No. (45) of 2026 increasing the Customs Tariff rate on certain selected items and products

**We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, Emir of the State of Qatar,**

After having reviewed the Constitution,

And the Law / Act of Customs issued by Law / Act No. (40) of 2002, as amended by Law / Act No. (10) of 2023,

And Law / Act No. (41) of 2002 amending the rate of Customs Tariff and canceling some Customs Exemptions,

And the International Agreement on the Harmonized System for the Classification and Coding of Goods, issued by approval for the State of Qatar to join it by Decree 34 No. (127) of 2004,

And Decree 34 No. (98) of 2024 concerning the integrated Customs Tariff,

And Decree 34 No. (63) of 2025 concerning the amendment of the integrated Customs Tariff,

And Decree 34 No. (31) of 2026 concerning the amendment of the integrated Customs Tariff,

And upon the proposal of the Minister of Finance,

And upon the draft decree submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

**We have decreed as follows:**

## Almawad (1-3)

### Article 1

The percentage of the Customs Tariff on chilled chicken items specified in Schedule No. (1) attached to this Decree 34 is increased to (18%).

### Article 2

The Customs Tariff rate is increased to (15%) during the peak agricultural production period on the categories of agricultural products listed in Schedule / Table No. (2) attached to this Decree 34, according to the timings specified in the same Schedule / Table beside each category.

### Article 3

All relevant authorities, each within their scope, must implement this Decree 34. It will take effect sixty days after its publication in the official gazette.
