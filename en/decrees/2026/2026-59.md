# Decree No. (59) of 2026 on the appointment of certain judges

**We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani Emir of the State of Qatar,**

After having reviewed the Constitution,

And the Judicial Authority Law issued by Law No. (8) of 2023,

And the Judges Affairs Regulation issued by Amiri Decision No. (52) of 2023,

And upon the proposal of the President of the Supreme Judiciary Council,

**We have decreed the following:**

## Almawad (1-5)

### Article 1

Each of the following is appointed to the position of Senior Vice President at the Appeal to Higher Court, and the seniority of each of them is determined as of 05/06/2026:

1. Mr. Abdulaziz Mithqal Sayir Al-Shammari.

2. Mr. Nasser Imran Ali Imran Al-Kuwari.

3. Mr. Abdullah Saud Yasser Al-Musallam.

### Article 2

Each of the following is appointed to the position of Senior Judge at the Appeal to Higher Court, and the seniority of each will be determined therein as of 08/05/2026:

1. Mr. Mohammed Hassan Jassim Al-Hail.

2. Mr. Hamad Saleh Taleb Al-Nabet.

3. Mr. Abdulaziz Hilal Yateem Al-Anazi.

### Article 3

Mr. Mohammed Ali Mohammed Al-Shaer Al-Sulaiti is appointed as a judge at the Appeal to Higher Court, and his seniority in it will be determined starting from 20/03/2026.

### Article 4

The following individuals are appointed to the position of Vice President at the Court of First Instance, and the seniority of each of them is determined from the date indicated next to their name:
