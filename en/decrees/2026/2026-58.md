# Decree No. (58) of 2026 Calling the Shura Council to the Second Ordinary Session of the Second Legislative Term

**Minister of Commerce and Industry,**

After reviewing Law / Act No. (8) of 2020 on the Regulation of the Auditing Profession,

And Resolution of the Amir No. (29) of 1996 regarding the Cabinet / Council of Ministers’ Resolutions submitted to the Amir for ratification and issuance,

And the Executive Regulation of Law / Act No. (30) of 2004 on the Regulation of the Auditing Profession issued by Decision / Resolution of the Minister of Business and Trade No. (18) of 2012,

And upon the Cabinet / Council of Ministers’ approval of the draft of this Decision / Resolution in its regular meeting (27) for the year 2025 held on 27/08/2025.

**Has decided as follows:**

## Almawad (1-2)

### Article 1

The Shura Council is convened for the second ordinary session of the second legislative term, corresponding to the fifty-fifth annual session, on the second Tuesday of the month of Jumada al-Awwal in the year 1448 Hijri, which corresponds to the thirteenth of October in the year 2026 Gregorian.

### Article 2

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, must implement this Decree 34. It shall take effect from the date of its issuance and be published in the official gazette.
