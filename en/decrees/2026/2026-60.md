# Decree No. (60) of 2026 appointing members in the Public Prosecution

**We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani Emir of the State of Qatar,**

After having reviewed the Constitution,

And the Public Prosecution Law promulgated by Law No. (9) of 2023,

And the Regulation of Affairs of Public Prosecution Members issued by the Amiri Decision No. (53) of 2023,

And upon the proposal of the Attorney General,

**We have decreed as follows:**

## Almawad (1-7)

### Article 1

The following individuals are appointed to the position of Senior Public Prosecutor, and the seniority of each of them in this position is determined as of 04/03/2026:

1. Mr. Saleh Ali Mohammed Al-Kubaisi.

2. Mr. Mubarak Shabeeb Nasser Al-Ramzani.

3. Sheikh Saud Faleh Saud Abdulrahman Al-Thani.

4. Mr. Khalifa Suleiman Faraj Ali Al-Abdullah.

### Article 2

The following individuals are appointed to the position of Public Prosecutor, and the seniority of each is determined as of 30/01/2026:

1. Mr. Ahmed Matar Dabet Mohammed Al-Dosari.

2. Mr. Youssef Jumaa Abdullah Obaid Al-Sulaiti.

### Article 3

The following individuals are appointed to the position of First Chief Prosecutor, and the seniority of each of them in this position is determined as of 20/05/2026:

1. Mr. Mansour Zayed Ali Al-Fuhaidah Al-Marri.

2. Mr. Nawaf Abdullah Mohammed Rabiah Al-Kuwari.

3. Mr. Saeed Mohsen Zayed Al-Khayarin.

4. Mr. Ahmed Ali Mohammed Al-Shaer Al-Sulaiti.

### Article 4

Mr. Abdulaziz Hamed Ali Namlan Al-Namlan is appointed to the position of First Deputy Prosecutor, and his seniority in this position is determined as of 25/04/2026.

### Article 5

The following individuals are appointed to the position of Deputy Prosecutor, and the seniority of each is determined from the date listed next to their name:

1. Mr. Mohammad Hussein Ali Al-Hawashleh Al-Dosari 10/03/2026.

2. Mr. Sultan Dahlan Jumaan Basheer Al-Hamad 10/03/2026.

3. Mr. Saeed Ibrahim Saeed Ibrahim Al-Maliki 10/03/2026.

4. Mr. Abdulrahman Nasser Mohammad Al-Humaidi 10/03/2026.

5. Mr. Ghanem Mubarak Rashid Al Hbab Al-Hajri 10/03/2026.

6. Mr. Hamad Abdullah Jassim Al-Murayziq Al-Marri 10/03/2026.

7. Sheikh Ghanem Ahmed Ghanem Al-Abdulrahman Al Thani 17/05/2026.

8. Mr. Ahmed Abdullah Mua’jib Jowaid Al-Dosari 21/05/2026.

9. Mr. Abdullah Majed Rashid Al-Bayat Al-Mansouri 21/05/2026.

10. Mr. Mansour Mohammed Hamad Al-Mashhadi Al-Maadid 21/05/2026.

### Article 6

Each of the following individuals is appointed to the position of First Deputy Prosecutor Assistant, and the seniority of each shall be determined from the date written next to his or her name:

1. Sheikh/ Abdul Majid Abdullah Khalid Hamad Al Thani 06/01/2026.

2. Mr./ Mohammed Ahmed Haji Abdul Malik Abdul Malik 26/01/2026.

3. Mr./ Faisal Hamdan Youssef Mohammed Al Marzouqi 26/01/2026.

4. Mr./ Saad Hamad Mohammed Hamad Al Qahtani 10/03/2026.

5. Mr./ Nasser Rashid Mohammed Al-Habab Al-Hajri 04/05/2026.

6. Mr./ Mohammed Jamal Mohammed Nasser Al Nuaimi 04/05/2026.

7. Ms./ Wadha Abdullah Jamaan Basheer Al Hamad 04/05/2026.

8. Mr./ Abdullah Zeidan Hosni Hussein Al Zeidan 04/05/2026.

### Article 7

All relevant authorities, each within their jurisdiction, must implement this Decree 34. It shall be effective from the date of issuance. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.
