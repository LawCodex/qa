# Decree No. (47) of 2018 ratifying the agreement between the State of Qatar and the Federal Republic of Germany regarding the encouragement and mutual protection of investments and the protocol Annex / Appendix thereto

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And the ratification document issued on the sixth day of Dhu al-Qidah in the year 1417 Hijri, corresponding to the fifteenth day of March in the year 1997 AD,

And upon the proposal of the Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Foreign Affairs,

And upon the draft Decree 34 submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

We have decreed as follows:

## المواد (1-2)

### Article 1

An agreement between the State of Qatar and the Federal Republic of Germany regarding the encouragement and mutual protection of investments, and the protocol Annex / Appendix thereto, signed in the city of Bonn on 14/6/1996, whose texts are attached to this Decree 34, was ratified, and they shall have the force of Law / Act, in accordance with Article (68) of the Constitution.

### Article 2

All relevant authorities, each within its own jurisdiction, must implement this Decree 34. It shall take effect from the date of its issuance and be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
