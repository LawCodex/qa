# Decree No. (45) of 2018 ratifying the Memorandum of Understanding regarding the conduct of a political dialogue on issues of mutual interest between the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the State of Qatar and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation of the Italian Republic

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And the ratification document issued on the twentieth day of Ramadan 1439 Hijri, corresponding to the fourth day of June 2018 AD,

And upon the proposal of the Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Foreign Affairs,

And upon the draft Decree 34 submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

We have decreed the following:

## المواد (1-2)

### Article 1

The Memorandum of Understanding regarding conducting a political dialogue on issues of common interest between the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the State of Qatar and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation of the Italian Republic, signed in Doha on 2/8/2017, the text of which is attached to this Decree 34, was ratified. It shall have the force of law, in accordance with Article (68) of the Constitution.

### Article 2

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, must implement this Decree 34. It shall come into force from the date of its issuance and shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
