# Decree No. (30) of 2018 ratifying the agreement on the promotion and mutual protection of investments between the Government of the State of Qatar and the Government of the Republic of Azerbaijan

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And the ratification document issued on the eighteenth of Rajab 1429 Hijri, corresponding to the twenty-first of July 2008 Gregorian,

And upon the proposal of the Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Foreign Affairs,

And upon the draft Decree 34 presented by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

We have decreed the following:

## المواد (1-2)

### Article 1

An agreement on the encouragement and reciprocal protection of investments between the Government of the State of Qatar and the Government of the Republic of Azerbaijan, signed in Baku on 28/8/2007, the text of which is attached to this Decree 34, was ratified and shall have the force of Law / Act, in accordance with Article (68) of the Constitution.

### Article 2

All relevant authorities, each in its field, must implement this Decree 34. It comes into effect from the date of its issuance. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
