# Decree No. (9) of 2018 ratifying the Economic Cooperation Agreement between the Government of the State of Qatar and the Government of the Republic of Poland

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And the ratification document issued on the seventh day of Dhu al-Qi'dah in the year 1438 Hijri, corresponding to the thirtieth day of July in the year 2017 Gregorian,

And upon the proposal of the Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Foreign Affairs,

And upon the draft Decree 34 submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

We have decreed as follows:

## المواد (1-2)

### Article 1

An agreement for economic cooperation between the Government of the State of Qatar and the Government of the Republic of Poland, signed in Warsaw on 5/5/2017, whose text is attached to this Decree 34, was ratified. It shall have the force of Law / Act, in accordance with Article (68) of the Constitution.

### Article 2

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, must implement this Decree 34. It shall come into effect from the date of its issuance. It shall be published in the official gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
