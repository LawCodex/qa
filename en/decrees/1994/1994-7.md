# Decree No. (7) of 1994 Amending Certain Provisions of Decree No. (22) of 1991 by Increasing the Percentage of Customs Duties on Tobacco and Its Derivatives

We, Khalifa bin Hamad Al Thani, Emir of the State of Qatar,

Having reviewed the Articles of Association (Amended Interim), and in particular Articles (23), (34) thereof,

and Law No. (2) of 1962 on the Regulation of Fiscal Policy in Qatar,

and Customs Law issued by Law No. (5) of 1988, and in particular Article (12) thereof,

and Decree by Law No. (22) of 1993 on the Regulation of the Ministry of Finance, Economy and Trade and determination of its competencies,

and Decree No. (22) of 1991 on increasing the percentage of Customs Duties on tobacco and its derivatives,

and upon the proposal of the Minister of Finance, Economy and Trade,

and upon the draft decree submitted by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

have decreed as follows:

## Materials (1-3)

### Article 1

It shall be replaced by the text of Article (1) of Decree No. (22) of 1991 referred to, with the following text:

«The rate of Customs Duties on imported tobacco and its derivatives, including cigarettes, cigars, and others, whether these goods are manufactured or raw materials, shall be increased to 50%.

The minimum Customs Duties to be collected shall be as indicated in the accompanying table».

### Article 2

Any Ruling that contradicts the provisions of this Decree 34 shall be repealed.

### Article 3

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, shall implement this Decree 34. It shall be effective from the date of its issuance and published in the Official Gazette.

Hamad Bin Khalifa Al-Thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
