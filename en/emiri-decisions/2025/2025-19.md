# Amiri Decree No. (19) of 2025 on the Establishment of the Qatari Institution for Supporting Innovation and Scientific Research

**We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, Emir of the State of Qatar,**

After reviewing the Constitution,

Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

**We have decided the following**:

## المواد (1-25)

### Article 1

For the purposes of applying the provisions of this Decision / Resolution, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings shown opposite each, unless the context requires otherwise:

**The Institution**:

The Qatar Institution for Supporting Innovation and Scientific Research.

**The Council**:

The Board of Directors of the Institution.

**The Chairman / President**:

The Chairman / President of the Council.

**Chief Executive Officer (CEO)**:

The Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of the Institution.

### Article 2

An institution called "The Qatari Institution for Supporting Innovation and Scientific Research" shall be established. It shall have legal personality, an independent budget, and shall be affiliated with the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

### Article 3

The main headquarters of the institution shall be in the city of Doha, and by a decision of the Council, other headquarters or offices may be established inside the State and abroad.

### Article 4

The institution aims to support and fund innovation and Scientific Research, improve their level, manage, develop and invest the resources allocated to them, and nurture and develop the capabilities necessary to establish the foundations of a diversified and sustainable national knowledge-based economy. It specifically works to achieve the following:

1- Spreading the culture of creativity, innovation, and Scientific Research among citizens, and raising awareness of the importance of Scientific Research and technology as a fundamental pillar in the system of progress and vision for the future.

2- Supporting and serving Development Plans in their comprehensive sense, linking Scientific Research to the needs of society and its local issues, and contributing to solving regional and international problems to achieve further progress.

3- Focusing on supporting research submitted by national bodies and institutions, which aim to develop techniques that lead to improved productivity and services, reduced costs, and achieving high levels of quality and safety in various sectors of the State.

4- Directing, encouraging, and supporting investment in the fields of innovation and Scientific Research, and providing necessary support to institutions, bodies, and researchers concerned with it.

### Article 5

In order to achieve its objectives, the institution may carry out all transactions and actions necessary for that purpose, and in particular, it may do the following:

1. Develop the national strategy for innovation and scientific research, in coordination with the relevant entities, supervise its implementation after approval by the Board of Directors, and review it periodically.

2. Develop and update policies and propose legislation related to innovation and scientific research.

3. Manage programs to finance and stimulate innovation and scientific research, approve regulations and standards for the disbursement of grants necessary to support innovation and scientific research capabilities and activities at the national level, and ensure optimal resource utilization.

4. Prepare the national Annual Report for innovation and scientific research, and follow up on Qatar’s progress in relevant International Indicators.

5. Strengthen international connections and exchange expertise and knowledge that enhance the capabilities of the national system for innovation and scientific research.

6. Allocate the necessary amounts to support and finance innovation, research, studies, and scientific research projects.

7. Obtain data, statistics, studies, reports, and research related to its work from ministries, other governmental entities, and other competent bodies.

8. Establish Bases and rules for cooperation with educational and scientific entities inside and outside the country, set policies, develop strategies related to this cooperation, and define implementation and follow-up mechanisms.

9. Work to support and involve the private sector and civil society institutions in innovation and scientific research activities.

10. Invest the institution’s funds and increase its revenues based on economic Bases and standards, and establish systems that ensure this.

11. Establish a system for investing the institution’s funds and disbursing from its returns, according to the regulations approved by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

12. Any other activities related to innovation and scientific research assigned to it by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

### Article 6

The institution is managed by a Board of Directors, whose formation and determination of remuneration are issued by an Amiri decree, based on a proposal from the President of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

### Article 7

The term of membership in the Council is four years, renewable for one or more similar periods.

### Article 8

The Council has the necessary authorities and powers to manage the affairs of the institution, and shall take whatever measures it deems appropriate to achieve its objectives. In particular, it is empowered to:

1.

Set the general policy for the institution and supervise its implementation.

2.

Approve plans and strategies and formulate policies related to innovation and scientific research.

3.

Issue the Organizational Structure of the institution.

4.

Issue administrative and financial regulations.

5.

Approve the institution's annual budget and its final accounts.

6.

Issue a system for investing the institution's funds.

7.

Accept grants, gifts, donations, and bequests provided to the institution by individuals, organizations, or national and foreign entities.

8.

Establish entities affiliated with the institution.

The Council's decisions stipulated in items (1), (3), (4), (6), (7), (8) of this article shall not be effective unless they are approved by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

### Article 9

The Council meets at the invitation of the Chairman / President at least once every month, and whenever necessary.

The meeting shall not be valid except in the presence of the majority of its members, provided that the Chairman / President or his deputy is among them. The Council’s decisions are issued by a majority of the votes of the members present, and in the event of a tie, the side of the Chairman / President prevails. Proxy attendance or Voting is not permitted, and the sessions of the Council are confidential.

### Article 10

The Council may invite to its meetings those it deems beneficial to consult from among experts or individuals of scientific standing, whether they are employees of the institution or others. The invitees have the right to participate in discussions but do not have the right to Voting.

### Article 11

The Council may form one or more committees from among its members to assist it in studying any topics it wishes to consider. It may also include in these committees members with expertise and competence from within or outside the institution.

### Article 12

The minutes of the Council's sessions and its decisions are recorded in a special register with numbered pages, or on individual numbered minutes and any other suitable electronic means, which are kept in a special file. It is signed by the Chairman / President and the Secretary.

### Article 13

The president has the right to sign on behalf of the institution in all matters related to its affairs, and he may delegate one of the Council members to sign in the matters he specifies.

### Article 14

The Chairman / President represents the institution before the judiciary and in its relations with others.

### Article 15

The institution shall have a Chief Executive Officer, whose appointment shall be decided by the Council.

### Article 16

The Chief Executive Officer (CEO), under the supervision of the Council and within the general policy of the institution, manages the administrative and financial affairs of the institution, in accordance with the internal regulations and systems and the Decisions / Resolutions issued by the Council, and within the limits of the approved annual budget, and in particular the following :

1.

General supervision of the administrative Body / Organ of the institution.

2.

Implementation of the Council’s Decisions / Resolutions.

3.

Proposing draft regulations specific to the institution.

4.

Preparing the draft annual budget and the final account of the institution.

5.

Preparing a report on the institution's activities during the fiscal year.

6.

Proposing the Organizational Structure of the institution.

7.

Any other tasks assigned to him by the Council in accordance with the provisions of this Decision / Resolution.

### Article 17

The Chairman / President, or their deputy, or any member of the Council, or the Chief Executive Officer (CEO), or any employee of the institution, may not have a direct or indirect personal interest in contracts concluded with the institution or on its behalf, or in projects carried out by it, or in any area of its activity.

### Article 18

The institution's resources consist of the following:

1.

The proceeds from the percentage allocated from government revenues in the general budget of the State.

2.

Other Financial Appropriations allocated to it by the State.

3.

The revenues generated by the institution from practicing its activities.

4.

Grants, donations, gifts, and bequests accepted by the Council.

Spending from the other Financial Appropriations allocated by the State shall be according to the rules approved by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on the proposal of the Council.

### Article 19

The financial year of the institution begins on the first of January and ends at the end of December of each year. The first financial year of the institution shall start from the date of enforcement of this Decision / Resolution and end at the end of December of the following year.

### Article 20

The State Audit Bureau is responsible for monitoring and auditing the accounts of the institution in accordance with the provisions of the Law / Act, and submitting its annual report to the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

The Cabinet / Council of Ministers has the authority to appoint one or more auditors to audit the accounts of the institution. The auditor has the right, at any time, to inspect all the institution's books, records, and documents, and to request any data deemed necessary for properly fulfilling their duties. The auditor may also verify the assets and liabilities of the institution, and if unable to exercise these rights, the auditor shall submit a report to the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

### Article 21

The الرئيس submits to the مجلس الوزراء a detailed تقرير on the aspects of the institution’s activities, its projects, the progress of work within it, and its financial position, no later than three months from the end of the fiscal year. The تقرير should include his proposals and recommendations, and a copy of the auditor’s تقرير, if available, should be attached.

### Article 22

The Council of Ministers may request the Council to submit reports on the administrative, financial, and technical conditions of the institution, or any aspect of its activities, or any information related to it. The Council may issue general directives regarding what the Council should follow in matters related to public policy.

### Article 23

All ministries, other government agencies, authorities, and public institutions must provide the Institution with any data and information it requests, and cooperate with it in implementing the decisions issued by the Council within its area of jurisdiction.

### Article 24

Every Ruling that contradicts the provisions of this Decision / Resolution shall be cancelled.

### Article 25

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall take effect from the date of its issuance and shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
