# Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (22) of 2025 amending certain provisions of Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (15) of 2021 establishing Qatar Tourism (QT)

**We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani**

**Amir of the State of Qatar**,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And the Law / Act No. (20) of 2018 regarding the regulation of tourism,

And the Law / Act No. (21) of 2018 regarding the regulation of business events,

And the Decision / Resolution No. (15) of 2021 establishing Qatar Tourism (QT),

And the Decision / Resolution No. (69) of 2024 regarding the reorganization of the Supreme Committee for Delivery and Legacy,

Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

**We have decided as follows**:

## المواد (1-4)

### Article 1

The definition of the tourism and hospitality sector, as stated in Article (1) of the referred Amiri Decision No. (15) of 2021, shall be replaced with the following definition:

**“Tourism and Hospitality Sector**: All activities, services, and entities operating in any of the following five areas, and any sub-sector subordinate to the tourism and hospitality sector as determined by the President:

**A- Accommodation and Stay**: Includes hotels, tourist resorts, hotel apartments and furnished apartments, short-term rental chalets, time-share accommodations, cabins, lodges, rural houses, desert resorts, youth hostels, tourist camps, and any other places designated for tourists’ accommodation.

**B- Entertainment and Relaxation**: Includes amusement parks, water parks, marine parks, public parks, public beaches, shopping malls, major real estate development projects, museums, cultural and heritage sites, and public events.

**C- Tourism and Travel**: Includes Destination Management companies, tour guide offices, travel agencies, tour operators, online travel agencies, cruises, sailing and seaplane tours, taxis, public transportation, and other land, sea, or air activities, but excludes air transportation regulated by the provisions of the Law Regulating Civil Aviation.

**D- Meetings, Conferences, and Exhibitions**: This includes high-profile commercial events as follows:

**Meetings**: A gathering of individuals in a specific place for consultation, engaging in a particular activity, or holding a celebration where certain individuals are invited to recognize their achievements and honor them. These meetings may be held in an organized or unorganized manner or according to a specified schedule, such as annual meetings, company committee meetings, various parties, weddings, engagements, and birthdays of non-residents in the State.

**Conferences**: Gatherings held for specific purposes with the participation of a large group for consultation, discussion, fact-finding, problem solving, and giving advice within a limited timeframe.

**Exhibitions**: Commercial events for showcasing products and services.

Excluded from all the above are meetings, conferences, and exhibitions organized by the State.

**E- Food and Beverage Services**: Includes beach clubs, night clubs, cafes, catering companies for events, restaurants, and food and beverage outlets, except for independent restaurants and outlets not subordinate to any of the areas stated in this section.

### Article 2

The text of Article (3/paragraphs 6, 11) of the Amiri Resolution No. (15) of 2021 referred to, shall be replaced with the following texts:

**Article (3 / paragraphs 6, 11):**

“6- Supporting the construction, development, and operation of assets in the tourism and hospitality sector owned by the State, in accordance with the plans approved by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.”

“11- Proposing the State’s annual plan for holding and organizing seasons, festivals, events, exhibitions, conferences, and other related tourism and hospitality sector activities, inside and outside the State, and supervising their implementation, after coordinating with the Supreme Committee for Delivery and Legacy.”

### Article 3

The following texts are added to the Decision / Resolution No. (15) of 2021 referred to:

**Article (3 items 15, 16, 17):**

«15- Expressing opinions regarding matters related to State investments allocated for the development of tourism and hospitality sector assets in the State of Qatar.

16- Ensuring excellence in the provision of services in the tourism and hospitality sector by setting and applying Quality Standards to enhance the services and products offered at the level of the entire tourism sector, in coordination with the relevant governmental and non-governmental entities.

17- Supporting and sponsoring festivals, tourism events, and business events at the local, regional, and international levels inside and outside the State, which promote and stimulate tourism and increase the number of visitors to the State.»

### Article 4

All concerned authorities, each within its own jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into effect from the day following its publication in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
