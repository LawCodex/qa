# The Instrument for Re-establishing Qatar Foundation for Education, Science and Community Development

After reviewing Decree-Law No. 21 of 2006 on private foundations for the public benefit;

According to our will in supporting the goals and implementing the targets for which Qatar Foundation for Education, Science, and Community Development was established, and how this requires its release from the scope of private assemblies and foundations under which it was established and its re-establishment according to the provisions of the aforesaid Decree-Law No. 21 of 2006 because it reflects more clearly the management of the Foundation's specializations and the implementation of its goals;

We therefore decided to re-incorporate the Foundation according to the following:

First: Qatar Foundation for Education, Science and Community Development shall be re-established in accordance with the provisions of Decree-Law No. 21 of 2006 on private foundations for the public benefit.

Second: Registering the Foundation shall be in accordance with the provisions of the aforesaid Decree-Law No. 21 of 2006. The statute and all instruments related to the Foundation shall be amended in accordance with these provisions. All necessary procedures shall be taken to cancel the registration in the Ministry of Civil Service Affairs and Housing.

Third: No change in the Foundation's ownership in respect of the money, projects, rights and obligations shall result from its re-establishment.

Hamad Bin Khalifa Al-Thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
