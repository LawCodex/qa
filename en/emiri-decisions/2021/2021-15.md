# Amiri Decision No. (15) of 2021 on the Establishment of Qatar Tourism (QT)

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And Law No. (20) of 2018 concerning the regulation of tourism,

And Law No. (21) of 2018 concerning the regulation of business events,

And the Amiri Decision No. (44) of 2008 endorsing the comprehensive development vision "Qatar National Vision 2030",

And the Amiri Decision No. (74) of 2018 establishing the National Tourism Council,

Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

We have decided as follows:

## المواد (1-14)

### Article 1 Amended

In the application of the provisions of this Decision / Resolution, the following word and two phrases shall have the meanings set out next to each of them, unless the context requires otherwise.

**Chairman / President**: Chairman / President of Qatar Tourism (QT).

**Director General**: Director General of Qatar Tourism (QT).

**The Tourism and Hospitality Sector**: All activities, services and entities operating in any of the following five areas, and any sub-sector of the Tourism and Hospitality Sector as determined by the Chairman / President:

**Accommodation and Lodging**: Includes hotels, tourist resorts, hotel apartments, furnished apartments, short-term rental chalets, timeshare accommodation, cabins, cottages, country houses, desert resorts, youth hostels, tourist camps, and any other places designated for the accommodation of tourists.

**Entertainment and Leisure**: Includes amusement parks, water parks, marine parks, public parks, public beaches, shopping malls, major real estate development projects, museums, cultural and heritage sites, and public events.

**Tourism and Travel**: Includes destination management companies, tourist guide offices, travel agencies, tour operators, online travel agencies, cruises, yacht and seaplane excursions, taxis, public transportation and other land, maritime, or air activities. It does not include air transport regulated by the provisions of the civil aviation law.

**Meetings, Conferences and Exhibitions**: Includes commercial events of a distinctive nature, as follows:

**Meetings**: A gathering of individuals in a specific place for consultation, conducting a certain activity, or a celebration to which some are invited to honor and recognize their achievements. These meetings may be held in an organized or unorganized manner, or based on a set timetable, such as annual meetings, company committee meetings, and various parties such as weddings, engagements, and birthday celebrations for non-residents of the State.

**Conferences**: A gathering held for specific purposes with the participation of a large group of participants for consultation, discussion, fact-finding, problem-solving, and providing advice within a limited period.

**Exhibitions**: Commercial events for showcasing products and services.

Excluded from all of the above are meetings, conferences, and exhibitions organized by the State.

e- **Food and Beverage Services**: Includes beach clubs, nightclubs, cafes, companies providing catering services for events, restaurants, and food and beverage outlets, except for independent restaurants and outlets not affiliated with any of the fields mentioned in this paragraph.

### Article 2

A government entity called "Qatar Tourism (QT)" shall be established, and it shall report to the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

It shall have legal personality, and its budget shall be annexed to the budget of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

Its headquarters shall be in the city of Doha, and it may establish offices affiliated to it outside the State.

### Article 3 Amended

Qatar Tourism (QT) aims to regulate, develop and enhance the tourism and hospitality industry, stimulate private sector investment in it, work to improve the tourism experience in the State, elevate it and showcase the cultural, civilizational, artistic, and touristic features of the State, and cooperate in all activities and services with entities working in the tourism and hospitality sector within the State. In order to achieve its objectives, it undertakes the following competencies: 1 - Setting the national strategy for the tourism and hospitality sector, the related public policies, following up on their implementation after approval, and reviewing them periodically. 2 - Regulating, supervising, and monitoring all entities and activities reporting to the tourism and hospitality sector, including issuing Licenses and classifying institutions and activities in this sector, in coordination with the relevant entities. 3 - Proposing draft legislative tools related to the activities of the tourism and hospitality sector in the State, and providing opinions on draft legislative tools that may affect this sector. 4 - Coordinating with ministries and other governmental and non-governmental entities in all areas related to the tourism and hospitality sector, ensuring integration between the national strategy for tourism and hospitality and its optimal implementation. 5 - Identifying and approving initiatives and projects related to the national strategy for the tourism and hospitality sector, whether implemented directly by Qatar Tourism (QT), by entities reporting to it, or by ministries or other governmental and non-governmental entities, and supervising their implementation. 6 - Supporting the construction, development, and operation of tourism and hospitality sector assets owned by the State, according to the plans approved by the Council of Ministers. 7 - Supporting and encouraging innovation in the tourism and hospitality sector, in coordination and cooperation with the relevant and concerned entities in the State. 8 - Establishing and organizing necessary training courses to qualify all workers in the tourism and hospitality sector, supervising these courses in coordination with the relevant authorities, and supporting and developing educational and training curricula related to the tourism and hospitality sector in Universities and educational and professional institutions in the State, in coordination with the relevant authorities. 9 - Collecting, publishing, and analyzing statistical data and other research on the tourism and hospitality sector, contributing to the formulation of policies and plans for this sector, in coordination with the relevant authorities. 10 - Marketing the State as a prime destination in tourism and hospitality using all modern and innovative means, setting marketing guidelines and rules, and publishing them to all entities and organizations reporting to the tourism and hospitality sector. 11 - Proposing the State’s annual plan for organizing and holding seasons, festivals, events, exhibitions, conferences, and other related activities of the tourism and hospitality sector inside and outside the State, and supervising their execution, after coordination with the Supreme Committee for Delivery & Legacy. 12 - Organizing festivals, events, exhibitions, conferences, and other local, regional, and international activities related to the tourism and hospitality sector, inside and outside the State, and participating in them. 13 - Concluding Agreements, memoranda of understanding, and contracts, and taking any legal measures necessary to exercise its competencies and authorities. 14 - Representing the State in all national, regional, and international bodies, organizations, conferences, meetings, and seminars related to the tourism and hospitality sector. 15 - Providing opinions on matters related to the State’s investments allocated for developing tourism and hospitality sector assets in the State of Qatar. 16 - Ensuring excellence in service delivery in the tourism and hospitality sector by setting and implementing quality standards to enhance the services and products provided in the entire tourism sector, in coordination with the relevant governmental and non-governmental entities. 17 - Supporting and sponsoring festivals, tourism events, and business activities at local, regional, and international levels inside and outside the State, aiming to stimulate tourism, promote it, and increase the number of visitors to the State.

### Article 4

Qatar Tourism (QT) shall have a Chairman / President, appointed by an Amiri decision.

The Chairman / President shall represent Qatar Tourism (QT) before the judiciary and in its relations with others.

### Article 5

The Chairman / President assumes all the powers and responsibilities necessary to achieve the goals of Qatar Tourism (QT), and to manage its administrative, financial, and technical affairs, in accordance with the prescribed laws, regulations, and systems, within the limits of the approved annual budget. The Chairman / President specifically has the following:

1 - Establishing the national strategy for the tourism and hospitality sector, and its related programs and policies, reviewing them, and taking measures to present them to the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

2 - Approving the general strategies and policies, plans, programs, and projects of Qatar Tourism (QT), within the framework of the national strategy for the tourism and hospitality sector, and supervising their implementation.

3 - Issuing the Organizational Structure for Qatar Tourism (QT).

4 - Issuing the internal and technical regulations for Qatar Tourism (QT).

5 - Determining the fees and charges for services provided by Qatar Tourism (QT).

6 - Approving the annual estimated budget of Qatar Tourism (QT) and its final account.

7 - Proposing legislative instruments related to the competences of Qatar Tourism (QT).

8 - Reviewing Periodic Reports and follow-up reports regarding workflow.

9 - Encouraging the private sector to invest in the tourism and hospitality sector.

10 - Approving Qatar Tourism (QT)'s plans to participate in local, regional, and international seminars, events, festivals, and exhibitions inside and outside the country.

11 - Any other tasks assigned to him by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers within his area of jurisdiction.

The decisions of the Chairman / President stipulated in clauses (1), (3), (4), (5), (6) shall not be valid unless approved by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

The Chairman / President may grant Authorization / Delegation of some of his powers to the Director General.

### Article 6

Qatar Tourism (QT) shall have a Director General, appointed by a decision from the Chairman / President of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, who shall, under the supervision of the Chairman / President, and within the general policy framework of Qatar Tourism (QT), implement the decisions of the Chairman / President, manage the administrative, financial, and technical affairs of Qatar Tourism (QT) within the bounds of the established laws, regulations, and systems, and shall, in particular, be responsible for the following:

1 - Proposing the strategic plan and executive plans for Qatar Tourism (QT).

2 - Proposing plans, programs, and projects of Qatar Tourism (QT).

3 - Preparing the annual draft budget for Qatar Tourism (QT), and its final account.

4 - Proposing fees and the charges for services provided by Qatar Tourism (QT).

5 - Preparing periodic reports on the activities of Qatar Tourism (QT), and preparing an annual report on its work progress, programs, and achievements.

6 - Any other tasks assigned to him by the Chairman / President within his competence.

### Article 7

The financial resources of Qatar Tourism consist of:

1 - The Financial Appropriations allocated to it by the State.

2 - Other resources approved by the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on a proposal from the Chairman / President.

### Article 8

The Cabinet / Council of Ministers may appoint one or more auditors to audit the accounts of Qatar Tourism (QT). The auditor, at any time, has the right to review all its books, records, and documents, and to request the data he deems necessary to perform his duties and functions. He may verify the assets and liabilities of Qatar Tourism (QT), and the auditor shall submit a report to the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, including his suggestions and recommendations.

### Article 9

The Chairman / President shall submit to the Cabinet / Council of Ministers a detailed annual report on the activities, projects, and workflow of Qatar Tourism (QT) within a period not exceeding three months from the end of the financial year, including his proposals and recommendations.

### Article 10

The Council of Ministers may, at any time, request the Chairman / President to submit reports on the administrative, financial, and technical conditions of Qatar Tourism (QT), or any aspect of its activities or any information related to it, and it may issue general directives regarding what Qatar Tourism (QT) should follow in matters related to general policy.

### Article 11

The Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (74) of 2018 establishing the National Tourism Council referred to is hereby cancelled, as is any Ruling that contravenes the provisions of this Decision / Resolution.

All fixed and movable funds allocated to the National Tourism Council, as well as all rights prescribed to it, shall devolve to Qatar Tourism (QT) from the effective date of this Decision / Resolution, and it shall assume all its obligations.

### Article 12

Employees of the National Tourism Council who are deemed suitable for transfer are moved to Qatar Tourism (QT) by a decision of the Chairman / President, based on a proposal from the Director General. The Ministry of Administrative Development, Labor and Social Affairs undertakes the settlement of the status of employees who are not transferred to Qatar Tourism (QT).

### Article 13

The Chairman / President issues the regulations and Decision / Resolution necessary for implementing the provisions of this Decision / Resolution, and until they are put into effect, the existing regulations and Decision / Resolution shall remain in force, provided they do not conflict with the provisions of this Decision / Resolution.

### Article 14

All relevant authorities, each within their jurisdiction, must implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the date of issuance. It shall be published in the official gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
