# Amiri Decision No. (35) of 2021 appointing an Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to the Republic of Rwanda

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

Having reviewed the Constitution,

And Decree 34 by Law No. (13) of 2009 concerning the diplomatic and consular corps,

And upon the proposal of the Deputy Chairman of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers and Minister of Foreign Affairs,

We have decided the following

## المواد (1-2)

### Article 1

يُعيّن السيّد/ مسفر فيصل مبارك العجب الشهواني، سفيراً فوق العادة مُفوّضاً من لدنّا لدى جمهورية رواندا.

### Article 2

All competent authorities, each within its jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into force from the date of its issuance and shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
