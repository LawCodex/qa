# Amiri Decision No. (37) of 1998 appointing an Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to the Arab Republic of Egypt

We, Jassim bin Hamad Al Thani, Deputy Amir of the State of Qatar,

Having reviewed the Amended Provisional Articles of Association, particularly Articles (22), (23) thereof,

And Law / Act No. (13) of 1993 on the Regulation of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and defining its competencies,

And Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (18) of 1998 transferring an Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to the General Diwan of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs,

And upon the proposal of the Minister of Foreign Affairs,

Have decided as follows:

## Articles (1-2)

### Article 1

Mr. Mohammed bin Hamad bin Mubarak Al Khalifa shall be appointed as Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to the Arab Republic of Egypt.

### Article 2

All competent authorities, each within its respective jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into force from the date of its issuance and shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Jasem Bin Hamad Al-Thani — Deputy Amir of the State of Qatar
