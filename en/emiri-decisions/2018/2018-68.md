# Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (68) of 2018 amending certain provisions of Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (16) of 2014 on assigning the competences of ministries

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

Having reviewed the Constitution,

and Amiri Resolution No. (16) of 2014 on assigning the competences of ministries, as amended by Amiri Resolution No. (4) of 2016,

and Amiri Resolution No. (19) of 2014 on the organizational structure of the Ministry of Energy and Industry,

and Amiri Resolution No. (28) of 2014 on the organizational structure of the Ministry of Development Planning and Statistics,

Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

have decided as follows:

## المواد (1-4)

### Article 1

The texts of Articles (7) and (11) of the referred Amiri Decision No. (16) of 2014 are replaced with the following texts:

Article (7):

"The Ministry of Finance is responsible for supervising and directing financial and economic activity, proposing, reviewing and revising the objectives, tools, and programs of Financial Policies and economic policies in line with the requirements of national development, supervising credit policies, government procurement, and public debts, providing financial resources, concluding Public Loans and collecting Public Revenues, maintaining the State's accounts, preparing the draft general budget of the State and supervising its implementation, preparing the Final Accounts of the State, auditing expenditures in payment requests and settlements of government entities, providing, supervising, and maintaining the decided needs for government housing.".

Article (11):

"The Ministry of Commerce and Industry is responsible for supervising and directing commercial and industrial activity in accordance with national development requirements, working to promote business, contribute to attracting investments, support and develop exports, and improve the methods and procedures for providing public services to the business and investment sector. It also supervises the practice of commercial professions, registration and recording of commercial and investment establishments, issuance of the necessary Licenses for their activities, supervision, organization and monitoring of markets within its competence, taking necessary measures to protect consumers, combat commercial fraud, protect competition, prevent monopolistic practices, and protect Intellectual Property Rights. It sets the general policy for industrialization, develops national industries, develops and utilizes the Industrial Zones Reporting to it, and proposes the establishment of new Industrial Zones."

### Article 2

Articles (6) and (15) of Decision / Resolution No. (16) of 2014 referred to above shall be repealed.

### Article 3

Decision / Resolution No. (19) of 2014 and Decision / Resolution No. (28) of 2014, referred to above, are hereby cancelled.

### Article 4

All relevant authorities, each within its competence, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into effect from the date of its issuance. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
