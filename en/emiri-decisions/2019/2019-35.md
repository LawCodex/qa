# Amiri Decision No. (35) of 2019 appointing an Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to the Republic of Turkmenistan

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

Having reviewed the Constitution,

And Decree 34 by Law No. (13) of 2009 concerning the diplomatic and consular corps,

And Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (21) of 2017 transferring Ambassadors Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to the General Diwan of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs,

And upon the proposal of the Deputy Chairman of the Council of Ministers and Minister of Foreign Affairs,

Have decided as follows:

## المواد (1-2)

### Article 1

Mr. Mubarak Abdulrahman Mubarak Al-Nasr is appointed as our Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to the Republic of Turkmenistan.

### Article 2

All competent authorities, each within its respective jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into force from the date of its issuance and shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
