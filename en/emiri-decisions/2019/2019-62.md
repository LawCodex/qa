# Amiri Decision No. (62) of 2019 appointing an Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to the Kingdom of Thailand

Having reviewed the Constitution,

and Decree 34 by Law No. (13) of 2009 regarding the diplomatic and consular corps,

and Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (29) of 2019 transferring Ambassadors Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to the General Diwan of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs,

and upon the proposal of the Deputy Chairman of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers and Minister of Foreign Affairs,

We have decided as follows:

## المواد (1-2)

### Article 1

Mr. Ahmed Ali Abdulrahman Al Jameel Al Tamimi is hereby appointed as our Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to the Kingdom of Thailand.

### Article 2

All competent authorities, each within its respective jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into force from the date of its issuance and shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
