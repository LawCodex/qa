# Deputy Ruler Decree No. (26) of 1966 regarding the regulation of issuing regular and commemorative postage stamps

Deputy Ruler,

After reviewing Law / Act No. (1) of 1962 on the Regulation of the Senior Management of the Government Apparatus,

And Law / Act No. (2) of 1962 on the Regulation of Fiscal Policy in Qatar,

And Decisions No. (13) of 1964, (1) of 1965, and (3) of 1965(1),

And based on the presentation made to us by the Director General of the Government and its Legal Advisor,

We have decided as follows:

## Materials (1-9)

### Article 1

1 - To regulate the activities related to the issuance of postage stamps, and to establish rules for their supervision, the following records shall be established in the Postal Department:

(a) A record for ordinary stamps.

(b) A record for commemorative stamps issued by the Government of Qatar.

(c) A record for foreign stamps received by the Postal Department.

(d) A record for stamp collector accounts.

(e) A record for stamps distributed outside Qatar, whether for payment or free of charge (gratis).

2 - Data shall be entered in the aforementioned records promptly, with utmost accuracy in this regard.

### Article 2

The post office manager prepares monthly statements showing the balance of each type of regular and commemorative stamps. He sends a copy of these statements to the Financial Affairs Department and another to the government auditors.

### Article 3

Government auditors shall:

(a) Follow up on the weekly supervision of the records of the Postal Department mentioned in Article 1 of this Decision / Resolution.

(b) Review the monthly statements referred to in the previous article, and the government auditors shall submit a report on the results of supervision and review to the Postal Stamp Issuance Committee mentioned in the following article.

### Article 4

(a) A Committee called (Committee for Issuing Postage Stamps) shall be established at the Post Department, and shall be formed from:

Mr. Hosni Abu Khalil, Supervisor of the Post Department

Mr. Abdel Qader the Judge, Director of the Office of the Deputy Governor

Mr. Ibrahim Al-Daghmah, Head of the Legal Research Section

Mr. Mahmoud Al-Zoubi, the Inspector of Finance

Mr. Mohammad Saif Al-Mu’addadi, Auditor at the Post Department

(b) The Committee's secretarial work shall be undertaken by an employee of the Post Department in addition to his own duties.

(c) The Committee shall convene at the headquarters of the Post Department whenever necessary. Its recommendations are issued by majority vote.

(d) The Committee may seek assistance from experts, whether employees or others, in matters presented to it.

### Article 5

The Committee referred to in the previous article is responsible for the following:

(a) Studying the following matters:

1 - Issuing regular and commemorative stamp series proposed for issuance by the Chairman of the Committee or any of its members.

2 - The designs of the proposed series, their denominations, quantities, and the period during which they are valid.

3 - Information regarding the printing presses in terms of their adequacy and the prices they offer.

4 - The system for distributing stamps free of charge, and how to carry out this distribution in a manner that ensures fairness and accuracy.

5 - Requests from hobbyists.

6 - Methods of distributing new issues.

7 - Ways to announce each new issue, setting a deadline for submitting requests from hobbyists, method of distributing the issue, and means of supervising the implementation of this method.

8 - Reports of the auditors mentioned in Article Three of this Decision / Resolution.

(b) Approving the letters addressed to the printing presses selected by Decision / Resolution of the Deputy Ruler for printing new issues.

(c) Overseeing the process of receiving new stamps, auditing them, and depositing them in the stores of the Post Directorate. This process shall be carried out by a Committee composed of representatives from each of the government auditors, the Directorate of Financial Affairs, and the Post Directorate, in accordance with a Report / Official Record signed by them.

(d) Monitoring the operations of overprinting stamps and supervising their implementation.

### Article 6

A special file is created at the Committee’s headquarters for each new issue, numbered with a serial number. The file contains the minutes of the Committee’s meetings and the original copies of its decisions, as well as the issue templates. The documents are attached to the inside cover of the file according to their order within it.

### Article 7

The Committee submits the result of its study of the matters referred to in paragraph (a) of Article (5) of this Decision / Resolution, along with its recommendations regarding them, accompanied by the issuance file, to the Director General of the Government and its Legal Advisor for their study, and to present them to the Deputy Ruler for consideration of issuing his Decision / Resolution in this regard.

### Article 8

Decisions / Resolutions numbers (13) for the year 1964, (1) for the year 1965, and (3) for the year 1965, as well as any provisions that conflict with this Decision / Resolution, are hereby cancelled.

### Article 9

This Decision / Resolution shall be published in the Official Gazette and shall enter into force as of the date of its issuance.

Khalifa Ben Hamad Al Thani
