# Amiri Decree No. (34) of 2026 amending certain provisions of Amiri Decree No. (28) of 2023 regarding the establishment of the General Authority for Regulation of the Real Estate Sector

**We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,**

After reviewing the Constitution,

And Law No. (22) of 2017 on the regulation of real estate mediation activities, as amended by Law No. (13) of 2026,

And Amiri Decision No. (28) of 2023 establishing the Public Authority for the Regulation of the Real Estate Sector,

And upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

**We have decided as follows:**

## Almawad (1-2)

### Article 1 (1)

The following clauses are added to Article (4) of the Decision / Resolution No. (28) of 2023 referred to:

«13- Issuing and renewing licenses for practicing real estate Mediation, collecting the prescribed fees, and supervising them in accordance with the provisions of the Law / Act.

14- Conducting the necessary studies to develop the provision of real estate Mediation services, and proposing suitable solutions to the problems and obstacles they face.

15- Organizing, monitoring, following up, and supervising the compliance of real estate brokers with Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and combating terrorism financing requirements.»

### Article 2 (2)

All relevant authorities, each within its competence, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into effect as of 1/1/2027 and shall be published in the official gazette.
