# Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (35) of 2026 to cancel Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (14) of 2002 to establish the Steering Committee for the Hamad International Airport Expansion Project

**We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, Emir of the State of Qatar,**

After having reviewed the Constitution,

And the Amiri Resolution No. (14) of 2002 establishing the Steering Committee for the Hamad International Airport Expansion project, and its amending Amiri Resolutions,

And the Amiri Resolution No. (66) of 2018 reorganizing the Civil Aviation Authority,

**Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,**

**has decided as follows:**

## Almawad (1-4)

### Article 1

The Decision / Resolution No. (14) of 2002 referred to is hereby cancelled.

### Article 2

All movable and immovable assets of the Steering Committee for the Hamad International Airport Expansion project, as well as all rights and obligations assigned to or against it, shall be transferred to the General Civil Aviation Authority.

### Article 3

The President of the General Authority of Civil Aviation supervises the procedures for dissolving the Committee, settling its affairs, collecting its rights, and fulfilling its obligations.

### Article 4

All relevant authorities, each within their respective scope, must implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the date of issuance. It shall be published in the official gazette.

تميـم بن حمد آل ثاني — أمير دولة قطر
