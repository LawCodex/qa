# Amiri Decision No. (5) of 2026 appointing a Deputy Commander of the Amiri Guard

**We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani Amir of the State of Qatar,**

Having reviewed the Constitution,

And the Military Service Law issued by Decree-Law No. (31) of 2006, and the laws amending it,

And Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (9) of 2007 establishing the Amiri Guard,

And Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (49) of 2024 appointing a Deputy Commander of the Amiri Guard,

**We have decided as follows:**

## Almawad (1-3)

### Article 1

Brigadier General (Staff)/ Ali Thalab Hamad Al-Shahwani Al-Hajri is appointed as Deputy Commander of the Amiri Guard.

### Article 2

Every Ruling that contradicts the provisions of this Decision / Resolution shall be repealed.

### Article 3

All competent authorities, each within its respective jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into force from the date of its issuance. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
