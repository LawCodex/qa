# Emiri Resolution No. (56) of 2015 appointing an Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

Having reviewed the Constitution,

And Decree 34 by Law No. (13) of 2009 regarding the diplomatic and consular corps,

And upon the proposal of the Minister of Foreign Affairs,

We have decided the following:

## المواد (1-2)

### Article 1

Mr. Bandar Mohammed Abdullah Al Attiyah is hereby appointed as Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary by Us to the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan.

### Article 2

All competent authorities, each within its respective jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into force as of the date of its issuance and shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
