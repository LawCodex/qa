# Amiri Decree No. (58) of 2015 concerning Hamad Port

We, Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And Decree 34 Law No. (29) of 1966 on the Regulation of Qatar Ports, and its amending laws,

And Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (37) of 2007 regarding the establishment of the Steering Committee for the New Doha Port Project, and its amending Amiri Decisions / Resolutions,

And Decree 34 No. (35) of 2009 regarding the allocation of a plot of land for the establishment of the New Doha Port,

And upon the proposal of the Minister of Transport,

We have decided the following:

## المواد (1-2)

### Article 1

The maritime boundaries of Hamad Port are determined according to the map and coordinates attached to this Decision / Resolution.

### Article 2

All relevant authorities, each within its own jurisdiction, must implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the date of its issuance. It shall be published in the official gazette.

Tamim Bin Hamad Al-thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
