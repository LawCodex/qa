# Amiri Decree No. (14) of 2002 on Establishing the Steering Committee for the New Doha International Airport Project

We, Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani, Emir of the State of Qatar,

after reviewing the Articles of Association, and in particular Article (23) thereof,

and Law No. (5) of 1989 concerning the State General Budget,

and Decree 34 by Law No. (16) of 2001 establishing the General Civil Aviation Authority,

have decided as follows:

## Materials (1-11)

### Article 1 Amended

A committee called the "Hamad International Airport Expansion Project Steering Committee" shall be established, and it shall have a budget attached to the State's general budget.

### Article 2 Amended

A Steering Committee for the Hamad International Airport Expansion project is formed, chaired by the President of the General Civil Aviation Authority, with the Chief Executive Officer of Qatar Airways as Vice Chairman, and includes a representative from each of the following entities:

1- Ministry of Interior.

2- Ministry of Finance.

3- Ministry of Transport and Communications.

4- State Security Bureau.

5- General Civil Aviation Authority.

6- Hamad International Airport.

7- Qatar Railways Company (Rail).Each entity selects its representative, and a decision Nominating the Committee members is issued by the Minister of Transport and Communications.

The secretariat of the Committee is handled by one or more employees, whose assignment, duties, and remuneration are determined by a decision from the Minister of Transport and Communications, based on a proposal from the Chairman of the Committee.

### Article 3 Amended

The Committee is responsible for overseeing the Hamad International Airport Expansion, in coordination with the relevant authorities. For this purpose, it may carry out all necessary activities in this regard, and in particular the following:

1- Monitoring tender procedures related to the construction of the airport and providing all its needs, including devices, equipment, and others.

2- Signing the necessary contracts for the construction of the airport and following up on their implementation.

3- Monitoring the progress of work on the project and working to overcome obstacles and difficulties encountered.

4- Carrying out any tasks assigned to it that are necessary for the implementation of the project.

### Article 4

The Committee meets at least once a month upon the invitation of its chair, and it holds its meetings outside official working hours unless necessity requires otherwise.

### Article 5

Committee meetings are only valid with the presence of the majority of its members, including the Chairman / President. The Committee issues its decisions by a majority of the attending members, and in case of a tie, the side of the Chairman / President prevails. The meetings and deliberations of the Committee are confidential.

### Article 6

The Committee may seek the assistance of such employees from ministries, other government agencies, or other individuals with competence and expertise as it deems appropriate, to provide the advice, information, or clarifications it requests. These individuals may attend Committee meetings and participate in discussions without having the right to Voting.

### Article 7

A Report / Official Record is prepared for each meeting of the Committee and is signed by the Chairman / President and the Secretary.

### Article 8

The chairman of the Committee has the right to sign on its behalf in all matters related to its affairs, and he may authorize one of the members of the Committee to sign in the matters he specifies.

### Article 9

The Committee shall have an annual estimated budget prepared in accordance with the rules in force in the State, and the financial year of the Committee shall begin on the first of January and end on the last day of December of each year, provided that the first financial year shall begin from the date of entry into force of this Decision / Resolution until the end of December of the following year.

### Article 10

The Committee submits a report to the Prince about its activities every three months, including its proposals and recommendations.

### Article 11

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, must implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the date of issuance. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Hamad Bin Khalifa Al-Thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
