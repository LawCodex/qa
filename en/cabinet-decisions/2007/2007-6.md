# Council of Ministers Decision No. (6) of 2007 on the establishment of investment free zones, designation of their areas, and the activities permitted to be carried out therein

The Council of Ministers,

after reviewing the Constitution,

and Law No. (34) of 2005 regarding Free Zones for investment,

and Emiri Resolution No. (29) of 1996 regarding the decisions of the Council of Ministers that are submitted to the Emir for ratification and issuance,

and Emiri Resolution No. (21) of 2006 on the formation of the Steering Committee for the establishment of investment Free Zones,

and upon the proposal of the Minister of Economy and Commerce,

has decided as follows:

## Articles (1-3)

### Article 1

Investment free zones are established in the areas whose spaces and boundaries are shown on the attached maps to this Decision / Resolution.

### Article 2

Investment is permitted in the Free Zones mentioned in the previous article, to engage in the following activities:

1- Establishment of clean light and medium industries, medical and pharmaceutical industries, marine industries, high-tech industries, and supporting industries.

2- Establishment of electricity stations and networks, water desalination, Telecommunications systems, and information technology.

3- Provision of services, especially auditing, accounting, taxation, consulting, and legal services.

4- Financial and investment consultancy, and all types of investment services.

5- Activities related to company headquarters, Department / Directorate offices, business, relevant activities and tasks, of all types.

6- General company management activities.

7- Activities related to the media, entertainment, and tourism sectors.

8- Supporting activities such as goods transit, shipping, and unloading.

9- Construction of buildings and storage areas.

### Article 3

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, must implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the date of its issuance. It shall be published in the official gazette.

Abdulah Bin Khalifa Al-Thani, Hamad Bin Khalifa Al-Thani — Prime Minister, Amir of the State of Qatar
