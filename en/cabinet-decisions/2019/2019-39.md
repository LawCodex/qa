# Council of Ministers Decision No. (39) of 2019 issuing the Executive Regulation of the Income Tax Law issued by Law No. (24) of 2018

Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And Law / Act of Income Tax issued by Law / Act No. (24) of 2018,

And Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (29) of 1996 regarding Cabinet / Council of Ministers decisions submitted to the Amir for ratification and issuance,

And Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (77) of 2018 establishing the General Tax Authority (GTA),

And Executive Regulation of the Tax Law / Act of Income Tax issued by Law / Act No. (21) of 2009, issued by Decision / Resolution of the Minister of Economy and Finance No. (10) of 2011,

And upon the proposal of the Minister of Finance,

It has decided the following:

## الباب الأول: نطاق سريان الضريبة (1-4)

### الفصل الأول: الدخل الخاضع للضريبة (1)

#### Article 1 Amended

"1 - For the purposes of applying Article (1) of the Law / Act, the term "permanent establishment" includes, in particular, the following:

A - Office.

B - Factory.

C - Workshop.

D - Sales rescue.

E - Warehouse, in relation to a person providing storage facilities for others.

F - Mine or oil or gas well or quarry or any other place for the exploration, extraction, and exploitation of Natural Resources.

2 - The term "permanent establishment" also includes the following:

A - A construction site, or a project for construction, assembly, or installation, or related supervisory activities, provided that this site, project, or those activities continue for more than six months.

B - Provision of services, including consultancy services, by a project, through employees or other individuals assigned by the project for this purpose, provided these activities continue within the State for a period

or periods totaling more than one hundred and eighty-three days during twelve months that begin or end in the relevant financial year.

3 - The term "permanent establishment" does not include the following:

A - Use of facilities solely for the purpose of storing or displaying goods or merchandise owned by the project.

B - Maintaining a stock of goods or merchandise owned by the project, for the purpose of storage or display, or for processing by another project.

C - Maintaining a fixed place of business, for the purpose of purchasing goods or merchandise or collecting information for the project, or for conducting any other activity for the project.

D - Maintaining a fixed place of business solely for any combination of the activities mentioned in the preceding sub-paragraphs of this item, provided that such activity, or the overall activity of the fixed place of business, is preparatory or auxiliary in nature.

4 - The provisions of item (3) of this Article do not apply to a fixed place of business used or kept by a project if the project itself or another project closely related to it carries out activities at that place or at another place in the State.

That place or the other place shall not constitute a permanent establishment for the project or the closely related project in accordance with this Article.

The overall activity resulting from combining activities conducted by both projects at the same place, or carried out by the same project or closely related project at the two places, shall not have preparatory or auxiliary character.

It is required that the business activities carried out by both projects at the same place, or carried out by the same project or closely related projects at the two places, constitute complementary functions, Part of a cohesive business operation.

5 - By way of exception from the provisions of item (2) and subject to the provisions of item (6) of this Article, if a person acts in the State on behalf of a project, that project is considered to have a permanent establishment in the State concerning any activities carried out by such person for the benefit of the project, if that person performs any of the following:

A - Concludes contracts regularly, or performs, on a regular basis, the principal role leading to the conclusion of contracts that are routinely concluded without substantial modification by the project, whether such contracts are in the name of the project, or for the transfer of ownership, or for granting the right to use, assets of the project or assets to which the project is entitled to use, or for the project to provide services, unless the activities of that person are limited to the activities mentioned in item (3), which, if practiced through a fixed place of business, other than a fixed place of business subject to item (4), such place is not considered a permanent establishment under the aforementioned item.

B - Maintains, on a regular basis, in the State a stock of goods or merchandise from which goods or merchandise are regularly delivered on behalf of the project.

6 - By way of exception to the previous provisions, and subject to the provisions of item (7) of this Article, a foreign insurance project, except regarding reinsurance, is considered to have a permanent establishment in the State, if it collects insurance premiums in the State or insures risks located therein through a person.

7 - Items (5) and (6) of this Article do not apply if the person acting in the State on behalf of a foreign project carries out business in the State as an independent agent and acts for the project in the normal course of such business.

However, if a person acts exclusively or almost exclusively on behalf of one or more projects closely related to them, that person is not considered an independent agent in those projects in accordance with the provisions of this item.

8 - If a company resident in the State controls, or is controlled by, a company resident in a foreign State, or carries out its activity in that foreign State (whether through a permanent establishment or otherwise), that alone does not make either company a permanent establishment of the other.

9 - For the purposes of this Article, a person or project is closely related to a project if one has control over the other, or both are under the control of the same persons or projects.

In all cases, a person or project is considered closely related to a project as follows:

- If one directly or indirectly owns more than (50%) of the ownership benefit rights in the other (or in the case of a company, more than (50%) of the total votes and value of the company’s shares or ownership benefit rights in the company).

B - If another person or project directly or indirectly owns more than (50%) of benefit rights (or in the case of a company, more than (50%) of the total votes and value of the company's shares or ownership benefit rights in the company) in the person or project or in both projects.".

### الفصل الثاني: الإعفاءات الضريبية (2)

#### Article 2 Amended

"1 - The banking interests and returns stipulated in Article (4/Item 2) of the Law include the income earned by a natural person from savings and deposit accounts and other investment instruments with Banks, including Islamic banks.

2 - The interests and returns of Public Debt securities, Islamic Securities, and bonds of public bodies and institutions, stipulated in Article (4/Item 3) of the Law, include profits realized from the disposal of these Securities and bonds.

3 - For the purposes of applying the provisions of Article (4/Item 4) of the Law, the real estate and Securities Reporting to taxable activity assets refers to those Reporting to the assets of the taxable activity practiced by the taxpayer.

Securities include shares and bonds of Qatari joint stock companies and any other Securities licensed for trading, other investment instruments, and similar instruments in accordance with the provisions of applicable legislation.

4 - For the purposes of applying Article (4/Item 5) of the Law, and in the event of any breach of the conditions stipulated in that Item, capital gains resulting from the revaluation of company assets are subject to Income Tax starting from the year of exemption benefit.

5 - For the purposes of applying the provisions of Article (4/Items 6, 7) of the Law, the following is required:

A - The project must pay tax abroad on capital gains or payments to members of the Board of Directors and other similar payments.

B - The project must, as required, fulfill the requirements of substantive activity stipulated in the Law and decisions issued in implementation thereof and in these regulations.

6 - The exemption stipulated in Article (4/Item 8) of the Law includes the surplus distributed by the liquidator to partners after settling the company's debts and returning the cash value of their shares of the capital to them, considering the Ruling in sub-items (A) and (B) of Item (8) referred to.

7 - "Machines" as stipulated in Article (4/Item 9) of the Law refers to tools and equipment used to obtain the final product and does not include small and manual tools and equipment used to facilitate or complete the craftsman's work.

The average number of workers during the tax year is calculated by multiplying the number of workers by the number of days this number is maintained and dividing the result by three hundred and sixty days, and establishments used only for storage are not counted when calculating the number of establishments activity is exercised through.

8 - The exemption stipulated in Article (4/Item 10) of the Law applies to gross income arising from agricultural and marine fishing activities and does not apply to any industrial or commercial activity complementary to or related to these activities.

9 - For the purpose of applying Article (4/Item 11) of the Law, profits earned by a company engaged in air or sea navigation resident in another State and derived from operating aircraft or ships in International Transport are exempt from Income Tax in the State to the extent a Qatari navigation company is exempt from tax in that State on its profits derived from operating aircraft or ships according to the nature of the activities generating those profits, based on a Testimony issued by the tax authorities of that State or a mutual exemption agreement.

10 – The exemption stipulated in Article (4/Item 13) of the Law is granted on the condition that:

A - The legal person is resident in the State.

B - The legal person keeps accounting records that comply with applicable accounting standards in the State.

C - Qataris are residents in the State.

D - Qataris are beneficial owners of the legal person.

E - Qataris own the entire capital throughout the accounting period during which the exempted income is realized.

11 – The exemption stipulated in Article (4/Items 4, 14) of the Law is granted on the condition that the persons are resident in the State.

12 - The exemption stipulated in Article (4/Item 14) of the Law is granted under the following conditions:

A - The legal person eligible for exemption and the legal persons mentioned in paragraphs (B) and (C) of Article (4/Item 14) of the Law must keep accounting records that comply with applicable accounting standards in the State.

B - The persons holding shares of the exempted profits must be residents of the State and owners of those shares throughout the accounting period in which the exempted income is realized.

C - The Qatari natural persons must be direct owners and beneficiaries of the legal person mentioned in paragraphs (B) and (Y) of Article (4/Item 14) of the Law.

13 - For the purposes of applying Article (4/Item 16) of the Law, the exemption stipulated regarding the share of the non-Qatari investor does not apply to his share in the profits of companies owned by the company whose shares are offered for trading in the financial market inside the State.

14 - Citizens of the Gulf Cooperation Council States benefit from the same exemptions, and in accordance with the conditions established for Qatari citizens by Article (4) of the Law, and Law No. (9) of 1989 regarding the equal treatment of GCC citizens in tax transactions.".

### الفصل الثالث: الفترة المحاسبية (3-4)

#### Article 3

1- The accounting period, for the taxpayer who practices an activity, is the tax year, and the accounting period for the taxpayer is (12) twelve months, taking into account the following:

a- If the taxpayer starts the activity after the beginning of the tax year, the first accounting period is calculated from the date the activity starts, and the taxpayer must end it at the end of the tax year in which the activity began, provided that the accounting period is not less than (6) six months; if it is less than that, it ends at the end of the following tax year.

b- In case of liquidation of the activity, the accounting period extends from the end of the previous accounting period to the date of completion of the liquidation, provided that the accounting period does not exceed (12) twelve months; if it exceeds this duration, a new accounting period begins.

c- In the event of cessation of activity, assignment, or sale, the accounting period starts from the end of the previous accounting period to the date of cessation, assignment, or sale, provided that the Authority is notified within the statutory deadline. Cases of cessation, assignment, or sale include, but are not limited to, merger of the company, acquisition, or division according to the provisions of the Law / Act Regulating commercial companies. The period between the end of the accounting period before assignment or sale and the beginning of the new accounting period is considered an independent accounting period, provided that it is not less than (6) six months; if it is less, it is added to the first accounting period after the assignment or sale.

d- If the taxpayer undertakes a temporary activity not exceeding (18) eighteen months, the accounting period is the period of the activity.

e- In all cases, tax is calculated based on the income earned during the accounting period.

2- The taxpayer may request to use a different accounting period in the following cases:

a- If they are a member of a group of companies, or a branch of a foreign company, that uses an accounting period different from the tax year, the taxpayer may request approval of the accounting period used by the group, parent company, or head office.

b- If the nature of their activity requires the use of an accounting period different from the tax year.

3- In the event of a change in the accounting period, the period between the end of the previous accounting period and the beginning of the new one is considered an independent accounting period, provided it is not less than (6) six months; if it is less than this, it is added to the first accounting period after the change.

4- Taxpayers wishing to adopt a different accounting period must submit a request to the Authority when submitting the Registration application or no later than (30) thirty days before the end of the previous accounting period for which the taxpayer is about to submit the declaration and financial statements. Failure of the Authority to reply to the request within (60) sixty days from the date of submission is considered an implicit rejection of the request.

5- The Authority may withdraw approval to adopt an accounting period different from the tax year if it deems it necessary, and such withdrawal only takes effect from the end of the accounting period during which the withdrawal decision was issued. The first accounting period after the withdrawal decision is enforced shall be treated as the first accounting period after the change in accordance with item (3) of this article.

#### Article 4

1- The taxpayer must determine their income on the basis of accrual-based accounting applied in commercial accounting and in accordance with the accounting standards in force in the State, taking into consideration the provisions of the Law / Act and these regulations.

Income is recorded under accrual-based accounting when the taxpayer becomes entitled to receive it, even if it is paid in arrears or in installments, and expenses are recorded when the related obligation arises, through the occurrence of the event creating the obligation, regardless of the payment date.

2- A taxpayer whose gross income does not exceed one million (1,000,000) riyals during the previous accounting period may submit a request to the Authority to approve determining their taxable income on a cash basis. In this case, income is calculated upon its receipt or when it is ready for receipt, and expenses are calculated upon payment. The Authority must respond to the request within sixty (60) days, and if the Authority does not respond within this period, the request is considered implicitly rejected. If the gross income exceeds this amount, the taxpayer must adopt accrual-based accounting.

3- The annual gross income for Long-Term Contracts is determined by following the percentage-of-completion method in accordance with the accrual principle.

Long-Term Contracts refer to contracts carried out by the taxpayer for the benefit of others based on a specified value and whose actual duration exceeds eighteen (18) months.

4- Taking into account the exemptions stipulated in Article (4) of the Law / Act, capital gains arising from the disposal of shares or interests in companies resident in the State within the framework of a company merger or a total division of companies are subject to tax within the taxable income of the merged company or the company subject to division for the fiscal year in which the merger or division occurred, as the case may be.

## الباب الثاني: حساب الضريبة (5-24)

### الفصل الأول: الدخل الخاضع للضريبة (5-20)

#### Article 5

1- To determine the gross income, all revenues arising from transactions carried out by the taxpayer are taken into account, including the disposal of assets and incidental operations, unless exempted, and the Compensation due for the damage of an asset takes the Ruling of income arising from the disposal of that asset. The excess value arising from the revaluation of assets is not taken into account unless it is actually realized.

2- The excess value arising from the disposal of tangible and intangible assets is calculated as follows:

A- In the case of disposal of non-depreciable assets, the excess value is calculated based on the difference between the value of the consideration received for the asset or the market price, whichever is higher, and the cost of the asset.

B- In the case of disposal of depreciable assets, the excess value is calculated based on the difference between the value of the consideration received for the asset or the market price, whichever is higher, and the net book value.

C- In the case of disposal of equity shares of legal persons, the excess value is calculated based on the difference between the selling price or fair value, whichever is higher, and the seller’s share in the capital, provided that all supporting documents are submitted, taking into account all circumstances surrounding the transaction.

D- In the case of disposal of real estate owned by non-residents who do not carry out activities within the State, the excess value is calculated based on the difference between the selling price or the market price, whichever is higher, and the acquisition cost of the property.

3- To determine the taxable income, expenses and costs that meet the following conditions shall be deducted from the gross income:

A- They must be necessary for the purposes of the activity, in such a way that gross income would not be realized without them, excluding costs incurred for personal purposes or another taxpayer’s activity.

B- They must have been actually incurred and supported by documents, and documents especially include contracts, invoices, receipts, and others. In the case of deductible depreciation and provisions, this condition is considered met if the depreciation or provision is recorded in the accounting records, with supporting documents provided.

C- They must not increase the value of the fixed assets used in the activity, and fixed assets are determined according to the accounting standards in force in the State.

D- They must relate to the relevant tax year and be recorded in the accounting records.

4- The taxpayer may deduct the losses incurred during the tax year from the net income of subsequent years in accordance with the provisions of Article (7) of the Law / Act, taking into account the following:

A- Losses may not be carried forward for more than (5) five years starting after the end of the tax year in which they were incurred.

B- Losses arising from exempt or non-taxable sources of income may not be deducted.

#### Article 6

The taxpayer may not deduct expenses and costs incurred to achieve income exempt from tax as stipulated in Article (8/Item 1) of the Law / Act. If part of his income is exempt and another part is subject to tax, expenses and costs are deducted within the limits of the taxable income. This limit is calculated, in the absence of precise and regular data, by dividing the taxable revenues by the total revenues earned by the taxpayer.

#### Article 7 Amended

"1 - Subject to the provisions of tax Agreements, if a foreign enterprise carries on business in the State through a permanent establishment situated therein, the profits of the enterprise may be taxed in the State, but only so much of them as is attributable to the following:

a - The said permanent establishment.

b - Sales in the State of goods or merchandise of the same or similar kind as those sold through that permanent establishment.

c - Other business activities carried on in the State of the same or a similar kind as those effected through that permanent establishment.

2 - Subject to the provisions of paragraph (3) of this Article, where a foreign enterprise carries on business in the State through a permanent establishment situated therein, there shall in each State be attributed to that permanent establishment the profits which it might be expected to make if it were a distinct and separate enterprise engaged in the same or similar activities under the same or similar conditions and dealing wholly independently with the enterprise of which it is a permanent establishment.

3 - In determining the profits of a permanent establishment, there shall be allowed as deductions expenses incurred for the purposes of the permanent establishment, including executive and general administrative expenses so incurred, whether in the State or elsewhere.

By way of exception, no deduction shall be allowed in respect of amounts, other than reimbursement of actual expenses, paid by the permanent establishment to the head office of the enterprise or any of its other offices by way of royalties, fees, or other similar payments for the use of Patents or other rights, or by way of commissions, for specific services rendered, or for management, or by way of interest on moneys lent to the permanent establishment, except in the case of a banking enterprise.

Similarly, in determining the profits of a permanent establishment, no account shall be taken, except for reimbursement of actual expenses, of amounts charged by the permanent establishment to the head office of the enterprise or any of its other offices by way of royalties, fees, or other similar payments for the use of Patents or other rights, or by way of commissions for specific services rendered or for management, or by way of interest on moneys lent to the head office of the enterprise or any of its other offices, except in the case of a banking enterprise.

4 - For the purposes of this Article, the profits to be attributed to the permanent establishment shall be determined by the same method year by year unless there is good and sufficient reason to the contrary."

#### Article 8

1- Taking into account the conditions of the Litigant / Adversary stipulated in Article (5/Item 3) of these Regulations, the total expenses spent on entertainment, hospitality, restaurant meals, vacations, club subscriptions, and client gifts, as stipulated in Article (8/Item 5) of the Law / Act, shall be deducted up to (2%) of the total net income before making this deduction for the same accounting period, or (500,000) five hundred thousand riyals, whichever is higher. In all cases, expenses spent outside the State for these purposes shall not be deductible except up to (500,000) five hundred thousand riyals.

2- The total value of donations, contributions, grants, and subscriptions to Charitable Activities or paid in the State to any licensed non-profit entity in the State shall be deducted, provided that their value does not exceed (3%) three percent of net income before making this deduction. Zakat amounts paid by the taxpayer shall be treated as donations and deducted according to the same limits and conditions.

#### Article 9

Salaries and Wages, rewards, and their equivalents, including in-kind benefits, paid to members of the Board of Directors are not deducted, except for their salaries as employees in the same company.

#### Article 10

1- Interest on loans, and their equivalents, paid by the taxpayer to related parties as defined by International Accounting Standards, shall be deductible up to the limit of interest calculated on loans not exceeding three times the Equity Rights recorded in the taxpayer’s accounts during the relevant accounting period, provided that the loan contributes to generating economic benefits for the taxpayer, based on an agreement between the two parties specifying the loan term and its purpose.

2- It is not permissible to deduct interest paid by a permanent establishment in the State to the headquarters or main office or to any entity affiliated with it inside the State or outside it. (This clause has been repealed pursuant to Article 3 of Council of Ministers Decision No. 3 of 2023)

3- It is not permissible to deduct interest paid to the owner of a sole proprietorship in return for amounts deposited in his establishment.

#### Article 11

The deduction of the following expenses and costs is not allowed:

1- Income Tax paid by the taxpayer within the State and the taxes borne by the taxpayer outside the State.

2- Income Tax borne by the taxpayer instead of a person who is not resident in the State.

3- Indirect taxes that can be deducted or refunded in accordance with the provisions of the Regulating Law / Act.

#### Article 12

Bad debts are deductible if the following conditions are met:

1- The bad debt must have previously been included in the taxable revenues of the taxpayer in the year the debt became due.

2- At least twenty-four months must have passed since the debt became due.

3- The taxpayer must have formed sufficient provisions to cover the bad debt.

4- The taxpayer must provide evidence that collection of the debt is impossible despite having taken legal action to collect it.

5- The taxpayer must submit a certificate from the auditor stating that the debt has been written off from the books in accordance with applicable procedures.

6- Attach a list of bad debts, according to the form approved by the Authority, when submitting the tax return for the relevant year.

7- The taxpayer must commit to including the debt in his revenues in the year of collection in the event the debt is collected after being considered a bad debt.

#### Article 13

Only the following allowances are deductible:

1- Allowances for doubtful debts for Banks and finance companies, according to the following regulations:

a- These allowances must have been constituted in accordance with the limits and instructions issued by the Qatar Central Bank.

b- When the purpose for the allowance for doubtful debts no longer exists during any year, the portion of the allowance that was deducted pursuant to paragraph (a) of Item (1) of this article shall be returned to taxable income.

2- Allowances for outstanding risks and Compensation under settlement constituted by Insurance Companies and reinsurance companies, provided that these allowances have been constituted in accordance with the limits and instructions issued by the Qatar Central Bank. In the absence of such instructions, these allowances shall be deductible provided that the allowance for outstanding risks does not exceed 10% of the net income before deducting these two allowances and hospitality, entertainment and other expenses stipulated in article (8/Item 1) of these regulations, as well as donations, gifts and other amounts mentioned in article (8/Item 2) of these regulations.

The allowance for outstanding risks refers to the amount allocated by Insurance Companies and reinsurance companies at the end of the accounting period to meet their obligations towards risks that may materialize for insurance policies issued prior to the end of that accounting period and still in force during the next accounting period.

The allowance for Compensation under settlement refers to the amount allocated by Insurance Companies and reinsurance companies at the end of the accounting period to meet their obligations towards incidents that occurred and were reported before the end of that period and are still under settlement or have not yet been paid.

#### Article 14

No depreciation may be deducted for the following assets:

1- Land.

2- Goodwill or commercial reputation and similar items.

#### Article 15

Subject to the conditions stipulated in Article (5/Item 3) of these Regulations, the depreciation of fixed assets shall be deducted if the following conditions are met:

1- The asset subject to depreciation must be a fixed asset, according to the definition set forth in the accounting standards applicable in the State.

2- The asset must be used entirely for purposes of a taxable activity; in the event it is used partially for purposes of a taxable activity, depreciation shall be deducted only within the limits of such use.

3- The asset must be depreciable, so that its value decreases due to use, passage of time, or technological advancement.

4- The asset must be owned by the taxpayer by virtue of documents proving ownership, such as certificates of ownership, contracts, and the like.

Depreciation is calculated starting from the date of actual use or exploitation, based on the total cost actually incurred to acquire the asset and prepare it for use.

#### Article 18

Subject to the provisions of Articles (14) to (17) of these regulations, the rules and standards prescribed by the International Accounting Standards shall apply with respect to the depreciation of assets subject to finance or operating lease contracts.

#### Article 19

A person practicing a liberal profession may choose to deduct (30%) of the gross income instead of all the expenses and costs allowed to be deducted, and pay the tax based on (70%) of the gross income.

The selection is made by the taxpayer through a written request or by the electronic means specified by the Authority for the relevant year.

If the Authority does not respond to the request within (60) sixty days, it is considered accepted.

This choice remains valid for subsequent years until the taxpayer revokes it by submitting a request attached to the tax return.

A liberal profession means an activity practiced independently by the taxpayer, based on the use of scientific, technical, or practical knowledge and expertise.

#### Article 20

Commissions paid by foreign companies to their local agents are only deductible up to a limit of 3% of the total actual revenue from the activity.

### الفصل الثاني: الاستقطاع من المنبع (21-24)

#### Article 21

1- Amounts specified in Article (9/Item 2) of the Law, paid by natural persons conducting business in the State, and legal persons residing in the State, including ministries, other governmental agencies, public authorities and institutions, and permanent establishments in the State Reporting to persons not residing therein, are subject to withholding at source.

Except for amounts owed by ministries, other governmental agencies, public authorities and institutions, such amounts are deemed as actually paid by the passage of (12) twelve months maximum from the due date of those payments.

Administrative and general expenses of the headquarters specified in Article (7) of these regulations are not subject to withholding at source.

2- The remuneration for services specified in Article (9/Item 2) of the Law is subject to withholding at source at a rate of (5%) of the total amount, without deduction of any costs, if these services are performed wholly or partially in the State.

A service is considered performed wholly or partially in the State if any work necessary for its completion is conducted within the State, including particularly data collection, site inspection, and the completion of the service, even if done by a person other than the assignee; delivery of the service is not considered work necessary for its completion.

Services are also considered performed in the State as long as they are used, consumed, or utilized in the State, even if carried out wholly or partially outside the State.

3- The following activities are not considered as services whose remuneration is subject to withholding at source according to Article (9/Item 2) of the Law:

A- Reinsurance.

B- Shipping and ticket sales.

C- Maritime Transport of petroleum and its derivatives as well as manufactured materials derived therefrom.

4- The following interests are not considered interests subject to withholding at source:

A- Interests on deposits in Banks and financial institutions located in the State.

B- Interests on notes and bonds issued by the State, authorities, institutions, and public companies wholly or partially owned by the State.

C- Interests on transactions, facilities, and loans concluded with Banks and financial institutions.

D- Interests paid by a permanent establishment in the State to the headquarters or to a Reporting to entity of the headquarters outside the State.

#### Article 22 (Repeated)

2- Contracts and transactions concluded by any authorized debtor with a person residing in another contracting State.

3- Contracts and transactions that have been concluded and for which the implementation still entails the payment of financial amounts.

His Decision / Resolution must be justified, and this Capacity cannot be reassigned until one year has passed from the date of the withdrawal Decision / Resolution. Taxpayers who have disposed of their activity or ceased to exercise it, wholly or partially, must notify the Authority of this, using the form prepared by the Authority for this purpose, within thirty (30) days following the date of disposal or cessation, as the case may be.

Partial cessation of activity means the taxpayer has terminated an aspect of their activity or has terminated one or more branches through which this activity is carried out.

Total cessation includes the termination of all aspects of the activity, including mergers and divisions.

If cessation of activity is due to the taxpayer's death, their heirs must notify the Authority within sixty (60) days from the date of death.

The liquidator must notify the Authority of their appointment as a liquidator as well as the completion of liquidation proceedings, within thirty (30) days from the date of their appointment or from the date of completing the liquidation proceedings, as the case may be.

#### Article 36

### الفصل الثالث: إجراءات الفحص الضريبي (39)

#### Article 39

1- Taking into account the provisions of limitation stipulated in the Law / Act, the Authority has the right to examine the Tax Returns of the taxpayer, the attached documents, and any records, books, or other data it deems necessary to ensure that what is stated in the return complies with the provisions of the Law / Act and these regulations. For this purpose, the Authority may do any of the following:

B- Request the taxpayer or their tax agent to provide any information, documents, books, records, or data required for the examination within twenty (20) days from the date of notification.

C- Visit the place of activity during the taxpayer's working hours to conduct field inspections of their activity as well as to examine the books and records maintained by the taxpayer.

D- Retain copies of any books, records, or documents, whether paper or electronic, and seize their originals if the Authority deems it necessary.

E- Examine the books, records, and data of the taxpayer for the purpose of collecting information related to tax due from another taxpayer.

F- Access and examine the subsidiary information programs, systems, and applications used to record the taxpayer’s accounts and prepare their Tax Returns.

G- Access and examine the data and information necessary to utilize these programs, systems, and applications, as well as the restrictions and processes resulting from them, and also the databases used for handling transactions, issuing invoices, revenues, receipts, assets, or inventory.

2- When carrying out the examination, the Authority may notify the taxpayer using the form it prepares for this purpose. The notification must be given at least fifteen (15) days prior to the start of the tax examination process and must include the scheduled start date and the period or periods under examination.

3- The examination process shall be carried out at the Authority's headquarters or the taxpayer's premises, as determined by the Authority.

4- The taxpayer subject to examination has the right to:

A- Request the tax examiner’s official ID.

B- Obtain copies of any original documents or records that are seized or obtained by the Authority.

5- The taxpayer undergoing the tax examination must provide all necessary facilities and assistance to the tax examiner to enable them to perform their duties properly.

6- The Authority, when conducting the tax examination, may seek the assistance of specialists and technical experts from government entities or the private sector, provided they are not competitors of the taxpayer, if necessary.

7- The Authority may, before issuing the assessment decision, notify the taxpayer of the results of the tax examination. The taxpayer must submit their observations within no more than thirty (30) days from the date of notification. The taxpayer may also request to review or obtain copies of the documents and data the Authority relied upon in amending and assessing the tax due.

8- The Authority may request foreign tax authorities to conduct simultaneous audit and examination processes, and it may, upon request from a foreign tax authority, conduct simultaneous examinations in accordance with the controls and procedures determined by a decision of the Chairman / President.

### الفصل الرابع: الربط الضريبي (40-41)

#### Article 40

The Authority establishes the tax assessment as follows:

1- The declaration is considered as the tax assessment.

2- An amended assessment decision is issued on the form prepared for this purpose if the Authority makes any adjustment to the tax declaration submitted by the taxpayer.

3- An estimated assessment decision is issued on the form prepared for this purpose in all cases where it is not possible to assess the tax based on the taxpayer's actual income, including but not limited to the non-submission of the tax declaration or supporting data or documents, within the time limits specified in these regulations. Such data and documents include in particular the following:

a- Books or records stipulated in the Law / Act and these regulations, provided they are correct and properly kept in accordance with the prevailing laws and accounting standards in the State.

b- Information, statements and other documents that the Authority requests from the taxpayer for tax audit purposes, in accordance with these regulations.

4- The tax is estimated based on the objective evidence and indicators available to the Authority, especially the following:

a- The data available in the taxpayer’s accounts, even if not relied upon.

b- The nature and characteristics of the taxpayer’s activity.

c- Data relating to comparable cases.

d- Reports and data issued by independent parties related to the taxpayer's activity.

5- The Authority may, if the taxpayer fails to submit their tax declaration within the timeframes specified in these regulations and before issuing the assessment decision, send a notice requiring the submission of the declaration within no more than thirty (30) days from the date of notification.

6- The Authority may amend the assessment decision by reducing or increasing the amounts due, in order to correct material errors related to the calculation of the tax, and notify the taxpayer accordingly.

#### Article 41

1- The decision to assess the tax by amendment or Evaluation must in particular include the following data:

a- The facts, information, and evidence on which the tax assessment was based, including identification of the provisions of the Law / Act and the regulation that were relied upon to make the assessment.

b- The taxable income, the tax due, and the financial penalties related to it.

c- The period during which the tax must be paid, the financial penalties related to it, and the place of payment.

d- The right of the taxpayer to object and appeal against the assessment decision, in accordance with the provisions of the Law / Act and this regulation.

2- The Authority shall notify the taxpayer of the tax assessment decision using the form prepared for this purpose, in accordance with the provisions of Articles (67) and (68) of this regulation.

3- The Authority may correct clerical or mathematical errors that occur in the decisions and notifications it issues.

## الباب الخامس (42-43)

### الفصل الأول: الإعتراض (42)

#### Article 42

1- The taxpayer may object to the Decision / Resolution to assess the tax, by registered letter or any means that ensures notification, within (30) thirty days from the date of being notified, and the objection is submitted to the Authority, and submitting it results in suspension of the implementation of the Decision / Resolution to assess the tax.

2- The objection to the Decision / Resolution to assess the tax must specifically include the following data:

a- The taxpayer’s name and tax identification number.

b- The assessment Decision / Resolution subject to the objection.

c- The elements of the assessment subject to the objection and all supporting reasons for the objection, with any supporting documents attached.

d- The accepted elements of the assessment, if any.

3- The Authority shall consider the objection, and, for this purpose, it may request any additional information or documents, and the taxpayer must respond to this request within no more than thirty days. This period is not counted as part of the period granted to the Authority to respond to the objection.

4- The Authority shall notify the taxpayer or the Official with its Decision / Resolution on the objection, by any means that ensures notification, within (60) sixty days from the date the objection is submitted, and failure of the Authority to respond to the objection within this period is considered an implicit rejection of it.

5- If the taxpayer acknowledges in writing acceptance of the Authority's Decision / Resolution on the objection, or does not Raise a Grievance against this Decision / Resolution before the tax grievance committee in accordance with the provisions of Article (43) of this regulation, the Authority's Decision / Resolution on the objection or the Decision / Resolution to assess the tax, as the case may be, becomes final, and the tax and related financial penalties become due and payable.

### الفصل الثاني: التظلم (43)

#### Article 43

1- The taxpayer has the right to Raising a Grievance against the decision of the Authority issued regarding the objection, before the Tax Grievance Committee stipulated in Article (19) of the Law / Act, within thirty (30) days from the date of notification of the decision or from the expiration of the period specified for deciding on the objection without a response.

2- In accordance with the provisions of item (1) of this article, the taxpayer may also Raise a Grievance before the Tax Grievance Committee against decisions of the Authority in accordance with the Law / Act and these regulations within thirty (30) days from the date of notification of the Authority's decision or from the expiration of the periods specified for deciding on his requests.

## الباب السادس: تحصيل واسترداد الضريبة (44-47)

### الفصل الأول: تحصيل الضريبة (44-46)

#### Article 44

1- The taxpayer shall pay the due tax based on the tax declaration, on the same day of its submission.

2- The tax due based on the tax assessment decision and its related financial penalties shall be payable, and if the Authority issues a reassessment or estimation decision and the objection period stipulated in Article (17) of the Law / Act elapses without an objection being submitted, the taxpayer is obligated to pay the tax and its related financial penalties within thirty (30) days from the end date of the mentioned objection period.

3- If the Authority issues a tax assessment decision by amendment or estimation, and the taxpayer objects within the period stipulated in Article (17) of the Law / Act and a decision is made regarding the objection, the payable tax based on the Authority's decision on the objection and its related financial penalties are to be paid within thirty (30) days from the date the taxpayer is notified of the Authority's decision on the objection.

4- If the taxpayer objects to the tax assessment decision by amendment or estimation, and the Authority does not respond to the objection within sixty (60) days as stipulated in Article (18) of the Law / Act, the tax due is to be paid based on the tax assessment decision and its related financial penalties, within thirty (30) days from the expiration of the aforementioned sixty (60) days.

#### Article 45

1- The due tax and the related financial penalties shall be paid in a single payment, and it is permissible, with the approval of the Authority, to pay them in installments, based on the taxpayer's request, if the following conditions are met:

A- It must be proven that the taxpayer's financial situation is unstable, making it difficult to pay the tax and financial penalties in a single payment.

B- The taxpayer must not have previously obtained approval for installment payments and then failed to comply with the schedule of installments.

C- The amount requested to be paid in installments must not include tax due and financial penalties related thereto or fines arising from the taxpayer committing any of the Crimes stipulated in Article (26) of the Law / Act, or tax withheld at source or financial penalties related thereto.

2- Non-response from the Authority to a request for installments within sixty (60) days shall be considered an implicit rejection. If approved, the financial penalty stipulated in Article (24/Item 2) of the Law / Act with respect to the amounts covered by the request shall be suspended.

3- In all cases, if the taxpayer fails to pay any installment on the specified date, all remaining installments shall become immediately due, and the financial penalty stipulated in Article (24/Item 2) of the Law / Act shall be recalculated on the unpaid amounts, starting from the date of approval of the installment request.

#### Article 46

1- The Chairman / President shall take executive seizure measures on the taxpayer’s assets, necessary for the collection of the tax, whether these assets are in the possession of the taxpayer or others, in the cases where the tax assessment decision and the related financial penalties become final and have not been paid on the specified date, and this includes the following:

A- The lapse of the period specified for objection in accordance with Article (17) of the Law / Act, without the taxpayer objecting to the assessment decision.

B- The taxpayer’s written approval of the assessment decision or the decision of the Authority in the objection.

C- Issuance of a final decision by the Tax Grievance Committee.

D- Issuance of a final judicial Ruling regarding the tax assessment decision or the related financial penalties.

2- Taking into account the provisions of the laws regulating executive seizure, the Authority notifies the taxpayer by one of the means stipulated in Article (67/Item 1) of these regulations, of its intention to carry out an executive seizure on his assets, and requests payment of the amounts due within (30) thirty days, failing which the seizure will be executed within the limits of the amounts due.

3- If the taxpayer does not pay the amounts due within the period stipulated in Item (2) of this article, the Authority shall seize the taxpayer’s assets according to the established procedures, and the amounts due shall be collected in the following order:

A- Seizure and sale expenses.

B- Tax.

C- Financial penalties.

### الفصل الثاني: استرداد الضريبة (47)

#### Article 47

1- The taxpayer has the right to recover amounts of tax and related financial penalties collected from them without due right by submitting a request to the Authority, accompanied by all supporting documents proving their right to the recovery, in accordance with the provisions of Article (23) of the Law.

For recovery requests related to withholding at source, the applicant must attach to their request all documents and supporting evidence proving their right to recovery, especially the following:

A- A tax residency Testimony from the country of residence for the year subject to withholding and for which recovery is requested.

B- Evidence of the appointment of a tax agent or representative to carry out recovery procedures in the event that the recovery request is submitted by someone other than the beneficiary.

C- Withholding at source Testimonies from the party that carried out the withholding.

D- The Contract or agreement concluded with the party that carried out the withholding at source.

E- A list of shareholders in the capital of the company requesting recovery and the actual beneficiaries of the amounts paid by the party that carried out the withholding at source.

F- A bank Testimony for the account and bank identity of the applicant.

G- Rulings of the agreement under which withholding at source occurred without justification.

The Authority reviews the recovery request and notifies the taxpayer of its decision regarding the request by one of the means stipulated in Article (67/Item 1) of these regulations, within sixty (60) days from the date of submitting the request. Failure of the Authority to respond within the aforementioned period is considered an implicit refusal.

The taxpayer has the right to Raising a Grievance against the Authority’s decision on the recovery request before the Tax Grievance Committee, in accordance with the provisions of Article (43) of these regulations.

2- The Authority, as part of the review and verification of recovery requests, has the right to request any additional data or clarifications it deems necessary to decide on such requests. The taxpayer must submit them within a maximum period of thirty (30) days from the date of being notified of the Authority’s request. This period is not counted within the period for the Authority’s response to the recovery request stipulated in Item (1) of this Article. Failure by the taxpayer to submit the additional data or clarifications requested by the Authority results in rejection of the recovery request.

3- The taxpayer is entitled to Compensation / Indemnity at a rate of (0.1%) of the amount of the tax and related financial penalties collected without due right, for each month of delay or Part thereof after the lapse of sixty (60) days from the date of submitting the recovery request to the Authority, provided that all supporting documents proving entitlement to the recovery are submitted. Compensation / Indemnity calculation stops if a final judicial Ruling is issued in this regard.

4- The calculation of the delay period in recovery requests based on an international agreement does not begin until the date the Authority rejects the request or the expiry of the period set for decision without response.

5- The Authority must carry out reconciliation between the excess amounts paid by the taxpayer in tax or related financial penalties, and what is legally due and payable by them.

## الباب السابع: الجزاءات المالية (48-49)

### Article 48

1- The Chairman / President or his authorized representative imposes on the taxpayer the financial penalties stipulated in Article (24) of the Law / Act as follows:

A- In the case of issuing a linking decision by amendment or estimation, the financial penalties are included in the linking decision.

B- In cases other than the one referred to in the previous Item, the financial penalties are imposed by a separate decision and the taxpayer is notified thereof.

2- The period for calculating the delay stipulated in Article (24/Item 1 and 2) of the Law / Act begins on the day following the expiration of the period specified for submitting the declaration, or its extension, if the deadline for submission has been extended according to the provisions of these regulations.

3- For the purposes of calculating the financial penalty stipulated in Article (24/Item 2) of the Law / Act, a part of a month is considered a full month.

4- The financial penalty for delay in submitting the tax return, stipulated in Article (24/Item 1) of the Law / Act, is imposed in addition to the penalty stipulated in Item (4) of the same article if the taxpayer is benefiting from a tax exemption.

5- For the purposes of applying the provisions of Article (24/Item 6) of the Law / Act, the financial penalty is imposed for each contract, agreement, or transaction that the entity has not notified the Authority of, each individually.

6- Taking into account the provisions of Article (24/Item 6) of the Law / Act, the financial penalties stipulated in Article (24/Item 3) of the Law / Act shall apply to all notifications stipulated in these regulations.

### Article 49

1- For the purposes of applying the provisions of Article (25) of the Law / Act, the exemption limit prescribed for the president shall be applied for each tax year separately.

2- Exemption from financial penalties shall be granted based on a request submitted by the taxpayer or his tax agent, supported by justifications accepted by the Authority. The failure of the Authority to respond to the request within (60) sixty days from the date of its submission shall be considered an implicit rejection.

3- The exemption decision issued in accordance with the provisions of Article (25) of the Law / Act shall apply to the financial penalties imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article (24) of the Law / Act for the period prior to the issuance of the exemption decision.

## الباب الثامن: تجنب الضريبة (50-64)

### الفصل الأول: منع التجنب الضريبي (50-51)

#### Article 50

1- Tax avoidance of the tax due, in accordance with the provisions of Article (33/Paragraph One) of the Law / Act, refers to the taxpayer entering into agreements, operations, or transactions where one of their main objectives is to reduce the amount of income subject to tax, create a loss or increase in loss, or the taxpayer using double taxation avoidance agreements for this purpose. This includes situations where the amount of tax due becomes negligible.

2- The tax advantage, in the application of the provisions of Article (33/Paragraph One) of the Law / Act, in particular means the following:

a- Reducing the amount of tax due by decreasing total income or increasing deductions or losses.

b- Obtaining tax exemption.

c- Reclaiming amounts of tax or financial penalties previously paid.

3- The agreements, operations, and transactions stipulated in Article (33/Paragraph One) of the Law / Act, in particular, include the following:

a- Agreements, operations, and transactions that are organized and implemented through an arrangement or a series of interconnected arrangements aimed at tax avoidance. This Judgment / Ruling does not apply to agreements, operations, and transactions carried out in good faith for commercial purposes, where tax avoidance is not one of their main objectives.

b- Agreements, operations, and transactions whereby the taxpayer splits their income and wholly or partially transfers it to another person or persons related to them, with the aim of avoiding payment of tax, in whole or in part.

4- The Judgment / Ruling regarding the aforementioned agreements, operations, and transactions also applies to artificial avoidance of permanent establishment status through arrangements by agents and similar strategies, including, in particular, the following:

a- Where a person customarily plays the principal role in concluding contracts in the State on a routine basis, on behalf of another person who is not a resident therein, without substantial modification of those contracts by the person, if one of the following arrangements is met:

- The contracts are in the name of the non-resident.

- The contracts involve the transfer of ownership, or granting the right to use assets owned by the non-resident.

- The contracts involve the provision of services by the non-resident to a service recipient resident in the State.

b- Where a person, in the ordinary course of their activity as an independent agent, exclusively or almost exclusively carries out activities in the State on behalf of one or more related non-resident entities.

In both of these cases, the non-resident is considered to own a permanent establishment in the State with respect to any activity carried out in the State on their behalf. (This clause was cancelled pursuant to Article 3 of the Council of Ministers Decision No. 3 of 2023)

5- The tax advantage shall be withdrawn by the Authority in the cases stipulated in Article (33) of the Law / Act pursuant to a tax assessment decision in accordance with the provisions of Article (14) of the Law / Act. For the purpose of determining the tax due in this case, to the taxable income shall be added an amount equivalent to the benefit obtained by the taxpayer from the withdrawn tax advantage.

#### Article 51 Amended

Any Qatari project must carry out a substantive activity and have a real presence in the State. Residents in the State are not permitted to facilitate structures or arrangements aiming to generate profits that do not reflect a substantive activity in the State.

#### Article 51 Amended Canceled

In the application of the provisions of Article (33/Second Paragraph Item 1) of the Law / Act, full competition is applied according to the comparable uncontrolled price method, which is the price of the good or service that would have been applied if the transaction had taken place between unrelated parties. In the absence of the necessary data to apply the comparable uncontrolled price method, the taxpayer submits a request to the Authority to apply any of the other pricing methods approved by the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development. In the absence of comparability elements specific to the case under review, the Authority may use comparisons with similar activities, other sources of income, or any objective evidence available to the Authority.

### الفصل الثاني: التحويل غير المباشر للأرباح بين الكيانات المرتبطة (52-64)

#### Article 52

Any entity associated with other entities must give appropriate consideration to transfer pricing requirements in determining the prices and other terms of transactions between them, and in reporting the income generated from these transactions in its Tax Returns.

Associated entities refer to any entity considered as such according to international accounting standards.

Transfer prices refer to the prices at which an entity transfers tangible or intangible assets or provides services to entities associated with it.

#### Article 53

For tax calculation purposes, each entity must determine the transaction prices between itself and its related entities according to the arm’s length principle, based on reasonably available information, and must assess those prices at the time of the transaction, and in any case no later than the time of submitting the tax return for the accounting period during which the transaction took place.

Arm’s length pricing refers to the pricing applied between two independent entities.

#### Article 54

Every entity associated with other entities must conduct a functional analysis as reported in its tax declarations and review the comparable data available to it.

The functional analysis consists of the entity describing its position and economic role with the related entities, identifying the functions performed, potential risks, and the tangible and intangible assets used.

#### Article 55

For the purpose of applying the arm's length principle based on full competition, each entity related to other entities must update, every year, the financial data of the comparable transactions that take place between that entity and an independent entity, or between two independent entities.

The entity related to other entities may update searches in financial databases for comparable transactions every three (3) years, provided the business circumstances remain unchanged.

#### Article 56

An entity affiliated with other entities must provide the Authority with the necessary information to identify and assess transfer pricing risks related to it, and to audit its transfer pricing practices.

An entity affiliated with other entities must submit, with the annual tax return, a declaration on transfer pricing using the form prepared by the Authority for this purpose, if its total revenues or total assets shown in its balance sheet reach or exceed the amount determined by the Authority.

The Authority may request the entity affiliated with other entities to provide information that assists in identifying and assessing transfer pricing risks related to it or in conducting an audit of its transfer pricing practices within thirty (30) days at most from the date of the request, and the Authority may provide it with a questionnaire on transfer pricing in the areas it specifies using the form prepared by the Authority for this purpose.

The Authority may, during the tax examination process, request the affiliated entity to supplement the information provided in the transfer pricing form or in the questionnaire with additional information and documents.

#### Article 57

The resident entity connected to other entities must submit, within the same period specified for filing the tax return or at any other date specified by the Authority, a master file and a local file in the format adopted by the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development, unless the Authority adopts its own formats, provided that both of the following conditions are met:

1- Its total revenues or total assets, as shown in its financial statements, reach or exceed the amount determined by the Authority.

2- One of the connected entities is resident outside the State.

This obligation applies starting from the tax year that begins on or after the date specified by the Authority in a decision issued by the Chairman / President for this purpose.

#### Article 58

The Authority may rely on the information available in the master file and the local file when assessing transfer pricing risks and conducting tax audits.

#### Article 59

The entity must demonstrate to the authority that its transactions with related parties comply with the arm's length principle, and provide sufficient documents to prove this.

#### Article 60

The authority may request from the entity all information and documents in its possession that are necessary to conduct an audit of its transfer pricing practices in relation to its transactions with related entities, including:

1- Information and documents related to the entity’s operations and functions.

2- Information and documents relating to the operations, functions, and financial results of the related entities with whom it has entered into transactions.

3- Information related to potential comparisons, including internal comparisons of its related entities.

4- Documents related to the operations and financial results of unrelated entities that may be used for comparison, and the transactions conducted between them.

5- Other information and documents held by the entity or its related entities.

#### Article 61

For the purposes of applying the provisions of this section, a claim by an entity that other entities affiliated with it are responsible for complying with transfer pricing rules is not sufficient reason for that entity not to submit the required documents.

Full documentation of transactions between affiliated entities does not prevent the adjustment of their prices if it is proven that they were not conducted on the basis of full competition.

#### Article 62

The entity retains the information and documents related to transfer pricing regarding its transactions with related entities in accordance with the requirements of the Law / Act.

#### Article 63

The profits that have been indirectly transferred to another related entity, whether by increasing or decreasing the prices of the agreed transactions between them or by any other means, are added to the taxable income when calculating the tax due on the entity. This is done by determining the profits that have been indirectly transferred in comparison with the profits that would have been realized in the absence of any relationship between the two entities or any other relevant comparisons.

The provisions of the previous paragraph also apply to transactions conducted between:

1- Any entity resident in the State and another unrelated entity in the following two cases:

a- If one of the entities benefits from a preferential tax regime.

b- If the other entity is resident in a non-cooperative State or territory. A State or territory is considered non-cooperative if it has not concluded an agreement with the State of Qatar that allows for the exchange of information for tax purposes. The determination of non-cooperative States and territories is issued by a decision of the minister.

2- The entity and one of its permanent establishments if either conducts business in the State.

#### Article 64

The minister shall issue a decision on the conditions and procedures for the advance bilateral agreement on transfer pricing, as well as any necessary regulations for the application of the provisions of this section.

## الباب التاسع: أحكام عامة (65-71)

### Article 65

The taxpayer may appoint a registered tax Deputy / Assistant with the Authority to represent him, act on his behalf, and stand in for him regarding his tax affairs without prejudice to the taxpayer's legal responsibility. If the taxpayer or his representative before the Authority does not possess appropriate scientific accounting qualifications, he must appoint a tax Deputy / Assistant. In all cases, it is not permissible for the person who audited the taxpayer’s financial data to be his representative or tax Deputy / Assistant before the Authority.

The minister shall issue a decision regarding the procedures and conditions for appointing the tax Deputy / Assistant, his obligations, and the cases exempted from appointing a tax Deputy / Assistant.

### Article 66

Notifications and correspondence between the Authority and the tax agent of the taxpayer, who has been appointed in accordance with the provisions of these regulations, have the same legal validity as notifications and correspondence with the taxpayer himself.

### Article 67

1- Correspondence and notifications directed from the Authority to taxpayers or other persons addressed by the provisions of the Law / Act and this regulation shall be carried out by one of the following means:

a- Hand delivery with a receipt signature.

b- Registered mail.

c- Electronic means capable of proving acknowledgment of receipt, such as e-mail.

d- Electronic systems, software, and information applications approved by the Authority.

2- Correspondence and notifications directed from the taxpayer or any person addressed by the Law / Act to the Authority shall be carried out by one of the following means:

a- Registered mail to the postal address of the Authority.

b- Hand delivery to the Authority, in accordance with the procedures approved by it.

c- Electronic systems, software, and information applications approved by the Authority.

### Article 68

1- For the purposes of applying the provisions of the previous article, the following contact addresses shall be relied upon for the person to be notified:

A- The last postal address of the taxpayer registered with the Authority, or the one specified by the person to be notified, or disclosed by him, or the one he usually uses for his correspondence, or the last known place of residence or business.

B- The last email address of the taxpayer registered with the Authority, or the one specified by the person to be notified or disclosed by him.

2- In the case of notification via email to a legal person, this shall be in the following order:

A- The email address previously provided.

B- The email address of the legal representative of the legal person to be notified.

C- The last known email address of a person acting on behalf of the person to be notified.

### Article 69

The Authority establishes the electronic systems, programs, and information applications necessary for the transition towards the digital implementation of the provisions and procedures stipulated in the Law / Act and these regulations. The rules and procedures included in these systems shall apply insofar as they do not conflict with the Law / Act or these regulations.

Persons subject to the provisions of the Law / Act and these regulations must adhere to communicating with the Authority and fulfilling all their tax obligations through these electronic systems, programs, and information applications as of the date announced for the commencement of their operation on the Authority's website and in at least two widely circulated local daily newspapers.

All taxpayers registered at the time of implementation of these electronic systems, programs, and information applications must regularize their status and re-complete Registration with the Authority in accordance with the provisions of these regulations within ninety (90) days from the date of implementation of these systems. The President may extend this period for a similar period or periods.

### Article 70

In the case of a merger of companies in accordance with the Commercial Companies Law, the company into which the merger has taken place replaces the merged companies in all their Tax Obligations arising before the completion of the merger process.

Furthermore, companies established as a result of division succeed the company subject to division in all the Tax Obligations of the company subject to division arising before the completion of the division process, to the extent of the liabilities transferred to them from the company subject to division.

### Article 71

Ministries, other government agencies, Authorities, and public institutions must provide the Authority with any information or data it requests that are necessary for performing its duties, and cooperate with it in its areas of specialization.
