# Council of Ministers Decision No. (23) of 2019 on the terms and conditions for the participation of companies and entities established and registered with the Free Zones Authority and Qatar Financial Centre or with other entities legally authorized to do so in Tenders and Bids, practices, competitions, and direct agreement.

Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And the Judicial Authority Law / Act promulgated by Law / Act No. (10) of 2003, and its amendments,

And the Qatar Financial Centre Law / Act promulgated by Law / Act No. (7) of 2005, and its amendments,

And Law / Act No. (34) of 2005 regarding Free Zones, as amended by Decree by Law / Act No. (21) of 2017,

And the Law / Act Regulating Tenders and Bids promulgated by Law / Act No. (24) of 2015, as amended by Decree by Law / Act No. (18) of 2018,

And Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (29) of 1996 concerning the decisions / resolutions of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers submitted to the Amir for ratification and promulgation,

And the Executive Regulation of the Law / Act Regulating Tenders and Bids issued by Cabinet / Council of Ministers Decision / Resolution No. (16) of 2019,

And upon the proposal of the Minister of Finance,

Has decided as follows:

## المواد (1-8)

### Article 1

For the purposes of implementing the provisions of this Decision / Resolution, the term companies and entities refers to companies and entities established and registered with the Free Zones Authority or the Qatar Financial Centre or with any other entities legally licensed for that purpose.

### Article 2

Companies and entities, except for Insurance Companies and banks, are allowed to participate in Tenders and Bids, practices, competitions, and direct agreements, according to the terms and regulations stipulated in this Decision / Resolution.

### Article 3

The provisions of the Tenders and Bids Regulation Law and its aforementioned executive regulations apply to companies and entities when participating in Tenders and Bids, practices, competitions, and direct agreements.

### Article 4

Companies and entities must submit a request to the competent Department / Directorate at the Ministry of Finance to classify them according to the provisions of the Tenders and Bids Law and its referenced executive regulations. They must provide proof of Registration with the entity Reporting to them, and this Registration substitutes Registration in the Commercial Register.

### Article 5

The participation of companies and entities in Tenders and Bids, practices, competitions, and direct agreements is restricted to the activities they are licensed to carry out by the Reporting to entity.

### Article 6

Companies and entities, when participating in Tenders and Bids, practices, competitions, and direct agreements, are not entitled to any exemptions from taxes or fees or any other incentives or benefits granted to them under the laws governing their Reporting to entity.

### Article 7

The courts established in accordance with the provisions of the Judicial Authority Law referred to, exclusively and to the exclusion of other courts, have jurisdiction over disputes arising from contracts concluded by government entities with companies and entities, regarding Tenders and Bids, practices, competitions, and direct agreements.

### Article 8

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, must implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the date of its issuance. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.
