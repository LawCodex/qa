# Council of Ministers Decision No. (33) of 2026 regarding the amendment of the organization of certain administrative units comprising the Ministry of Municipality and assignment of their competencies

**The Council of Ministers,**

After having reviewed the Constitution,

And on the Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (29) of 1996 regarding the Decisions / Resolutions of The Council of Ministers that are submitted to the Amir for ratification and issuance,

And on the Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (57) of 2021 appointing the responsibilities of ministries, amended by Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (2) of 2025,

And on the Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (40) of 2022 regarding the organizational structure of the Ministry of Municipality,

And on the proposal of the Minister of Municipality,

**Decided / Resolved as follows:**

## Almawad (1-6)

### Article 1

يُعدل تنظيم بعض الوحدات الإدارية التي يتكون منها الهيكل التنظيمي لوزارة البلدية المنصوص عليها في المادة (1) من القرار الأميري رقم (40) لسنة 2022 المشار إليه ، والخريطة التنظيمية المرفقة به ، على النحو التالي :

أولًا : الوحدات الإدارية التابعة للوزير :

1. مكتب الوزير .

2. لمكتب الفني .

3. إدارة التدقيق الداخلي .

4. إدارة برامج البنية التحتية .

5. إدارة المشاريع والتطوير .

6. إدارة الشؤون القانونية .

ثانيًا : الوحدات الإدارية التابعة لوكيل الوزارة :

1. مكتب وكيل الوزارة .

2. إدارة التخطيط والجودة والابتكار .

3. إدارة التعاون الدولي .

4. إدارة العلاقات العامة .

5. مركز نظم المعلومات الجغرافية .

6. إدارة خدمة العملاء ومركز الاتصال الموحد .

ثالثًا : الوحدات الإدارية التابعة لوكيل الوزارة المساعد لشؤون التخطيط العمراني :

1. مكتب وكيل الوزارة المساعد .

2. إدارة التخطيط العمراني .

3. إدارة نزع الملكية .

4. إدارة الأراضي .

5. إدارة المساحة .

6. إدارة تخطيط البنية التحتية .

7. إدارة أملاك الدولة .

رابعًا : الوحدات الإدارية التابعة لوكيل الوزارة المساعد لشؤون الخدمات العامة :

1. مكتب وكيل الوزارة المساعد .

2. إدارة النظافة العامة .

3. إدارة الحدائق العامة .

4. إدارة الأعتدة الميكانيكية .

5. إدارة تدوير ومعالجة النفايات .

خامسًا : الوحدات الإدارية التابعة لوكيل الوزارة المساعد لشؤون الزراعة والأمن الغذائي :

1. مكتب وكيل الوزارة المساعد.

2. إدارة الشؤون الزراعية.

3. إدارة الثروة الحيوانية.

4. إدارة الثروة السمكية.

5. إدارة البحوث الزراعية.

6. إدارة الأمن الغذائي.

سادسًا : الوحدات الإدارية التابعة لمدير عام بلدية :

1. مكتب مدير عام بلدية.

2. إدارة الشؤون الفنية.

3. إدارة شؤون الخدمات.

4. إدارة الرقابة البلدية.

5. إدارة الشؤون العامة.

سابعًا : الوحدات الإدارية التابعة لوكيل الوزارة المساعد لشؤون الخدمات المشتركة :

1. مكتب وكيل الوزارة المساعد.

2. إدارة الموارد البشرية.

3. إدارة الشؤون المالية.

4. إدارة الخدمات الإدارية.

5. إدارة نظم المعلومات.

6. إدارة التدريب.

ثامنًا : الوحدات الإدارية التابعة لمدير عام الإدارة العامة لمجمع رخص البناء :

1. مكتب مدير عام الإدارة العامة لمجمع رخص البناء.

2. إدارة تراخيص البناء .

3. إدارة نظم وإجراءات التراخيص .

### Article 2

The Department / Directorate of Building Permits is responsible for the following:

1. Studying and reviewing the architectural designs submitted in the permit applications for villa projects with more than four residential Units, embassies, residential buildings, labor accommodation, major commercial, administrative, industrial, logistical, and service buildings, and assessing their compliance with the Building Code of the State of Qatar, then referring them to the relevant municipalities to proceed with issuing building permits.

2. Studying and reviewing technical requests related to building permits, maintenance, demolition, and certificates of completion of construction referred by the relevant municipalities.

3. Proposing design requirements and draft regulations for buildings, and referring them to the competent administrative Unit.

4. Carrying out technical inspections within the scope of the Department / Directorate, as well as others referred from the relevant municipalities.

5. Coordinating with the Department / Directorate of Urban Planning and various related service agencies concerning applications for projects assigned for its study.

### Article 3

The Licenses Systems and Procedures Management is responsible for the following:

1. Preparing and updating manuals, technical policies, and executive regulations related to the construction field, in coordination with the relevant administrative units and related entities.

2. Standardizing technical and organizational requirements and ensuring their compatibility among the related entities.

3. Preparing, developing, and improving the final procedures and forms for issuing construction, maintenance, and demolition Licenses, as well as building completion certificates, in coordination with the concerned administrative units, and working on the integration of related electronic systems.

4. Preparing and updating the performance evaluation guide for engineering consultancy offices (designers and supervisors) and contracting companies in the field of construction, and taking appropriate action according to approved standards.

5. Providing technical support to users of the electronic building permit system and related entities, in coordination with the relevant administrative units.

6. Preparing periodic reports on the performance of entities participating in the electronic building permit system and proposing the necessary improvements based on regular data analysis.

7. Receiving, studying, and following up on inquiries and complaints from reviewers relating to the construction field and suggesting appropriate solutions in coordination with the competent authorities, along with conducting a comprehensive periodic evaluation of the level of services provided through the electronic building permit system and submitting the necessary reports to the concerned entities to ensure the continuous improvement of service quality.

8. Coordinating with engineering consultancy offices and government agencies regarding Licenses requirements and their updates.

9. Issuing regulations and instructions organizing the procedures that engineering consultancy offices must adhere to within the electronic building permit system.

10. Coordinating with relevant entities to develop the systems applied in the business environment of the construction sector and their mechanisms, and determining mandatory timelines for their completion.

### Article 4

The competencies of "Department / Directorate of Public Relations", "Department / Directorate of Customer Service and Unified Call Center", and "Department / Directorate of Technical Affairs" stipulated in Article (9/Item 7), (12/Item 3), and (28) of the Decision / Resolution issued by the Emir No. (40) of 2022 referred to, are replaced by the following competencies :

**Article (9/Item 7):**

"7- Receiving requests from distinguished persons and persons with disabilities, in coordination with the relevant administrative Units to complete their transactions and overcome any problems or difficulties they may face."

**Article (12/Item 3):**

"3- Coordination with the relevant administrative Units to complete the transactions related to distinguished persons and persons with disabilities."

**Article (28):**

1. Supervising the service support areas Reporting to the ministry, and coordinating with the relevant authorities regarding public facilities and the services required for them.

2. Studying and reviewing the architectural designs included in License applications for residential villa projects up to (4) residential Units and private farms, and ensuring their compliance with the Building Requirements Manual of the State of Qatar.

3. Issuing Licenses for construction, excavation, maintenance, demolition, and certificates for completion of construction, including construction Licenses for projects studied by the Department / Directorate of Construction Licenses after verifying the approval of engineering plans and completion of other administrative requirements.

4. Issuing advertising Licenses in accordance with the laws and Decision / Resolutions regulating them.

5. Coordination with the administrative Units concerned with construction Licenses, systems, and License procedures regarding License applications studied by the Department / Directorate, which require a technical opinion.

6. Determining the locations for placing models and memorials on roads, squares, public places, or commercial complexes, in coordination with the administrative Unit concerned with urban planning.

7. Conducting inspections within the municipality's scope, and delivering sites for new construction Licenses.

### Article 5

The management of the Building Permit Complex stipulated in Decision / Resolution No. (40) of 2022 referred to is abolished.

### Article 6

A decision by the minister is issued to specify the responsibilities of the Office of the Director General of the Department / Directorate General of the Building Permit Complex.
