# Council of Ministers Decision No. (30) of 2026 amending certain provisions of the Executive Regulations of Law No. (25) of 2018 regarding the excise tax, issued by Council of Ministers Decision No. (5) of 2019

**مجلس الوزراء ،**

بعد الاطلاع على الدستور ،

وعلى قانون الجمارك الصادر بالقانون رقم (40) لسنة 2002 ، المعدل بالقانون رقم (10) لسنة 2023 ،

وعلى القانون رقم (25) لسنة 2018 بشأن الضريبة الانتقائية ، المعدل بالقانون رقم (2) لسنة 2026 ،

وعلى القرار الأميري رقم (29) لسنة 1996 بشأن قرارات مجلس الوزراء التي ترفع للأمير للتصديق عليها وإصدارها ،

وعلى القرار الأميري رقم (77) لسنة 2018 بإنشاء الهيئة العامة للضرائب ،

وعلى اللائحة التنفيذية لقانون الجمارك الصادرة بقرار مجلس الوزراء رقم (21) لسنة 2004 ، والقرارات المعدلة لها ،

وعلى اللائحة التنفيذية للقانون رقم (25) لسنة 2018 بشأن الضريبة الانتقائية ، الصادرة بقرار مجلس الوزراء رقم (5) لسنة 2019 ،

وعلى اقتراح وزير المالية ،

**قرر ما يلي :**

## Almawad (1-3)

### Article 1

The texts of Articles (3), (12), (14), (17), (22), (27), (30), (34), (36), (45), (51), (52), (53) of the Executive Regulation of Law No. (25) of 2018 referred to above shall be replaced with the following texts:

**Article (3):**

"1. For the purposes of applying the provisions of paragraph one of Article (4) of the Law, the value of excise goods shall be represented by the higher of the following two prices:

a. The standard price announced for the excise goods within the price list issued by a Decision of the Authority.

b. The declared retail sale price for the goods by the producer or importer, minus the included tax.

2. For the purposes of applying the provisions of paragraph two of Article (4) of the Law, the tax shall be imposed on a fixed value basis according to the total sugar content (natural and added) and other sweeteners in the concerned excise goods.

3. For the purposes of clause (1) of this Article, the value of the excise goods equals half the declared retail sale price for the goods.

4. By way of exception to the provisions of paragraph (b) of clause (1) of this Article, the value of excise goods for concentrates, powders, gels, or extracts that can be converted into energy drinks shall be calculated according to their declared sale price by the person liable to pay the tax, minus the included tax as per clause (2) of this Article, or according to the standard price stipulated in paragraph (a) of clause (1) of this Article, whichever is higher.

5. By way of exception to the provisions of clause (2) of this Article, the tax amount imposed on concentrates, powders, gels, or extracts that can be converted into sweetened drinks shall be calculated based on the total sugar content (natural and added) and other sweeteners in the final diluted product as per the attached guidelines to the product, and in the absence or proven inaccuracy of such guidelines, the Authority may establish a methodology to determine the dilution rate used for the purpose of calculating the tax amount."

**Article (12):**

"The Authority may decide to cancel registration sua sponte if it is proven that the registrant is involved in a tax evasion crime according to the provisions of the Law. In this case, the Authority shall directly notify the registrant of the cancellation of his registration and the effective date of cancellation.

The Authority may cancel the registration of the person liable to pay the tax sua sponte if the activities related to the excise tax are suspended for a period equal to or exceeding six months, unless the person liable to pay the tax proves the continuation of his activity. In such a case, the Authority shall notify the registrant of the cancellation of his registration and the effective date of cancellation.

In all cases, the cancellation of registration shall entail the cancellation of the license for the tax warehouse, and the registrant is obliged to pay the outstanding tax amounts and unpaid financial penalties immediately upon being notified of the cancellation of registration."

**Article (14):**

"The form referred to in the previous Article must include the following:

1. Name, address, and tax number.

2. Nature of the activity.

3. The tax period relevant to the Tax Return.

4. Type of excise goods.

5. Specified retail sale price (where applicable).

6. Sugar content (natural and added) in grams per (100) ml (where applicable).

7. Sweeteners content (artificial or otherwise) in grams per (100) ml (where applicable).

8. Taxable value and applicable tax rate.

9. Total calculated tax.

10. Tax amount eligible for deduction or refund, if any.

11. Net tax due for payment.

12. Declaration as to the accuracy and correctness of the information provided in the application.

13. Signature of the registrant.

14. Customs release showing the quantities and description of imported goods.

15. Official record of inventory count."

**Article (17):**

"The registrant is entitled to amend the Tax Return previously submitted to the Authority without being subject to a financial penalty according to clause (4) of Article (16) of the Law, provided the amendment is made within thirty days from the date of expiry of the period specified for submitting the return as stipulated in the Law and this Executive Regulation. A financial penalty shall apply if the amendment is made after the thirty-day period, provided that the increase in the tax due after the amendment exceeds ten percent (10%) of the total tax due after amendment relating to the return.

The financial penalty mentioned in clause (4) of Article (16) of the Law shall also apply if the amendment is made by the Authority after thirty days from the date of expiry of the period specified for submitting the return, under the same conditions stated in the previous paragraph of this Article.

The amended return shall be submitted via the same mechanism used for submitting the Tax Return, using a special form which, in addition to the information contained in the original return, includes a description of amended and original amounts, resulting differences, and the reason for the amendment. The amended return substitutes the original return.

The person liable to pay the tax may not amend the original Tax Return to reduce the tax if a tax assessment and related financial penalties decision has been issued.

In all cases, it is not permitted to amend the original return to reduce the tax after the lapse of five years following the year in which the return was submitted."

**Article (22):**

"Subject to the provisions of Article (23) of this Executive Regulation, the person liable to pay the tax must pay the tax due within the legal period for submitting the Tax Return.

In the case where the person liable to pay the tax is notified by a Decision of the Authority to amend the assessment or with estimated assessment, and the objection period expires without an objection being submitted, he must pay the tax differences and the due financial penalties within thirty days from the date of expiry of the objection period. In the case of an objection, the tax differences and due financial penalties must be paid within thirty days from the date of notification by the Authority of its response, or from the expiry of the period for ruling on the objection without a response, as the case may be.

The tax due and related financial penalties must be paid in one installment; the Authority may, upon its approval, permit payment of the tax due and financial penalties in installments if the person liable to pay the tax submits a substantiated application supported by documents, subject to the following conditions:

1. Proven instability of his financial situation, making it difficult to pay the tax and financial penalties in one installment.

2. Proven adherence to payment of all installments on time if prior approval has been obtained from the Authority to pay the tax and related financial penalties in installments.

3. No conviction by judicial ruling for committing any of the crimes stipulated in Article (18) of the Law during the three years prior to submitting the application.

The Authority shall rule on the installment request within sixty days from the date of submission. The applicant shall be notified of the Authority’s decision. If this period lapses without a response, the request is implicitly rejected. If approved, calculation of the financial penalty stipulated in Article (16/clause 3) of the Law on the amounts covered by the application is suspended starting from the date of submission of the approved installment request.

If the applicant fails to pay any installment on time, all remaining installments become immediately due and calculation of the financial penalty stipulated in Article (16/clause 3) of the Law resumes for unpaid amounts, effective from the date of submission of the Authority-approved installment request."

**Article (27):**

"Accounting records and supporting documents shall be preserved for ten years from the end of the year to which these records and documents relate, in a manner that prevents damage and that ensures they remain readable and reviewable throughout this period."

**Article (30):**

"Subject to the provisions on limitation stipulated in the Law, the Authority has the right to examine the Tax Returns of the person liable to pay the tax, the attached documents, and any other records, books, or data, including Customs Data and their supporting documents as deemed necessary to ensure compliance of the return with the provisions of the Law, this Executive Regulation, and any Executive Regulations.

The Authority has the right to do any of the following:

1. Invite the person liable to pay the tax or his tax agent to attend for discussion and provide any clarifications or information related to his activity, Tax Returns, records, books, and other data within a period specified by the Authority, recording his statements and clarifications in an Official Record prepared by the Authority for this purpose.

2. Request the person liable to pay the tax or his tax agent to provide any information, documents, books, records, or data required for examination within twenty days from notification.

3. Visit the place of business during the Working Hours of the person liable to pay the tax to conduct field inspections of his business and examine the books and records held by the person liable to pay the tax.

4. Retain copies of any books, records, or paper or electronic documents, and seize originals if necessary in the Authority’s opinion.

5. Examine the books, records, and data of the person liable to pay the tax for the purpose of collecting information related to tax due on another person liable to pay the tax.

6. Access and examine subsidiary software, systems, and applications used to record accounts of the person liable to pay the tax and prepare his Tax Returns.

7. Access and examine the data and information necessary for utilizing these software, systems, and applications, including entries and processing resulting from them, as well as databases used for handling transactions, issuing invoices, revenues, receipts, assets, or inventory."

**Article (34):**

"1. The tax warehouse allows the licensee to produce or convert excise goods, as well as to possess, store, or receive excise goods under a suspended tax status, according to one of the types of tax warehouses stated in clause (6) of this Article.

A person wishing to obtain a license for a tax warehouse must carry out actual activities in the State and submit a detailed application to the Authority specifying the following:

a. A list of goods to be produced or stored in the warehouse and their characteristics.

b. A description of the activity type for the tax warehouse.

c. Specification of detailed security procedures for safeguarding facilities, documents, and excise goods as determined by the Authority.

d. Estimation of the quantity and value of goods to be produced or stored in the warehouse when operating at maximum capacity.

e. Locations of industrial warehouses and inventory of machines and equipment supplied to them and their production capabilities.

f. A detailed description of control systems to be used in the tax warehouse, including a system for controlling the inventory of excise goods such that at any time the movement of excise goods to and from the tax warehouse and stock levels therein can be determined.

2. The applicant must attach the following documents to the application:

a. A bank guarantee specified by the Authority to cover risks related to production, conversion, possession, storage, or receipt of excise goods upon receiving preliminary approval for the license application. The Authority shall establish a mechanism and criteria for determining the guarantee value.

b. A commitment to pay the annual fee.

c. A copy of the lease contract for the building or buildings owned by the company, or proof of ownership, specifying the area and boundaries of the warehouse.

d. Official record of inventory of goods.

e. Any additional documents deemed necessary by the Authority.

3. The Authority may require a minimum quantity or value of excise goods to be produced or stored in the tax warehouse as a condition for granting the tax warehouse license.

4. The license application specified in clause (1) of this Article must be submitted for each warehouse to be licensed as a tax warehouse.

5. The following are not eligible for a tax warehouse license:

a. Places where excise goods are sold directly to consumers, except stores located in free markets and any other places specified by the Authority.

b. Places where concentrates, powders, gels, and extracts are converted into final excise goods for direct sale to consumers.

6. The tax warehouses eligible for licensing include the following types:

a. Private warehouse: the place licensed for the person liable to pay the tax to deposit excise goods for his own account according to the provisions of the Law and this Executive Regulation.

b. Public warehouse: the place licensed as a Customs warehouse upon Import where excise goods are deposited on behalf of third parties according to the provisions of the Law and this Executive Regulation.

7. Subject to other conditions stipulated in this Executive Regulation for licensing a tax warehouse, the following shall be considered:

a. Commitment to recording excise goods in accounting records and systems in the name of the owners of the goods.

b. Commitment to joint liability for payment of tax due and any financial penalties resulting from violations by owners of excise goods of the provisions of the Law, its Executive Regulation, and its Executive Regulations.

c. Commitment to providing any data, information, or documents required by the Authority regarding excise goods, their owners, and movement into and out of the public warehouse.

d. Commitment to the conditions and procedures stipulated in the Customs Law, its Executive Regulations, and Executive Regulations relating to public warehouses."

**Article (36):**

"The licensee must adhere to the following requirements:

1. Notify the Authority of any changes in the information on the basis of which the license was issued.

2. Maintain the necessary accounting records as specified in Article (25) of this Executive Regulation.

3. Record transfer of excise goods when under a suspended tax status, as per the approved mechanism.

4. Retain approved reports according to the mechanism specified by the Authority, enabling identification of the natural reasons for loss of excise goods (evaporation, leakage, damage within permitted limits) so that they are not considered consumption."

**Article (45):**

"1. The right to tax refund is established from the date of occurrence of the event giving rise to the refund right as stipulated in Article (12) of the Law. The refund request must be submitted to the Authority within five years from the end of the tax period in which the refund event occurred in accordance with an approved form containing information and supporting documents.

2. For the purposes of paragraph three of Article (12) of the Law, a refund request may be submitted for the tax paid on excise goods released for consumption and for which tax was paid in the State and subsequently transferred to another member State and taxed there, as proven according to the mechanism agreed between Council States for this purpose.

3. Subject to the refund provisions stipulated in this Executive Regulation, the refund request for tax amounts collected by the General Authority of Customs shall be submitted by the person entitled to the refund according to the approved form and provided there are no outstanding tax amounts or unfiled Tax Returns with the Authority at the date of submission.

The General Authority of Customs shall review the application and refund the amounts due according to the procedures stipulated in the Customs Law, its Executive Regulations, and the decisions issued in this regard. The General Authority of Customs shall send all information and documents related to the tax refund to the Authority as soon as the decision on the request is finalized and the amounts transferred to the beneficiary. Settlements of amounts between the Authority and the General Authority of Customs shall be made in accordance with the agreed mechanism.

4. For the purposes of refunding tax paid on excise goods implementing the provisions of paragraph four of Article (12) of the Law, the following conditions must be met:

a. The requesting entity submits an application via the electronic system approved by the Authority for obtaining an identification number according to the procedures specified by the Authority, if it is not required to undergo Tax Registration for excise tax purposes.

b. The person liable to pay the tax notifies the Authority of damage or loss of excise goods in accordance with the procedures and deadlines stipulated in Article (4) of this Executive Regulation.

c. The International Agreements concluded between non-Qatari military entities and the State of Qatar must contain provisions granting exemption from excise tax or any similar tax.

d. Duty-free shops, airlines, and International Maritime Transport companies must comply with the exemption conditions in Council of Ministers Decision No. (16) of 2020 regarding the definition of exempt excise goods and exemption conditions.

e. Health institutions must submit a certificate from the Ministry of Public Health proving exclusive use of excise goods for therapeutic purposes, as per Council of Ministers Decision No. (16) of 2020 regarding the definition of exempt excise goods and exemption conditions.

f. A person not liable to pay the tax must notify the Authority in advance of his intent to Export or re-Export excise goods according to the stipulated procedures, and subject to the following:

- Exporting or re-Exporting excise goods within a period not exceeding one Gregorian year from the date of Import by the actual importer or acquisition locally, with the goods valid for use at the date of Export or re-Export.

- Proof of payment of tax on excise goods imported or acquired locally.

- Evidence of relationship with the importer who performed the actual Import or the local producer, in accordance with the same procedures entitling Customs Duties refund.

5. A refund request may be submitted pursuant to the provisions of paragraph four of Article (12) of the Law by:

a. The person liable to pay the tax on damaged or lost goods outside the suspended tax status.

b. Non-Qatari military entities concluding International Agreements with the State.

c. Duty-free shops, airlines, and International Maritime Transport companies for excise goods released for consumption and acquired by them in the local market.

d. Health institutions regarding excise goods used exclusively for therapeutic purposes bought locally, excluding pharmacies and pharmaceutical manufacturing/promotion/distribution companies.

e. Anyone who has actually borne payment of the excise tax due on Export or re-Export of excise goods.

6. Additional refund cases pursuant to the provisions of paragraph four of Article (12) of the Law shall be subject to the refund procedures stipulated in this Article and Articles (46) to (49) of this Executive Regulation."

**Article (51):**

"1. Subject to the provisions of clause (2) of this Article, employees of the Authority empowered with the capacity of Law enforcement Officer under Article (24) of the Law are entitled, without prior notification, to enter the premises where the person liable to pay the tax conducts his activity and its annexes, his tax warehouses, or any other location where records or inventory are kept.

2. Inspection and control shall be carried out during the Working Hours of the person liable to pay the tax."

**Article (52):**

"1. The Authority may do the following:

a. Conduct a field inspection of the place of business during the Working Hours of the person liable to pay the tax.

b. Examine the records, accounts, and data stipulated in the Law and this Executive Regulation, and take copies or seize them if necessary.

c. Examine the books, records, and data of the person liable to pay the tax for the purpose of collecting information about another person liable to pay the tax.

2. If the Authority has reasonable grounds indicating a violation of the provisions of the Law and this Executive Regulation, and for the purpose of protecting State revenues, Law enforcement Officer are entitled to enter tax warehouses and premises where the person liable to pay the tax conducts his activity and its annexes outside Working Hours."

**Article (53):**

"A person subject to control and inspection procedures is entitled to:

1. Request employees of the Authority to present an official identification card.

2. Attend the control and inspection process if conducted at his place of business or at the location of accounting records or inventory.

3. Obtain a copy of the books, records, and documents that were seized."

### Article 2

The following texts are added to the Executive Regulations of Law No. (25) of 2018 referred to:

**Article (4/ Item 4):**

« 4- The provisions and procedures in force according to the provisions of the Customs Law are applied with regard to the destruction processes of selective goods under suspended tax status in a tax customs warehouse.»

**Article (6 bis):**

« The person obliged to pay the tax must submit to the Authority a Registration request for selective goods before import or local production of these goods, according to the template prepared for this purpose.

The Authority decides on the Registration request and sets a minimum value for the goods on which the tax is calculated, in accordance with the provisions of the Law and these Executive Regulations.

The person obligated to pay the tax may submit a justified request to amend the selective goods Registration data.

The Authority may, sua sponte / motu proprio, register the selective goods and amend them based on the substantive elements available to it, provided that the person obligated to pay the tax is notified of this.

The person obliged to pay the tax must attach the necessary documents issued by the competent authorities of the State relating to approval for import or production of selective goods and their classification, in accordance with the procedures followed in the State.»

**Article (13/ Second and Third Paragraphs):**

« Producers of selective goods may, within the tax declaration, deduct the tax paid on goods used in producing other selective goods subject to tax from the amount of tax due for the tax period during which those goods were produced, and this deduction is considered as a refund for the tax paid on selective goods if they are used in producing other selective goods subject to tax according to Article (12) of the Law.

Holders liable to pay the selective tax may also, within the tax declaration, deduct the tax paid on goods that were exported or re-exported, and this deduction is considered as a refund for the tax paid on selective goods that were exported or re-exported in accordance with Article (12) of the Law, in which case the supporting documents of the export or re-export process must be attached to the relevant tax declaration.»

**Article (30 bis):**

« The Authority, when conducting an inspection, may notify the person obliged to pay the tax on the template it prepares for this purpose. The notification must be made no less than fifteen days before the commencement of the tax inspection. The prior notification must include the start date of the inspection and the tax period(s) subject to inspection.

The inspection is conducted at the headquarters of the Authority or at the headquarters of the person obliged to pay the tax, as decided by the Authority.

The person obligated to pay the tax subject to inspection has the following rights:

1. Request to display the official card by the tax inspector.

2. Obtain copies of any original documents or records retained or obtained by the Authority.

The person obliged to pay the tax subject to tax inspection must provide all necessary facilities and assistance to the tax inspector to enable him to perform his duties fully.

The Authority may, when conducting a tax inspection, seek the assistance of specialists and technical experts from government agencies or the private sector (who are not competitors of the person obliged to pay the tax) if required.

The Authority may, before issuing the assessment decision, notify the person obliged to pay the tax of the results of the tax inspection. The person obliged to pay the tax must make remarks within thirty days from the date of notification and may request to review or obtain copies of documents and data used by the Authority to amend the Evaluation of the tax due.

The Authority may request government agencies, authorities, public institutions, associations, private entities, charitable organizations, institutions of public benefit, persons obliged to pay the tax, companies, and other individual establishments to notify it, whenever requested or periodically, of any information, data, or documents it deems necessary for tax inspection purposes or for information exchange in accordance with regional and international agreements.

**Article (30 bis 1):**

« The Authority issues its decision to assess the tax, as follows:

1. If the Authority makes amendments to the declaration, it issues a tax assessment decision by amendment on the template prepared for this purpose.

2. If the Authority assesses the tax on an estimated basis, it issues a tax assessment decision by Evaluation on the template prepared for this purpose, including in particular the following cases:

a. Failure to submit the declaration or its attachments within the periods specified by these Executive Regulations.

b. Failure to keep records as stipulated in this Law and its Executive Regulations, or if these records are incorrect or irregular.

c. Failure to provide information, clarifications, other records, and documents requested by the Authority, or when incorrect information, records, or documents are provided, or they are incorrect or irregular.

The tax is assessed on an estimated basis relying on substantive evidence and indications available to the Authority, in particular:

a. The data available in the accounts of the person obliged to pay the tax, even if not relied upon.

b. The nature and characteristics of the activity of the person obliged to pay the tax.

c. Data related to comparable cases.

d. Reports and data issued by independent entities related to the activity of the person obliged to pay the tax.

The Authority may, if the person obliged to pay the tax does not submit his tax declaration within the periods specified by these Executive Regulations, and before issuing the assessment decision, issue him a notice to submit his declaration within thirty days from the date of notification.

The Authority may also amend the assessment decision by reducing or increasing the amounts due to address material errors related to the calculation of the tax, with notification to the person obliged to pay the tax.»

**Article (49/ Second Paragraph):**

« The Authority deducts the taxes and financial penalties due from the amount of tax refundable at the date of completion of the disbursement procedures.»

**Article (55):**

« The Chairman or his delegate signs on the person obliged to pay the tax the financial penalties stipulated in Article (16) of the Law, as follows:

1. In case of issuing an assessment decision by amendment or Evaluation, the financial penalties are included in the assessment decision.

2. Other than the case referred to in the previous Item, financial penalties are signed by a separate decision and the person obliged to pay the tax is notified of it.

3. The period for calculating the delay specified in Article (16/ Items 2, 3) of the Law starts from the day following the expiry of the specified deadline for submitting the declaration or its extension, if the deadline for submitting the declaration was extended according to the provisions of these Executive Regulations.

4. For the purposes of calculating the financial penalty stipulated in Article (16/ Item 3) of the Law, part of a month is considered a full month.»

**Article (56):**

« For the purposes of applying the provisions of Article (17) of the Law, the exemption limit determined for the Chairman or his delegate is applied for each year separately.

The exemption from financial penalties is granted based on a request submitted by the person obliged to pay the tax or his tax agent, based on justifications accepted by the Authority, and the Authority’s failure to respond to the request within sixty days from its submission is considered an implicit refusal.

The exemption decision issued under the provisions of Article (17) of the Law applies to financial penalties imposed under the provisions of Article (16) of the Law for the period prior to the issuance of the exemption decision.»

**Article (57):**

« Correspondence and notifications addressed from the Authority to persons obliged to pay the tax or others addressed under the Law and these Executive Regulations are made by one of the following means:

1. Hand delivery with signed receipt.

2. Registered mail.

3. Electronic means able to prove receipt, like email.

4. Electronic systems, software, and information applications approved by the Authority.

Correspondence and notifications addressed from the person obliged to pay the tax or any person addressed under the Law to the Authority are made by one of the following means:

1. Registered mail to the Authority’s postal address.

2. Hand delivery to the Authority according to its approved procedures.

3. Electronic systems, software, and information applications approved by the Authority.»

**Article (58):**

« Without prejudice to the provision of the previous Article, the following contact addresses are relied upon for the person to be notified:

1. The last postal address of the person obliged to pay the tax recorded with the Authority or that specified by the person to be notified, or disclosed, or used ordinarily in correspondence, or the last known place of residence or business.

2. The last email address of the person obliged to pay the tax recorded with the Authority or specified by the person to be notified, or disclosed.

Notification via email for a legal person is in the following order:

1. The email address previously provided.

2. The email address of the legal representative of the legal person to be notified.

3. The last known email address of a person acting on behalf of the person to be notified.»

### Article 3

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into force the day following its publication in the Official Gazette.
