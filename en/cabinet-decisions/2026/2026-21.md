# Council of Ministers Decision No. (21) of 2026 amending some provisions of Decision / Resolution No. (28) of 2020 regarding the determination of areas in which non-Qataris may own and benefit from real estate, and the conditions, controls, advantages, and procedures for their ownership and benefit.

Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

After reviewing the Constitution,

and the Law / Act No. (16) of 2018 concerning the regulation of non-Qatari ownership and use of real estate, as amended by Law / Act No. (1) of 2025,

and Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (29) of 1996 on Cabinet / Council of Ministers decisions presented to the Emir for ratification and issuance,

and Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (28) of 2023 establishing the General Authority for Regulating the Real Estate Sector,

and Council of Ministers Decision No. (28) of 2020 on determining the areas in which non-Qataris may own and use real estate as well as the conditions, controls, privileges, and procedures for their ownership and use,

and upon the proposal of the Minister of Municipality,

**decided as follows:**

## المواد (1-4)

### Article 1

The phrase "Minister of Municipality" shall be replaced with the phrase "Minister of Justice" as mentioned in Article (1) of Decision / Resolution No. (28) of 2020 referred to.

### Article 2

The text of Article (6) of Decision / Resolution No. (28) of 2020 referred to, shall be replaced with the following text:

**Article (6):**

"The Real Estate Registration Department at the Ministry of Justice shall undertake the procedures for the Registration of ownership or usufruct of real estate, or any transactions pertaining thereto, in accordance with the provisions stipulated by law."

### Article 3

Schedule No. (1) attached to this Decision / Resolution is replaced with Schedule No. (1) attached to Decision / Resolution No. (28) of 2020 referred to.

The organizational and cadastral map attached to Schedule No. (1) attached to this Decision / Resolution is also replaced with the organizational and cadastral map attached to Schedule No. (1) attached to Decision / Resolution No. (28) of 2020 referred to.

### Article 4

All relevant authorities, each in its area of responsibility, must implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the day following its publication in the official gazette.
