# Council of Ministers Decision No. (31) of 2026 concerning the tables of sub-excise goods names and the Harmonized System code for each of them

**The Council of Ministers,**

After having reviewed the Constitution,

and Law / Act of Customs issued by Law / Act No. (40) of 2002, as amended by Law / Act No. (10) of 2023,

and Law / Act No. (25) of 2018 regarding the selective tax, as amended by Law / Act No. (2) of 2026,

and Emiri Decision / Resolution No. (29) of 1996 regarding the Council of Ministers Decisions submitted to the Amir for approval and issuance,

and Council of Ministers Decision No. (9) of 2021 issuing the table of designations for sub-selective goods and the harmonized system code for each of them,

and upon the proposal of the Minister of Finance,

**has decided as follows:**

## Almawad (1-6)

### Article 1 (Article 2)

The tables of selective commodity sub-names and the Harmonized System code for each of them, numbers (1), (2), (3), (4), attached to this Decision / Resolution shall be applied.

### Article 2

Tobacco and its derivatives include all categories stated in Schedule No. (1) attached to this Decision / Resolution.

### Article 3

Energy drinks refer to the following goods:

1. Drinks to which stimulating substances are added, according to the definition provided in the technical regulations and Gulf standards and the technical regulations and Qatari standards for energy drinks.

2. Concentrates, powders, gels, or extracts that can be converted into energy drinks.

Energy drinks include the types specified in Schedule No. (2) attached to this Decision / Resolution.

Energy drinks do not include tea, coffee, or beverages containing alcohol, even if they are energy drinks.

If the products referred to in item (2) of this article have already been subject to tax, the final energy drink composed of mixing those products with any other products or agents, whether carbonated or non-carbonated, by someone who is not obligated to pay the tax, is not considered a selective good for the purposes of Law / Act No. (25) of 2018 mentioned, and no tax is due on it.

If any product meets the definition of energy drinks referred to in this article and the definition of sweetened beverages referred to in Article (4) of this Decision / Resolution, it is considered among the energy drinks.

### Article 4

1. Sweetened beverages, in the application of Schedule No. (3) attached to this Decision / Resolution, means any product to which a sugar source or sweeteners have been added, produced for consumption as a beverage whether it is ready to drink or in concentrates or powders or gels or extracts or any form that can be converted into a beverage.

2. For the purposes of this Article, sugar includes any type of sugar specified according to the Specifications standard No. (148) by the Gulf Cooperation Council Standardization Organization under the title “Sugar”, and any subsequent and relevant Specifications standards.

3. For the purposes of this Article, artificial sweeteners include any type of sweeteners specified according to the Specifications standard No. (995) by the Gulf Cooperation Council Standardization Organization under the title “Permitted Sweeteners Used in Food Products”, and any subsequent and relevant Specifications standards.

4. For the purposes of Article (2) of Law / Act No. (25) of 2018 referred to and in this Article, the following goods are exempted from the definition of sweetened beverages:

a. Energy drinks.

b. Beverages that contain alcohol.

c. 100% natural fruit and vegetable juices (which have not had any sugar source or other sweeteners added), including the following:

1. 100% fresh fruit and vegetable juice.

2. Mashed fruit.

3. Dried fruit juice.

4. Fruit powder juice.

5. Beverages made from fruit or vegetable juice with no sugar source or other sweeteners added.

6. Beverages prepared by mixing liquids.

All these types are subject to the Specifications standard No. (1820) by the Gulf Cooperation Council Standardization Organization for “Fruit Juices and Nectars” and Specifications standard No. (2456) by the Council Organization for “Fresh Fruit Juice (Non-Pasteurized)”, and any updates thereto.

d. A beverage that contains at least (75%) milk of the ready-to-drink beverage content, including types of milk as follows:

1. Animal milk.

2. Reconstituted milk.

3. Milk powder.

4. Yogurt (fermented milk).

5. Whey.

6. Reconstituted whey.

7. The definition of milk does not include cream.

e. A beverage that contains at least (75%) milk alternatives of the ready-to-drink beverage content, which is classified as a milk alternative beverage if all of the following conditions are collectively met:

1. Contains (120) mg of calcium per (100) ml.

2. Extracted from legumes or grains or nuts or seeds or any other type of plants.

3. Used in all or most uses of milk.

4. Has a density similar to milk.

5. Does not contain gaseous ingredients.

f. Beverages and concentrates intended for nutritional and special medical uses, which are subject to Specifications standard No. (654) by the Gulf Cooperation Council Standardization Organization for “General Requirements for Packaged Foods with Special Nutritional Uses” or Specifications standard No. (1366) by the Council Organization for “General Requirements for the Circulation of Foods Used for Special Medical Purposes”, and any updates thereto.

g. Beverages and concentrates containing any source added from sugar or other sweeteners, prepared by natural persons for personal consumption or for other non-commercial purposes.

h. Beverages containing any added source of sugar or other sweeteners, prepared in restaurants and similar places and served in an open, non-sealed container to final consumers for immediate consumption as a beverage.

If the products referred to in the previous paragraph have previously been subject to tax, the final sweetened beverage resulting from the mixing of those sweetened products by someone who is not liable to pay the tax shall not be considered a selective good for the purposes of Law / Act No. (25) of 2018 referred to, and no tax is due on it.

### Article 5

Council of Ministers Decision No. (9) of 2021 referred to is hereby cancelled.

### Article 6

All relevant authorities, each within their remit, must implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into effect the day following its publication in the Official Gazette.
