# Council of Ministers Decision No. (32) of 2026 amending the organization of certain administrative units that comprise the Ministry of Finance and determining their functions

**The Council of Ministers,**

After having reviewed the Constitution,

And after reviewing the Amiri Resolution No. (29) of 1996 regarding the resolutions of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers that are submitted to the Emir for ratification and issuance,

And after reviewing the Amiri Resolution No. (57) of 2021 appointing the functions of ministries, amended by Amiri Resolution No. (2) of 2025,

And after reviewing the Amiri Resolution No. (34) of 2022 regarding the organizational structure of the Ministry of Finance,

And upon the proposal of the Minister of Finance,

**Resolved as follows:**

## Almawad (1-4)

### Article 1

The responsibilities of "Management of Public Projects Organization," "Accounts and General Treasury Management," and "Public Relations and Communication Management," as stipulated in Articles (9), (13/Item 5), and (16/Item 9) of the referred Amiri Decision No. (34) of 2022, are replaced with the following responsibilities:

**Article (9):**

1. Proposing the policies and mechanisms necessary for organizing and **Project Monitoring**, in coordination with the competent authorities.

2. Evaluating existing and proposed public projects from entities whose budgets are funded by the State, according to budget ceilings, the general budget, and annual financial flows, in coordination with government financial Units.

3. Project Monitoring of public projects to achieve the optimal use of the State’s resources, and directing them in line with the comprehensive development policy of the State in this regard.

4. Establishing an integrated database for public projects, and working to update it with the necessary data through linkage with the competent authorities in the State, in coordination with the relevant administrative Unit in the Ministry.

5. Policy Formulation, governance frameworks, and operational models for Asset Management, and Project Monitoring of their implementation after approval, in coordination with the relevant administrative Unit in the Ministry and competent authorities.

6. Providing support to improve Asset Management and maximize its value, and increasing the efficiency of its use and evaluation, in coordination with competent authorities.

**Article (13/Item 5):**

"5- Managing and accounting for fixed assets in government entities, according to the approved accounting framework, and preparing relevant records and reports concerning them."

**Article (16/Item 9):**

"9- Receiving applications from senior citizens and persons with disabilities, in coordination with the competent administrative Units, to complete their transactions and remove any problems or difficulties they may encounter."

### Article 2

An administrative unit called the "Fourth Industrial Revolution Center" shall be added to the administrative units that constitute the Ministry of Finance, as stipulated in Article (1) of the aforementioned Amiri Decision No. (34) of 2022 and its attached organizational chart. This unit shall be affiliated with the Assistant Undersecretary of the Ministry for Economic Affairs.

### Article 3

The Fourth Industrial Revolution Center specializes in the following:

1. Coordination with relevant entities regarding studies related to the activities of the World Economic Forum related to providing supportive environments for the development of new ideas and projects that accelerate progress and integrate technology into human and community life.

2. Coordination with relevant entities to study the changes occurring in local and regional economies with the aim of uniting efforts to benefit from technological tools and digital transactions in State sectors, addressing challenges, and exploiting related opportunities.

3. Coordination with the concerned administrative Unit and relevant entities to support, incentivize, and promote programs for integrating technology into human and community life within the State, contributing to accelerating the achievement of sustainable economic growth and activating the main priorities of national visions.

4. Coordination with relevant entities to enhance the development, transfer, and application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies, robotics, biotechnology, and other related applications for priority sectors.

5. Coordination and cooperation with relevant entities and Fourth Industrial Revolution centers worldwide to exchange expertise and successful experiences in employing technologies and applications related to current and future projects in priority sectors.

6. Coordination and cooperation with governmental and private entities, research and academic institutions regarding the design and experimentation of policies and regulatory frameworks that contribute to enhancing opportunities for benefiting from Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies, robotics, biotechnology, and other related applications, and minimizing their risks at the local level, in accordance with the objectives of national visions for transformation toward an innovation-based economy.

7. Coordination with competent entities to contribute to raising awareness of the accelerating impact of modern technologies and Fourth Industrial Revolution applications in various sectors, and integrating their concepts into General Education and university curricula, to prepare the upcoming generations required for the labor market.

### Article 4

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the date of its issuance. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.
