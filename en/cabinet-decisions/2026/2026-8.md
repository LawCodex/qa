# Council of Ministers Decision No. (8) of 2026 amending certain provisions of the Executive Regulations of the Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Counter-Terrorism Financing Law issued by Council of Ministers Decision No. (41) of 2019

**Cabinet / Council of Ministers,**

After reviewing the Constitution,

And Law of Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Combating Terrorist Financing issued by Law No. (20) of 2019, and its amending laws,

And the Executive Regulations of the Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Combating Terrorist Financing Law issued by Cabinet / Council of Ministers Resolution No. (41) of 2019, as amended by Cabinet / Council of Ministers Resolution No. (14) of 2021,

And Amiri Resolution No. (29) of 1996 regarding Cabinet / Council of Ministers Resolutions that are submitted to the Emir for ratification and issuance,

And upon the proposal of the Governor of Qatar Central Bank,

**Has resolved as follows:**

## Almawad (1-2)

### Article 1

A new Executive Regulation is added to the Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Terrorism Financing Law, as referred to, under Article number (53 bis), with the following text:

**Article (53 bis):**

«The head of the Unit shall have the necessary powers to administer the Unit's affairs and achieve its objectives, in particular the following:

1. Establishing the Unit's plans, programs, and projects, including training and employment, and following up on their implementation.

2. Issuing the Organizational Structure of the Unit.

3. Approving the Unit's annual estimated budget and its final account.

4. Proposing the approval of draft Agreements and memoranda of understanding in which the Unit is a party, with such projects being submitted to the Council of Ministers for the appropriate Decision thereon.

The decisions of the head of the Unit stipulated in items (2) and (3) shall not enter into force except after being approved by the Governor.»

### Article 2

All concerned authorities, each within its jurisdiction, must implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall take effect from the day following its publication in the Official Gazette.
