# Council of Ministers Decision No. (21) of 2023 amending certain provisions of Decision / Resolution No. (36) of 2014 on the establishment of the National Committee for the Customs Clearance System and Trade Facilitation Across Borders

The Council of Ministers,

After reviewing the Constitution,

And the Emiri Decision / Resolution No. (29) of 1996 concerning the Council of Ministers Decisions / Resolutions submitted to the Emir for ratification and issuance,

And the Council of Ministers Decision No. (9) of 1993 regarding the organization of the work of joint and specialized Committees, and its amending Decisions / Resolutions,

And the Council of Ministers Decision No. (36) of 2014 on the establishment of the National Committee for the Customs Clearance System and Trade Facilitation Across Borders, and its amending Decisions / Resolutions,

And upon the proposal of the Minister of Finance

Decided as follows:

## المواد (1-2)

### Article 1

The text of Article (1) of Council of Ministers Decision No. (36) of 2014 referred to above shall be replaced with the following text:

Article (1):

"A committee called the 'National Committee for the Customs Clearance System and Trade Facilitation across Borders' shall be established at the General Authority of Customs. It shall be formed under the chairmanship of the Assistant President of the General Authority of Customs,

with the membership of a representative from each of the following:

1. Ministry of Interior (Director of the Ports Security Department).

2. Ministry of Public Health.

3. Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

4. Ministry of Municipality.

5. Ministry of Environment and Climate Change.

6. General Authority of Customs.

7. Qatar General Organization for Standardization and Metrology.

8. Communications Regulatory Authority (CRA).

9. General Tax Authority (GTA).

10. QatarEnergy.

11. Qatar Chamber of Commerce and Industry.

12. Qatar Ports Management Company.

13. Qatar Airways Company.

Each entity shall choose its representative in the membership of the Committee, provided that their grade is not less than Director of a department related to the specialties of the Committee, and the nominating of the Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, and members of the Committee shall be by a decision of the Minister of Finance.

The secretariat of the Committee shall be handled by one or more employees of the General Authority of Customs, whose assignment, competencies, and remuneration shall be determined by a decision of the Minister of Finance."

### Article 2

على جميع الجهات المختصة، كُل فيما يخُصّه، تنفيذ هذا القرار. ويُعمل به من تاريخ صدوره. ويُنشر في الجريدة الرسمية.
