# Council of Ministers Decision No. (26) of 2018 amending the organization of certain administrative units forming the Ministry of Transportation and Communications and assigning their functions

Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

After reviewing the Constitution,

and on Emiri Decision / Resolution No. (29) of 1996 regarding the Council of Ministers’ Decisions / Resolutions submitted to the Amir for ratification and issuance,

and on Emiri Decision / Resolution No. (8) of 2016 concerning the organizational structure of the Ministry of Transport and Communications,

and upon the proposal of the Minister of Transport and Communications,

decided as follows:

## Materials (1-7)

### Article 1 Canceled

Three administrative units shall be added to the administrative units that constitute the Ministry of Transport and Communications as stipulated in Article (1) of the Amiri Decision No. (8) of 2016 referred to, and the attached organizational chart. These units shall be under the Assistant Undersecretary of the Ministry for Cybersecurity Affairs, with the following names:

A- Office of the Assistant Undersecretary of the Ministry.

B- Critical Information Infrastructure Protection Department.

C- Qatar Computer Emergency Response Team (Q-CERT) Department.

D- Compliance and Data Protection Department.

### Article 2 Canceled

The Infrastructure Protection Directorate is responsible for the following:

1- Developing a national strategy for Information Security and monitoring its implementation after approval in coordination with the competent entities.

2- Establishing policies and standards to enhance the efficiency of protection measures for the operation systems of the national infrastructure for vital information in all sectors, as well as the necessary procedures to ensure compliance.

3- Coordinating with global organizations, governmental and non-governmental bodies, and ensuring the security of infrastructures, protecting networks and systems of vital missions, and evaluating their performance.

4- Applying the rules of Information Security for vital application systems to prevent leakage or dissemination of confidential data, taking necessary actions to prevent breaches, and ensuring business continuity.

5- Establishing Controls and Procedures for communication and maintaining Information Security, protecting Data Privacy exchanged through vital electronic systems and networks, monitoring their implementation, and certifying the commitment of relevant entities.

6- Setting standards and mechanisms for inspecting and accrediting Telecommunications and information technology devices to ensure they are free of leakage mechanisms before their installation and use on vital communications networks such as national networks.

7- Proposing legislative tools projects related to the security of vital information infrastructure, and providing opinions on disputes related to them.

### Article 3 Canceled

The Qatar Computer Emergency Response Team Administration (Q-CERT) specializes in the following:

1- Establishing an electronic monitoring mechanism to activate proactive protection methods and develop applications for malware analysis.

2- Taking the necessary measures to implement the incident response framework to contain and support the recovery of government and non-government devices from harmful cyber activities, in coordination with relevant authorities.

3- Analyzing cyber risks and studying the best ways to avoid them, as well as the ability to flexibly respond to cyber crises and develop solutions through participation with academic institutions and Scientific Research centers.

4- Developing training and awareness programs to support relevant authorities in implementing the established policies.

5- Coordinating with similar organizations and authorities at the regional and international levels in the field of protecting information systems from breaches and cyberattacks.

6- Implementing laws, regulations, and decisions related to electronic transactions and relevant e-commerce.

7- Setting mechanisms for organizing the work of electronic authentication service providers and establishing the necessary procedures to deliver their services with quality.

8- Establishing mechanisms to regulate the use of digital identities to prevent the leakage of confidential information.

9- Providing opinions on disputes related to relevant electronic transactions and e-commerce.

### Article 4 Canceled

The Compliance and Data Protection Department is responsible for the following:

1- Implementing laws, regulations, and decisions related to Data Privacy protection.

2- Issuing certification for technology and information security service providers in the State, according to National Policies standards.

3- Issuing compliance certificates for information security standards and National Policies and reviewing and auditing them.

4- Issuing, reviewing, and developing policies related to the protection of personal Data Privacy.

5- Establishing Controls and Procedures for communication between controllers, processors, and individuals.

6- Publishing the mechanism for preparing studies on Data Privacy protection in general and personal and sensitive data in particular, and developing them.

7- Investigating Complaints and violations related to Data Privacy protection.

8- Proposing draft legislative tools related to Data Privacy protection and providing opinions in disputes related to them.

### Article 5

The two competencies stipulated in Articles (14/ Item 6), (15/ Item 5), respectively, are removed from the competencies of the Department of Road Principles, Traffic Engineering, and Road Safety, as stipulated in Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (8) of 2016 referred to above.

### Article 6

The Cybersecurity Administration stipulated in Article (1/ Fifthly/Item 5) of Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (8) of 2016 referred to and the organizational chart attached thereto, is hereby cancelled. Its responsibilities stipulated in Article (22) of the same Decision / Resolution are also hereby cancelled.

### Article 7

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the day following the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
