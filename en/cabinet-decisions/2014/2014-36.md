# Council of Ministers Decision No. (36) of 2014 Establishing the National Committee for the Customs Clearance System (Single Window)

Cabinet / Council of Ministers,

Having reviewed the Constitution,

And Amiri Decision No. (29) of 1996 regarding Cabinet / Council of Ministers decisions submitted to the Amir for ratification and issuance,

And Amiri Decision No. (37) of 2014 establishing the General Authority of Customs,

And Council of Ministers Decision No. (9) of 1993 regarding the regulation of the work of joint and specialized committees, and the decisions amending it,

And upon the proposal of the Minister of Finance,

Has decided as follows:

## Materials (1-8)

### Article 1 Amended

"A Committee shall be established at the General Authority of Customs called the "National Committee for the Customs Clearance System and Cross-Border Trade Facilitation", formed under the chairmanship of the Assistant Chairman of the General Authority of Customs and with the membership of a representative from each of:

1 - Ministry of Interior.

2 - Ministry of Public Health.

3 - Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

4 - Ministry of Municipality and Environment.

5 - General Authority of Customs.

6 - Qatar General Authority for Standards and Metrology.

7 - Communications Regulatory Authority (CRA).

8 - General Tax Authority (GTA).

9 - Qatar Petroleum.

10 - Qatar Chamber of Commerce and Industry.

11 - Qatar Ports Management Company.

12 - Qatar Airways Company".

### Article 2

The term of membership of the Committee shall be three years, renewable for another similar term or terms.

### Article 3

The Committee shall be responsible for the following:

1- Contributing to facilitating trade exchange operations between the State and all countries of the world, by simplifying Customs Procedures and non-customs restrictions at ports, and consolidating all Import and export operations into a Single Window so that all subject entities can complete all their transactions through the customs system.

2- Ensuring that all entities related to Import and export operations have aligned their electronic systems for integration with the Customs Clearance system (Single Window).

3- Coordinating among the competent entities to ensure Information Exchange between them electronically through the Customs Clearance system.

4- Proposing coordinated border measures that ensure cooperation among all government entities and border operating entities and unify their procedures at all ports with the aim of simplifying procedures.

5- Identifying any procedures or entities that obstruct the movement of goods across borders and submitting Recommendations thereon.

6- Participating in the study of draft trade facilitation agreements proposed by International Organizations or regional organizations and submitting any proposals or Recommendations thereon.

7- Taking the necessary measures that would simplify the procedures for releasing goods across borders, even if they are restricted goods, and deferring final approvals for such goods or those requiring laboratory tests until after their temporary release by customs departments.

8- Enhancing communication and coordination with the private sector with the aim of involving it in issuing decisions related to cross-border trade, and strengthening importers’ and exporters’ compliance with the relevant legislation and regulations.

### Article 4

The Committee shall meet at the invitation of its Chairman / President at least once every two weeks. Its meetings shall not be valid unless attended by a majority of its members, including the Chairman / President or his deputy. The Committee shall issue its recommendations by a majority vote of the members present, and in the event of a tie, the side on which the Chairman / President voted shall prevail.

The Committee shall establish rules for its work, including the place of its meetings, the dates of its meetings, and the rules necessary for exercising its functions.

### Article 5

The Committee may form, from among its members or from other technicians and specialists in its areas of competence, subcommittees or working groups to study matters that arise before it, or may assign one of its members to study any matter falling within its competence.

### Article 6

The Committee may request such documents and data as it deems necessary from ministries or other government bodies, and may invite to attend its meetings any employees of those entities, or other persons with competence and experience in its field of work, whose attendance it deems necessary, to provide the advice, clarifications, or data it requests, without having the right to Voting.

### Article 7

The Committee shall submit to the President of the General Authority of Customs a report on the results of its work every three months, or whenever requested to do so, accompanied by its recommendations and proposals thereon.

### Article 8

All competent authorities, each within its respective jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into force from the date of its issuance and shall be published in the Official Gazette.
