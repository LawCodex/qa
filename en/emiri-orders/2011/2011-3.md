# Amiri Decree No. (3) of 2011 amending the formation of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers

We, Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani, Emir of the State of Qatar,

after reviewing the Constitution,

and Law No. (21) of 2004 regarding ministers,

and Amiri Order No. (1) of 2008 on the Reformation/Restructuring of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, and its amended orders,

and upon the proposal of the Prime Minister,

have ordered as follows:

## Materials (1-3)

### Article 1

Dr. Mohammed Saleh Al-Sadda is appointed as Minister of Energy and Industry.

### Article 2

Every Ruling that contradicts the provisions of this Amiri Decree shall be cancelled.

### Article 3

This Amiri decree shall take effect from the date of its issuance. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.

Hamad Bin Khalifa Al-Thani — Amir of the State of Qatar
