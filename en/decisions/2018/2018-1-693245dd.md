# Decision No. (1) of 2018 on converting Al-Noor Institute for the Blind into a private institution of public benefit

We, Moza bint Nasser,

After reviewing Decree 34 Law No. (21) of 2006 concerning private public benefit institutions and its amendments,

And the Document of Establishment and the amended Articles of Association of the Qatar Foundation for Social Work,

And in accordance with the requirements of the public interest,

We have decided as follows:

## المواد (1-6)

### Article 1

Al-Noor Institute for the Blind is converted into a private institution of public benefit, established by a founding document and articles of association, in accordance with the provisions of Decree 34 referred to above, and shall be called "Al-Noor Center for the Blind", and operates under the umbrella of the Qatar Foundation for Social Work.

### Article 2

All fixed and movable assets owned by the Al-Noor Institute for the Blind, as well as all rights and obligations assigned to or against it, shall be transferred to the "Al-Noor Center for the Blind," established in accordance with the provisions of the previous article, immediately upon completion of the procedures for its establishment as a private institution of public benefit.

### Article 3

The employees of Al-Noor Institute for the Blind will continue to work at Al-Noor Center for the Blind with the same employment status, financial grades, monthly salaries, and all the benefits granted to them, until the official establishment document of the center, its bylaws, and the approval of its internal regulations are finalized.

### Article 4

The current regulations, decisions, systems, and rules in force at Al Noor Institute for the Blind shall remain applicable until the Qatar Foundation for Social Work issues the regulations, decisions, and systems pertaining to the center.

### Article 5

Every Ruling that contradicts the provisions of this Decision / Resolution shall be annulled.

### Article 6

All relevant authorities, each within their respective jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the date of issuance and shall be published in the Official Gazette.
