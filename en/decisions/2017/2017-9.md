# Decision of the Minister of Economy and Commerce No. (9) of 2017 to extend the grace period for adjustment of status pursuant to the provisions of Law / Act No. (20) of 2014 amending certain provisions of Law / Act No. (25) of 2005 regarding the Commercial Register

**Minister of Economy and Commerce,**

After reviewing Law / Act No. (21) of 2005 regarding the ministers, as amended by Law / Act No. (12) of 2010,

and Law / Act No. (20) of 2014 amending certain provisions of Law / Act No. (25) of 2005 regarding the Commercial Register, and in particular Article (3) thereof,

and the Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (20) of 2014 regarding the organizational structure of the Ministry of Economy and Commerce,

and with the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers at its first regular meeting of 2017 held on 4/1/2017, to extend the grace period for companies registered in the Commercial Register to regularize their status for another six months, starting from 8/1/2017, by a Decision / Resolution from the Minister of Economy and Commerce, and in accordance with Cabinet / Council of Ministers Memorandum No. QMW/9/2017 dated 8/1/2017,

**has decided as follows:**

## Almawad (1-2)

### Article 1

The period for regularizing the situations in accordance with the provisions of Law / Act No. (25) of 2005 referred to above, shall be extended for a period of six months starting from 08/01/2017.

### Article 2

All competent authorities, each within its own jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into force as of 8/01/2017, and shall be published in the Official Gazette.
