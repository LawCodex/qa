# Decision of the Chairman of Qatar Tourism (QT) No. (7) of 2026 issuing the Organizational Structure of Qatar Tourism (QT)

**Chairman of Qatar Tourism (QT),**

After reviewing Amiri Resolution No. (29) of 1996 regarding the Cabinet / Council of Ministers' Decisions that are submitted to the Emir for ratification and issuance,

And Amiri Resolution No. (15) of 2021 on the establishment of Qatar Tourism (QT), as amended by Amiri Resolution No. (22) of 2025,

And upon the Cabinet / Council of Ministers' approval of the draft of this Decision / Resolution in its fourth ordinary meeting for the year 2025 held on 22/1/2025,

**Decided as follows:**

## المواد (1-19)

### Article 1

The Organizational Structure of Qatar Tourism consists of the administrative units shown in the organizational chart attached to this Decision / Resolution, which are:

**First: Administrative units Reporting to the Chairman / President:**

1.

Office of the Chairman / President.

2.

Technical Office.

3.

Internal Audit.

4.

Planning, Quality, and Innovation Management.

5.

Legal Affairs Management.

6.

Public Relations and Communication Management.

7.

Hayya Application Management.

**Second: Administrative units Reporting to the Director General:**

1.

Office of the Director General.

2.

Department of International Cooperation.

3.

Tourism Marketing and Promotion Management.

**Third: Operational Activities Affairs:**

1.

Tourism Licensing Management.

2.

Asset Development Management.

3.

Service Excellence Management.

**Fourth: Shared Services Affairs:**

1.

Human Resources Management.

2.

Financial Affairs Management.

3.

General Services Management.

4.

Information Systems Management.

### Article 2

**The Office of Technical Affairs is responsible for the following:**

1. Providing technical opinions on topics referred to it by the Chairman of Qatar Tourism (QT).

2. Studying and evaluating reports, projects, and programs related to the activities of Qatar Tourism (QT), which are referred to it by the Chairman of Qatar Tourism (QT).

3. Researching, studying, and following up on topics referred to it by the Chairman of Qatar Tourism (QT), in coordination with the concerned administrative units.

4. Preparing summaries of topics, reports, and studies that need to be presented to the Chairman of Qatar Tourism (QT), and providing opinions on them.

### Article 3

**The Internal Audit Department specializes in the following:**

1.

Preparing the draft annual risk-based audit plan for Qatar Tourism (QT), submitting it to the President of Qatar Tourism (QT) for approval, and preparing reports on audit results.

2.

Monitoring compliance with the implementation of laws, regulations, and decisions governing operations.

3.

Reviewing financial instructions, proposing necessary amendments, and monitoring their implementation.

4.

Monitoring administrative work and employee appointment procedures, leave, promotions, and other related personnel matters, and verifying compliance with the laws, regulations, and decisions governing these.

5.

Monitoring financial and accounting operations, payment vouchers, procedures of record in accounting books, and ensuring the application of approved rules in this regard.

6.

Risk Assessment related to Financial Auditing and administrative auditing, and proposing procedures to mitigate them.

7.

Following up on corrective actions required from relevant administrative units according to audit results, and preparing Periodic Reports regarding them.

8.

Ensuring the safety of funds, custody, furniture, tools, buildings, and other properties owned by Qatar Tourism (QT) or under its supervision.

9.

Participating in periodic and surprise inventory tasks in stores and custody, ensuring the accuracy of Registration in books, and the integrity of applied procedures.

10.

Following up on the results of reports from regulatory entities and providing suitable Recommendations regarding them.

### Article 4

**The Department / Directorate of Planning, Quality, and Innovation is responsible for the following:**

1.

Preparing the strategic plan for Qatar Tourism (QT), in coordination with the competent authorities and concerned Department / Directorate units, and taking the necessary steps for its approval.

2.

Identifying existing challenges, determining and conducting Risk Assessment of institutional risks that may affect the achievement of Qatar Tourism (QT)'s objectives, and providing recommendations in this regard, in coordination with the concerned Department / Directorate units.

3.

Managing and preparing alternative plans to ensure business continuity at Qatar Tourism (QT) in cases of emergencies and crises, in coordination with the various Department / Directorate units as relevant.

4.

Submitting draft executive plans, projects, and programs for various Department / Directorate units for approval by the Head of Qatar Tourism (QT).

5.

Following up on the implementation of plans related to Qatar Tourism (QT)'s activities, evaluating their outcomes, and submitting periodic reports in this regard.

6.

Studying problems and obstacles encountered in implementing the strategic and executive plans, and proposing appropriate solutions for them.

7.

Evaluating the quality of services provided by Department / Directorate units, in coordination with the competent Department / Directorate.

8.

Reviewing and evaluating performance quality at the level of Department / Directorate units, and proposing ideas to increase their overall efficiency.

9.

Ensuring that work systems and methods are in line with the functions and objectives of Qatar Tourism (QT), proposing plans to develop these systems and methods, and simplifying procedures, in coordination with the concerned Department / Directorate units and the competent Department / Directorate.

10.

Studying problems and obstacles to work at Qatar Tourism (QT), examining their causes, and proposing appropriate solutions.

11.

Receiving initiatives, proposals, and creative ideas, and studying them in coordination with the concerned Department / Directorate units and the competent Department / Directorate.

12.

Monitoring, collecting, and analyzing statistical data related to Qatar Tourism (QT)'s activities, and coordinating with the competent Department / Directorate units regarding the statistical reports required from Qatar Tourism (QT).

13.

Coordinating with concerned Department / Directorate units and the competent authority regarding international indicators related to the activities and functions of Qatar Tourism (QT).

### Article 5

**The Legal Affairs Department is responsible for the following:**

1.

Examining, studying, and following up on legal matters related to the activities of Qatar Tourism (QT), in coordination with the relevant administrative units.

2.

Preparing draft legislative instruments related to Qatar Tourism (QT), and providing opinions on draft laws referred to it.

3.

Providing legal opinions on topics referred to it.

4.

Preparing draft contracts, agreements, and memoranda of understanding relating to the competencies of Qatar Tourism (QT), in coordination with the relevant administrative units.

5.

Investigating facts and violations attributed to Qatar Tourism (QT) employees, preparing the necessary reports on the results of the investigations accompanied by legal opinions and recommendations, presenting them to the Competent Authority, and following up on the implementation of decisions made in this regard.

6.

Following up on disputes and cases in which Qatar Tourism (QT) is a party, in coordination with the competent authorities.

### Article 6

**The Public Relations and Communication Department is responsible for the following:**

1.

Issuing bulletins and media programs aimed at introducing the role, activities, and specializations of Qatar Tourism (QT), in coordination with the relevant administrative units.

2.

Monitoring and following up on topics published in newspapers and media related to Qatar Tourism (QT) and its specializations, presenting them to officials, and preparing responses to them.

3.

Coordinating with the relevant administrative units regarding the preparation of awareness and education plans and programs related to the activities and specializations of Qatar Tourism (QT), and implementing them through various events and activities in coordination with the relevant entities.

4.

Receiving requests and complaints from visitors and referring them to the relevant administrative units, as well as responding to their inquiries.

5.

Handling travel, hospitality, and accommodation arrangements for guests of Qatar Tourism (QT) and for incoming and outgoing delegations, in coordination with the relevant administrative units.

6.

Organizing cultural, social, and sports activities for the employees of Qatar Tourism (QT), and supervising the celebrations organized or participated in by Qatar Tourism (QT).

7.

Organizing Conferences and Seminars and exhibitions held by Qatar Tourism (QT), and preparing the necessary budget for this, in coordination with the relevant administrative units.

### Article 7

**The administration of the Hayya application is responsible for the following:**

1.

Proposing the development of procedures for tourists’ entry into the State through the Hayya application, in coordination with the relevant authority.

2.

Studying tourists’ entry requests and identifying missing requirements according to the approved regulations, in coordination with the relevant authority.

3.

Receiving and studying tourists’ ticket-related issues concerning the Hayya application, and proposing suitable solutions and recommendations in coordination with the relevant authorities.

4.

Carrying out the technological development of the application and ensuring secure operation according to security measures and Data Protection protocols on the application, in coordination with the concerned administrative units and competent entities.

### Article 8

**The Department of International Cooperation is responsible for the following:**

1.

Preparing working papers related to Conferences and Seminars and local, regional, and international meetings concerning Qatar Tourism (QT) activities, in coordination with the relevant administrative units and competent authorities.

2.

Preparing for attendance at conferences and meetings held by regional and international organizations and bodies concerned with issues of Qatar Tourism (QT), in which the State holds membership.

3.

Preparing reports and responses requested by regional and international organizations and bodies regarding Qatar Tourism (QT) activities, in coordination with the relevant administrative units.

4.

Studying and following up on the implementation of the recommendations of regional and international organizations and bodies, regarding Qatar Tourism (QT) activities, in coordination with the competent authorities in the State.

5.

Preparing the necessary reports on the contributions of regional and international organizations and bodies to the development and support of various activities in the work areas of Qatar Tourism (QT), in coordination with the competent authorities.

6.

Following up on the payment of the State’s financial contributions to organizations concerned with the work areas of Qatar Tourism (QT), in which the State is a member, in coordination with the competent authorities.

7.

Studying ways to activate the role of the State in the field of Qatar Tourism (QT) activity with Arab, regional, and international organizations.

8.

Collecting information and research issued by organizations and bodies related to issues of Qatar Tourism (QT) locally, regionally, and internationally, and publishing them for benefit.

9.

Translating documents, books, scientific research, and foreign correspondence related to Qatar Tourism (QT)’s areas of competence.

### Article 9

**The Marketing and Tourism Promotion Department specializes in the following:**

1.

Developing marketing campaign strategies that align with Qatar Tourism (QT)'s objectives and the targeted audience to deploy in tourist source markets.

2.

Preparing tourism and hospitality-related marketing guidelines and rules, publishing them, and following up on their implementation after approval.

3.

Preparing Assessment studies for marketing campaigns, and following up on the application of clear branding guidelines to all marketing materials.

4.

Designing, developing, and updating digital assets, and preparing studies to measure the performance of the digital ecosystem in the tourism and hospitality sector in Qatar.

5.

Developing the tourist journey at the level of the digital ecosystem and the integration of its digital touchpoints to enhance the overall experience, and establishing a database for content displayed on the website and the "Visit Qatar" application.

6.

Collecting and analyzing visitor data through digital channels, analyzing their interests and preferences to determine the objectives and targets of events, and preparing Assessment studies to measure their success.

7.

Coordinating with relevant entities regarding scheduled events throughout the year, proposing the State's annual plan for organizing and holding seasons, festivals, events, exhibitions, and conferences, and other related tourism and hospitality sector events inside and outside the State, and following up on their implementation after approval in coordination with administrative units and related entities.

8.

Studying opportunities to attract the private sector to host related events within the State, analyzing the means of support and facilitation offered to international and local companies to enhance the organization of meetings, conferences, and exhibitions in the State, within the framework of promoting investment and developing the economic sector.

9.

Analytical study of market data and travel trends with the aim of identifying suitable tourism attraction opportunities, cooperating with partner airlines to market and sell tourism packages, and negotiating strategic tourism deals targeting relevant tourist segments, through low-cost charter flights, to enhance the concept of tourism investment.

10.

A comprehensive study of the incentives package offered to travel trade partners, tour operators, and others with the aim of attracting tourists from abroad, coordinating commercial offers, events, and familiarization trips for international partners, and organizing delegations to participate in international trade exhibitions to represent the State, in cooperation with relevant entities, ensuring investment within these initiatives.

### Article 10

**The Tourism Licenses Department is responsible for the following:**

1.

Preparing studies to assess the impact of Regulatory Regulations in the tourism and hospitality sector and aligning them with the objectives and directives of Qatar Tourism (QT) in accordance with best international practices, collecting and analyzing feedback and comments, and preparing Periodic Reports with the necessary Recommendations in this regard.

2.

Preparing and updating the classification standards for Hospitality Establishments, tourism establishments, hospitality facilities, offices organizing festivals and tourism events, entertainment facilities, and tourism and hospitality activities, and following up on their implementation after approval.

3.

Preparing new Regulatory Regulations and guidelines for the tourism and hospitality sector to be in line with international standards and the nature of local conditions, and following up on their implementation after approval.

4.

Receiving and studying applications for Licenses for Hospitality Establishments, tourism establishments, hospitality facilities, tourism and hospitality activities, festivals and tourism events, and the offices organizing them, and deciding on them according to the provisions of the Law / Act.

5.

Classifying, registering, and recording tour guides and workers in the tourism and hospitality sector after issuing or renewing Licenses in accordance with the provisions of the Law / Act.

6.

Studying the processing of applications for issuing tourism Licenses and permits for applicants who have obtained Prior Approval, while specifying the scope of the authorized activities.

7.

Preparing a centralized database that includes tourism and hospitality establishments and the licensed operating entities in the tourism and hospitality sector, and tourists’ complaints related to tourism or hospitality activities or establishments.

8.

Preparing annual Inspection plans for tourism and hospitality establishments and activities and the offices organizing them in accordance with the provisions of the Law / Act.

9.

Launching campaigns to supervise various tourism and hospitality activities and evaluate them, and monitoring the License status for each licensed establishment to require their renewal after payment of the prescribed fees.

10.

Verifying the validity of the Licenses and classifications of the inspected establishments, in addition to safety, cleanliness, and maintenance of facilities including accommodation, dining areas, and entertainment facilities, monitoring compliance with sustainability practices, environmental protection, and waste management in coordination with the relevant authorities, and identifying the violating establishment and reporting it to the concerned authorities to take the necessary actions to enforce the Law / Act.

11.

Studying the cancellation of Licenses for Hospitality Establishments, tourism, or hospitality establishments, or tourism or hospitality activities, or festival and events organizing offices, or festival or tourism event Licenses that violate the provisions of the tourism Regulatory Regulations, recording violations committed by the licensees under this Law / Act and the measures taken regarding them in a special register, proposing a ban on the practice of licensed tourism or hospitality activities, or festivals and events in the event of repeated or serious violations.

12.

Receiving and studying appeals and disputes related to Licenses and Regulatory Regulations, and proposing appropriate Recommendations in this regard.

13.

Coordinating with training entities to accredit tourism control specialists to improve supervision of tourism and hospitality activities in the State.

### Article 11

**The Asset Development Department is responsible for the following:**

1.

Establishing and updating a database for the assets of the tourism and hospitality sector in the State, identifying and assessing the tourism assets in the State that are considered attractive sites preferred by tourists.

2.

Preparing a roadmap for the development and investment of tourism and hospitality sector assets, identifying the tourism development areas to be developed in both private and governmental sectors, and studying the prioritization of asset development plans in these areas in coordination with the competent authorities.

3.

Studying opportunities to attract the private sector to develop tourism and hospitality sector assets in the State, preparing Financial Assessment studies for those assets, determining the financial feasibility of their development, and the necessary requirements regarding them.

4.

Cooperating with entities from both the government and private sectors to find main financing partners for the development of tourism and hospitality sector assets, and proposing partnership models in coordination with the competent authorities.

5.

Preparing standards and guidelines for private sector companies operating assets owned by Qatar Tourism, monitoring the compliance of tourism and hospitality sector asset operators, and preparing Periodic Reports with appropriate recommendations regarding them.

6.

Preparing and updating guidelines for the use of tourism and hospitality sector assets to ensure long-term use with continuous Assessment of their development.

### Article 12

**The Service Excellence Administration is responsible for the following:**

1.

Preparing a comprehensive plan to collect visitor experience data (visitor journey) and identify the main touchpoints of the experience, as well as the strategic locations of tourist information service centers.

2.

Preparing analytical studies of visitor data and feedback and recommending appropriate corrective actions to develop products and services provided to them, in coordination with tourism sector partners.

3.

Preparing a comprehensive strategy for the visitor journey and supporting the tourist by providing hotlines, disseminating information, and the main services such as tourist maps, information centers in strategic locations, the virtual travel companion, and others, in cooperation with relevant entities in the tourism and hospitality sector.

4.

Evaluating the interests and preferences of tourists to develop customized tourism products in collaboration with the International Markets Administration and relevant partners, and aligning pricing strategies with demand volume and seasonal trends.

5.

Establishing a system for continuously collecting feedback and opinions to continuously improve quality based on ongoing insights, inputs, and assessments.

6.

Preparing a competency framework for employees of the tourism and hospitality sector, and supporting and developing education and training curricula associated with the tourism and hospitality sector in Universities and educational and vocational institutions in the State in coordination with the competent authorities.

7.

Organizing and arranging the necessary training courses to qualify all employees in the tourism and hospitality sector, and supervising them, in coordination with the competent authorities.

8.

Collecting feedback and opinions from participants, trainers, and other partners to refine and improve curricula and develop learning resources to enhance learning and effective communication within the tourism ecosystem.

9.

Proposing the necessary legislation in the field of tourism and hospitality to achieve Sustainable Development, in coordination with the competent administrative Unit.

10.

Establishing partnerships with entities that grant accredited certificates to assess the sustainability of tourism companies.

11.

Preparing accreditation programs and awards for tourism and hospitality establishments whose performance exceeds the specified standards.

### Article 13

**Human Resources Management is responsible for the following:**

1.

Implementing laws, regulations, and systems related to personnel affairs.

2.

Preparing studies on Human Resources planning at Qatar Tourism (QT) and identifying the job and staff needs of Qatar Tourism (QT), in coordination with the Competent Authority and various administrative units.

3.

Preparing drafts for job description, classification, and ranking, as well as following up on their implementation and development.

4.

Preparing the draft budget for Chapter One, in coordination with the relevant administrative units.

5.

Implementing Performance Evaluation procedures for employees.

6.

Carrying out procedures for appointment, transfer, secondment, and delegation.

7.

Examining employees’ entitlement to leaves in accordance with the law.

8.

Preparing studies related to the organizational structure of Qatar Tourism (QT), and providing necessary proposals to develop its administrative organization.

9.

Identifying training needs for Qatar Tourism (QT) staff, in coordination with various administrative units, and following up on their implementation and evaluating their effectiveness.

10.

Taking necessary measures to allocate government housing and disburse furniture allowances for Qatar Tourism (QT) employees, in coordination with the Competent Authorities.

11.

Establishing and updating databases related to Qatar Tourism (QT) staff affairs.

### Article 14

**The Financial Affairs Department is responsible for the following:**

1.

Enforcement of financial laws, regulations, and systems related to the work of Qatar Tourism (QT).

2.

Preparation of the annual budget draft and final accounts for Qatar Tourism (QT), in coordination with the relevant administrative units.

3.

Conducting procurement, tenders, and auctions, according to the systems and rules in force in the State.

4.

Supervising the implementation of allocations and auditing revenue and expenditure accounts.

5.

Preparation of payment documents and all other financial transactions.

6.

Collecting fees and service charges provided by Qatar Tourism (QT).

### Article 15

**The Public Services Department specializes in the following:**

1.

Implementing the laws, regulations, and administrative systems related to Qatar Tourism (QT).

2.

Providing the requirements of Qatar Tourism (QT) and its various administrative units with the necessary supplies and equipment to perform their tasks, in coordination with the concerned administrative units.

3.

Providing and securing the transportation needs of Qatar Tourism (QT) in coordination with the specialized administrative units and ensuring their optimal and proper usage.

4.

Receiving, delivering, classifying, and recording incoming and outgoing mail.

5.

Organizing the archive of Qatar Tourism (QT) and preserving documents using the latest methods.

6.

Providing, allocating, and maintaining administrative buildings for Qatar Tourism (QT).

7.

Providing office services and furniture to all Qatar Tourism (QT) buildings.

8.

Supervising cleaning operations in all Qatar Tourism (QT) buildings.

9.

Providing the necessary agricultural services for Qatar Tourism (QT).

10.

Carrying out all administrative service tasks.

11.

Supervising the warehouses of Qatar Tourism (QT).

### Article 16

**The Information Systems Department specializes in the following:**

1.

Preparing plans and policies for the use of computers in Qatar Tourism (QT) activities, and following up on their implementation.

2.

Programming, storing, retrieving, and developing information systems and data necessary for the activities of Qatar Tourism (QT).

3.

Preparing plans for disaster recovery in the event of electronic incidents, or to reduce or escalate their occurrence, in accordance with information security policies and guidelines, in coordination with the relevant administrative units.

4.

Providing and maintaining the devices, programs, and electronic networks necessary for automated work systems at Qatar Tourism (QT), in coordination with the relevant administrative units.

5.

Designing, operating, and managing databases and information of various kinds.

6.

Providing the necessary technical support to users of electronic networks at Qatar Tourism (QT), and training them on the use of systems, programs, computers, and their accessories.

7.

Creating, monitoring, and updating the Qatar Tourism (QT) website on the World Wide Web.

### Article 17

A decision from the Chairman / President is issued to specify the responsibilities of both the Office of the Chairman / President and the Office of the Director General.

### Article 18

A decision may be issued by the President of Qatar Tourism (QT), based on the proposal of the Director General of Qatar Tourism (QT), and the approval of the Civil Service and Government Development Bureau, after coordination with the General Secretariat of the Council of Ministers, to establish, abolish, or merge departments in the administrative units that constitute Qatar Tourism (QT), as required by the public interest, and to assign or amend their competencies.

### Article 19

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall be effective from the date of issuance and published in the official gazette.
