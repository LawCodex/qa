# Decision / Resolution of the Director General of the Qatar News Agency No. (110) of 2026 amending some provisions of Decision / Resolution No. (157) of 2024 regarding the determination of the fees for news services provided by the Qatar News Agency

**Director General of the Qatar News Agency,**

After reviewing the Amiri Decision No. (50) of 2014 on the Regulation of the Qatar News Agency,

And the Decision of the Director General of the Qatar News Agency No. (157) of 2024 specifying the fees for news services provided by the Qatar News Agency,

And the approval of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers for the draft of this Decision in its regular meeting (22) for the year 2026 held on 17/06/2026,

**Has decided as follows:**

## Almawad (1-2)

### Article 1

The following Item number (4) is added to Article (1/ Third Item) of Decision / Resolution No. (157) of 2024 referred to above, with the following text:

**Article (1) Third / Item 4:**

«4 - Issuance/annual renewal of the press accreditation card for non-Qatari correspondents and editors assigned by their media organizations and accredited by the agency: (100) Qatari Riyals or its equivalent in foreign currencies».

### Article 2

All relevant authorities, each within its jurisdiction, must implement this Decision / Resolution, and it shall be effective from the day following its publication in the Official Gazette.

أحمد بن سعيد الرميحي — Director General of Qatar News Agency
